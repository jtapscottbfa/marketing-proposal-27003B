## How to Use

1. Populate `REQ ID` and `Requirement` columns from `rfp_shred.md`
2. For each requirement, identify the proposal **Volume** and **Section** that responds
3. Note the **Page / Para** once the draft is written
4. Mark `✅` when the reviewer confirms the requirement is fully addressed
5. Any `❌` at Gold Team = submission risk
