# Bottom Line

- **Opportunity:** G Force (FBSG, 8(a)) needs a Pennsylvania-licensed PE to serve as the Contractor's **Delegated Design Engineer** for a new 9,000-10,000 CFM wood-dust collection and explosion-protection system (NFPA 664 / 68 / 69) at the NFPC pattern shop, Bldg 592.
- **What G Force asked for:** "peer review on the mechanical calculations needed" (E. Bruno, 4 Nov 2026).
- **What the contract requires:** a complete, PA-sealed delegated design (duct aerodynamics, NFPA 68 venting, NFPA 69 isolation, structural/seismic anchorage, controls integration), PE witness of the duct leakage test, and a sealed field **Certification of Compliance** (UFGS 23 51 43.00 20).
- **How we priced it:** BFA verifies and seals the manufacturer-engineered design across six trades, with two site visits. A full independent design is priced as an option.
- **Draft base fee: $49,641** (lump sum $47,775 labor + $1,866 expenses; 276 hours; blended $173/hr). **Labor rates are placeholders.** Replace them with BFA's rate sheet.
- **Target submission: Fri 13 Nov 2026, 12:00 ET (assumed).** G Force has not stated a due date.
