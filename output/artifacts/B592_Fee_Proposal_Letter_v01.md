# Scope of Services

Bowman, Foster & Associates, Inc. will review the delegated design
mechanical calculation package furnished by the Client (prepared by the
dust collector manufacturer and/or the Contractor's Delegated Design
Professional Engineer) for conformance with the contract documents and
the codes and standards listed above, and will issue one round of
written review comments.

***<span class="smallcaps">Project Phasing</span>***

1.  *Kickoff and Document Review*

    1.  Receive the delegated design calculation package and
        manufacturer data from the Client.

    2.  Review the mechanical drawings, schedules (M-601, M-602), and
        specifications to establish the review basis.

2.  *Mechanical Calculation Peer Review*

    1.  *Duct Aerodynamic Calculations*

        1.  Verify node-by-node static pressure losses, duct and runout
            sizing, system balance, and minimum transport velocities
            (mains 3,500 FPM minimum; runouts 4,000 FPM minimum) per
            ACGIH and NFPA 664.

    2.  *Deflagration Venting (NFPA 68)*

        1.  Verify vent area based on collector volume, reduced pressure
            (Pred), and the specified dust characteristics (Kst 200
            bar-m/s minimum, Pmax 8.5 bar minimum), and vent reaction
            forces.

    3.  *Explosion Isolation (NFPA 69)*

        1.  Verify flap valve placement (Lmin/Lmax) against the
            manufacturer's test certification, and the rotary airlock
            isolation criteria.

    4.  *Recirculation (NFPA 664)*

        1.  Confirm whether exhaust air is recirculated and whether an
            abort gate is required.

    5.  *Collector and Fan Performance*

        1.  Verify fan selection against the system curve, air-to-cloth
            ratio, filter pressure drop, and duct pressure class.

    6.  *Review Comments*

        1.  Issue one consolidated written comment log to the Client.

3.  *Construction Period Services*

    1.  Not included. Available as an Additional Service.
