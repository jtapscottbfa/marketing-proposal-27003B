## What Is a Win Theme?

A win theme is a **client-centric, benefit-focused statement** that:
- Addresses a hot button (something the client cares most about)
- Highlights our discriminating capability or approach
- Is provable (backed by past performance or data)
- Is woven consistently through every volume
