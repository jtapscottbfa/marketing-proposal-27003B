Structural engineering scope on this project centered on foundation design and equipment platform design for the replacement substation at a waterfront site in Portsmouth, Virginia. The project's location on private property adjacent to the Elizabeth River, with ground conditions typical of the tidal Virginia coastal plain, required a foundation solution suited to soft or variable soils at a site with flood-code elevation requirements.

The structural design utilized helical pile foundations and cast-in-place concrete mat foundations to support the new substation equipment, switchgear, and associated infrastructure. New equipment platforms were designed in cast-in-place concrete at elevations compliant with applicable flood codes, ensuring the replacement substation devices could remain operational and code-conforming through design flood events. This integration of foundation depth and platform elevation was a primary driver of the structural design approach.

Structural scope also supported the overall shoreline stabilization effort. While the steel sheet pile bulkhead was primarily a civil/waterfront scope element, the structural design coordinated on the interface between the bulkhead system and the substation's foundation and platform elements within the constrained 3,000 SF compound.

The structural scope was completed as part of the Final Basis of Design issued June 21, 2021, and developed into a full Design-Bid-Build construction package. The construction scope included concrete cast-in-place work and structural steel, with an Estimated Construction Cost of $3,000,000 across all disciplines.
