Geotechnical engineering was part of the design team for the Replace Deperming Station Substation DS-20 project in Portsmouth, Virginia. The discipline supported the foundation design effort for a waterfront substation site on private property adjacent to the Elizabeth River — a setting with tidal soil conditions that informed the structural team's selection of helical pile foundations and cast-in-place concrete mat foundations for the new substation equipment and platforms.

A topographic survey covering approximately 0.42 acres was performed, encompassing the substation compound and the adjacent access corridor necessary to reach the site through the neighboring industrial property. This survey area, larger than the roughly 3,000 SF substation compound itself, reflects the access constraints imposed by the unusual site arrangement of leased private property accessed through an adjacent facility.
