Electrical engineering was the primary discipline on this project, encompassing full design of the replacement substation at a Navy deperming station in Portsmouth, Virginia. The core scope was replacement and upgrade of the existing DS-20 step-down transformer and secondary switchgear, combined with modernization of substation protection systems for 34.5 kV and 13.8 kV distribution equipment.

The electrical design addressed the full substation protection scheme — including grounding systems and lightning protection — for an outdoor facility serving a specialized military mission: supplying high-voltage power to the deperming range used to reduce the magnetic signature of fleet vessels. The design was required to comply with UFC electrical standards applicable to Navy utility infrastructure.

A notable design intervention was the correction of a client-specification error identified during design development. The SAES statement of A-E services had specified a single-phase 50 kVA service transformer; the electrical design team identified this as inconsistent with the three-phase nature of the distribution system and the facility's actual load requirements. The Basis of Design formally revised the specification to a three-phase 75 kVA transformer (34,500 V Delta – 208Y/120 V) — a change documented in a BOD Clarification attachment that prevented a code-noncompliant procurement and potential construction change order.

The electrical scope was developed to design-complete status, with the Final Basis of Design issued June 21, 2021, and a full Design-Bid-Build construction contract package prepared for the $3,000,000 estimated construction cost.
