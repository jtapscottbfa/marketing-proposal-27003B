Plumbing engineering on the Crawford Circle Downtown Gateway Project centered on the design of a multi-nozzle decorative fountain system for the City of Portsmouth, Virginia. The system is built around a DBVB-Series Direct Burial Vault — a pre-assembled, temperature-controlled RCF80 PVC vault with stainless-steel top-access grates — which consolidates the sand filter pump, main circulation pump, sump pump, double-check valve backflow preventer, water meter, and water fill line into a single maintainable unit buried beneath the roundabout island.

Type K copper piping distributes pressurized water from the vault to multiple nozzle stations: rigid copper runs feed individual spray heads while flexible copper manifolds form the inner and outer distribution loops. Two nozzle types define the display: GEY-Series Geyser Nozzles (machined cast-bronze) produce vertical jets to 10 feet, and VEN-Series Vee Nozzles (one-piece brass, water-level-independent) produce fan-spray jets to 4 feet. A brass ball-valve manifold provides independent spray-height control at each station without specialized automation. Supporting system components include an FAS-08 Anti-Vortex/Diverter Plate and Sump, a CWL-002WD Wall-Mounted Overflow/Sensor for water-level monitoring and overflow drainage, FDF-Series flush-mount in-pool floor drain fittings, FSK-150J front-access skimmers for debris collection, and wailer-line scuppers. All fountain equipment is from Fountain People, Inc. (San Marcos, TX). Domestic water piping complies with Specification Section 22 11 16.
