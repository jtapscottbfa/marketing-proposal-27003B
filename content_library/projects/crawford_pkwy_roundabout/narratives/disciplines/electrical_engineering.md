Electrical engineering on the Crawford Circle Downtown Gateway Project provided power distribution, lighting, and grounding infrastructure for the decorative fountain installation at Crawford Circle in downtown Portsmouth, Virginia. The electrical scope was developed for the City of Portsmouth under a design-bid-build delivery and is documented through seven specification sections: LED Exterior Lighting (26 56 19), Low-Voltage Electrical Power Conductors and Cables (26 05 19), Grounding and Bonding for Electrical Systems (26 05 26), Raceway and Boxes for Electrical Systems (26 05 33), Sleeves and Sleeve Seals for Electrical Raceways and Cabling (26 05 44), Identification for Electrical Systems (26 05 53), and Wiring Devices including GFCI receptacles (26 27 26). An existing electrical vault shown on the fountain plan drawing (sheet P401) was incorporated into the site electrical design.

The specification suite covers the full complement of low-voltage site electrical infrastructure required for exterior lighting and fountain power: conductors, conduit systems, grounding and bonding for safety in a water-feature environment, and GFCI protection consistent with NEC requirements for fountain and wet-location installations. Detailed electrical drawings — fixture layout, circuiting, load schedule, and panel schedule — are not captured in the four-sheet drawing subset on file; those sheets were not included in the available project documents. The electrical scope description above is derived from the specification section inventory in the source record.
