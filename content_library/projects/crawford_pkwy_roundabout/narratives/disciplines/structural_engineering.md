Structural engineering was part of the multi-discipline design team for the Crawford Circle Downtown Gateway Project in Portsmouth, Virginia. McPherson Design Group served as structural engineer, listed alongside the engineer of record in the title blocks of the final drawing set. The structural scope supported the fountain installation and associated site construction within the roundabout island, including concrete work around buried fountain equipment — the plumbing drawings specify, for example, that a 4-inch-thick concrete surround is required around the anti-vortex sump body.

Specific structural scope details beyond this — foundation design, retaining elements, equipment pad sizing, or other structural analyses — are not captured in the source documents on file. The four drawing sheets included in the project record cover the plumbing discipline only (sheets P001, P401, P501, P502 of a 14-sheet set); structural drawings were not part of the provided subset. A full description of the structural engineering scope would require review of the complete drawing set and any structural design reports not currently in the project record.
