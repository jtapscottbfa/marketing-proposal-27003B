The Crawford Circle Downtown Gateway Project transformed a traffic roundabout in downtown Portsmouth, Virginia into a prominent civic amenity centered on a multi-nozzle decorative fountain with a sculptural element. Commissioned by the City of Portsmouth, the project occupies the central island of Crawford Circle at the intersection of Crawford Street and Bart Street, and represents a design-bid-build engagement delivered under the lead firm's internal project number 20-056. A topographic survey dated April 2020 marks the start of the project record; final design drawings were issued in mid-2021 with a title-block revision date of August 23, 2021.

The design scope encompasses plumbing engineering for the fountain system, electrical engineering for exterior lighting and power distribution, and structural engineering provided by a subconsultant. The fountain system is anchored by a direct-burial vault housing circulation, filtration, and pump equipment; copper piping distributes water to multiple spray stations equipped with cast-bronze geyser nozzles (10-foot vertical jets) and brass vee-spray nozzles (4-foot fan jets). A ball-valve manifold provides variable spray height control, and integrated water-level sensing and overflow drainage support reliable operation. LED exterior lighting completes the nighttime character of the installation. The lead firm served as prime consultant and engineer of record, with Virginia-licensed professional engineering oversight. The project demonstrates capability in specialty water feature plumbing, civic electrical systems, and public realm infrastructure for a local government client.
