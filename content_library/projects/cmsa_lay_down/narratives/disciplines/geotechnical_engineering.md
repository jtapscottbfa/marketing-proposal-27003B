Geotechnical engineering services for the CMSA Yard Expansion were provided by subconsultant G E T Solutions under a separate subconsultant authorization with the prime A-E team. The scope of services encompassed subsurface exploration and geotechnical engineering in support of the civil site design for the gravel laydown yard expansion at Jefferson Lab's Newport News, Virginia campus.

G E T Solutions' authorized fee for geotechnical services was $16,805.50. The geotechnical investigation provided subsurface characterization of the expansion area to support earthwork design recommendations, subgrade assessment, and design of the gravel surface section — specified as 9 inches of crushed stone over a woven geotextile subgrade stabilization layer in the conceptual design documents.
