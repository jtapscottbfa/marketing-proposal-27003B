Surveying and mapping services for the CMSA Yard Expansion supported the development of the existing conditions base plan used across all three design alternatives. Jefferson Lab provided a compiled site survey of the CMSA area to the design team; subconsultant VHB performed a limited on-ground verification survey to confirm the accuracy of the JLab-provided base data before incorporating it into the design documents.

The final conceptual design's existing conditions drawing (Sheet C001) presents existing site features derived from the compiled survey: the existing CMSA gravel laydown yard extents, Buildings 32, 34, and 52, utility corridors (sanitary sewer, domestic water, cryogenic, electrical, and conduit lines), drainage structures with invert elevations, the existing wetland area, and the woodland treeline. Horizontal and vertical control referenced Jefferson Lab site control. Spot elevations and drainage structure inverts are shown throughout the base plan.

The surveying approach for this project — reliance on an owner-furnished compiled survey supplemented by VHB's limited field verification — is consistent with conceptual design practice for active federal facilities where comprehensive new topographic survey may not be authorized until a design option is selected and detailed design is initiated. The source record does not include a standalone topographic survey report or field notes. The surveying and mapping discipline contributed to the project through coordination with Jefferson Lab's facilities data and VHB's field verification work; a full new topographic survey engagement is not documented in the available project record.
