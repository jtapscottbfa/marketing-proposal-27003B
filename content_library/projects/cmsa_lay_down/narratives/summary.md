The design team served as prime A-E consultant for the Jefferson Lab CMSA Yard Expansion at Thomas Jefferson National Accelerator Facility in Newport News, Virginia — a U.S. Department of Energy national particle physics research campus operated by Jefferson Science Associates (JSA). The project expanded the Central Material Storage Area (CMSA) gravel laydown yard to increase material storage capacity in support of active accelerator facility operations. Work was contracted under federal IDIQ Subcontract No. JSA 21-C0683, Task Order 1, with a total project budget of $800,000 to $1,000,000 encompassing design, construction, and construction administration.

The design team developed three site layout alternatives ranging from approximately 1.85 to 2.80 acres of new gravel laydown area, each providing a secured and lit expansion yard with stormwater management best management practices (BMPs). All options featured a 9-inch crushed stone gravel surface over woven geotextile, 7-foot chain-link security fencing with barbed wire, a powered sliding gate with card-reader access control, and 250W LED site lighting with radiation monitors integrated into each light pole — a requirement driven by radioactive material storage on the DOE campus. Stormwater design incorporated Level 2 Constructed Wetlands and Extended Detention Basins to meet Virginia regulatory requirements. Civil engineering design was led by subconsultant VHB; geotechnical investigation services were provided by G E T Solutions. Preliminary and final conceptual design reports, including full drawing sets and opinions of probable construction cost, were delivered to Jefferson Lab in October and November 2021, respectively.
