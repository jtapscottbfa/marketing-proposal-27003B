Fire protection engineering services for the Building 9023 project at Fort Gregg-Adams involved the consolidation of two existing fire alarm and mass notification systems into a single integrated system. This system merger is technically significant: combining separate systems requires coordination of the fire alarm control panel, device layouts (smoke detectors, pull stations, notification appliances), and integration with Army mass notification system (MNS) requirements under applicable NFPA standards and DoD fire protection criteria. The fire protection scope is documented in the SOW under section 28 46 11.

Design drawings for the fire alarm and MNS system were produced across sheets F-101 through F-104, with F-102 confirmed available in the source record. The project number PB00003-4J is identified in the F-102 title block. Text extraction from the fire alarm drawing sheet yielded minimal content (coordinate grid labels only); detailed device inventory and system configuration are not captured in the source record and were not legible without visual rendering of the fire alarm plan sheets. Three additional fire alarm sheets (F-101, F-103, F-104) are referenced in the drawing index but were not included in the available intake files. A complete fire protection narrative would benefit from the full drawing set and the fire alarm panel submittal or system description.
