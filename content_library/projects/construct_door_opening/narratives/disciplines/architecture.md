Architecture services for the Building 9023 renovation at Fort Gregg-Adams encompassed design documentation and construction document deliverables for the full interior reconfiguration of the single-story facility. The architectural scope included demolition drawings documenting removal of existing partitions, flooring, ceiling grid and acoustical tile, casework, and a modular cubicle system (Demolition Plan D-101). The proposed layout (A-101) introduced new metal stud partitions with 5/8" gypsum board and sound batt insulation — approximately 40 LF of new partitions — creating a new Corridor 114 and reconfigured Multi-Purpose Room 110 within the existing building footprint.

Door and opening work included the primary task of constructing a new 3'-0" × 7'-0" opening through an existing masonry wall, installation of three new solid-core wood doors in metal frames, and an aluminum transaction window. Interior finishes specified new 24 oz 2'×2' carpet tile and VCT flooring, acoustical ceiling tile, Roppe 700 series wall base, and Level 4 interior painting in Sherwin-Williams SW6119 Antique White or approved equal. Architecture coordinated with structural, mechanical, electrical, and fire protection engineering disciplines to integrate all systems within the new floor plan. Record drawings documenting as-built conditions were issued in June 2025.
