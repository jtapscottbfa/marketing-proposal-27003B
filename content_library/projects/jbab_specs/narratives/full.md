## Project Overview

This record documents the firm's role as MEP engineer of record on project 21-015, producing the specification set for the Joint Base Anacostia-Bolling (JBAB) Mini-JOC (Job Order Contract) program under NAVFAC Washington's Multiple Award Construction Contract (MACC). The primary deliverable is a UFGS-derived MEP specification set issued as a progress submittal on June 9, 2021, covering Division 01 general requirements and Division 02 asbestos remediation requirements. The record also encompasses four MACC technical standard attachments — Electrical Standard Requirements (2019), Mechanical Systems Standard Requirements (2020), Fire Protection Standards (2020), and Environmental Protection Standards (2020) — which are the base standards governing all MACC construction work at JBAB and Joint Base Andrews (JBA).

This is a reference standards library record rather than a single discrete construction project. It represents the firm's specification-authoring engagement and its accumulated technical knowledge of JBAB MACC facility requirements across all MEP and fire protection disciplines. The intake folder does not include a basis of design, statement of work, or drawings — no construction financials or project completion dates are available in the source record.

## Scope

The specification set was written to support multi-traded JOC task orders covering maintenance, repair, alteration, and minor new construction work. Per Section 01 11 00: "The work includes providing the labor, equipment, and materials to perform multi-traded maintenance, repair, alteration, and minor new construction projects as shown on the Contract Documents provided with each Task Order." The geographic coverage spans multiple DoD sites in Maryland, Virginia, and the District of Columbia, with Joint Base Anacostia-Bolling as the primary named installation. Normal working hours are 7:00 a.m. to 4:30 p.m., Monday through Friday, or as specified by individual task order.

The specification set addresses: mechanical/HVAC systems, electrical power distribution and lighting, plumbing, fire suppression, fire alarm and mass notification, hazardous material abatement (including asbestos remediation), and security and access control requirements for work on a federally secured joint base. Contractors accessing JBAB must be US citizens, provide a minimum of five working days' advance notice to the Security Department, submit to criminal history review, and enroll in DBIDS or NCACS.

## Key Challenges and Resolutions

**JBAB-specific approved manufacturer requirements.** JBAB's MACC program imposes strict approved-manufacturer requirements that differ from typical commercial or general federal facility standards. All chiller specifications must name Trane as the sole approved manufacturer; no other manufacturer is accepted without prior JBA or JBAB approval. Thermostat control systems must specify Siemens as the sole approved manufacturer, with no substitutions permitted without prior approval. Variable refrigerant flow (VRF) systems are not permitted unless specifically requested by JBA or when no other suitable option is available and approved by the JBA project manager. All new HVAC control systems must integrate with the base's Energy Monitoring and Control System (EMCS). Specification writing for this program required incorporating these restrictions as hard technical requirements rather than preferred alternatives, protecting both the client and contractors from non-compliant submittals.

**Dual-jurisdiction regulatory environment.** JBAB's location within the District of Columbia places it under DC's regulatory authority (DCMR) rather than Maryland or Virginia state regulations, which is the more common regulatory environment for NAVFAC Washington projects. Environmental compliance under MACC Attachment 7 requires adherence to DC Department of Energy and Environment (DOEE) permitting for air quality, stormwater, and erosion and sediment control. Disturbance thresholds under DC regulations are notably low: any land disturbance of 50 square feet or more triggers an ESC plan submission to DOEE; 5,000 square feet triggers a stormwater management plan; and 1 acre triggers an NPDES Construction General Permit and SWPPP. The specification set incorporates these thresholds to ensure contractors understand their regulatory obligations for even minor earthwork associated with MEP task orders.

**Fire alarm and mass notification system specificity.** JBAB's fire alarm program mandates a system type not commonly encountered on standard commercial or even other federal projects: the Monaco MAAP X addressable panel is the only approved fire alarm/mass notification system. All facilities must provide point-to-point reporting to the Monaco D-21 receiver at fire station #1 (building 1287). The system must function as a combination fire alarm and mass notification system with specific prescribed mass notification messages. The fire alarm radio frequency for JB Andrews is 138.9250 MHz. Incorporating these requirements into the specification set ensures that task order contractors do not inadvertently specify non-compliant systems that would require costly replacement.

**Security and access control rigor.** The joint base environment imposes access control requirements that directly affect contractor scheduling and staffing. All contractor and subcontractor personnel must be US citizens, and a minimum of five working days' advance notice is required to initiate the enrollment process. Criminal history checks and DBIDS or NCACS enrollment are mandatory before any personnel may access the installation. The specification set encodes these requirements in Division 01 to ensure they appear in every task order package and cannot be overlooked.

## Outcomes

The firm produced and issued a comprehensive UFGS-derived MEP specification set as a progress submittal, establishing the technical framework for multi-traded JOC task orders at JBAB under the JOC2021 contract vehicle. This specification set, combined with the MACC base technical standard attachments, provides the complete technical and regulatory framework for MEP maintenance, repair, alteration, and minor new construction work across JBAB and affiliated DoD sites in the National Capital Region. The record documents the firm's detailed working knowledge of JBAB-specific MEP and fire protection standards, the DC regulatory environment, and the NAVFAC Washington MACC program structure — expertise directly applicable to future JBAB Mini-JOC task orders and other NAVFAC Washington MACC pursuits.
