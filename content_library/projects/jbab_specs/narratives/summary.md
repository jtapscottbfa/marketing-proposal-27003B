This record documents the preparation of MEP specification standards for the Joint Base Anacostia-Bolling (JBAB) Mini-JOC (Job Order Contract) program, performed under NAVFAC Washington's Multiple Award Construction Contract (MACC). As prime consultant and MEP engineer of record (project 21-015), the firm authored a comprehensive UFGS-derived specification set — a progress submittal issued June 9, 2021 — covering multi-trade maintenance, repair, alteration, and minor new construction work at JBAB and other DoD sites in Maryland, Virginia, and the District of Columbia. The specification package spans mechanical, electrical, plumbing, fire protection, and hazardous material abatement scopes, structured to support individual task order delivery across a broad range of facility types on the joint base.

Beyond the BFA-authored specification set, this record incorporates the MACC base technical standard attachments governing all JBAB construction work: Electrical Standard Requirements (2019), Mechanical Systems Standard Requirements (2020), Fire Protection Standards (2020), and Environmental Protection Standards (2020). These standards establish JBAB-specific requirements including 7,600/13,200V medium-voltage distribution, Trane-only chillers, Siemens-only thermostat controls, mandatory EMCS integration, Monaco MAAP X fire alarm/mass notification systems, and DCMR-compliant environmental protocols under DC's regulatory jurisdiction. This record demonstrates deep familiarity with the technical and regulatory environment for JBAB MACC task order work across all MEP and fire protection disciplines, and serves as a reference library for future NAVFAC Washington MACC pursuits.
