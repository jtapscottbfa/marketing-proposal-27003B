The mechanical engineering scope for the JBAB Mini-JOC specification set addresses HVAC system design and maintenance requirements across task order work at Joint Base Anacostia-Bolling and other DoD sites under the NAVFAC Washington MACC program. The MACC Mechanical Systems Standard Requirements (2020, Attachment 8) establish the technical baseline governing all new and replacement HVAC work on the installation.

Key JBAB-specific mechanical requirements include HVAC outdoor design conditions of 94°F dry-bulb / 75°F wet-bulb for summer (0.4% occurrence, with an outside air humidity ratio of 102 grains per pound and mean coincident 82°F DB) and 18°F for winter. All chiller specifications must name Trane as the sole approved manufacturer; no other manufacturer is accepted without prior JBA or JBAB approval. Thermostat control systems must specify Siemens as the sole approved manufacturer, with no substitutions permitted without prior approval. Variable refrigerant flow (VRF) systems are not permitted unless specifically requested by JBA or when no other suitable option exists and is approved by the JBA project manager. All new HVAC control systems must integrate with the base's Energy Monitoring and Control System (EMCS).

The UFGS-derived specification set (project 21-015, progress submittal dated June 9, 2021) provides the governing administrative and technical framework for multi-traded task orders that include mechanical/HVAC maintenance, repair, alteration, and minor new construction work at the installation. The firm served as MEP engineer of record, incorporating JBAB's base-specific mechanical standards into the project manual.
