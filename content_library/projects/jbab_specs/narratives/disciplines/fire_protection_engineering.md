The fire protection engineering scope for the JBAB Mini-JOC specification set addresses fire suppression, fire alarm, and mass notification system requirements for task order work at Joint Base Anacostia-Bolling and other DoD sites under the NAVFAC Washington MACC program. The MACC Base Fire Protection Standards (Attachment 9, v1.1, January 30, 2020) establish the technical baseline governing all fire alarm and fire protection work on the installation.

JBAB's fire alarm requirements are highly specific and differ from standard addressable systems used on typical commercial or federal projects. The Monaco MAAP X addressable panel is the required fire alarm and mass notification system for all facilities. The building fire alarm system must provide point-to-point reporting to the Monaco D-21 receiver located at fire station #1 (building 1287). Every facility's system must function as a combination fire alarm and mass notification system, with specific prescribed mass notification messages. The fire alarm radio frequency for JB Andrews is 138.9250 MHz. These requirements apply to all task orders involving fire alarm or mass notification work and must be reflected in task-order-specific scope documents and contractor submittals.

Fire suppression scope under the specification set includes wet sprinkler systems as part of the multi-traded task order framework. The Division 01 general requirements govern contractor qualifications, submittals, and coordination for fire protection work. The MACC base fire protection standards, signed by the JBAB Fire Chief, represent standing technical requirements for all MACC task order work on the installation. The firm incorporated these requirements as MEP engineer of record under project 21-015 (progress submittal dated June 9, 2021).
