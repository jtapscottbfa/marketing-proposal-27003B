The electrical engineering scope for the JBAB Mini-JOC specification set addresses power distribution, lighting, emergency power, and related electrical systems work across task order projects at Joint Base Anacostia-Bolling and other DoD sites under the NAVFAC Washington MACC program. The MACC Electrical Standard Requirements (2019, Attachment 5) establish the technical baseline governing all electrical work on the installation.

JBAB's main base electrical distribution is medium voltage: 7,600/13,200 Volts Delta for both overhead and underground distribution systems. Low-voltage secondary systems operate on a WYE configuration. Identified scope areas within the specification set include duct bank construction, LED lighting systems, airfield lighting, emergency generators, and transformer installations. The MACC Electrical Standard Requirements define preferred manufacturers and design criteria for each system type within these categories.

The specification set structure supports electrical task orders covering power distribution, interior and exterior lighting, and emergency power systems. The UFGS-derived specification set (project 21-015, progress submittal dated June 9, 2021) incorporates Division 01 general requirements alongside the MACC base electrical standards, providing the complete administrative and technical framework for JOC2021 task order work involving electrical scope. The firm served as MEP engineer of record, responsible for incorporating JBAB's base-specific electrical standards into the project manual. Contractor personnel performing electrical work at JBAB are subject to the installation's security and access control requirements, including US citizenship, five-day advance notice, criminal history review, and DBIDS or NCACS enrollment.
