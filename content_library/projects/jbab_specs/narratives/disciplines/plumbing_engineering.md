The plumbing engineering scope for the JBAB Mini-JOC specification set addresses domestic water, sanitary drainage, and related plumbing systems work across task order projects at Joint Base Anacostia-Bolling and other DoD sites under the NAVFAC Washington MACC program. Plumbing systems — including domestic water distribution and sanitary/drain-waste-vent (DWV) systems — are identified within the specification set's construction scope as part of the multi-traded task order framework.

The specification set's Division 01 general requirements and the MACC Mechanical Systems Standard Requirements (2020, Attachment 8) provide the governing administrative and technical framework for plumbing work at JBAB. As a District of Columbia installation, all plumbing work is subject to DCMR (DC Municipal Regulations) in addition to applicable federal standards, with the DC Department of Energy and Environment (DOEE) serving as the regulatory authority for related environmental compliance matters, including stormwater and erosion and sediment control thresholds that can be triggered by even minor excavation associated with underground plumbing work.

Specific plumbing design criteria beyond the installation's general MACC standards are not captured in the available source record; MACC Attachment 8 addresses mechanical and plumbing design criteria collectively. The specification set provides the procedural and administrative framework and references the MACC base standards for technical requirements. Plumbing was part of the multi-discipline MEP scope for which the firm served as engineer of record under project 21-015, with the UFGS-derived progress submittal issued June 9, 2021.
