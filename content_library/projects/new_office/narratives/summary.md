The Office and Warehouse Facility for Blackwater Electric Corp. is a new-construction commercial building located at 400 Network Station, Chesapeake, Virginia 23320. The project delivers approximately 16,551 square feet of building footprint across two program components: a two-story office wing (10,963 SF footprint, with a mezzanine-level second floor) and an attached single-story warehouse (5,588 SF), on a 3.31-acre Planned Unit Development parcel. The facility includes site-planned provisions for a future 6,286 SF expansion.

The design addressed the distinct environmental conditioning requirements of a mixed-use occupancy within a single building envelope. The office wing is served by a Variable Refrigerant Flow (VRF) ductless split system with per-room wall-mounted air handling units across both floors; the warehouse is heated by gas-fired unit heaters (UH-1 through UH-4, 200 MBH each). Electrical design provides panelboards, branch circuit distribution for the VRF system, and lighting for both floors, with a 400A generator tap box at the first floor electrical room. Civil design achieved a post-development runoff coefficient of C=0.53 against a site allowable of C=0.75, and provided 76 parking spaces against a code-required 72. The project was permitted by the City of Chesapeake (AC# 194109_00) in 2019 and is recorded as complete. No construction contract value or project budget data is available in the source documents.
