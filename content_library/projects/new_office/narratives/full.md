## Project Overview

The Office and Warehouse Facility for Blackwater Electric Corp. is a new-construction commercial development at 400 Network Station, Chesapeake, Virginia 23320, on a 3.31-acre (144,305 SF) parcel zoned Planned Unit Development (PUD). The facility delivers approximately 16,551 square feet of building footprint in a mixed-use configuration: a two-story office wing (10,963 SF footprint, with a mezzanine-level second floor) and an attached single-story warehouse (5,588 SF). Site provisions include a planned future expansion of 6,286 SF. The building, as depicted in the permit drawing set, features metal panel and masonry construction with a maximum permitted height of 75 feet. The property lies in FEMA Flood Zone X (minimal flood hazard, per Map 51003C4030D) with no wetlands within the project limits. The site is served by City of Chesapeake water and sewer utilities. The project was permitted by the City of Chesapeake in 2019 (Permit AC# 194109_00) and is recorded as complete.

## Scope

Design services spanned six disciplines: architecture, civil engineering, structural engineering, mechanical engineering, electrical engineering, and plumbing engineering.

**Architectural** scope encompassed the building program and permit package. RRMM Architects PC served as architect of record, issuing 100% Permit Drawings in October 2019. The drawing package references a full architectural set (sheets A-001 through A-801) and a structural set (S-001 through S-402); only civil, mechanical, and electrical sheets are available in the source documents for this record.

**Civil** scope, provided by Timmons Group, included site grading and drainage, utility coordination, and stormwater BMP design. An existing conditions survey was performed in June 2019. The civil design achieved a post-development runoff coefficient of C=0.53 (without future expansion) against a site allowable of C=0.75, and provides 76 parking spaces (4 handicapped, 2 van-accessible) against a code-calculated requirement of 72. Total disturbed area is 112,285 SF (2.58 acres).

**Structural** scope was provided by Broyles and Associates, Chesapeake, Virginia. The structural drawing set (S-001 through S-402) is referenced in the permit index but was not available in the source documents for this record; structural system details are not captured here.

**Mechanical** scope, provided by Elizabeth River Mechanical / Infinity Design Group, addresses the building's two-zone HVAC strategy: a Variable Refrigerant Flow (VRF) ductless split system throughout the office wing, and gas-fired unit heating for the warehouse. The office wing receives multiple wall-mounted indoor air handling units (AHU-1 through AHU-3 series) on both floors, individually sized per room. The warehouse is served by four gas-fired unit heaters (UH-1 through UH-4, 200 MBH each). A gas-fired rooftop unit (RTU-1, 150 MBH gas input) serves common areas. Total natural gas meter demand is 1,250 MBH, including a 300 MBH future allowance. The design references the 2015 Virginia Mechanical Code and 2012 International Energy Code.

**Electrical** scope, provided by Bowman Foster & Associates, covers power distribution and lighting for both occupied floors. Panelboards include Panel A1, Panel B1, and Panel MOP; a 400A generator tap box at the first floor electrical room indicates provision for standby generator connection. Three-phase 60A NEMA 3R disconnects serve VRF outdoor units (OU-1, OU-2) and RTU-1. Branch circuits for AHU units (2P-20A and 2P-30A) are distributed throughout both floors; warehouse unit heaters are served by dedicated 2P-30A circuits.

**Plumbing** scope includes domestic water service (electric water heater) and sanitary drainage connected to the City of Chesapeake system. No dedicated plumbing drawings were present in the source documents.

The design team was led by RRMM Architects PC as architect of record, with Timmons Group (civil/surveying), Broyles and Associates (structural), Elizabeth River Mechanical / Infinity Design Group (mechanical), and Bowman Foster & Associates (electrical) as engineering subconsultants. The role of the primary consultant associated with this project record on the design team is not explicitly identified in the available source documents, which consist entirely of drawing sets; role assignment is based on project archive classification. The project was delivered under a design-bid-build contract vehicle for private commercial owner Blackwater Electric Corp.

## Key Challenges and Resolutions

The central design challenge was coordinating a mixed-use program with fundamentally different environmental conditioning requirements within a single building envelope. The office wing required the precision zone-by-zone comfort control appropriate for an occupied workplace; the warehouse required robust heating for a large open industrial volume. The mechanical design resolved this by applying VRF ductless technology to the office spaces — enabling individual room control without a central ducted system — while specifying gas-fired unit heaters for the warehouse, where continuous precise temperature control was not required. The two strategies share a single natural gas service and are documented in a single coordinated mechanical drawing package.

A minor address discrepancy was identified during extraction: the mechanical progress print drawings carry zip code 23283 for the project site, while the civil permit drawings and electrical drawings both carry 23320. The civil permit document (prepared by the civil engineer of record for the City of Chesapeake permit submission) was treated as authoritative; zip code 23320 is confirmed by two independent sources.

The mechanical drawing package for this project carries a "PROGRESS PRINT — NOT TO BE USED FOR CONSTRUCTION" stamp, suggesting the mechanical design was still being developed when these documents were assembled for reference. System specifications and equipment schedules drawn from these sheets should be understood as indicative of the final design intent rather than confirmed construction-document values.

## Outcomes

The project delivers a new commercial facility for Blackwater Electric Corp. at 400 Network Station, Chesapeake, Virginia — providing combined office and warehouse space for the client's operations on a 3.31-acre PUD parcel. The 16,551 SF building footprint accommodates the owner's program, with 76 parking spaces exceeding the code minimum and a site layout designed to permit future expansion. The civil design met the City of Chesapeake's stormwater BMP requirements with capacity to spare relative to the allowable runoff coefficient. The project was permitted by the City of Chesapeake and is recorded as complete in the design team's project archive. Construction contract value, project budget, and construction schedule data are not available in the source documents for this record.
