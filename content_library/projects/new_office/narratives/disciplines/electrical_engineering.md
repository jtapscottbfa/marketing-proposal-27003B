Electrical engineering services for the Office and Warehouse Facility for Blackwater Electric were provided by Bowman Foster & Associates (Project No. 19/43-00), with RRMM Architects PC as architect of record. The electrical design, issued March 2020, covers new electrical work throughout the building.

Power distribution is served by panelboards Panel A1, Panel B1, and Panel MOP, with a 400A generator tap box and meter/CT cabinet at the first floor electrical room — indicating provision for emergency or standby generator connection. Three-phase 60A NEMA 3R disconnects serve the VRF outdoor units (OU-1 and OU-2) and the rooftop unit (RTU-1), confirming three-phase service. Branch circuit feeds for the VRF indoor air handling units (2P-20A and 2P-30A circuits from Panel B1) are distributed throughout the first and second floor office areas. Warehouse unit heaters UH-1 and UH-2 are served by 2P-30A circuits. The roof plan shows RTU-1, VRF outdoor units, and exhaust fan HF-1 (2P-20A). Lighting circuits for both occupied floors are noted on the electrical plans. All new work is specified to be concealed in ceilings and walls; conduit in concrete slabs is positioned in the middle third of slab thickness. Circuitry is noted as diagrammatic. The design references the NEC and local jurisdiction requirements.
