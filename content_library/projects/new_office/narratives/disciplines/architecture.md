The architectural scope for the Office and Warehouse Facility for Blackwater Electric encompassed the design of a new-construction mixed-use building at 400 Network Station, Chesapeake, Virginia. RRMM Architects PC served as architect of record. The building program combines a two-story office wing (approximately 10,963 SF footprint) with an attached single-story warehouse (approximately 5,588 SF), with the office portion including a mezzanine-level second floor. The building exterior, as depicted in the permit drawing set, features metal panel and masonry construction. The maximum permitted building height is 75 feet.

The architectural permit drawing set was issued as 100% Permit Drawings in October 2019. The drawing index references a complete architectural package spanning sheets A-001 through A-801, as well as structural drawings (S-001 through S-402). The architectural sheets were not available in the source documents provided for this record; specific architectural details, finish schedules, room data, and floor area calculations from architectural drawings are therefore not captured here. Discipline participation is confirmed by the sheet index on drawing G-001 and the RRMM Architects title block appearing across all three drawing-set PDFs in the source package. The permit was issued by the City of Chesapeake under Application Control Number 194109_00.
