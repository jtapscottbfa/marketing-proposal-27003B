Mechanical engineering services for the Office and Warehouse Facility for Blackwater Electric were provided by Elizabeth River Mechanical / Infinity Design Group (758 Thimble Shoals Boulevard, Suite 804, Newport News, Virginia 23606). The mechanical design addresses two distinct conditioning strategies for the mixed-use program: a Variable Refrigerant Flow (VRF) ductless split system for office areas and gas-fired unit heating for the warehouse.

The office wing is served by multiple wall-mounted indoor air handling units (AHU-1 through AHU-3 series) distributed across both the first and second floors, with units individually sized to the rooms they serve — up to 5 tons per unit. VRF outdoor condensing units (OU-1, OU-2) are roof-mounted, with refrigerant piping routed vertically through a dedicated duct chase between floors. The warehouse is heated by four gas-fired unit heaters (UH-1 through UH-4, 200 MBH each). A gas-fired rooftop unit (RTU-1, 150 MBH input) serves common areas. Total natural gas meter demand is 1,250 MBH, including a 300 MBH future capacity allowance. Exhaust fans serve restrooms and utility rooms. The design references the 2015 Virginia Mechanical Code and 2012 International Energy Code. The mechanical drawings for this project are stamped "PROGRESS PRINT — NOT TO BE USED FOR CONSTRUCTION" and represent an intermediate design iteration; system specifications derived from these sheets should be understood as indicative of the design intent rather than confirmed construction-document values.
