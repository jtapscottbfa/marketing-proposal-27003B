Civil engineering services for the Office and Warehouse Facility for Blackwater Electric were provided by Timmons Group (Virginia Beach, Virginia), who also performed land surveying. The civil scope covered site planning, grading and drainage, utility coordination, and stormwater best management practices for a 3.31-acre (144,305 SF) Planned Unit Development parcel at 400 Network Station, Chesapeake, Virginia 23320. An existing conditions survey was performed by Mid-Atlantic Surveying and Land Design on June 26, 2019. Civil permit drawings were issued to the City of Chesapeake in September 2019 (Permit AC# 194109_00).

The site lies in FEMA Flood Zone X (minimal flood hazard, per Map 51003C4030D, December 2014) with no wetlands within the project limits. Civil design provides 76 parking spaces — including 4 handicapped spaces (2 van-accessible) — against a calculated code requirement of 72, and achieves a post-development runoff coefficient of C=0.53 (without future expansion) against the allowable maximum of C=0.75, representing meaningful stormwater attenuation relative to the site's permitted capacity. Total disturbed area is 112,285 SF (2.58 acres). Total impervious area, including the planned 6,286 SF future expansion, reaches 61,440 SF (1.41 acres). The site is served by City of Chesapeake water and sewer utilities. The civil design references VDOT Road Design Manual (2007) and Hampton Roads Planning District Commission (HRPDC) regional stormwater standards.
