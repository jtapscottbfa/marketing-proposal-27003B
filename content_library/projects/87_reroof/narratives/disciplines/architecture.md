# Architecture — Building 87 Re-Roof Design

Architectural services for the Building 87 Re-Roof Design project at Jefferson Lab (Newport News, VA) encompassed the full design of the replacement roof assembly and all associated envelope and rooftop elements for the 6,720-square-foot, one-story pre-engineered metal building. Dills Architects served as architect of record (Clay C. Dills, AIA, VA License 013034), with architectural sheets sealed on the final submittal dated August 16, 2022.

The architectural scope included the new roof build-up — a mechanically adhered EPDM membrane over ½-inch cover board, over 1½-inch polyisocyanurate insulation between existing metal roof panel flutes — along with new prefinished metal coping at the parapets, replacement gutters and downspouts, and associated metal edge flashings. Existing solatube tubular daylighting devices were removed, stored, and reinstalled on new 12-inch prefabricated curbs that integrate with the new roof system, preserving the building's daylighting infrastructure. Vent-through-roof plumbing penetrations were retained with new prefabricated pipe boot flashings. The design also addressed miscellaneous rooftop items: roof walkway pads, a new fall restraint system (Honeywell Miller Fusion roof anchor posts), a commercial safety ladder dock, and replacement concrete splash blocks.

Demolition drawings (AD-101) documented the selective removal of existing roofing components — coping, flashing, solatubes, exhaust vent caps, gutter and downspout systems, and the lightning protection grid (the last of which coordinated with the electrical discipline drawings). The design was governed by Jefferson Lab's high-performance building standards and required that staff remain in the building without major work interruption throughout construction.
