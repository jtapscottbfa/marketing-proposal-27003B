Plumbing engineering on the MVLA Existing Climate Control Infrastructure project encompassed the design of a copper pipe distribution system for the conditioned supply serving conservation display cases at the Donald W. Reynolds Museum and Education Center, Alexandria, Virginia. The physical delivery system — copper piping, fittings, isolation valves, and flexible connectors — constitutes the plumbing scope of this specialty conservation climate control project.

The plumbing scope included new 2-inch copper distribution mains, ¾-inch copper branch lines, 2×2×2-inch copper tees at branch takeoffs, and manual ball valves at each display case technical compartment. The existing 3-inch copper supply from a lower-level conservation machine served as the fixed point of connection for the new distribution work. Flexible silicone tube connections provide the final link between the rigid copper system and each individual display case inlet, accommodating minor dimensional tolerances at the case interface. Work was designed in compliance with the 2018 International Plumbing Code, and the drawing set was stamped by a Virginia-licensed Professional Engineer.
