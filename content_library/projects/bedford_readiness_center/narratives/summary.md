The Bedford Readiness Center HVAC Upgrades was a design project for the Virginia Department of Military Affairs (DMA), providing complete HVAC system replacement at the Bedford Readiness Center, a Virginia National Guard facility in Bedford, Virginia. The project addressed aging infrastructure — including 50+ year old ductwork and piping — while incorporating contemporary indoor air quality (IAQ) technologies such as bipolar ionization, high-efficiency filtration, UV lighting, and cold fusion components to meet the owner's infectious disease prevention requirements. The design also included an upgraded Building Automation System (BAS) with humidity and temperature controls and HVAC zoning configured to limit airborne particle migration across facility spaces.

The firm served as prime A/E consultant under a design-bid-build delivery structure, procured by DMA through Virginia's CPSM qualifications-based A/E selection process. A/E services encompassed comprehensive site investigation, full design through working drawing phase, multi-agency coordination (DEB, DEQ, SFMO), detailed cost estimating at each submission milestone, and full construction administration services. The project's not-to-exceed construction budget was $1,127,000. Design was completed through 100% Basis of Design in October 2023, with construction bid separately under DMA's CPSM procurement process.
