The electrical engineering scope for the Bedford Readiness Center HVAC Upgrades addressed the power distribution requirements necessary to support complete HVAC system replacement at the Virginia National Guard facility in Bedford, Virginia.

A site electrical investigation conducted in December 2022 documented the facility's existing electrical infrastructure prior to design. The main distribution panel (MDP), located in the boiler room, provided a 400A, 240/120V, 1-phase, 3-wire service. A separate service — a 240 Delta/120V, 3-phase, 4-wire feed from a north-end power pole — served pad-mounted mechanical equipment and two rooftop units. Multiple 1-phase panelboards were distributed throughout the facility, including panels in the boiler room, assembly hall, custodial room, and corridors. The site investigation noted that posted panel schedules for some boards may not have been accurate, requiring field verification during design.

The electrical design scope encompassed new circuits to serve all replacement HVAC equipment, removal of existing electrical devices, wiring, and conduit back to the existing panels, and temporary support and reinstallation of existing lighting fixtures in all ceiling areas disturbed during ductwork removal and replacement. Electrical work was coordinated with the mechanical replacement to support phased construction sequencing across the facility. The firm served as prime A/E consultant for the Virginia Department of Military Affairs under design-bid-build delivery.
