The environmental engineering scope on the Repair Soil Erosion at Reservoir Berms project addressed regulatory compliance associated with the berm repair and stabilization work at Norfolk Naval Shipyard, Portsmouth, Virginia. The project disturbed less than one acre of land in total — a threshold documented in the RFP's stormwater management provisions — which informed the permitting and erosion and sediment control obligations for the work. Installation-level NAVFAC environmental review requirements applied throughout, even at this sub-acre disturbance scale.

The environmental scope encompassed stormwater management planning and erosion and sediment control design, integrated with the civil grading and geotechnical stabilization scope. Protection of existing site drainage infrastructure — including the storm drain pipes and catch basins on and around the berm — was required throughout construction to prevent discharge of disturbed soils from the repair area. The erosion control program was directly tied to the surface treatment and slope geometry design, as the repaired berm slopes were steep (maximum 1.5H:1V) and required engineered surface stabilization to prevent post-construction re-erosion.
