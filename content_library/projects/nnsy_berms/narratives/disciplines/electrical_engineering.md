The electrical engineering scope on the Repair Soil Erosion at Reservoir Berms project involved the conversion of existing overhead exterior lighting circuits to a new underground distribution system serving the berm area at Norfolk Naval Shipyard, Portsmouth, Virginia. The existing configuration routed an overhead lighting circuit along poles #280 through #284 across the berm area undergoing stabilization; the overhead conductors were vulnerable to disruption during berm reconstruction and represented a long-term maintenance liability on the reconstructed slope face.

The design converted the overhead circuit to a new direct-buried underground system. Overhead conductors from poles #280 to #284 were disconnected and removed, while the existing poles and luminaires were retained. A new underground circuit was installed in minimum 3/4-inch galvanized rigid steel conduit, routed from pole #284 to poles P280, P281, P282, and P283. At pole #284, the existing circuit breaker and disconnect switch were replaced with new NEMA 4X stainless steel enclosures housing a 20A circuit breaker and a 30A non-fused disconnect switch. An optional scope item (Option 3) extended the underground circuit further to perimeter poles PPA and LPB. Coordination with an existing overhead telephone line sharing pole #284 was required — the line was temporarily disconnected and protected in place during underground circuit installation. The electrical design narrative was authored in August 2021; the electrical basis of design was completed in January 2022, documenting the engineer-of-record's design decisions for the design-build submission.
