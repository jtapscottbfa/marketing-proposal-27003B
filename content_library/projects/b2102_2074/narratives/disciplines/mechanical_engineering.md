# Mechanical Engineering — Building 2102 and Building 2074 SCIF/Secured Facility Conversion, NAS Patuxent River

BFA provided mechanical engineering design for the conversion of Buildings 2102 and 2074 at NAS Patuxent River into accredited classified facilities. The mechanical scope encompassed complete removal of both buildings' existing HVAC systems — split-system heat pumps, motorized outside air dampers, and wall-mounted programmable thermostats — followed by the design of replacement systems appropriate for secure-facility occupancy.

Replacement HVAC equipment consisted of new split-system heat pumps with electric supplemental heat. New spiral ductwork was specified to be hung below the ceiling without insulation in conditioned spaces, a configuration well-suited to ICD-705 SCIF geometry that requires all penetrations through the acoustic and RF boundary to be carefully controlled. A motorized outside air louver was designed to deliver ventilation per ASHRAE 62.1-2007 requirements.

Design conditions were established for the Patuxent River climate: a cooling design of 91°F dry bulb / 80°F wet bulb and a heating design of 19°F dry bulb outside, with interior conditions held at 78°F dry bulb / 50% relative humidity (cooling) and 70°F dry bulb (heating) for admin and shop areas. Design criteria referenced NAVFAC Planning & Design Policy, UFC 3-410-02N (Heating, Ventilating, Air Conditioning, and Dehumidifying Systems), IMC-2010, and applicable ASHRAE standards including ASHRAE 55-2008 and the SMACNA HVAC Commissioning Manual. BFA's mechanical deliverable was incorporated into the Final Basis of Design dated April 7, 2023.
