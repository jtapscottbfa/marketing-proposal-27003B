# Plumbing Engineering — Building 2102 and Building 2074 SCIF/Secured Facility Conversion, NAS Patuxent River

BFA provided plumbing engineering design for the conversion of Buildings 2102 and 2074 at NAS Patuxent River from general-purpose office space to classified operational facilities. The plumbing scope at both buildings was defined by the change in occupancy: the existing restroom infrastructure — flush-tank water closets, wall-mounted lavatories, and water heaters — was removed entirely in both buildings, consistent with the mission-focused character of the new SCIF and "Open Secret" workspaces.

In place of the removed fixtures, kitchenette plumbing was added to support operational personnel at both buildings. The kitchenette program included a refrigerator, sink, and millwork with upper and lower storage, providing the domestic water service appropriate to the new occupancy.

Design criteria referenced NAVFAC Planning & Design Policy, UFC 3-410-02N, the International Plumbing Code, ASPE publications, and applicable ASHRAE standards. BFA's plumbing design was delivered as part of the Final Basis of Design dated April 7, 2023. While the plumbing scope on this project was limited relative to the full construction program, it reflects BFA's role in providing complete MEP documentation for federal secure-facility conversions under NAVFAC design standards.
