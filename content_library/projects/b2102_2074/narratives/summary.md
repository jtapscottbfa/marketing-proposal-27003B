# Summary — Building 2102 and Building 2074 SCIF/Secured Facility Conversion, NAS Patuxent River

Bowman Foster & Associates (BFA) provided mechanical and plumbing engineering design for the conversion of two existing buildings at Naval Air Station Patuxent River, Maryland into accredited secured facilities for the Naval Air Warfare Center Aircraft Division (NAWCAD). Building 2074 was converted to a fully accredited ICD-705 Sensitive Compartmented Information Facility (SCIF) meeting Wall Type A, Sound Group 4 acoustic standards and RF shielding requirements per CNSSAM TEMPEST/1-13. Building 2102 was converted to an "Open Secret" classified operational workspace with comparable environmental and power infrastructure requirements.

BFA's scope encompassed complete removal of existing split-system heat pumps, outside air dampers, restroom fixtures, and water heaters in both buildings, followed by design of replacement mechanical systems. New split-system heat pumps with electric supplemental heat were specified for both buildings, with new spiral ductwork hung below the ceiling and a motorized outdoor air louver sized to ASHRAE 62.1 ventilation requirements. Design conditions were established for the Pax River climate: 91°F DB / 80°F WB cooling design and 19°F DB heating design, with interior conditions held at 78°F DB / 50% RH (cooling) and 70°F DB (heating). Kitchenette plumbing was added at both buildings to support the new operational mission.

The project additionally required 400Hz 3-phase AC and 28VDC specialty power conversion/generation systems, LED lighting, VCT flooring, fire alarm, and comprehensive classified and unclassified data drops — reflecting the full scope of transforming general-purpose office space into mission-ready secure facilities under strict federal intelligence community construction standards. Design was produced as a Final Basis of Design submittal dated April 7, 2023, with construction governed by a NAWCAD-issued Scope of Work requiring design-management milestones at 30%, 60%, and 100% completion levels.
