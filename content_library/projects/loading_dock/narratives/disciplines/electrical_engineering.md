Electrical engineering on this design-build project provided the power distribution infrastructure required to operate the new dock lift at Bay-1 of Building 2484, Kimbrough Ambulatory Care Center (KACC), Fort George G. Meade, Maryland.

The electrical scope involved providing the infrastructure needed to power the new dock lift using the existing electrical distribution system in place at Building 2484. The design team verified that the building's existing 3-phase distribution system — serving the main distribution panel and several sub-panels — carried sufficient spare capacity to support the added dock lift load, eliminating the need for a service upgrade. A new branch circuit was designed from an existing distribution panel to serve the dock lift equipment.

Design criteria included UFC 3-501-01 (Electrical Engineering), UFC 3-520-01 (Interior Electrical Systems), UFC 4-510-01 (Design: Military Medical Facilities), NFPA 70 (National Electrical Code), and NESC. Conduit distribution used EMT, intermediate metallic conduit, and rigid metallic conduit. All conductors were copper with THHN/THWN insulation, sized in accordance with voltage drop limits specified under UFC criteria.

The electrical design-build scope was delivered under contract with USACE Huntsville Engineering and Support Center, with the Final Basis of Design issued October 17, 2023.
