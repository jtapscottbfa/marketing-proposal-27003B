# Loading Dock Lift Installation — Building 2484, KACC, Fort George G. Meade, MD

## Project Overview

Building 2484 serves as the Logistics Division hub for Kimbrough Ambulatory Care Center (KACC) at Fort George G. Meade, Maryland, a Defense Health Agency (DHA) military medical treatment facility. The approximately 18,200 GSF pre-engineered metal structure warehouses medical supplies and temperature-sensitive pharmaceutical products, receiving deliveries through a two-bay loading dock.

The existing loading dock presented a persistent operational safety problem: the fixed dock height did not accommodate the range of delivery truck bed heights arriving at the facility, and no leveling mechanism was in place to bridge the height differential. Materials — including medical supplies for KACC — were being offloaded under unsafe conditions. This project corrected the deficiency by installing a dock lift with dock leveler at Bay-1, providing an adjustable platform that accommodates variable vehicle configurations and enables safe material transfer at an active DoD medical logistics operation.

## Scope

The complete design-build scope encompassed design and construction of the dock lift and dock leveler installation at Bay-1 of the existing loading dock. The structural scope involved modifying the existing concrete loading dock at the Bay-1 overhead door opening: demolishing a portion of the existing concrete slab, subgrade, and loading dock wall, then constructing a reinforced concrete pit with sloped drainage and steel edge protection to receive the dock lift mechanism. The electrical scope provided the power distribution infrastructure to operate the new dock lift equipment using the building's existing electrical distribution system, adding a new branch circuit from the existing distribution panel.

The design-build contract structured delivery as a 100% design submittal package followed by separate construction line items. Design criteria included UFC 1-200-01 (DoD Building Code), UFC 4-510-01 (Design: Military Medical Facilities), IBC 2018, IEBC 2018, ASCE 7-16, ACI 318-14, UFC 3-501-01 (Electrical Engineering), UFC 3-520-01 (Interior Electrical Systems), NFPA 70, and the Ft. George G. Meade Design and Construction Guidelines.

## Role on Project

The firm held the prime design-build contract with USACE HNC under the FY22 Eastern Region Miscellaneous Projects multi-project IDIQ (contract W912DY-22-C-XXXX; USACE P2 #507741; internal designation BFA #22-060A). As prime design-builder, responsibilities included site investigation, hazardous materials survey, complete design development through the Final Basis of Design submittal, and integrated construction services. Project coordination required ongoing collaboration with DHA and KACC facility management staff throughout design and construction.

## Key Challenges and Resolutions

**Active medical logistics operation.** Building 2484 remained in active operation as a medical supplies warehouse throughout construction. The scope required implementing temporary safety measures to adequately separate construction zones from surrounding active spaces, and collaborating with DHA and KACC facility management to develop a phasing approach that minimized adverse impact on ongoing logistics operations for the medical treatment facility.

**Dock height differential.** The installation of a dock lift with a platform providing a range of vertical travel resolved the fundamental mismatch between the fixed dock height and variable delivery truck bed heights, enabling safe material transfer from a range of vehicle types — the direct operational problem that generated the project requirement.

**Existing electrical capacity.** The electrical design team evaluated the building's existing 208Y/120V, 3-phase distribution system prior to designing the new branch circuit, confirming that the existing system carried sufficient capacity to support the added dock lift load and that no service upgrade was required.
