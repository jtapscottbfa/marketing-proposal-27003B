This project involved the design-build installation of a dock lift and dock leveler at Building 2484, the Logistics Division facility serving Kimbrough Ambulatory Care Center (KACC), Fort George G. Meade, Maryland. The existing loading dock at the approximately 18,200 GSF logistics building created unsafe offloading conditions: delivery vehicle bed heights did not consistently align with the fixed dock height, and no leveling mechanism was in place to bridge the gap for medical supply deliveries to the facility. The dock lift installation directly addressed this deficiency, enabling safe and efficient loading dock operations for a facility storing medical supplies and temperature-sensitive pharmaceutical products in support of an active military medical treatment facility.

The firm served as prime design-builder under a task order issued by the US Army Corps of Engineers, Huntsville Engineering and Support Center (USACE HNC), within an FY22 multi-project miscellaneous IDIQ. The Defense Health Agency (DHA) was the using agency, operating KACC as a DoD ambulatory care center. Structural engineering scope encompassed modification of the existing concrete loading dock — including demolition of existing slab and dock wall at Bay-1 and construction of a reinforced concrete pit to receive the dock lift mechanism. Electrical engineering provided the power distribution infrastructure to serve the new equipment from the building's existing distribution system. The Final Basis of Design was issued October 17, 2023 (BFA Project #22-060A; USACE P2 #507741).
