Electrical engineering was the sole discipline on this project. BFA designed the complete electrical systems for Overhead Crane Support Building #3 at Nansemond Pre-Cast Concrete Co.'s Suffolk, Virginia facility — a new industrial manufacturing plant built around the ePAK 150 Machine Plant production system.

The electrical scope covered utility service, power distribution, and interior lighting. A new 4,000-ampere, 3-phase 4-wire 277/480-volt service was designed with 700 kcmil underground feeders terminating at outdoor switchgear, coordinated through the Dominion Energy nonresidential load letter process. Power distribution addressed a dense industrial equipment schedule including 15-ton and 20-ton overhead cranes, heavy production machinery (ePAK 150, PipePlus, Smart Set, DTE Wiredraw, batch plant, mixer, cement silo, and auxiliary units), a 50 kW lithium-ion battery charger, compressors, and overhead door operators — all served by 3-phase 480-volt branch circuits from multiple panelboards fed through a 30 kVA dry-type transformer. A dedicated control room received power and conduit for high-speed internet. LED industrial high-bay fixtures with occupancy sensors, exit signs, and emergency units were provided throughout the building. Sub-disciplines engaged include electrical power distribution, electrical lighting, and electrical motor control. Final drawings were issued January 26, 2023.
