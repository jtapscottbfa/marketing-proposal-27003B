# Nansemond Pre-Cast Concrete Co. — Overhead Crane Support Building #3

## Project Overview

Nansemond Pre-Cast Concrete Co. engaged Bowman Foster & Associates (BFA), operating under TAM Consultants, to design the electrical systems for a new industrial manufacturing building at their facility located at 3737 Nansemond Parkway in Suffolk, Virginia. Designated "Overhead Crane Support Building #3," the project involved the complete electrical fit-out of a large single-story industrial plant configured around a new ePAK 150 Machine Plant — a heavy-duty precast concrete production system manufactured by Hawkeye. The building was being brought online as a fully equipped production facility with overhead crane infrastructure, automated manufacturing equipment, and a dedicated control room.

The client required separately metered electrical service for the new building, all-copper wiring throughout, and exposed conduit where acceptable — preferences established at the initial project meeting in February 2022 and carried through to the final design. The owner also expressed a preference for Square-D switchgear and requested turn-key design and construction pricing, though the BFA engagement was ultimately scoped to the design phase. Final electrical drawings were issued January 26, 2023.

## Scope

BFA's scope was electrical engineering only. No mechanical, plumbing, or fire protection services were provided for this project. The electrical scope encompassed three primary areas:

**Electrical service and main distribution.** BFA designed a new 4,000-ampere, 3-phase 4-wire 277/480-volt electrical service sourced from Dominion Energy. Service conductors are 700 kcmil, 9 sets per phase, delivered underground with termination at outdoor switchgear and an outdoor meter. BFA prepared and coordinated the Dominion Energy nonresidential load letter (Form 725071), executed by Dominion on February 23, 2023, to obtain utility service approval. The main distribution system feeds multiple panelboards — designated 'N,' '1,' '2,' and '3' — distributed throughout the facility, plus a 30 kVA dry-type transformer for lower-voltage branch circuits.

**Power distribution for production equipment.** The building's equipment load is dense and varied. BFA designed 3-phase 480-volt feeders and branch circuits for the full equipment schedule, which included: the ePAK 150 Machine Plant and associated Hawkeye equipment (PipePlus, batch plant, cement silo, mixer); a Smart Set machine; DTE Wiredraw machine; MBK machine; WM-3450 device; Telekin units; a butt welder; a 50 kW lithium-ion battery charger; hydraulic units; compressors; and COL overhead door motor operators. Overhead crane power feeds were designed for both a 15-ton and a 20-ton crane. Equipment power requirements were derived from Hawkeye drawing No. 100369482A (owner-furnished equipment layout), which BFA referenced directly in construction notes. Multiple high-velocity industrial fans (1 HP, 480V, 3-phase) were also included. A dedicated control room received both power distribution and conduit infrastructure for high-speed internet connectivity.

**Interior lighting.** LED industrial high-bay fixtures were provided throughout the building, with the lighting plan organized across four quadrant plan sheets covering the full building footprint. Occupancy sensors were specified building-wide per energy efficiency requirements. Exit signs and emergency battery units were distributed throughout. Specifications embedded in the drawing set (sheets E-002 and E-003) establish material standards: all copper conductors, EMT conduit throughout, #10 AWG minimum stranded wire, color-coded conductors per voltage level, UL-listed products, and two-year warranty requirements.

## Role on Project

BFA served as prime consultant and engineer of record for the electrical design. The firm stamped and sealed the 16-sheet electrical drawing set through TAM Consultants. BFA prepared BFA internal load calculations (April 2022 preliminary and August 2022 final versions signed by the project's electrical engineer), coordinated with the equipment manufacturer (Hawkeye) for equipment power and utility data, prepared and submitted the Dominion Energy load letter for utility service approval, and issued final construction documents in January 2023.

## Key Challenges and Resolutions

**High-density industrial load coordination.** The ePAK 150 Machine Plant and supporting production equipment represent a large, heterogeneous electrical load. BFA coordinated power requirements across multiple sources: BFA's own load calculations (two versions), the Hawkeye owner-furnished equipment layout drawing, and a separate Hawkeye drawing referenced for internet requirements. The Dominion Energy load letter tabulates the full motor equipment and electrical load schedule, forming the basis for utility service sizing at 4,000 amperes. The evolving equipment list between the April 2022 and August 2022 BFA load letters — and again in the February 2023 Dominion submission — reflects a scope that grew and was refined over roughly ten months of design development.

**Utility service coordination.** Designing for a 4,000-ampere industrial service required formal coordination with Dominion Energy under their nonresidential load letter process. BFA managed the submission of Form 725071, with Dominion executing the load letter approximately two years after the initial project meeting, reflecting the design development timeline. Service parameters — 700 kcmil conductors, 9 sets per phase, underground delivery, outdoor switchgear termination — were resolved through this process.

**Separately metered new building.** The owner's requirement for a separately metered building (established at the February 2022 kickoff meeting) shaped the service design from the outset, requiring a standalone service entrance rather than a tap from an existing building distribution system.

## Outcomes

BFA completed the electrical design for Overhead Crane Support Building #3, issuing a 16-sheet final electrical drawing set on January 26, 2023 (BFA job no. 22-015; drawing tracking no. MAC-22-0001). The design delivers a 4,000-ampere industrial electrical service capable of supporting the full ePAK 150 Machine Plant production load, overhead crane infrastructure, and facility-wide LED lighting. The project demonstrates BFA's capability to design complex industrial power distribution systems — including high-ampacity utility service coordination, multi-circuit 480V motor feeder design for heavy manufacturing equipment, and LED industrial lighting — for private commercial industrial clients in the Hampton Roads region.
