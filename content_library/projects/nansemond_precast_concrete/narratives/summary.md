Bowman Foster & Associates (BFA), operating as TAM Consultants, provided complete electrical engineering design services for the Overhead Crane Support Building #3 at Nansemond Pre-Cast Concrete Co.'s facility in Suffolk, Virginia. The project involved the electrical fit-out of a large single-story industrial manufacturing building being commissioned as a new ePAK 150 Machine Plant, integrating heavy production equipment including overhead cranes, concrete mixing and batching equipment, and a full suite of industrial manufacturing machinery.

The scope encompassed design of a new 4,000-ampere, 3-phase 4-wire 277/480V electrical service from Dominion Energy, delivered underground to an outdoor switchgear termination. BFA coordinated directly with Dominion Energy to satisfy the utility's load letter requirements and obtain service approval for the high-demand industrial facility. Power distribution design addressed the complex, high-density equipment load schedule — including 15-ton and 20-ton overhead cranes, the ePAK 150 machine plant, a 50 kW lithium-ion battery charger, compressors, and numerous auxiliary production units — as well as a dedicated control room with data and internet infrastructure. Lighting design provided LED industrial high-bay fixtures throughout the building, supplemented by occupancy sensors, exit/emergency illumination, and compliance with energy efficiency requirements. Final electrical drawings were issued January 26, 2023, completing the design phase.
