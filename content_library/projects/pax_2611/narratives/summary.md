The design team provided architect-engineer services for the renovation and security upgrade of Building 2611 at Naval Air Station Patuxent River, Maryland, on behalf of Naval Air Warfare Center Aircraft Division (NAWCAD). The project converted an approximately 3,900 square-foot, single-story concrete masonry and brick veneer building — originally constructed in 2000 as a radar operations facility — into a Sensitive Compartmented Information Facility (SCIF) compliant with ICD/ICS 705v1.5.1 and TEMPEST electromagnetic security standards. The work was procured as a design-build contract through an 8(a) sole-source solicitation, with construction magnitude in the $6 million to $6.5 million range. The design team served as architect-engineer subconsultant to the design-build contractor.

The renovation encompassed full interior demolition and reconstruction to SCIF/SAPF standards, with new mission control rooms, server and processing spaces, and support areas created within the existing building envelope. MEP systems were designed and installed throughout, with TEMPEST requirements imposing coordinated constraints across all disciplines: HVAC ductwork incorporates man-bars, waveguides, and dielectric breaks at all secure-perimeter penetrations; electrical conduits include dielectric breaks at the secure boundary; and fire alarm circuits include dielectric breaks at the SCIF perimeter. Emergency power is provided by a 360 kW diesel generator, 1200A automatic transfer switch, and an online double-conversion uninterruptible power supply housed in a new detached electrical room addition. The existing co-ed restroom was replaced with separate ADA-compliant men's and women's facilities. A new addressable fire alarm system with mass notification and voice evacuation replaced the existing system. Construction ran from May 2025 through March 2026.
