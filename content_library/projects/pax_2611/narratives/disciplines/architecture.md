Architecture services for this project encompassed design and documentation for the full interior renovation of an approximately 3,900 square-foot, single-story concrete masonry and brick veneer building at Naval Air Station Patuxent River, converting it from a legacy radar operations facility into a Sensitive Compartmented Information Facility (SCIF) compliant with ICD/ICS 705v1.5.1 and TEMPEST electromagnetic security requirements.

The architecture scope included complete interior demolition — all existing partitions, finishes, doors, and systems were removed — followed by design of new SCIF-compliant wall construction throughout. New interior spaces include two mission control rooms (MCR-1 and MCR-2), server rack and workstation areas, a processing room, a reception area, and support spaces. Interior finishes and a suspended acoustic tile ceiling system were specified throughout. The existing single co-ed restroom was redesigned into separate ADA-compliant men's and women's facilities. Doors, frames, thresholds, and openings were detailed to satisfy both ADA accessibility requirements and the physical security requirements of the SCIF envelope.

A new detached CMU/stucco electrical room addition was designed to house the automatic transfer switch and uninterruptible power supply, providing dedicated infrastructure space separate from the classified interior. The existing building exterior — CMU/brick veneer walls, steel joist roof framing, and modified bitumen roofing — was retained and served as the structural and envelope substrate for the renovation. The project was delivered under a design-build contract; the architecture team served as the A-E subconsultant to the design-build contractor.
