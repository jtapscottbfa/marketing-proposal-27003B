Plumbing engineering services provided domestic water and sanitary drainage design for the Building 2611 renovation. The existing single co-ed restroom was demolished and replaced with separate ADA-compliant men's and women's restroom facilities to meet current accessibility and building program requirements.

New plumbing fixtures include three water closets with automatic flush valves, one urinal with automatic flush valve, two wall-hung lavatories with automatic faucets, one kitchen sink, one mop sink, and one ADA-compliant drinking fountain with integrated bottle filler. Domestic hot water is supplied by a new 6,000-watt electric water heater equipped with a thermostatic mixing valve and expansion tank.

Accommodation of the expanded and relocated fixture count required cutting the existing concrete slab to install new underground sanitary drainage and domestic water rough-in piping. The existing building water service, meter, and backflow preventer were evaluated and retained as the building's connection to the Naval Air Station utility system, avoiding the cost and coordination of a new service tap.

The plumbing design was coordinated with the architecture discipline for restroom layout and with the mechanical and electrical disciplines for utility clearances and service routing within the renovated interior.
