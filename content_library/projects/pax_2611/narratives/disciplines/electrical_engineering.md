Electrical engineering services for the Building 2611 SCIF renovation addressed power distribution, emergency power, lighting, grounding, lightning protection, and the full range of TEMPEST and RF shielding requirements mandated by ICD/ICS 705v1.5.1.

The emergency power system provides layered continuity for mission-critical classified operations: a 360 kW, 120/208V, 3-phase 4-wire diesel generator on an elevated reinforced concrete slab; a 1200A 208V 4-pole automatic transfer switch (ATS); and a 20-minute online double-conversion three-phase uninterruptible power supply (UPS) at 208V input/output. The ATS and UPS are housed in a new detached electrical room addition constructed as part of the project.

Interior lighting was designed to a 40 foot-candle target throughout using LED recessed fixtures, with occupancy sensors and dimmers on all circuits. LED exit and emergency lighting were provided. Red rotary and flashing beacon lights were specified at required locations to alert uncleared personnel when access is restricted.

TEMPEST compliance required dielectric breaks on all incoming electrical conduits at the secure perimeter boundary, with RF-treated surface-mounted conduits and electrical boxes throughout the classified interior. Cable trays above the ceiling system provide structured pathways for low-voltage systems. A Class II lightning protection system was designed with four down conductors and three ground rods at each building corner. A surge protection device was provided on the main electrical service. The facility grounding system was designed to achieve 5 ohms resistance or less.
