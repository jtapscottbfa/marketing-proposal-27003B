The project was a comprehensive fire protection systems site engineering investigation for the Naval Special Warfare Development Group (NSWDG) Compound at Naval Air Station Oceana, Dam Neck Annex, Virginia Beach, Virginia — a secured military installation encompassing approximately 200 acres with approximately 81 buildings and structures of various sizes. Commissioned by Naval Facilities Engineering Systems Command Mid-Atlantic (NAVFAC Mid-Atlantic) under indefinite-quantity A-E services contract N40085-15-D-0003, the work entailed an assessment of two parallel water distribution systems serving the compound: a dedicated high-pressure non-potable fire main (Fire Pump Station 352; Water Storage Tank 351, 200,000 gallons) and the Installation domestic water distribution system (Water Storage Tank 531, 250,000 gallons elevated). Field investigation activities included hydrant flow testing, system valve and pipe surveys, and development of calibrated hydraulic models in Bentley WaterGEMS. System performance was evaluated against UFC 3-600-01 (Fire Protection Engineering for Facilities), yielding eleven deficiency categories. Seven prioritized recommendations with rough-order-of-magnitude (ROM) cost estimates were developed for the five highest-priority deficiencies — addressing fire water storage capacity, domestic pump station replacement, dead-end distribution main elimination, and hydrant coverage gaps — with individual ROM costs ranging from no-cost SCADA reprogramming to $3.35 million for elevated storage tank replacement. The study was delivered as an Interim Final Submission in February 2023, establishing a documented infrastructure investment portfolio of approximately $5.77 million in identified fire protection system improvement needs across the compound.
