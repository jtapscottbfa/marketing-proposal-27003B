West Point Readiness Center HVAC Upgrades is a complete mechanical system replacement for the Virginia Army National Guard armory in West Point, Virginia, commissioned by the Department of Military Affairs, Commonwealth of Virginia. The 20,491 GSF facility—a heavy timber armory comprising assembly, educational, and storage use groups—required full HVAC system replacement within a $967,000 not-to-exceed construction budget under a qualifications-based state A/E contract.

The design team led a life cycle cost analysis using TRACE 700 energy modeling calibrated to the West Point, Virginia climate (summer 97°F DB, winter 19°F DB), evaluating multiple system alternatives including variable refrigerant flow. The selected design features rooftop variable air volume air handling units with energy recovery wheels, supply and return fans, and reheat hot water coils served by a propane-fired condensing boiler. Pathogen mitigation was a primary design driver—per the client's post-COVID commander's intent—with the system incorporating HVAC zoning to limit airborne particle migration, bipolar ionization, high-efficiency filtration, in-duct UV lighting, and cold fusion air treatment. A building automation system upgrade provides integrated humidity and temperature control across the facility's zones. The firm served as prime consultant and delivered the 65% design submittal in June 2023.
