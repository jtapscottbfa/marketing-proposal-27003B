The electrical engineering scope for the Warehouse Machine/Welding Shop Ventilation project supported the mechanical ventilation upgrade at a US Customs and Border Protection warehouse facility in Harpers Ferry, West Virginia, providing power distribution design for all new ventilation equipment.

The electrical scope included demolition of the existing exterior wall-mounted exhaust fan and its associated wiring and conduit, removed back to the existing distribution panel, eliminating legacy circuits that would otherwise conflict with the new installation. New power connections were designed to serve the replacement inline exhaust fan and the new dedicated outside air unit. Wiring was specified using EMT conduit with compression fittings and copper conductors with THHN/THWN 600V insulation. Motors rated at 1/2 HP and larger were specified for service from 208V or 480V three-phase, 60 Hz power, consistent with the mechanical equipment specifications for the exhaust fan and air-handling unit.

The electrical design was coordinated with the mechanical scope to align equipment power requirements, confirm panel capacity and circuit routing for new connections, and ensure complete removal of superseded infrastructure. Design was completed to 100% submittal in November 2024.
