The mechanical engineering scope for the Warehouse Machine/Welding Shop Ventilation project encompassed complete design of a dedicated industrial ventilation system for an existing machine/welding room within a US Customs and Border Protection warehouse facility in Harpers Ferry, West Virginia.

The design replaced an inadequate existing exterior wall-mounted exhaust fan with a purpose-designed system suited to the air quality demands of active welding and machining operations. The new system introduced a 1,500 CFM inline exhaust fan to actively remove metal fumes and airborne contaminants at the source, discharging through a new gooseneck termination. A new dedicated outside air unit was designed to supply controlled makeup air to the room. The combined exhaust and supply system was configured to maintain negative pressure within the welding room relative to adjacent spaces, preventing migration of contaminants into the broader facility. New ductwork and diffusers complete the air distribution system within the room. The existing exterior wall-mounted exhaust fan was included in the demolition scope to eliminate legacy equipment conflicts with the new installation.

The mechanical design was coordinated with electrical engineering to ensure equipment power requirements were fully specified and that the demolition scope for the existing fan aligned across both disciplines. Design was completed to 100% submittal in November 2024.
