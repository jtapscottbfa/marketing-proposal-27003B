The Warehouse Machine/Welding Shop Ventilation project provided complete design services for a dedicated industrial ventilation system upgrade at a US Customs and Border Protection facility in Harpers Ferry, West Virginia (BFA Project #24-059). The project addressed deficient air quality conditions in an existing machine/welding room within the facility's warehouse building, replacing an aging exterior wall-mounted exhaust fan with a purpose-designed mechanical ventilation system capable of managing the fume and contaminant loads generated by active welding operations.

The mechanical design introduced a new 1,500 CFM inline exhaust fan with discharge gooseneck and a new dedicated outside air unit, connected via new supply ductwork and diffusers throughout the welding room. The system was configured to maintain negative pressure relative to adjacent spaces, preventing migration of airborne contaminants into the broader facility. The electrical scope included demolition of the existing fan and its associated wiring back to the distribution panel, and new power connections—EMT conduit with compression fittings, copper THHN/THWN conductors, and three-phase motor circuits at 208V or 480V—serving the replacement exhaust fan and air-handling unit. The design team served as prime consultant across both mechanical and electrical disciplines. The 100% design submittal was completed in November 2024 under a design-bid-build procurement.
