## Project Overview

This project involved the structural engineering design and fabrication of steel additions to an existing rail rack at the CHEMRES chemical plant in Chesapeake, Virginia. The facility is a private industrial chemical processing plant. The work scope was a targeted structural modification: integrating new structural steel framing into an existing two-tower X-braced rail rack to support new process equipment. The project dates to early 2022 and is classified as complete.

## Scope

The structural scope comprised three new steel members added to the existing rail rack:

| Member | Type | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| New W12×35 | Wide-flange beam | Runs longitudinally along the new support structure |
| New W8×31 | Wide-flange member(s) | Forms the new portal frame columns/legs on the modified tower |
| New fabricated rolled beam | Custom fabricated steel | Curved/bent transition element bridging across the rail rack assembly |

The existing rail rack — a two-tower X-braced lattice/truss structure — remained in place. The new steel was designed to carry the loads imposed by a Flexicon Corporation System 01 flexible screw conveyor (Arrangement 100625), a dry bulk material-handling unit on a wheeled base frame that mounts to or rides on the rail rack. The structural concept was developed at concept level (3D isometric, not a full construction document set) with final member sizes noted in the drawing annotations.

The Flexicon conveyor's general arrangement was finalized in April 2022 (Rev A, approved), approximately three months after the structural concept was drawn in January 2022 — a typical sequence where the structural framing concept precedes full equipment coordination.

## Key Challenges and Resolutions

**Integrating new steel into an existing industrial structure.** The core engineering challenge was sizing and detailing new structural members to transfer conveyor equipment loads into an existing rail rack that was not originally designed for that purpose. The W12×35 and W8×31 framing and the custom fabricated rolled beam represent the structural solution — new members sized to carry the conveyor load and frame into the existing rack geometry. The concept drawing shows the spatial arrangement; detailed connection design would have been captured in construction-level drawings not present in the source record.

**Equipment coordination.** The structural framing had to accommodate the Flexicon conveyor's wheeled mobile base frame geometry, including spreader bars and outriggers. The three-month gap between the structural concept (January 2022) and the approved vendor general arrangement (April 2022) is consistent with an iterative coordination sequence between the structural designer and the equipment supplier.

## Outcomes

The project is classified as complete. The structural steel additions to the CHEMRES rail rack were designed, fabricated (by East Coast Steel Fabricators, Inc.), and installed to support the Flexicon flexible screw conveyor system.
