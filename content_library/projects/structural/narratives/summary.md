The project involved structural steel additions to an existing industrial rail rack at the CHEMRES chemical plant in Chesapeake, Virginia. New wide-flange members — a W12×35 beam and W8×31 members — along with a custom fabricated rolled beam were integrated into the existing two-tower X-braced rail rack structure to provide structural support for a Flexicon flexible screw conveyor system (System 01, Arrangement 100625). The work represents a targeted industrial upgrade: new structural steel framing was designed and fabricated to carry new process equipment loads on an existing materials-handling structure, avoiding replacement of the host rack.

The structural concept was developed in January 2022; the Flexicon vendor general arrangement for the conveyor system was approved in April 2022 — a normal sequence where structural framing is defined ahead of final equipment coordination. The project is classified as complete. Available source documentation consists of two drawings (a fabricator structural concept and the vendor equipment general arrangement); no basis-of-design, contract, or narrative engineering documents are present in the source record.
