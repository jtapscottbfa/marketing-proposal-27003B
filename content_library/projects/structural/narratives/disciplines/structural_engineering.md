Structural engineering was the primary discipline on this project, which involved designing new steel framing additions to an existing industrial rail rack at the CHEMRES chemical plant in Chesapeake, Virginia. The structural scope centered on three new members — a W12×35 wide-flange beam, W8×31 wide-flange members, and a custom fabricated rolled beam — integrated into an existing two-tower X-braced rail rack to carry the loads imposed by a Flexicon flexible screw conveyor system. The new steel was sized and arranged to frame into the existing rack geometry while accommodating the conveyor unit's wheeled base frame, spreader bars, and outriggers.

The structural concept was developed at a 3D isometric concept level in January 2022, preceding the finalized vendor general arrangement by approximately three months — a sequencing consistent with structural framing being defined ahead of full equipment coordination. Fabrication was performed by East Coast Steel Fabricators, Inc. The sub-discipline is structural steel; the building systems context is superstructure and structural steel frame, applied to an industrial process equipment support structure rather than a conventional building.
