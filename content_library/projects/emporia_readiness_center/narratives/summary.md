The Emporia Readiness Center HVAC Upgrades is a Virginia Department of Military Affairs (DMA) project to replace the complete HVAC system at the Emporia Readiness Center, a one-story readiness center at 410 Courtland Road, Emporia, Virginia. Serving as prime A/E consultant, the design team was engaged via Request for Fee Proposal (State PN 123-18578-020 / VAFM PN 22-0023) to provide full architectural and engineering design services through construction administration.

The project's driving mandate — stated explicitly in DMA's commander's intent — was to design a pathogen-adaptive HVAC system capable of responding to a broad range of future biological threats rather than optimizing for a single pathogen. The scope called for complete replacement of all HVAC units, ductwork, and piping, supplemented by a layered pathogen mitigation array: bipolar ionization, high-efficiency filtration, UV lighting, visible lighting, and cold fusion technology. A Building Automation System (BAS) upgrade provided wide-range humidity and temperature control for operational adaptability. HVAC zoning was incorporated to limit airborne particle migration across the facility.

The not-to-exceed construction budget was $1,407,000, with a 210-day design schedule from Notice to Proceed under a design-bid-build delivery method. The A/E scope included comprehensive existing-conditions investigation, full construction documents through DEB working-drawing review, and complete construction administration services.
