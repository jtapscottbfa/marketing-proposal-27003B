The mechanical engineering scope for the P-1127U Composite Shop at Naval Station Norfolk encompassed full HVAC system design for a new military aviation composite repair facility. Design criteria were anchored to UFC 3-410-01 (Heating, Ventilating, and Air Conditioning Systems) and UFC 3-410-02 (Direct Digital Control for HVAC and Other Building Control Systems), with outdoor design conditions of 94.1°F DB / 75°F MCWB (0.4% cooling) and 23.8°F DB (99.6% heating) for the Norfolk, Virginia climate zone. Interior setpoints were established at 75°F DB with relative humidity below 60% for cooling and 68°F for heating.

The primary conditioning system is a 4-pipe fan coil configuration — both ducted and ductless fan coil units with chilled water and hot water coils — served by an air-cooled chiller with scroll compressors and variable primary constant-speed pumping. A factory-packaged Dedicated Outdoor Air System (DOAS) provides 100% outside air ventilation, decoupling ventilation delivery from sensible load management and ensuring code-compliant ventilation rates independent of occupancy and process variations. Supplemental ductless mini-split systems (air-cooled, rated per ARI 210/240) serve areas with distinct thermal zones or occupancy patterns.

Heating is provided by gas-fired boilers conforming to ASME CSD-1 and ASME BPVC Section IV, supplying hot water to the 4-pipe coil circuit. In-line exhaust fans serve point-source and general exhaust needs, with ventilation in paint mixing, paint booth, and hazardous storage areas designed per ACGIH Industrial Ventilation (31st Edition) requirements. System-wide control is provided by DDC/electronic controls with programmable thermostats compliant with UFC 3-410-02.
