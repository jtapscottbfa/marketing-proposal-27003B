The electrical engineering scope for the P-1127U Composite Shop at Naval Station Norfolk delivered a complete new electrical service and distribution infrastructure for the design-build facility. Design was governed by UFC 3-501-01 (Electrical Engineering), UFC 3-520-01 (Interior Electrical Systems), UFC 3-530-01 (Interior and Exterior Lighting and Controls), UFC 3-580-01 (Telecommunications Building Cabling Systems Planning and Design), and NFPA 70 NEC 2020 Edition.

New utility primary conductors were designed from the nearest utility switch, feeding a new utility transformer sized for the facility load. The distribution hierarchy flows from a 480/277V main distribution panel through two 480/277V sub-fed panels, a step-down 480-208/120V transformer, and a 208/120V sub-fed panel — providing voltage levels for HVAC equipment, shop machinery, specialty equipment connections spanning 480V to 120V, interior and exterior lighting, and convenience loads. All interior branch wiring uses copper THHN/THWN conductors in EMT and rigid steel conduit.

Lighting is all-LED throughout, with enclosed/gasketed and explosion-proof fixtures specified for paint booth, paint mixing, and hazardous material storage areas classified under NEC Article 500. Occupancy and vacancy sensors serve common areas, bathrooms, and offices. Emergency egress lighting uses battery-unit fixtures; exit signs include integral battery backup. Exterior wall-mounted fixtures incorporate integral battery backup and photocell controls for dusk-to-dawn operation.

Communications, telecom, and low-voltage infrastructure pathways — including conduit systems for structured cabling, electronic security, and fire alarm — are included within the electrical scope, providing a complete rough-in infrastructure for low-voltage systems.
