The plumbing engineering scope for the P-1127U Composite Shop at Naval Station Norfolk covered domestic water distribution, sanitary drainage, compressed air, and specialty safety plumbing for a new military aviation maintenance facility. Design was governed by UFC 3-420-01 (Plumbing Systems) and ICC IPC 2021.

Domestic cold and hot water distribution uses copper piping per ASTM B88 (Type K, L, or M per service requirements). Hot water is generated by gas-fired storage water heaters conforming to CSA/AM Z21.10.1 and Z21.10.3. Sanitary waste and vent piping is cast iron in hub-and-spigot or hubless configurations, with cast iron floor drains featuring integral seepage pans installed at maintenance and shop areas. Reduced pressure zone (RPZ) backflow preventers protect the potable water supply from the facility's industrial processes.

A duplex factory-packaged compressed air system serves the shop's aviation maintenance workstations. The system uses electric-motor-driven compressors with an ASME BPVC Section VIII–compliant pressure vessel rated at 200 PSI. The duplex configuration provides operational redundancy, ensuring continuous compressed air availability during maintenance or servicing of one compressor unit.

Emergency safety plumbing includes an eyewash and safety shower station compliant with ANSI/ISEA Z358.1 (2014, revised 2020), with tempered water supply serving the paint mixing room, paint booth, and hazardous chemical storage areas where personnel exposure to corrosive or hazardous substances is a design consideration. The integrated floor drain and compressed air distribution systems reflect the composite shop's industrial program requirements that distinguish it from standard administrative or light commercial facilities.
