The P-1127U Composite Shop is a new-construction design-build project at Naval Station Norfolk, Norfolk, Virginia, delivering a purpose-built intermediate maintenance level composite repair shop for the CMV-22B aircraft. Procured by NAVFAC Mid-Atlantic under an 8(a) Multiple Award Construction Contract (MACC) task order (N40085-23-R-2541), the project carried an estimated combined design-and-construction budget of approximately $3,870,000 and a contractual task order duration of 515 calendar days from award.

The facility accommodates composite component repair operations and includes shops, administrative space, a paint mixing room, paint booth space, hazardous material storage, and other support areas. The structure employs a reinforced concrete masonry unit (CMU) wall system with exterior split-faced CMU veneer, a concrete pile foundation, and a standing-seam metal roof system.

The design subconsultant provided full plumbing, mechanical, and electrical (PME) engineering services under the design-build team. Mechanical systems are anchored by a 4-pipe fan coil system — served by an air-cooled chiller and a dedicated outdoor air unit — supplemented by ductless mini-splits, gas-fired boilers, and DDC controls compliant with UFC 3-410-01 and UFC 3-410-02. Electrical design delivers a new utility transformer, multi-tier 480/277V and 208/120V distribution, full LED lighting with hazardous-area-rated fixtures, emergency battery lighting, and conduit infrastructure for telecom, electronic security, and fire alarm. Plumbing systems cover domestic cold and hot water, sanitary drainage, a duplex 200 PSI compressed air system, RPZ backflow prevention, emergency eyewash/shower, and specialty floor drainage — all per UFC 3-420-01.
