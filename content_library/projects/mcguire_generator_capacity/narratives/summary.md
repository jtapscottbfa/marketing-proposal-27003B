The design team provided architectural and engineering design services for an emergency power capacity upgrade at the Hunter Holmes McGuire VA Medical Center in Richmond, Virginia (BFA Project 13-025N, VA Project 652-17-100, A/E IDIQ Contract VA246-12-D-0105). The scope extended emergency-power backup across the entirety of Buildings 500 and 501 with 20% future-load growth, all on the 13.2kV system upstream of the existing main switchgear. The design included a multi-generator paralleling-switchgear plant with new Caterpillar gensets, ASCO paralleling switchgear, a new medium-voltage transfer switch, a load bank, new generator pads, concrete-encased MV duct bank, and coordination with Dominion Power for Level 3 soft-closed-transitions interconnection. The design complies with N+1 redundancy requirements for emergency power systems. Construction was awarded to Greenland Enterprises, Inc. (Hampton, VA) under Contract 36C4618C0099, with BFA providing construction-phase support including documented advisory on a 2021 ASCO paralleling-switchgear damage event. Design was issued through final submittal in February 2018; construction record drawings are dated March 2022.
