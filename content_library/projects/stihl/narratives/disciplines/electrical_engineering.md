The electrical engineering scope spanned the full range of industrial power distribution services across a multi-building manufacturing campus in Virginia Beach, Virginia, executed through six discrete engagements between 2020 and 2025.

Core work included short circuit and protective device coordination analysis performed using SKM Power Tools, establishing available fault current at 53,829A symmetrical at the main switchboards and identifying multiple downstream circuit breakers whose interrupting ratings were exceeded under the new system configuration — with replacement recommended prior to energization. Design deliverables for the MS3 and MS4 switchboard replacement program included construction drawings (legend, demolition plan, new work plan, switchboard elevations, single-line diagram, and panel schedules) and a complete Division 26 specification package covering switchboards, busway, grounding, conductors, and equipment identification.

Subsequent electrical engineering work included a new 5,000A, 480/277V main circuit breaker switchboard for Plant 5 / Warehouse 5, sized through a formal electrical service and load calculation at 3,191.4 kVA / 3,835.8A total demand, with distribution panels serving EV charging stations, industrial battery chargers, infrared and unit heaters, and mechanical systems. Emergency power system design was delivered for the Guide Bar Plant, with initial and final drawing packages produced across two design cycles. Additional electrical design covered data center main circuit breaker analysis, injection molding machine accessories power, and tap box / busway distribution design.

The discipline team served as prime consultant and sole engineering discipline across all engagements, self-performing analysis, design, and document production.
