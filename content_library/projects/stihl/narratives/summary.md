The design team provided electrical engineering services for an industrial manufacturing complex in Virginia Beach, Virginia, executing a series of discrete but interrelated electrical engineering engagements over a multi-year period spanning 2020 through 2025. Work encompassed short circuit and coordination studies, switchboard and switchgear replacement design, emergency power system design, data center electrical analysis, and expansion of power distribution infrastructure to support new manufacturing and warehousing operations.

Primary scope included replacement of two 4,000A, 480Y/277V main switchboards (MS3 and MS4), each served by 2,500 kVA pad-mounted utility transformers at 34,500V primary. A formal short circuit and coordination study quantified available fault current at 53,829A symmetrical, confirmed that Service #3 was operating at 60% of transformer capacity and Service #4 at 100% capacity (both with a 125% future-load factor applied), and identified multiple under-rated protective devices requiring immediate replacement. Engineering design deliverables included construction drawings, Division 26 specifications, and panel schedules for the replacement switchgear. A subsequent engagement designed a new 5,000A, 480/277V switchboard for Plant 5 / Warehouse 5 (SWBD-W5), sized to serve a total demand of approximately 3,191 kVA / 3,836A and accommodating EV charging infrastructure, battery chargers, infrared heating, and mechanical equipment loads. Additional work addressed Guide Bar Plant emergency power, a data center electrical study, injection molding machine accessories power, and tap box / busway design. The design team served as prime consultant throughout, self-performing all electrical engineering scope.
