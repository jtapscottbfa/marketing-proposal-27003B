The electrical engineering scope on this project consisted entirely of an existing-conditions survey: field investigation and documentation of all electrical systems on the mezzanine level of NSWC Building 77H West, supporting Phase 2 roof repair demolition. The electrical team inventoried and mapped lighting fixtures (1×4 and 1×8 fluorescent types mounted to steel beam undersides and flush-recessed in the gypsum deck), embedded 1/2-inch conduit throughout the deck, 3/4-inch conduit on steel beams, flush and surface junction boxes, smoke and CO detectors, and NMCI fiber cabling infrastructure. Electrical distribution was traced from ground-floor panels — Panel L-16 (250A, 240/120V, 3-phase) and Panel MDP (600A, 480Y/277V, 3-phase) — through circuits serving the mezzanine lighting and elevator disconnect, and diagrammed in a one-line drawing to confirm which circuits required demolition and which were to remain.

The survey was documented in a four-sheet PE-sealed drawing set (E-101 through E-103) covering the full west mezzanine from column lines D/E-10 through D/E-31. Sub-disciplines engaged were electrical power distribution and electrical lighting. The deliverable gave the general contractor the field-verified circuit and device data needed to safely price and execute the lighting removal scope without disrupting life-safety, elevator, or HVAC control systems that were to remain in service.
