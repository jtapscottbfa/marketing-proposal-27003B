The project involved an existing-conditions electrical survey of the mezzanine level at Naval Surface Warfare Center (NSWC) Building 77H West, performed in support of Phase 2 roof repair work. The survey team documented the full inventory of electrical systems embedded in and attached to the gypsum roof deck — including fluorescent lighting fixtures, flush-mounted and surface junction boxes, conduit runs embedded in the deck and routed along steel beams, smoke and CO detectors, and NMCI fiber cabling infrastructure. Electrical distribution serving the mezzanine was traced from ground-floor panels (Panel L-16, 250A, 240/120V, 3-phase; Panel MDP, 600A, 480Y/277V, 3-phase) through the mezzanine lighting circuit and elevator disconnect. Abandoned mechanical ductwork throughout the mezzanine was also inventoried as part of the survey scope.

The deliverable was a four-sheet set of existing-conditions drawings (sheets E-101 through E-103) covering the full west mezzanine zone from columns D/E-10 through D/E-31. The drawings provided the general contractor with the detailed field-verified information needed to safely plan removal of electrical devices from the gypsum deck prior to roof demolition. The project was executed for the Naval Facilities Engineering Command — Mid Atlantic (NAVFAC MIDLANT) under a federal IDIQ/task-order contract vehicle, with the survey drawings completed and sealed in February 2024.
