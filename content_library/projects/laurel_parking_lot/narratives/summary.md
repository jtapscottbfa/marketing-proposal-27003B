The design team provided architectural and engineering services for a new 40-space surface parking lot expansion at Camp Peary (AFETA), a Department of Defense facility in Williamsburg, Virginia. The project was procured under qualifications-based selection procedures consistent with the Brooks Act and FAR 36, with the design team serving as prime consultant.

Civil engineering scope encompassed site design and paving for the new parking field, earthwork, and a drive aisle reconfiguration in an adjacent existing lot that reduced its effective capacity by three spaces to create an integrated circulation connection. Electrical engineering scope covered complete exterior site lighting design for the new lot: six new 20-foot light poles plus one replacement pole at an existing location, all using CREE Edge Series LED fixtures (Type III Medium distribution, 4000K color temperature, bronze finish) mounted on Valmont soft-square steel poles. Power is distributed via underground conduit (Schedule 40 PVC) with copper conductors, and the design achieves a minimum of one foot-candle illumination across the new parking field.

Design services were completed through a final Electrical Basis of Design. All deliverables conformed to AFETA's A-E submission requirements, including applicable federal, DoD, and installation-specific codes and standards. The project demonstrates experience on federal Department of Defense site improvement work, including parking lot expansion, exterior site lighting design for secure installations, and compliance with installation-specific design submission frameworks.
