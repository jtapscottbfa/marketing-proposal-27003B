Electrical engineering scope for the Laurel Parking Lot expansion at AFETA Camp Peary, Williamsburg, Virginia encompassed complete exterior site lighting design for a new 40-space surface parking lot on a Department of Defense installation. The design provided a total of seven light poles: six new poles in the new parking field and one replacement pole at an existing installation location. All poles are Valmont DS330-400Q200-D1-DB-FBC-AB 20-foot soft-square steel poles finished in bronze. Luminaires are CREE ARE-EDG-3M-DA-06-E-UL-BZ-700-R (The Edge Series, Type III Medium distribution, 4000K color temperature, bronze finish), specified to achieve a minimum illumination level of one foot-candle uniformly across the new parking field.

Power distribution is routed via underground conduit (Schedule 40 PVC) with copper THHN/THWN conductors, providing a durable system appropriate for a permanent surface parking installation at a federal facility. Electrical power distribution design supported the full lighting circuit serving all seven poles.

Design deliverables were developed in conformance with AFETA's A-E submission requirements (Appendix II) and applicable federal and DoD electrical standards per Appendix I. A final Electrical Basis of Design was produced under BFA project number 22-052, establishing the complete technical basis for design-bid-build procurement of the electrical construction scope.
