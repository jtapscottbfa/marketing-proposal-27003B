Civil engineering scope for the Laurel Parking Lot expansion at AFETA Camp Peary, Williamsburg, Virginia addressed site design and paving for a new 40-space surface parking lot on a Department of Defense installation. Civil scope included site layout and pavement design for the new parking field, earthwork and grading, and site utility coordination to integrate the new lot with existing infrastructure.

The scope also included reconfiguration of an adjacent existing parking area: three spaces at the motor pool access location were removed to create an integrated drive aisle connection between the new lot and the existing circulation network. This reconfiguration was necessary to establish workable circulation between the new 40-space field and the two existing lots on the installation.

Civil design deliverables were developed in conformance with AFETA's A-E submission requirements (Appendix II) and applicable federal, DoD, and installation-specific site design standards (Appendix I).
