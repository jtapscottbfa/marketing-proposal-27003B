# Laurel Parking Lot — Architectural and Engineering Services

## Project Overview

The design team provided architectural and engineering services for the Laurel Parking Lot expansion at Camp Peary, home of the Armed Forces Experimental Training Activity (AFETA), a Department of Defense facility located at 1100 Executive Drive, Williamsburg, Virginia. The project was procured under RFP No. 2225095346, issued 19 July 2022, under qualifications-based selection procedures consistent with the Brooks Act and FAR 36. The design team served as prime consultant for the full scope of design services.

The project addressed installation-specific site improvement needs: expanding parking capacity at an existing DoD installation while integrating new exterior lighting infrastructure appropriate for a permanent, secure federal facility.

## Scope

The scope of work covered the design of a new 40-space surface parking lot located south of the existing building cluster at Camp Peary (Buildings 1601, 1602, 1604, 1639, 1649, 1657, 1658, and 1659). An adjacent existing lot was reconfigured by removing three spaces at the motor pool access location to establish a new drive aisle connection between the new lot and the existing circulation network, bringing the modified existing lot's effective capacity to 35 spaces.

**Civil engineering scope** included site design and paving layout for the new 40-space parking field, earthwork, and site utility coordination. Civil design addressed pavement construction and grading to integrate the new lot with existing site conditions, as well as the drive aisle reconfiguration in the adjacent lot.

**Electrical engineering scope** covered complete exterior site lighting design for the new parking facility. The design provided seven light poles in total: six new poles distributed across the new parking field and one replacement pole at an existing installation location. All poles are Valmont DS330-400Q200-D1-DB-FBC-AB 20-foot soft-square steel poles finished in bronze. Luminaires throughout are CREE ARE-EDG-3M-DA-06-E-UL-BZ-700-R (The Edge Series, Type III Medium distribution, 4000K color temperature, bronze finish). Power distribution is provided via underground conduit (Schedule 40 PVC) with copper THHN/THWN conductors. The lighting design was specified to achieve a minimum illumination level of one foot-candle uniformly across the new parking field.

## Role

The design team served as prime consultant for the full scope of A-E services. Work was performed under the AFETA A-E Services contract framework. All design deliverables were required to conform to AFETA's Appendix I (applicable codes, standards, regulations, and laws) and Appendix II (A/E submission instructions and requirements), which specify format, content, and sign-and-seal requirements across all design disciplines including site, structural, MEP, electrical, fire protection, and specifications. The code and standards environment encompassed federal, DoD, and installation-specific requirements as established by AFETA.

## Key Challenges and Resolutions

**Federal installation design compliance.** Work at an AFETA installation required navigation of a comprehensive, installation-specific submission requirements framework spanning all engineering disciplines. The team structured design deliverables to satisfy AFETA's Appendix II requirements covering organization, content, drawing format, and specification structure across a multi-discipline project.

**Photometric compliance for a permanent parking field.** Achieving a uniform minimum illumination of one foot-candle across the new 40-space lot required deliberate fixture selection and pole placement. The design resolved this through the Type III Medium distribution pattern of the CREE Edge Series luminaires mounted at 20 feet, sized and spaced to meet the specified minimum uniformly across the field.

**Circulation integration with existing lots.** Introducing the new lot required modifying the existing adjacent parking area's layout — removing three spaces to create a new drive aisle — without degrading overall installation circulation. The design coordinated new and existing conditions through the concept site plan to establish a workable circulation solution.

## Outcomes

Design services were completed through production of a final Electrical Basis of Design (BFA project number 22-052), establishing the technical basis for design-bid-build procurement of construction services. The project demonstrates the design team's capacity to execute site improvement A-E work on active DoD installations under qualifications-based procurement, meeting installation-specific submission and code compliance requirements across civil and electrical disciplines.
