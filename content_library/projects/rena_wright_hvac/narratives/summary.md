The design team provided architectural and engineering design services for a partial HVAC replacement at Rena B. Wright Primary School in Chesapeake, Virginia, under Chesapeake Public Schools' Department of New Construction (BFA Project 22-037A). The scope included full demolition of the existing rooftop multi-zone units, packaged rooftop units, kitchen hood and exhaust/makeup air system, roof-mounted exhaust fans, electric unit heaters, and the entire HVAC control system. New work included rooftop RTUs with fan-powered series VAV boxes serving the classrooms and media center; packaged gas rooftop units serving the gym, cafeteria, office areas, kitchen, and single-zone spaces; a new code-compliant kitchen hood with fire suppression; new roof-mounted exhaust fans; new electric unit heaters; 2×2 lay-in ceilings with new LED lights; a new Trane DDC wireless control system; ductless mini-splits for MDF and IDF closets; and new gas piping with supports. Chesapeake Public Schools pre-purchased the HVAC equipment, curbs, and isolation rails directly from Damuth Trane; the controls package and equipment start-up were included in the General Contract. The design proceeded through 50% Submittal to City (June 2023), 95% Submittal (September 2023), Final Submittal (October 2023), and Re-Final Submittal (November 2023). The fee proposal was signed by Michael E. Dixon, BFA VP, March 15, 2023.
