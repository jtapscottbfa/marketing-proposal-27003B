# Electrical Engineering — Lidl Lighting Retrofit (Stores #1057 & #1105)

The electrical engineering scope on this project centered on documentation and technical coordination for a full LED lighting retrofit across two Lidl grocery stores in Virginia Beach, Virginia. The design team produced electrical lighting floor plans (sheet E-101) and fixture schedules (sheet E-501) for each location, specifying the complete new LED system layout within the single-story sales floor footprints.

The specified lighting system comprised three TRILUX/Oktalite fixture types: primary-aisle linear suspended LEDs (TRILUX 7650OT LED5500-840 ET), shorter supplemental linear fixtures (TRILUX 7650OT LED2200-840 ETDD), and Oktalite EL370 LobuG2 spotlights for end-cap and perimeter positions. All fixtures were designed for 120V supply and mounted at 11'-6" above finished floor on a trunk-mounting accessory system. A key electrical constraint was the retrofit-overlay strategy: the design required reuse of existing lighting circuits in both stores rather than installation of new circuit infrastructure, which is explicit in the drawing general notes.

The electrical scope also included coordination with the fixture manufacturer (TRILUX/Trillux US) on product data and RFI resolution, and photometric calculation verification to confirm the resulting illuminance levels met the program's sales-floor targets. Lighting calculations confirmed an average of 53.9 footcandles across the primary sales floor at a 0.9 light loss factor for both stores. At Store #1105, the electrical drawings accommodated a partial prior-vendor installation on two aisles, incorporating field verification requirements into the drawing notes for those circuits.
