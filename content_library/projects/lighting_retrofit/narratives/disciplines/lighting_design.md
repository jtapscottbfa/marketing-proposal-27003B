# Lighting Design — Lidl Lighting Retrofit (Stores #1057 & #1105)

The lighting design scope on this project encompassed fixture selection, layout, and photometric validation for a full LED retrofit at two Lidl grocery stores in Virginia Beach, Virginia. The design team developed a standardized lighting system using TRILUX and Oktalite products, applied consistently across both Store #1057 (6196 Providence Road) and Store #1105 (1209 Edison Road) under a single set of coordinated deliverables.

The fixture palette consisted of three types: TRILUX 7650OT LED5500-840 ET linear suspended fixtures for primary aisle coverage, TRILUX 7650OT LED2200-840 ETDD short linear fixtures for fill zones, and Oktalite EL370 LobuG2 MF LED4000-831 spotlights for end-cap and perimeter accent positions, mounted at a 60-degree tilt angle. Fixture placement referenced lighting calculations for spotlight aiming angles, with the full photometric analysis documented in separate calculation packages dated March 18, 2021.

Photometric calculations confirmed a sales-floor average illuminance of 53.9 footcandles (maximum 112.7 fc, minimum 2.8 fc) at a 0.9 light loss factor for both stores — a consistent result reflecting the standardized layout approach. The lighting design was constrained by the requirement to reuse existing circuit infrastructure, which shaped fixture positioning and zoning on the floor plans. At Store #1105, lighting design coordination addressed partial prior installation on two aisles by another vendor, requiring the design documentation to account for the existing fixture condition rather than treating the sales floor as a clean-slate layout.
