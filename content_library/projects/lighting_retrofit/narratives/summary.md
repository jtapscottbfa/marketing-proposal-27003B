BFA served as engineering subconsultant to DWM, Inc. on a multi-store LED lighting retrofit program for Lidl US grocery locations in Virginia Beach, Virginia. The program encompassed two stores simultaneously — Store #1057 at 6196 Providence Road and Store #1105 at 1209 Edison Road — both single-story retail grocery facilities. Under BFA's project number 20-008, the team delivered CAD/as-built documentation, photometric calculations, and lighting fixture coordination across both locations, with progress drawings and calculation packages completed in March 2021.

The retrofit replaced existing lighting with a standardized TRILUX/Oktalite LED system throughout the sales floor. The design specified 242 TRILUX linear LED fixtures for primary aisle illumination, 40 shorter linear fixtures for supplemental coverage, and 132 Oktalite EL370 spotlights for end-cap and perimeter accent lighting — all mounted at 11'-6" above finished floor on a trunk-mounting system and supplied through the stores' existing 120V lighting circuits. Photometric analysis verified sales-floor average illuminance of 53.9 footcandles, confirming the design met target levels across both locations. A key coordination task at Store #1105 involved accommodating aisles that a prior vendor had already partially retrofit; BFA's drawings incorporated field verification requirements for those circuits and mounting conditions. The standardized fixture specification and common circuit overlay strategy across both stores reflect a program-level approach to phased chain-retail rollout.
