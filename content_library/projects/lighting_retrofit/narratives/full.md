# Lidl Lighting Retrofit — Stores #1057 & #1105

**Client:** DWM, Inc. | **Facility Owner:** Lidl US, LLC | **Location:** Virginia Beach, VA | **Completed:** March 2021

---

## Project Overview

This project was a commercial LED lighting retrofit program spanning two Lidl US grocery store locations in Virginia Beach, Virginia: Store #1057 at 6196 Providence Road and Store #1105 at 1209 Edison Road. Both are single-story, chain-format grocery retail facilities. The program was executed under a single BFA project number (20-008) with coordinated deliverables — lighting floor plans, fixture schedules, and photometric calculation reports — issued simultaneously for both locations in March 2021. The program represents a standardized, phased rollout approach to LED conversion across Lidl's Virginia Beach store portfolio.

## Scope

BFA's scope of work covered engineering and documentation services for the LED lighting retrofit at both stores, as engaged by DWM, Inc. under separate requests for quote (Job #155222-001 for Store #1105 and Job #162520-001 for Store #1057). Deliverables included:

- **CAD and as-built documentation** — Electrical lighting floor plans (E-101) showing the full new LED fixture layout overlaid on the existing store footprint for each location.
- **Lighting fixture schedule** (E-501) — Specification of all fixture types, mounting configurations, wattages, and product data, standardized across both stores.
- **Photometric calculations** — Lighting calculation packages for each store, authored by the project engineer of record, verifying illuminance levels across the sales floor calc zones.
- **RFI and manufacturer coordination** — Coordination with TRILUX/Trillux US on fixture specifications and product data.

The lighting system specified for both stores consisted of three fixture types from the TRILUX/Oktalite product line: 242 TRILUX 7650OT LED5500-840 ET linear suspended LED fixtures for primary aisle illumination (approximately 40W input, 5,800 lumens per lamp); 40 TRILUX 7650OT LED2200-840 ETDD short linear fixtures for supplemental coverage zones; and 132 Oktalite EL370 LobuG2 MF LED4000-831 L27 spotlights (approximately 32W) for end-cap, perimeter, and accent positions at a 60-degree tilt angle. All fixtures were mounted at 11'-6" above finished floor on a trunk-mounting system and supplied at 120V using the stores' existing lighting circuits, avoiding new circuit infrastructure.

## Role on Project

The engineering team served as subconsultant to DWM, Inc., a facility management and retrofit contractor retained by Lidl US to manage the LED conversion program. The three-party contractual structure — Lidl US as facility owner, DWM as program prime, and the engineering team as technical subconsultant — positioned the engineering role as focused on design documentation and photometric validation rather than construction administration or permit procurement. The design drawings are explicitly labeled as progress documents, consistent with a shop-drawing and coordination function within a facilities retrofit program.

## Key Challenges and Resolutions

**Partial prior-vendor installation at Store #1105.** Aisles #5 and #6 at Store #1105 had been partially retrofit by a different vendor before the engineering team's engagement. Rather than redesigning around those aisles, the drawings incorporated a field verification note requiring confirmation that previously installed fixtures were mounted at the standard 11'-6" AFF height. This accommodated the existing condition without overriding the prior installation or requiring full as-built survey of those circuits before drawing issue.

**Standardized design across two independently-contracted stores.** Although the two stores were issued under separate RFQ job numbers, the design team produced a single fixture specification and a single calculation methodology applicable to both, using identical fixture schedules and nearly identical floor plan configurations. This approach reduced documentation effort and established consistency for future stores in the program.

**Circuit overlay strategy.** The retrofit reused the stores' existing lighting circuit infrastructure — a significant constraint that affected fixture positioning and load calculations. Drawing note (1) on both E-101 plans explicitly directs the installer to use same lighting circuits for new retrofit fixtures, requiring the design to respect the existing circuit topology rather than replanning circuits from scratch.

## Outcomes

Photometric calculations confirmed that the LED retrofit design achieved an average sales-floor illuminance of 53.9 footcandles, with a maximum of 112.7 fc and a minimum of 2.8 fc across Calc Zone #1 (the primary sales floor), at a 0.9 light loss factor. The same result was verified for both Store #1057 and Store #1105, confirming that the standardized fixture layout met target illuminance levels across both locations. Progress drawings and lighting calculations were completed and dated March 18, 2021. Construction-phase outcomes (installation verification, commissioning data) are not captured in the available source documents.
