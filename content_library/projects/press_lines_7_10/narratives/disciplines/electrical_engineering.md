Electrical engineering scope for the Extrusion Press Line Repairs project at NSF Indian Head addresses complete replacement of aging electrical infrastructure across Buildings 561, 567, 568, 569, and ancillary structures at Press Line 7, and Buildings 564, 569, and ancillary structures at Press Line 10. The buildings are active explosive processing facilities, requiring all electrical systems within press bay environments to be designed to NEC hazardous location standards.

New underground electrical service (100-ampere) with a concrete duct bank was designed for the warming oven building clusters at both press lines, with a new disconnect, meter, and surge protection device at the utility pole. A new 120/240-volt panelboard was added in Building 561. Existing electrical panels throughout the affected buildings were demolished and replaced as part of the electrical system upgrade.

LED area lighting throughout the press bays was engineered to Class 1, Division 1, Groups C and D (flammable gases and vapors) and Class 2, Division 1, Groups E, F, and G (combustible dusts) hazardous location classifications, with explosion-proof fixture and conduit-seal assemblies throughout. Exterior LED wall pack fixtures (non-explosion-proof) were designed for building perimeter illumination. A secondary ground girdle system — #6 bare solid copper wire installed throughout the affected buildings — provides supplemental grounding continuity in the hazardous-location environment. New rigid conduit runs on the pressure-treated wood walkways provide circuits for walkway lighting and roughed-in future control wiring. The existing lightning protection system was specified for removal, salvage, and reinstallation with grounding continuity testing per OP-5 and NFPA 780, coordinated with the secondary ground girdle system to ensure continuity across all grounding paths.
