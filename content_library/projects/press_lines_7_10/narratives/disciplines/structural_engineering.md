Structural engineering scope for the Extrusion Press Line Repairs project at NSF Indian Head encompasses existing-conditions assessment and repair design across the building stock of both Press Line 7 (Buildings 561, 568, 567, and ancillary structures) and Press Line 10 (Buildings 564, 569, and ancillary structures). Existing structures are reinforced concrete and concrete masonry construction of mid-20th century vintage, ranging from the below-grade press bays with one-story above-grade control rooms (Buildings 561 and 564) to the single-story warming oven buildings (Buildings 567, 568, 569, and counterparts).

Structural assessment documented existing conditions — including wood-framed roof systems (2-inch by 8-inch rafters at 16 inches on center on CMU and concrete walls) on the warming oven buildings and 3-inch concrete slab-on-fill construction at press bay control room floors — as the basis for repair recommendations and construction documents.

Repair scope includes roofing replacement on the warming oven buildings; installation of a French drain system (4-inch slotted PVC underdrain with gravel, filter fabric, and 6-mil polyethylene sheeting) behind below-grade retaining walls at Buildings 561 and 564 to address moisture infiltration, with temporary removal and reinstallation of adjacent steam and ventilation lines as required; and construction of pressure-treated wood walkways (4-by-4 posts at approximately 92 inches on center, 2-by-10 joists, 2-by-6 decking, 3-foot clear width, safety yellow top rail) at both press lines. Additional scope includes interior finishes, miscellaneous metals, and exterior improvements across the press line building clusters. Structural basis-of-design documents reached the 65% submittal milestone in February 2025.
