This project covered design of repair and renovation work for two parallel extrusion press line facilities — Press Lines 7 and 10 — at Naval Support Facility (NSF) Indian Head, Indian Head, Maryland. The press lines occupy a cluster of small, specialized industrial buildings serving as active explosive processing facilities operated by the Naval Surface Warfare Center Indian Head Division (NSWC IHD). Design services were performed as prime consultant for NAVFAC / Public Works Department South Potomac, with structural basis-of-design documents at the 65% submittal stage as of February 2025.

The project encompasses structural assessment and repair, roofing replacement on historic wood-framed warming oven buildings, moisture remediation via French drain systems behind below-grade concrete retaining walls, and complete electrical system replacement across ten buildings spanning both press lines. Electrical scope required engineering for hazardous (explosive) locations — Class 1, Division 1, Groups C and D and Class 2, Division 1, Groups E, F, and G environments — including explosion-proof LED area lighting, secondary ground girdle systems using #6 bare solid copper wire, and new underground electrical service with concrete duct banks. Hazardous materials abatement (asbestos-containing materials at warming oven ceilings and lead-based paint throughout the building stock) is a primary scope driver. All buildings are within a designated historic district, requiring Section 106 consultation with the Maryland State Historic Preservation Officer (SHPO). The construction period of performance is 335 calendar days per press line, with estimated project costs in the range of $100,000 to $500,000 per press line.
