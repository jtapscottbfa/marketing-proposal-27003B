For the Enthalpy Analytics Labs – Lab Admin Area Renovations project in Richmond, Virginia, the mechanical engineering scope addressed complete HVAC design for the conversion of an existing warehouse into laboratory and administrative space.

The centerpiece of the mechanical design was a coordinated heating, cooling, and ventilation strategy using packaged split-system equipment. Five split-system heat pumps with controls were designed to condition the new lab and admin areas. A dedicated make-up air unit (MAHU) was designed to supply 1,250 CFM to offset exhaust from laboratory fume hoods furnished by others and to maintain positive building pressurization during hood operation — a laboratory safety and indoor air quality requirement central to the project program.

Distribution comprised a variable volume supply and return ductwork system with volume dampers at all branch connections, manual balancing dampers, and motorized control dampers. Thermostat and sensor locations were specified at a minimum of 48 inches above finished floor per applicable code requirements. BAS/DDC controls were incorporated for the full system. Test-and-balance (TAB) was required for the completed installation. Equipment placement was coordinated with the general contractor for openings, clearances, and compliance with panel access restrictions — including a requirement prohibiting mechanical equipment above electrical panels.
