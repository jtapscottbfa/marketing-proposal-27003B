The Enthalpy Analytics Labs – Lab Admin Area Renovations project involved engineering design for the conversion of an existing warehouse space at 1470 East Parham Road in Richmond, Virginia into dedicated laboratory and administrative areas for a private commercial client. The design team served as prime consultant and engineer of record, providing mechanical and electrical engineering services for the build-out.

Mechanical design centered on comfort conditioning and indoor air quality for the new spaces. Five split-system heat pumps were designed to condition the lab and admin areas, supplemented by a dedicated make-up air unit sized to supply 1,250 CFM to offset exhaust from laboratory fume hoods furnished by others. The make-up air system was designed to maintain positive building pressurization during hood operation — a critical requirement for laboratory safety and code compliance. Electrical design encompassed power and lighting for the renovated areas, including circuits supporting the new split systems, LED pendant low-bay lighting, emergency and exit lighting with battery backup, occupancy and dimming sensor controls, and receptacle circuits for lab and admin use. Both 208Y/120V and 480Y/277V distribution systems are represented in the design, with arc flash hazard labeling incorporated on all panelboards per NEC 2020 and NFPA 70E. Design documents were issued in October 2021.
