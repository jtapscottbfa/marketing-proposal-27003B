Electrical engineering on the Magruder Church CLIN 0002 Rehabilitation encompassed a full rewire of the historic chapel at Camp Peary (AFETA), Williamsburg, Virginia. Despite the building's modest scale, the condition of existing wiring required complete replacement — all existing electrical devices and wiring were to be removed and renewed as part of the rehabilitation.

The existing electrical infrastructure retained in place was Panelboard 'A': a 125A, MLO, 240V/120V, single-phase, 3-wire, surface-mounted GE load center (TLMG125 MOD, 10 kAIC minimum) fed through a 2-pole, 100A NEMA 3R exterior disconnect switch. The panel was extended with one new circuit — direct-buried Schedule 40 PVC conduit at minimum 24-inch depth — to serve new parking lot lighting on 14-foot area/street lighting poles, fed from a spare circuit breaker.

Demolition scope required disconnecting and removing wiring for all baseboard heaters (reconnected to new heaters with new wiring), removing outdoor flood light wiring, and saving and reinstalling the existing exterior weatherproof GFCI receptacles, disconnect switch, incandescent pendant light fixture above the entry door, and wall-mounted incandescent light fixtures inside the chapel. The save-and-reinstall approach for the historic pendant and wall fixtures preserved the building's interior character while satisfying rehabilitation requirements.

PE-stamped drawings were produced in compliance with UFC 3-560-01 (Arc Flash) requirements. The completed six-sheet drawing set — including the electrical legend/schedule (E-001), electrical site plan (ES101), and electrical demolition floor plan (ED101) — was issued for construction in October 2022. Arc flash warning labeling was specified per UFC requirements.
