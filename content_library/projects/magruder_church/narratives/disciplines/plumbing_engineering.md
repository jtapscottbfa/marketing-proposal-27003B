Plumbing engineering on the Magruder Church CLIN 0002 Rehabilitation addressed the domestic plumbing systems of the historic chapel at Camp Peary (AFETA), Williamsburg, Virginia. The building operates as a cold-water-only facility — no hot water heating equipment is present — and the plumbing scope was confined to a single restroom alcove within the chapel's modest footprint.

The rehabilitation replaced two fixtures: a new flush-tank water closet and a new wall-hung lavatory, both connected to existing waste piping and existing domestic cold water piping retained for reuse. Cold water supply distribution in the crawl space runs at 3/4-inch and 1/2-inch. An existing exterior yard hydrant was confirmed to remain in service. A frozen shut-off valve in the crawl-space handhole was replaced as part of the scope.

The demolition plan (sheet P-101) required removal of all existing plumbing fixtures while retaining the existing waste piping and domestic cold water piping — a reuse-first approach that minimized disturbance to the historic structure and limited new penetrations through the building's wood-frame floor system. The sole hot-water note is a general drawing note explicitly stating that the building has no hot water heating equipment, establishing the cold-water-only basis for the design.

The constrained plumbing scope — two fixtures, cold water only, with existing distribution reused — reflects both the building's occasional-use occupancy profile and the preservation design philosophy of limiting the intervention footprint within the 1887 historic structure. Construction documents were issued at IFC level in October 2022 as part of the CLIN 0002 rehabilitation drawing set.
