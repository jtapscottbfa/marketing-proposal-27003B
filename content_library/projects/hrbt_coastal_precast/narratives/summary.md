Provided electrical engineering study services for Coastal Precast Systems at their industrial precast concrete manufacturing plant in Cape Charles, Virginia — the fabrication facility supplying precast structural elements for the Hampton Roads Bridge-Tunnel (HRBT) expansion. The engagement comprised a comprehensive electrical systems study delivered as a Final Submittal in February 2021 (BFA Project No. 21-005).

The study encompassed four integrated analytical tasks: a short-circuit analysis of the facility's power distribution system, a protective device coordination study, an ANSI equipment evaluation, and an arc flash evaluation performed in accordance with IEEE 1584-2018, NFPA 70E, and OSHA requirements. The facility is served by a 1,500 kVA utility transformer feeding an 800A, 480V main distribution panel (Square D) that branches to production, welding, lighting, and quality-control panel circuits. All protective devices passed the ANSI equipment evaluation. Arc flash incident energy levels were characterized for each switchgear location, ranging from Level 0 (no PPE required) at the primary 480V distribution panels to Level 3 (arc flash suit required) at downstream 240V panels, establishing the PPE program basis for plant operations. Results were delivered in a Final Submittal study report accompanied by an analytical one-line diagram produced in SKM Power*Tools.
