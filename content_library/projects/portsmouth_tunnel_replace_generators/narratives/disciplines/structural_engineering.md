Structural engineering for the Portsmouth West Vent Building generator replacement was provided by Broyles and Associates, P.C. (Chesapeake, VA) under BFA's prime consultant coordination. The structural scope was the design of the new generator support platform: a reinforced concrete mat foundation and elevated slab system to carry the new Caterpillar 3512C, with a total equipment weight of 115,000 lbs.

The mat foundation measured approximately 32 feet by 12.5 feet and 1 foot thick, supported on 8-inch cast-in-place concrete walls below the elevated slab. The generator platform slab was set at elevation +7.50 feet — coordinated with the civil and electrical designs — to position the generator bottom at elevation 11.00 feet, above the site's base flood elevation of 8.00 feet. The foundation system was designed to Seismic Design Category D and Site Class D per the geotechnical report prepared by GeoEnvironmental Resources, Inc., with an allowable soil bearing capacity of 1,000 psf, a design wind speed of 125 mph (3-second gust), and design per the 2015 Virginia Uniform Statewide Building Code (VUSBC). Anchor bolt layout was coordinated with the generator support frame manufacturer (Chillicothe Metal Company). Galvanized steel railings were specified at the platform stairs and perimeter. The structural package — delivered as part of the coordinated 100% final submittal in November 2020 — included structural general notes, cast-in-place concrete notes, foundation and slab plans, sections, and reinforcing details.
