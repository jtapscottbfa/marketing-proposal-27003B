Civil engineering was provided by Hoggard/Eure Associates, P.C. (Portsmouth, VA) under BFA's prime consultant coordination. The civil scope addressed site development for the exterior generator platform at the Portsmouth West Vent Building, including site plan preparation and siting of the new generator pad within the existing facility footprint adjacent to the Elizabeth River tunnel portal.

The site is located within the Elizabeth River floodplain, with a base flood elevation of 8.00 feet — a governing siting constraint for the replacement generator. Drawing C-101 established the generator platform at a concrete pad elevation of 10.00 feet, with the platform top-of-slab at elevation +7.50 feet and the generator bottom at 11.00 feet, positioning the equipment above the base flood level. Existing building flood vent locations were identified on the site plan. The civil scope also coordinated the routing of new underground electrical conduit from the generator pad to the building (aligned with the electrical design), and located the fuel fill connection at elevation 4.37 feet for accessibility. The site plan was prepared at a scale of 1 inch equals 10 feet and documented the relationship of the new concrete generator platform, electrical conduit routing, and fuel fill access to the existing building and site context. The civil design was issued as part of the coordinated 100% final design submittal in November 2020.
