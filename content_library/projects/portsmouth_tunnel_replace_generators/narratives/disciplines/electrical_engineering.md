BFA served as electrical engineer of record for the Downtown Tunnel Portsmouth West Vent Building generator replacement, leading the complete design of the facility's emergency power system upgrade. The electrical scope centered on replacing an end-of-service standby generator with a new Caterpillar 3512C 1,500 kW / 1,875 kVA diesel unit operating at 480V three-phase, paired with a new 4,000A main switchgear lineup (MDPE) at 480Y/277V. Two ASCO Series 300 automatic transfer switches — an 800A/4-pole and a 400A/4-pole unit, both open-transition — were integrated into the distribution system to provide automatic transfer of critical loads. The design included a new underground conduit ductbank connecting the exterior generator pad to the building's switchgear room, and a complete 48-hour subbase diesel fuel system.

A 19-step construction phasing sequence was developed and documented on drawing E-001 to maintain live power to the tunnel's life safety and ventilation loads throughout the construction period. The sequence covered underground conduit installation, switchgear placement and connection, ATS installation and commissioning, emergency generator commissioning, fuel transfer from the existing underground tank to the new subbase tank, and phased abandonment of the existing generator, fuel tank, day tank, and exhaust systems. The phasing was structured so that active transfer paths were maintained at each construction step before the prior system was decommissioned. The electrical design was produced and submitted as part of the coordinated 100% final design package under Virginia BCOM/DGS project administration in November 2020.
