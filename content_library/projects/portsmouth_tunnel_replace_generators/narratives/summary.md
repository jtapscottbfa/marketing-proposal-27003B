The project replaced the end-of-life emergency diesel generator at Elizabeth River Crossings' Downtown Tunnel (DTT) Portsmouth West Vent Building — a critical tunnel ventilation support facility on the west bank of the Elizabeth River in Portsmouth, Virginia. The design team, led by BFA as prime A/E of record, replaced the existing standby unit with a new Caterpillar 3512C 1,500 kW / 1,875 kVA diesel generator operating at 480V three-phase, providing 48 hours of fuel autonomy to sustain tunnel ventilation and life safety systems during utility outages. The electrical scope included a new 4,000A main switchgear lineup, two ASCO Series 300 automatic transfer switches (800A and 400A, open-transition), and a new underground conduit ductbank interconnecting the exterior generator platform to the building's power distribution system. A 19-step construction phasing sequence was developed to maintain continuous operation of the active tunnel facility throughout construction. Structural subconsultant Broyles and Associates, P.C. designed a reinforced concrete mat foundation and elevated slab platform — approximately 32 feet by 12.5 feet — capable of supporting the 115,000-lb generator under Seismic Design Category D and 125-mph wind criteria. Civil subconsultant Hoggard/Eure Associates, P.C. addressed site grading and floodplain constraints, with the generator pad elevated to place the equipment bottom at elevation 11.00 feet, above the 8.00-foot base flood elevation. The 100% final design package was submitted November 5, 2020, under Virginia BCOM/DGS project administration. BFA's 100% construction cost estimate totaled $1,260,260.
