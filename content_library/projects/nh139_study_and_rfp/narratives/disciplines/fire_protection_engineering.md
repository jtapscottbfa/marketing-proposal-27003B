Fire protection engineering was engaged for the Design-Build RFP phase of the project, with Jensen Hughes providing fire protection services to the prime A-E team. Fire protection scope for the DB RFP addressed system requirements associated with the replacement power generation and UPS equipment areas: sprinkler system modifications required within UPS equipment rooms to accommodate the new installations, and dry-pipe sprinkler protection requirements for unheated areas associated with new equipment. The RFP also specified that the existing addressable fire alarm and mass notification system panels in the building would remain in service, with new equipment integrated into the existing system infrastructure. The 100% Study documented the existing fire protection systems — including wet-pipe sprinkler coverage in occupied areas and dry-pipe or pre-action sprinkler protection in applicable zones — and the existing addressable fire alarm system, establishing the baseline from which the DB RFP fire protection requirements were developed. Specific fire protection engineering scope detail beyond the RFP requirements is not captured in the source record; the fire protection discipline's primary contribution was developing the fire suppression and alarm requirements for the design-builder's use in the P-1401 replacement scope areas.
