Electrical engineering was the primary technical discipline for both phases of the project. During the 100% Study phase, the team performed a comprehensive field assessment of Building NH-139's complete electrical distribution system. The assessment began at the 34.5 kV primary service infrastructure — including SF6 switch SW-233 (38 kV rated, end-of-life) serving two pad-mounted substation transformers (1,000 kVA and 750 kVA, both end-of-life) via underground ductbank — and traced the distribution through Switchgear 1A (Westinghouse, 3,000 A / 208V, 1979 vintage, end-of-life), Switchboard Main 1 (3,000 A), and Switchboard Main 2 (Westinghouse, 2,000 A / 208V, 1989, Quonset hut, end-of-life). Switchboard 3 (Cutler Hammer Power-R-Line, 1,600 A / 208V, excellent condition) in the penthouse was designated for retention. Emergency power systems were documented in detail: Generators 1 and 2 (Caterpillar, 600 kW each, Room 158B, good condition, retain) and Generator 3 (Caterpillar, 600 kW, Quonset hut, recommend replacement under P-1401). UPS systems included two 500 kVA units in Room 199A (to be replaced under P-1401) and a 250 kVA unit in Room A116 (good condition, retain). Approximately 30 panelboards across the building were inventoried and assessed via one-line diagrams (Sheets E-601 through E-639). For the Design-Build RFP phase, electrical engineering produced performance and prescriptive specifications for replacement of the prioritized power generation and distribution assets — Generator 3, UPS units 1 and 2, Switchgear 1A, designated distribution panels, and PLCs — along with Bid Option provisions for additional scope.
