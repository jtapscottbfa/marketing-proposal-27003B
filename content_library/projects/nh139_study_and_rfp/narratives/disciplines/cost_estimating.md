Cost estimating was performed by Osmundson & Associates Cost Consultants, Inc. as a subconsultant to the prime A-E as part of the 100% HVAC/Electrical Systems Study. The primary deliverable was a Class 5 rough-order-of-magnitude cost estimate for comprehensive mechanical and electrical system renewal across Building NH-139, computed at approximately $25.8 million — labeled "Estimated Construction Cost (ECC)" in the study appendix, though properly understood as a ROM planning-level estimate for capital programming purposes. The estimate used a primary facility area of 78,800 square feet as the cost model basis and covered the full scope of system replacement identified during the field investigation, including HVAC system renewal, electrical service and distribution replacement, emergency power system upgrades, controls transition, and UPS replacements. This $25.8 million figure is substantially larger than the authorized MILCON P-1401 program amount of approximately $3,930,000 — which funds only the highest-priority backup power replacement subset. The cost estimate was a critical deliverable informing NAVFAC's capital planning decisions for the facility, formally quantifying the gap between the funded near-term project and the total capital investment the building's aging infrastructure requires. The distinction between the study ROM ($25.8M) and the authorized MILCON ECC ($3.6M) is material for any downstream proposal use of this project's cost data.
