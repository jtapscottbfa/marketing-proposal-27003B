Architectural services were provided by Dills Architects, P.C. as a subconsultant to the prime A-E. The architectural scope supported the 100% Study through documentation of the building's spatial configuration — a complex two-story structure with two mechanical penthouses (North and South) housing approximately 78,800 gross square feet, constructed in 1964 and expanded through subsequent additions over its sixty-year history. Architectural drawings produced for the study (Sheets A-101 through A-107, plus roof plans) documented partial first and second floor plans, penthouse floor plans, and roof plans in Revit, establishing the spatial context for the mechanical and electrical systems assessment. These drawings identified the locations of critical equipment rooms relative to occupied command spaces: electrical rooms 158A, 158B, 199A, and 199B on the first floor; UPS Room A116 and A116A in the first floor Area B; and the North and South penthouse mechanical rooms. The second-floor plan identified sensitive command spaces (NOC Room 211, GOC Room 215, planning rooms, and NIWDC/JRIC suite areas) that informed the HVAC zoning assessment and field survey scheduling constraints. For the Design-Build RFP phase, architectural documentation provided building configuration information required for the design-builder's design development. Architecture on this project was primarily a documentation and support discipline enabling MEP engineering assessment and DB RFP production; the project scope was system replacement in an existing federal facility rather than new construction or significant architectural design.
