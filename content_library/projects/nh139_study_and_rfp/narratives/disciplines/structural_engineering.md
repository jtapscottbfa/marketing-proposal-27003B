Structural engineering was provided by Broyles and Associates, P.C. as a subconsultant to the prime A-E for the Design-Build RFP phase. The structural scope addressed provisions for new emergency generation and UPS equipment installations — including structural requirements for new generator pads, equipment bases, and any penetrations or structural modifications associated with the replacement power systems specified in MILCON P-1401. The 1964-vintage building's structural system provided the context for the DB RFP's structural provisions, supporting the design-builder's design development for equipment support structures. Structural engineering on this project was a targeted supporting discipline scoped to the specific equipment replacement needs of the authorized P-1401 work, rather than a comprehensive structural assessment of the existing building fabric. Specific structural scope details beyond DB RFP participation are not captured in the source record.
