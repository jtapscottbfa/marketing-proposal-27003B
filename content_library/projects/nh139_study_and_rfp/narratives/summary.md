This project comprised two linked A-E scopes at Building NH-139, Naval Support Activity Hampton Roads, Norfolk, Virginia, performed for NAVFAC Mid-Atlantic (NAVFAC MIDLANT) under IDIQ contract N40085-20-D-0005. The design team, serving as prime A-E consultant, first conducted a comprehensive 100% HVAC/Electrical Systems Study (completed December 2023) to assess the condition of the 1964-vintage, 78,800-square-foot facility — a multi-story command building continuously occupied by Navy intelligence and warfighting tenants including the Navy Naval Information Warfighting Development Center (NIWDC), Joint Regional Intelligence Center (JRIC), United States Fleet Forces Command (USFF), and a Defense Intelligence Agency Joint Staff element. The study inventoried aging mechanical and electrical infrastructure spanning rooftop air-cooled chillers, penthouse air-handling units with pneumatic VAV systems, computer room air-conditioning units on R-22 refrigerant, a 34.5 kV primary electrical service with 1979-vintage switchgear, two 600 kW emergency diesel generators, and UPS systems — producing a retain-or-replace matrix and a rough-order-of-magnitude estimate of approximately $25.8 million for comprehensive system renewal.

Building on the study's findings, the team prepared a complete Design-Build Request for Proposals (Final DB RFP, November 2024) for FY22 MILCON Project P-1401: replacement of the building's backup power generation systems, UPS units, and PLCs — the highest-priority subset of the identified capital program, with authorized MILCON funding of approximately $3.9 million. The RFP included performance and prescriptive specifications, design development drawings across all disciplines, and competitive bid options (Generator 3 replacement; Switchboard Main 2 replacement) to maximize utilization of available appropriations for this operationally critical federal facility.
