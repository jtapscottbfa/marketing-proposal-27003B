## Project Overview

The Nansemond Treatment Plant Struvite Recovery Facility Improvements and Digester Capacity Upgrade is a capital improvement project for the Hampton Roads Sanitation District (HRSD) at the Nansemond Treatment Plant in Suffolk, Virginia (Contract NP013700, Hazen project number 32530-012). The project involves multiple facility upgrades across the treatment plant site, including a struvite recovery process and digester capacity expansion. The electrical scope included new medium-voltage power distribution to serve the upgraded process equipment. This record documents the engineering calculation work performed for the medium-voltage cable installation in Area 5 of the plant — specifically the PE-sealed wire pull calculations prepared to support the construction-phase cable installation.

## Scope

The electrical scope captured in the source record covers a focused but technically specific task: the design and calculation support for pulling new 5kV medium-voltage power cables through an existing underground conduit system in Area 5 of the Nansemond Treatment Plant. The conduit system is a 4-inch PVC Schedule 40 or HDPE raceway running 156 linear feet through the site, traversing manholes and process areas including the Pre-Dewatering Building (PA 130), Grit Tanks (PA 120), WASSTRIP (PA 110), and SNIR (PA 820), and interfacing with existing ductbank system segments E-33-0104, E-33-0106, and E-33-0107.

Two cable pulls were designed: Pull A and Pull B, each consisting of three 500 KCMIL, single-conductor, 5kV, 115 Mil EPR 133% insulated, copper tape shielded, non-armored, SIMpull-PVC jacketed power cables, plus one 1/0 AWG copper SIMpull THHN ground wire per pull, for a total of two sets. The cables terminate in a 5kV tee splice in a manhole serving Unit Substation USS-1. The pull route required a pulley insert at Manhole E-30 to manage directional change loads. A field-order rerouting near the Pre-Dewatering Building (per RFI 224) was incorporated into the final pull plan, and the design was coordinated with a High Mast Light 6 power re-route (WCD 4) also occurring in the area.

The construction documents were issued as a conformed set dated September 2022, with the applicable electrical site plan (Sheet E14) issued February 2022 and revised to Field Order No. 18 as of November 2023. The wire pull calculations were sealed June 5, 2024.

## Key Challenges and Resolutions

**Conduit routing through a complex multi-manhole path.** The 156-foot pull route was not a straight run — it navigated multiple manholes across an active wastewater treatment plant site with existing underground infrastructure. The pull plan addressed this by sizing the conduit system and cable jacket type (SIMpull low-friction jacket) to minimize pulling forces, and by specifying a pulley insert at Manhole E-30 to manage the load at a directional transition point.

**Field-order rerouting during construction.** The pull route was modified after the original conformed set through RFI 224, reflecting a reroute near the Pre-Dewatering Building. The calculation package was prepared against the updated pull path, incorporating the as-built routing rather than the original design routing.

**Cable installation feasibility verification.** The SIMpull calculations confirmed that both pulls remain within acceptable limits: maximum pulling tension of 416 pounds and maximum sidewall pressure of 202 pounds — both within cable manufacturer ratings. A coefficient of friction of 0.2 (SIMpull recommended value for PVC-jacketed cable in PVC/HDPE conduit) was used, reflecting the low-friction cable jacket system selected for this installation.

**Minor discrepancy in ground wire size.** The SIMpull calculation sheets document the ground wire as 1/0 AWG copper; the red-ink markup on Drawing E14 references a "#1 GRD 600V." This is a minor adjacent-AWG discrepancy (the difference between 1/0 and #1 AWG). The PE-stamped calculation sheets are the governing document; the drawing annotation appears to be a markup simplification.

## Outcomes

The wire pull calculation package — two PE-sealed SIMpull calculation sheets plus the pull plan markup on Sheet E14 — was completed and sealed June 5, 2024. The calculations confirmed that Pull A and Pull B are feasible as designed: both maximum pulling tension (416 lb) and maximum sidewall pressure (202 lb) are within acceptable limits for the specified cable system. The calculation package provided the construction contractor with a verified pull plan, including segment-by-segment tension and sidewall pressure data and a pull diagram.
