The electrical engineering scope on the Nansemond Treatment Plant Struvite Recovery Facility Improvements and Digester Capacity Upgrade focused on medium-voltage power distribution design and construction-phase engineering support for the Area 5 underground cable installation. The work centered on the preparation of PE-sealed wire pull calculations for two 5kV medium-voltage cable pulls (Pull A and Pull B) supplying Unit Substation USS-1 through an existing underground conduit system.

Each pull comprised three 500 KCMIL, single-conductor, 5kV, 115 Mil EPR copper tape shielded SIMpull-PVC jacketed power cables and a 1/0 AWG copper ground wire, routed through 4-inch PVC Schedule 40 or HDPE conduit over a 156-linear-foot path in two sets. SIMpull Cable Pull Calculator software was used to model the full pull route on a segment-by-segment basis. Calculations confirmed a maximum pulling tension of 416 pounds and maximum sidewall pressure of 202 pounds using the SIMpull-recommended coefficient of friction of 0.2 — confirming installation feasibility within the conduit system and cable manufacturer allowables. The pull plan incorporated a pulley insert at Manhole E-30 to manage loads at a directional change, and reflected a field-order rerouting near the Pre-Dewatering Building incorporated through RFI 224. The calculation package was sealed by Virginia PE Richard H. Williams (License No. 048025) on June 5, 2024.
