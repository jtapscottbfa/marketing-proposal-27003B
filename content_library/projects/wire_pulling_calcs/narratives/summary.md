The engineering team provided electrical engineering services for the Hampton Roads Sanitation District (HRSD) Nansemond Treatment Plant Struvite Recovery Facility Improvements and Digester Capacity Upgrade in Suffolk, Virginia (Contract NP013700). Work in Area 5 of the treatment plant required routing new 5kV medium-voltage power cables through an existing underground conduit system to feed Unit Substation USS-1. The team prepared PE-sealed wire pull calculations — performed using SIMpull Cable Pull Calculator software and sealed by a Virginia-licensed Professional Engineer — to verify that the proposed cable installation was feasible within the conduit system's physical constraints.

The scope involved two coordinated cable pulls (Pull A and Pull B), each comprising three 500 KCMIL, 5kV, 115 Mil EPR copper conductors plus a 1/0 AWG copper ground wire, pulled through 4-inch PVC Schedule 40 or HDPE conduit over a 156-linear-foot run in two sets. Using the SIMpull-recommended coefficient of friction of 0.2, calculations confirmed a maximum pulling tension of 416 pounds and a maximum sidewall pressure of 202 pounds — both within cable manufacturer allowables. The pull route navigated multiple manholes, required a pulley insert at Manhole E-30, and incorporated a field-order rerouting near the Pre-Dewatering Building. The calculation package was sealed on June 5, 2024, and supported the project's construction-phase electrical installation activities.
