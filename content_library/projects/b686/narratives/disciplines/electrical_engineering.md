Electrical engineering was included as a design discipline on the Building 686 Renovation at Naval Weapons Station Yorktown (WPNSTA Yorktown), a NAVFAC MIDLANT FY2020 design-bid-build project in Yorktown, Virginia. The electrical scope covered power distribution, lighting systems, and physical security and electronic safety systems infrastructure design, with construction documents produced through 35%, 100% Pre-Final, and Final submission stages.

The scope included electrical power distribution and lighting systems design for the renovated building. It also encompassed infrastructure design for Physical Security Equipment: Electronic Security Systems (ESS), Intrusion Detection Systems (IDS), closed-circuit television (CCTV), and Access Control Systems (AACS). These security systems required design of electrical rough-in, conduit routing, power feeds, and equipment coordination within the renovation construction documents, consistent with DoD physical security requirements for an active military installation.

Specific electrical system details — panel schedules, load calculations, lighting fixture selections, and security system component specifications — are not captured in the available source record. The Basis of Design document and drawing set, which would contain this level of detail, were unavailable during extraction.
