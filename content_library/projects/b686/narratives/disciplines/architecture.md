Architecture was included as a design discipline on the Building 686 Renovation at Naval Weapons Station Yorktown (WPNSTA Yorktown), a NAVFAC MIDLANT FY2020 design-bid-build project in Yorktown, Virginia. The architectural scope covered complete renovation construction documents for an existing military facility, produced through 35%, 100% Pre-Final, and Final design submission stages.

The construction documents package included building renovation drawings and specifications to support a competitive 8(a) set-aside public bid under NAVFAC delivery procedures. The architectural design incorporated demolition coordination, including integration with hazardous materials abatement design, and coordination of Audio-Visual (AV) equipment infrastructure within the renovation. Given the project's location on an active military installation, the architectural work was subject to DoD physical security and antiterrorism/force protection design criteria.

Specific architectural scope details — building size, floor count, existing building program, and room layouts — are not captured in the available source record.
