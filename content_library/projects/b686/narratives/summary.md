Building 686 at Naval Weapons Station Yorktown (WPNSTA Yorktown), Yorktown, Virginia, was renovated under NAVFAC MIDLANT FY2020 project RM 20-1055 (eProjects Work Order 1654651), administered under basic indefinite-delivery contract N40085-17-D-0347, and procured as a design-bid-build 8(a) set-aside task order. The authorized program amount was $1,105,000, with an Estimated Construction Cost of $974,441.

The scope encompassed complete construction documents for building renovation — architectural, mechanical, electrical, and fire protection — along with hazardous materials investigation, survey, laboratory testing, and abatement design integrated into the demolition package. Physical security infrastructure (Electronic Security Systems, Intrusion Detection Systems, CCTV, and Access Control Systems) was designed alongside Audio-Visual equipment infrastructure. Additional deliverables included a Site Engineering Investigation (SEI), environmental and construction permitting support, a Draft DD Form 1354, and Post-Construction Award Services (PCAS). Design submittals were produced at 35%, 100% Pre-Final, and Final stages. The project reached design completion.
