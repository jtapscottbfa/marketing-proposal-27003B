## Project Overview

Building 686 is an existing building at Naval Weapons Station Yorktown (WPNSTA Yorktown) in Yorktown, Virginia. The project was a renovation administered by Naval Facilities Engineering Command Mid-Atlantic (NAVFAC MIDLANT) as FY2020 project RM 20-1055 (eProjects Work Order 1654651), under basic indefinite-delivery contract N40085-17-D-0347. Delivery was design-bid-build, procured as a federal 8(a) set-aside. The authorized program amount was $1,105,000, with an Estimated Construction Cost of $974,441.

## Scope of Work

The scope covered complete design-bid-build construction documents for the renovation of Building 686, spanning architectural, mechanical engineering, electrical engineering, and fire protection engineering disciplines. Design submittals were required at 35%, 100% Pre-Final, and Final stages per NAVFAC task-order delivery standards.

Specific scope elements included:

- **Building renovation construction documents:** Full drawings and specifications for interior renovation, including an independent construction cost estimate.
- **Demolition and hazardous materials:** Demolition design incorporating hazardous materials investigation, field survey and laboratory testing, and abatement design. Environmental compliance documentation and construction permitting support were also required.
- **HVAC systems:** Mechanical HVAC systems design for the renovated building.
- **Electrical systems:** Electrical power distribution and lighting systems design.
- **Fire protection and life safety:** Fire protection systems and fire alarm design.
- **Physical security infrastructure:** Electronic Security Systems (ESS), Intrusion Detection Systems (IDS), closed-circuit television (CCTV), and Access Control Systems (AACS) infrastructure, designed per DoD physical security requirements.
- **Audio-Visual infrastructure:** AV equipment infrastructure design and coordination.
- **Site Engineering Investigation (SEI):** Site-specific investigation to support the renovation design.
- **DD Form 1354:** Draft preparation for DoD real property documentation.
- **Post-Construction Award Services (PCAS):** Construction-phase support services following design completion.

## Role on Project

The design was produced under the basic IDIQ contract structure held by Dills Architects (N40085-17-D-0347). The firm contributed as a subconsultant within this team, delivering engineering design services across the project's disciplines. The Basis of Design document for the project reflects the firm's technical authorship and involvement in the design narrative.

## Key Challenges and Resolutions

**Hazardous materials in a legacy military facility.** Renovation of an older building on a military installation requires systematic hazardous materials investigation before demolition can proceed safely. The project scope integrated hazmat survey, laboratory testing, and abatement design directly into the demolition package — ensuring the bid documents were fully constructable and compliant with applicable federal, state, and base environmental requirements.

**Physical security integration on an active installation.** WPNSTA Yorktown is an active military installation with DoD antiterrorism and force protection requirements. Incorporating ESS, IDS, CCTV, and AACS infrastructure into an interior renovation required careful coordination of low-voltage routing, conduit stub-outs, power circuit assignments, and physical security design criteria across all engineering disciplines to produce a complete and coordinated construction package.

**NAVFAC documentation requirements on a constrained-scope task order.** Federal DBB delivery under a NAVFAC IDIQ task order involves documentation requirements beyond the standard construction document package: SEI, environmental permitting, DD Form 1354 preparation, and multi-stage submittal reviews. Meeting these requirements within a program amount of $1,105,000 required disciplined scope management and coordinated documentation delivery.

## Outcomes

The project reached design completion. Construction documents were delivered at 35%, 100% Pre-Final, and Final submission milestones, supporting an 8(a) construction procurement within the authorized program amount. Post-Construction Award Services were scoped to carry the design team through the construction phase.
