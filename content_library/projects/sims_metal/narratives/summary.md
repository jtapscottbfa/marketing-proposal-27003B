A new 1,200-ampere electrical service upgrade at Sims Metal's industrial scrap facility in Chesapeake, Virginia. The project encompassed design of a 277/480V, 3-phase, 4-wire service entrance — including Dominion Energy utility metering with CT cabinet, a 1,200A main circuit breaker (service-entrance rated, ground-fault protected, NEMA 3R, 65 KAIC interrupting rating) — feeding a 1,200A, 480V distribution panel provisioned for substantial future expansion. Two active downstream transformer feeds were designed: a 40A, 15 kVA 480–240V transformer serving the facility scale house, and a 70A, 45 kVA 480–277/480V transformer supporting site lighting. Service conductors were routed via four 3-inch conduits carrying 350 MCM conductors; distribution feeders were similarly sized in a four-conduit arrangement. Two empty 6-inch conduits were stubbed at the utility point of demarcation, reserving capacity for future load growth.

The design delivered a code-compliant, scalable power infrastructure suited to the operational demands of a heavy industrial processing site. The engineer of record, Richard H. Williams, PE (Virginia License No. 048025), sealed the one-line diagram as BFA Job No. 20-070, with a drawing date of December 4, 2024. BFA served as prime engineering consultant on this private-sector, standalone engagement, providing sealed electrical construction documents for utility coordination and permitting.
