# 1200 Amp Service at Sims Metal — Chesapeake, VA

## Project Overview

Sims Metal, a large-scale industrial scrap metal processing operation, required a new primary electrical service at its facility located at 4300 Buell St., Chesapeake, Virginia 23324. The engagement called for the design of a 1,200-ampere, 277/480V, 3-phase service entrance in coordination with the local utility, Dominion Energy (Virginia Power). The sealed design documents, BFA Job No. 20-070, were completed under the seal of Richard H. Williams, PE (Virginia License No. 048025) with a drawing date of December 4, 2024.

The project falls within the private commercial / industrial sector, serving a heavy industrial facility whose operations demand reliable, high-capacity electrical infrastructure. The drawing record identifies the facility type as an industrial processing yard with specific powered loads including a scale house and site lighting system.

## Scope of Work

The engineering scope was electrical, consisting of a new service entrance and downstream distribution system designed to meet the facility's current needs while anticipating significant future load growth. Key scope elements included:

- **Utility metering and service entrance:** Dominion Energy meter base and CT cabinet, transitioning to a 1,200A main circuit breaker. The main breaker is service-entrance rated, ground-fault protected, NEMA 3R (outdoor-rated), and rated for a 65 kAIC interrupting duty.
- **Service conductors:** Four 3-inch conduits each carrying four 350 MCM conductors (3-phase, 4-wire) from the utility service point to the main circuit breaker.
- **1,200A distribution panel:** A 1,200A, 480V, 3-phase/3-wire distribution panel (NEMA 1, 65 kAIC) serving as the primary distribution point for the facility. The panel was designed with the majority of circuit slots provisioned as "Future," reflecting the client's intent to phase in additional loads over time.
- **Scale house transformer feed:** A 40A, 3-pole circuit breaker feeding a 15 kVA, 480–240V transformer to supply the facility scale house.
- **Lighting transformer feed:** A 70A, 3-pole circuit breaker feeding a 45 kVA, 480–277/480V lighting transformer to power the site's interior and exterior lighting system.
- **Distribution feeders:** Four 3-inch conduits carrying 350 MCM phase conductors and a 1/0 ground conductor (3-phase, 4-wire) for downstream distribution routing.
- **Future expansion provisions:** Two empty 6-inch conduits stubbed at the Dominion Virginia Power (DVP) demarcation point, providing a ready pathway for future service expansion without excavation.
- **Grounding:** System and equipment grounding per National Electrical Code (NEC) requirements.

## Role on Project

The engineering firm served as prime consultant on this standalone private-sector engagement. The scope was limited to electrical engineering; no HVAC, plumbing, or fire protection disciplines were engaged on this project. The deliverable was a sealed one-line diagram (sheet SKE-1) produced for utility coordination and permitting.

## Key Challenges and Resolutions

**Designing for future capacity while serving defined current loads.** The facility's distribution panel was designed with a majority of circuit spaces reserved as "Future," indicating the client required a service infrastructure sized well beyond immediate operational requirements. The 1,200A service and panel, paired with two empty 6-inch conduits stubbed at the utility demarcation, address this by providing both electrical headroom and physical pathway capacity for incremental expansion without revisiting the service entrance.

**Utility coordination for a heavy industrial service.** A 1,200-ampere, 277/480V service in a heavy industrial context requires close coordination with Dominion Energy on metering configuration, conductor sizing, and point-of-demarcation provisions. The design documented the CT cabinet and meter base arrangement and provided conduit stubs at the DVP service point as a standard utility handoff convention.

**Integrating mixed-voltage loads.** The facility's scale house and lighting system require different secondary voltages (240V single-phase and 277/480V 3-phase, respectively). The design addressed this via two dedicated transformer circuits from the main distribution panel, each appropriately sized and protected, rather than a single secondary bus — keeping the distribution arrangement clean and expandable.

## Outcomes

The project delivered a sealed electrical design package — specifically a one-line diagram — establishing a new 1,200A, 277/480V service entrance for the Sims Metal facility in Chesapeake, Virginia. The distribution system provides two active transformer-fed circuits for current facility loads (scale house and lighting), while the largely unpopulated distribution panel and pre-installed conduit stubs position the facility for future electrical expansion without service entrance redesign. The design was sealed by a licensed Virginia professional engineer and prepared for utility submission and permitting.
