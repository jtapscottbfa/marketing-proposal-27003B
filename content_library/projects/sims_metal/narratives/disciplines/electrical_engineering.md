Electrical engineering was the sole discipline on this project. The scope encompassed design of a complete new 1,200-ampere, 277/480V, 3-phase, 4-wire electrical service entrance and downstream power distribution system at an industrial scrap metal facility in Chesapeake, Virginia.

The design included utility metering components (Dominion Energy meter base and CT cabinet), a 1,200A main circuit breaker (service-entrance rated, ground-fault protected, NEMA 3R enclosure, 65 kAIC), and a 1,200A, 480V distribution panel provisioned primarily with future circuit capacity. Active distribution circuits comprised two transformer feeds: a 40A/15 kVA 480–240V circuit serving the facility scale house, and a 70A/45 kVA 480–277/480V circuit supplying the site lighting system. Service conductors were routed in four 3-inch conduits carrying 350 MCM conductors; distribution feeders were similarly configured with a dedicated equipment ground conductor. Two empty 6-inch conduits were provided at the utility demarcation point for future service expansion. Grounding was designed per NEC requirements.

The electrical design addressed both immediate facility power requirements and planned future growth, reflected in the largely unpopulated distribution panel and pre-installed conduit infrastructure.
