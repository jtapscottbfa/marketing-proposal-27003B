Commissioning services for this project were provided by the AE design team rather than a separately engaged third-party commissioning agent. Per SOW §6.0, the AE was required to serve as the Commissioning Authority for all new HVAC systems, a scope that consolidated design accountability and commissioning verification in a single team. This arrangement is common on smaller VA healthcare renovation projects where the HVAC system complexity is bounded and the design engineer's direct knowledge of the system basis of design allows efficient functional performance testing.

The commissioning scope encompassed all replacement HVAC equipment introduced under the project: the Daikin VRF/VRV system serving administrative and support spaces, the roof-mounted air handling units serving the catheterization OR suites, and the associated controls integration with the Johnson Controls Metasys building automation system. Commissioning activities were included within the construction period services contract, which extended for 485 calendar days from construction award with a minimum of 12 scheduled site visits.

The sub-discipline recorded for this scope is commissioning_new_construction, reflecting that the replacement equipment was new installation even within the existing facility shell.
