The project encompassed the complete replacement of HVAC systems serving the Cardiac Catheterization Lab at Hunter Holmes McGuire VA Medical Center, Building 500, Richmond, Virginia (VA Project No. 652-22-123). The renovation covered 2,734 SF distributed across two single-story, steel-framed cath lab additions situated on the north side of the main hospital complex, with an estimated construction cost of $750,000. The procurement was structured as a 100% Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small Business (SDVOSB) set-aside under the VA's Veterans First Contracting Program, with award on a qualifications-based selection basis.

The existing ceiling-mounted air handling units and ductless split systems were removed in full and replaced with a system configuration specifically engineered for cardiac catheterization suite environments. The base bid design incorporated a Daikin variable refrigerant flow/variable refrigerant volume (VRF/VRV) system for administrative, storage, and control room spaces, paired with two roof-mounted air handling units at N+1 redundancy serving the operating suite environments with 100% outside air and dedicated exhaust. A bid deduct alternate retained one existing ceiling-mounted unit and added three roof-mounted AHUs for the OR suites, providing procurement flexibility. Controls integration was accomplished through the existing Johnson Controls Metasys building automation system. The design team also served as the HVAC commissioning authority in lieu of a VA-engaged third-party agent, incorporating commissioning scope into the 485-calendar-day construction period services.
