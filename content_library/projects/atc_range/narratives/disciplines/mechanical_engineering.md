# Mechanical Engineering — ATC Firing Range Renovation
## Advanced Training Center, Harpers Ferry, WV | CBP | BFA Project No. 22-018

Mechanical engineering on the CBP ATC Indoor Firing Range #2 renovation was handled through a specialty subconsultant coordination arrangement. Range ventilation for indoor firing ranges is a highly specialized discipline — effective air management must capture airborne lead particulate and combustion byproducts from ballistic activity and maintain negative pressure relative to adjacent spaces to protect building occupants and comply with occupational health standards. BFA coordinated this scope with Range Ventilation Design, Inc. (Mike Bott), a firm specializing in firing range ventilation systems.

BFA's role in the mechanical scope was coordination and integration: ensuring that the mechanical ventilation design developed by Range Ventilation Design, Inc. was incorporated into BFA's overall design package before construction documents were issued. The electrical design was explicitly required to incorporate the mechanical scope prior to finalization, reflecting the interdependency between the range ventilation equipment and the electrical distribution and circuit design.

The source record does not identify the specific HVAC equipment or system configuration selected by the range ventilation subconsultant; that detail resided within the specialty subconsultant's scope. BFA's contribution was ensuring the overall multi-discipline design package maintained the necessary coordination interfaces between the ventilation, structural (ceiling system), and electrical (equipment power) scopes.

*Note: Mechanical engineering scope was performed by a specialty subconsultant under BFA coordination; BFA was not the primary mechanical designer.*
