# Electrical Engineering — ATC Firing Range Renovation
## Advanced Training Center, Harpers Ferry, WV | CBP | BFA Project No. 22-018

BFA's electrical engineering scope on the CBP ATC Indoor Firing Range #2 renovation addressed two primary systems: the lighting and the target system infrastructure, both replaced to match the configuration of the operational Indoor Range No. 3.

The lighting replacement specified KIM AFL13 Medium Halogen and Metal Halide Floodlights for general range illumination, and Galco 371DST Heavy-Duty Strobe Warning Lights in both red and blue for safety signaling — fixture types and locations coordinated to align with the existing switch configurations where possible. The target system replacement, supplied by Action Target, required BFA to coordinate the placement and electrical rough-in for control panels, cabinets, and server boxes throughout the range interior.

The electrical design criteria drew on federal and national standards appropriate to a DoD-regulated facility: ANSI C2 (National Electrical Safety Code), IEEE 142 (grounding of industrial and commercial power systems), NFPA 70 (National Electrical Code), UFC 3-500-10N (Electrical Engineering Design and Analysis), and UFC 3-520-01 (Interior Electrical Systems). Conduit was specified as EMT with compression fittings for indoor non-hazardous locations; all conductors were copper THHN/THWN rated at 600 volts. All feeder and branch circuits included a separate green-insulated equipment-grounding conductor in addition to the conduit grounding path, per NFPA 70.

A specific integration requirement governed the electrical package: mechanical HVAC design was required to be incorporated into the electrical design before construction documents were finalized, ensuring that range ventilation equipment power loads were reflected in the circuit and panel schedules.
