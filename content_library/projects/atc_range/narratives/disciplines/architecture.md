# Architecture — ATC Firing Range Renovation
## Advanced Training Center, Harpers Ferry, WV | CBP | BFA Project No. 22-018

BFA's architectural scope on the CBP ATC Indoor Firing Range #2 renovation was the design backbone of the project, governing the deconstruction of the existing angled baffle ceiling system and the installation of a replacement horizontal ballistic ceiling — the primary programmatic change intended to bring Range No. 2 into operational parity with the adjacent Range No. 3.

The existing ceiling used ballistic steel plates set at 30 degrees from horizontal on steel frame supports, with 1-inch Tectum acoustical panels on furring. BFA's design replaced this with a horizontal ballistic steel plate ceiling suspended from the existing roof structure, with new Tectum acoustical ceiling panels on furring matching Range No. 3's configuration. All existing Tectum wall acoustical panels — 2-inch panels on 7/8-inch furring with acoustic batt, applied over the precast concrete wall panels — were replaced with new panels throughout.

The architectural scope also addressed the physical damage accumulated in the range's service life: repair of projectile-related damage to the polished concrete floor slab, concentrated at the front face of the bullet trap, and repair of concrete wall panel damage at the ends of shooting lanes adjacent to side walls. All exposed surfaces were specified to receive new paint upon completion of the renovation.

This work reflects BFA's ability to develop precise architectural renovation documents for security-sensitive federal facilities with specialized ballistic and acoustical performance requirements, coordinating closely with structural, electrical, and specialty ventilation disciplines throughout.
