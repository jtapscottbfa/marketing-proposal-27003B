Architectural scope on this project was limited in extent, addressing building envelope and interior modifications at the elevator machine rooms and hoistway landings within Building 510 at Norfolk Naval Shipyard. Building 510 is a six-story, 372,031-SF cast-in-place concrete warehouse originally constructed in 1956, classified under IBC as a mixed-use Group A-3/B/F-1/S-1 occupancy within NNSY's Controlled Industrial Area.

Building 510's significance under the National Historic Preservation Act requires State Historic Preservation Office (SHPO) review under Section 106 for modifications to the historic fabric of the building. Machine room modifications — including changes to machine room windows — required SHPO coordination and compliance review as part of the design process.

The architectural scope encompassed hoistway entrance door replacements and coordination of elevator cab configurations. The two freight elevators (#63 and #64) received vertical bi-parting power door assemblies; the passenger elevator (#67) received a two-speed side-sliding door assembly. The machine rooms received door, wall, and access modifications to accommodate new equipment configurations, including a custom machine base modification in the shared machine room (Elevators #63/#67) to avoid the existing room wall. A fire-rated roll-up door with keypad access and fire-rated sight glass was designed at Elevator #63's first-floor interior landing to satisfy Controlled Industrial Area security requirements while maintaining code-compliant fire emergency operation access.
