The fire protection scope addressed fire alarm modifications and dedicated fire suppression provisions for the elevator machine rooms and hoistway landings across all three replacement elevators in Building 510. Coordination complexity was elevated by a concurrent base-wide fire alarm replacement program at NNSY (Project P-256) installing a new Mircom FX-MNS system, requiring the elevator fire alarm interface to be designed for the incoming system rather than an existing installation.

For fire alarm, heat detectors were specified at exterior landing locations: at the first floor for Elevator #64, and at the first floor and seventh floor (roof level) for Elevator #63. Elevator controller modules were relocated to within three feet of each elevator controller to satisfy fire emergency operation (FEO) relay requirements under ASME A17.1–2019. The fire alarm modifications were designed and coordinated with Project P-256's installation schedule.

For fire suppression, a dedicated sprinkler branch line was designed from the sixth floor to each machine room, routed outside the machine rooms where accessible. Each branch includes a supervised shut-off valve, check valve, waterflow switch (no time delay), and a test valve accessible outside the machine room. Sprinklers within the machine rooms are provided with protective guards. The waterflow switches were specified with a direct connection to the elevator controller's shunt-trip breaker for instantaneous shutdown upon waterflow detection, as required under UFC 3-490-06 and ASME A17.1 for elevator machine room sprinkler systems.
