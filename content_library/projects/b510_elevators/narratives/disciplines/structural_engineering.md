The structural scope addressed two primary requirements: assessment and reinforcement of the existing overhead machine beams supporting the new traction machines, and engineering of the car guide rail anchorage systems within each hoistway.

Prior to design, WadeLynn Geospatial LLC performed 3D laser scans of the Elevator #63 and Elevator #64 hoistways (June 2023), producing color-coded deviation maps and elevation sections that documented the as-built plumb condition of each hoistway. The scan data established maximum corner plumb deviations of 0'-1-11/32" for Elevator #63 and 0'-1-29/32" for Elevator #64 — the latter representing nearly two inches of out-of-plumb at the hoistway corners. These findings directly informed the rail bracket sizing and anchorage design for each elevator, requiring the structural design to account for the non-plumb hoistway geometry rather than assuming ideal conditions.

New car guide rails are welded to horizontal I-beam brackets with 3/4" post-installed anchors. Maximum bracket spacing is 3'-0"; tie brackets are required for any span exceeding 12'-2". Existing horizontal beam spacing within the hoistways varies from 2'-10" to 4'-0" based on as-built conditions. Structural assessments of the existing overhead machine beams determined adequacy for the new Hollister-Whitney #74 machines (Elevators #63 and #64) and Hollister-Whitney #54 machine (Elevator #67), with reinforcement designed where the existing framing was found to be inadequate. Structural engineering was performed by Broyles & Associates.
