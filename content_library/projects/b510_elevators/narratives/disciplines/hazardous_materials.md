The hazardous materials scope addressed the identification, abatement design, and construction specification for hazardous materials confirmed present within the elevator hoistways and machine rooms of Building 510 at Norfolk Naval Shipyard. Four categories of hazardous materials were documented in the project sources: asbestos-containing materials (ACM) — specifically floor tiles, sealants, and pipe insulation within the hoistway and machine room spaces; lead-based paint on hoistway and machine room surfaces; polychlorinated biphenyl (PCB) ballasts; and mercury-containing lamps.

The project documents identify these materials within the scope of construction activity for all three elevator replacements. Abatement was required prior to and during elevator removal and replacement work to protect workers and comply with applicable federal regulations within an active shipyard facility at NNSY. The design addressed abatement methods, sequencing, and regulatory compliance requirements — including safe handling, disposal manifesting, and any required notifications — coordinated with the overall construction phasing to maintain operational continuity in Building 510.
