The design team served as prime A-E consultant to Naval Facilities Engineering Command (NAVFAC) PWD Portsmouth for the complete replacement of three geared traction elevators in Building 510 at Norfolk Naval Shipyard, Portsmouth, Virginia, under IDIQ contract N40085-20-D0005. Constructed in 1956 and housing elevators original to approximately 1955, Building 510 is a six-story, 372,031-SF cast-in-place concrete warehouse within NNSY's Controlled Industrial Area. The project replaced two 20,000-lb Class C2 freight elevators (#63 and #64) and one 2,500-lb Class A passenger elevator (#67) across two concurrent NAVFAC work orders (WO 1741405, ECC $2.5M; WO 1741675, ECC $4.5M; combined ECC $7.0M). New Hollister-Whitney overhead traction machines with MCE controllers replaced equipment that had received only limited controls and safety upgrades since the 1980s.

The full multi-discipline scope included ductless split HVAC and DDC controls for each overhead machine room, 460-V/3-phase power service modifications fed from existing NNSY substations, LED machine room and hoistway lighting, elevator pit sump pumps, fire alarm and sprinkler system modifications coordinated with a concurrent base-wide fire alarm replacement program, structural reinforcement of overhead machine beams, and hazardous materials abatement (ACM, lead-based paint, PCB ballasts, and mercury lamps). A 3D laser scan of the hoistways by WadeLynn Geospatial LLC documented as-built plumb conditions and informed rail bracket engineering. SHPO coordination under Section 106 was required for machine room modifications. The Final Design Submittal was delivered December 8, 2023; construction was scheduled to begin May 2024.
