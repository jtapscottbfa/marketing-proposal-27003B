# Electrical Engineering — LPD NNS Welding Services

Electrical engineering was the sole design discipline on this project and encompassed the full scope of electrical power distribution design from 34.5kV medium-voltage primary service to point-of-use welding receptacles at a Newport News Shipbuilding (HII/NNS) shipyard facility in Norfolk, Virginia.

The electrical design covers four integrated systems: primary service protection, medium-to-low-voltage transformation, main switchboard distribution, and service rack assemblies. At the primary level, a 34.5kV overhead three-phase service is protected by a 60-ampere fused cutout and 27kV surge arrestors before entering a pad-mounted distribution transformer (34,500/480-277V, 4000/3kVA, 85kV insulation class). The transformer feeds a 4,000-ampere, 277/480V, NEMA 3R main switchboard on a concrete pad, with multiple 800-ampere main breakers, 400-ampere branch breakers, and spare positions. Ten 4-inch conduit runs — two of which are spares — distribute power from the switchboard to seven service rack locations, each equipped with a 400-ampere panelboard providing 200-ampere and 100-ampere three-phase, four-wire RS-pattern welding receptacles. Construction methods are suited to the outdoor, corrosive industrial environment: NEMA 3R enclosures, PVC conduit on galvanized strut supports, and treated-wood post rack structures.

Sub-disciplines engaged include electrical power distribution (primary service, transformation, switchboard, and branch circuit design) and electrical emergency power (spare circuit capacity provisions). All work was issued under Virginia professional engineer seal as drawing E-501.
