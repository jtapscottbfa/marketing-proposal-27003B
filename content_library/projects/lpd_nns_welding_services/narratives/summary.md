LPD NNS Welding Services is an electrical power distribution project for welding operations at Newport News Shipbuilding (Huntington Ingalls Industries) in Norfolk, Virginia. The design firm served as prime consultant, providing electrical engineering design under Virginia professional engineer seal for an industrial-grade power distribution system serving seven welding service locations at the shipyard facility.

The design begins at a 34.5kV overhead three-phase primary service, stepping down through surge protection and a pad-mounted transformer to a 4,000-ampere, 277/480V NEMA 3R main switchboard installed on a concrete pad. From the switchboard, ten 4-inch conduit runs — including two spares — distribute power to seven service rack locations across the work area. Each service rack is equipped with a 400-ampere, 277/480V three-phase panelboard mounted on exterior plywood backboards supported by salt-treated wood posts, providing both 200-ampere and 100-ampere three-phase, four-wire receptacles sized for high-current welding equipment. The design was issued under BFA project number 24-052 in August 2024.
