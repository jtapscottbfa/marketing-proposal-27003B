# Electrical Engineering — Building 310 Chiller Replacement

BFA served as the Electrical Designer of Record (DOR) for this NAVFAC design-build chiller replacement project at Building 310, NAS Oceana Dam Neck Annex, Virginia Beach, Virginia. BFA's electrical scope encompassed the demolition of existing chiller power circuits and the design of new power distribution for two replacement nominal 40-ton air-cooled chillers, coordinated with the project's mechanical and controls scope.

BFA produced a four-sheet electrical For-Sale Submission (FSS) drawing set (BFA project 23-053, NAVFAC drawing N40085-1909195) signed and sealed June 10, 2024, by Felix A. Pessig, PE (Virginia PE License No. 048025). The electrical design scope included:

- **Demolition:** Removal of existing 208V, 3-phase chiller feeds from Panelboard SP1 (800A MLO, 208Y/120V, 3-phase, 4-wire) to each chiller's integral disconnect, and removal of 120V heat trace and brazed plate heater circuits from Panelboard SP2 (200A MLO, 208Y/120V).
- **New chiller power:** New conductors (3-#3/0 AWG + 1-#6 AWG GND in 2" conduit) from existing SP1 to each new chiller, with a new NEMA 4X, 200A-3P non-fused integral disconnect at each unit. Each chiller operates at 194 MCA, 208V, 3-phase.
- **Auxiliary loads:** New 120V circuits for brazed plate evaporator heaters (one per chiller) and a new 5W/ft self-regulating electric heat trace system on the exterior chilled water piping, with supply and return circuits fed from SP2.
- **Panelboard coordination and one-line:** Updated panelboard schedules for SP1 and SP2 and a one-line riser diagram confirming integration with the building's existing 2000A, 208Y/120V main switchboard.

All electrical work was designed in compliance with the 2021 National Electrical Code (NFPA 70), UFC 3-501-01 Electrical Engineering, and NAVFAC design standards per FC 1-300-09N.
