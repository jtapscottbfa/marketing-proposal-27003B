# Building 194/194A Renovations — NSF Indian Head, MD

## Project Overview

Buildings 194 and 194A at Naval Support Facility (NSF) Indian Head, Maryland are two one-story support buildings that serve Naval Sea Systems Command (NAVSEA) staff on the installation. Building 194 (approximately 1,560 SF) functions as a change house; Building 194A (approximately 1,536 SF) functions as a lunch and locker room. Together the two buildings total approximately 3,096 square feet. Both buildings are located at the northeastern end of NSF Indian Head, on CAD PAD Drive, in a cluster of installation support structures on the eastern portion of the base.

The project was procured by the Naval Facilities Engineering Command (NAVFAC) / NSA South Potomac as a design-build contract under NAVFAC Work Order No. 1650958 (BFA Project No. 20-081). The project is complete.

## Scope

The scope encompassed a comprehensive interior and exterior renovation of both buildings. Demolition of existing finishes, fixtures, and systems preceded hazardous materials abatement addressing asbestos and lead-paint conditions present in the existing building fabric. Envelope scope included thermal roofing replacement, exterior wall rehabilitation, and replacement of windows and doors. Interior scope addressed full finish replacement — drywall, flooring, painting and wallcovering, acoustical ceilings — as well as new interior partitions and doors configured to suit the renovated occupancies.

All major building systems were replaced or upgraded across both buildings: HVAC (packaged equipment with new air distribution), plumbing (domestic water supply and sanitary drain-waste-vent), fire protection (fire alarm and clean-agent suppression), electrical (power distribution, interior lighting, and exterior lighting), and telecommunications (structured cabling). Civil site work addressed exterior grading, drainage, and site improvements at both buildings.

## Role

The design team served as Designer of Record (DOR) — a licensed architectural and engineering subconsultant to the design-build prime contractor, as defined under the NAVFAC Design-Build contract. Seven engineering and design disciplines were engaged: architecture, civil engineering, mechanical engineering, plumbing engineering, fire protection engineering, electrical engineering, and telecommunications engineering. The Basis of Design was authored and issued as an Issued-for-Construction (IFC) submittal on February 4, 2022.

## Key Challenges and Resolutions

**Historic preservation and SHPO consultation.** Both buildings on NSF Indian Head carry historic significance, and the renovation triggered a Maryland State Historic Preservation Office (SHPO) Section 106 consultation under the National Historic Preservation Act. Design decisions affecting the character and fabric of the buildings required coordination with SHPO requirements, and that consultation was completed prior to construction. The architectural design balanced the scope required for code-compliant, fully updated support facilities with the preservation obligations applicable to the historic structures.

**Hazardous materials abatement.** Both buildings contained asbestos and lead-paint conditions requiring pre-renovation abatement. The renovation design incorporated the abatement scope into the construction sequence, ensuring that hazardous conditions were properly remediated before interior demolition and renovation work proceeded. Hazmat scope was coordinated across the architectural and construction sequencing plans.

**Full building-systems scope in small, historic footprints.** Despite the modest footprint of both buildings — under 1,600 SF each — the renovation required the full complement of building systems disciplines: mechanical, plumbing, fire protection, electrical, and telecommunications. Coordinating seven disciplines within constrained single-story envelopes, while respecting the historic building fabric and SHPO requirements, demanded close inter-discipline coordination throughout the design process.

## Outcomes

The complete Basis of Design was issued as an Issued-for-Construction (IFC) submittal on February 4, 2022, providing construction-ready documents for the NAVFAC Design-Build prime contractor. The project is complete. The renovated facilities provide upgraded support space for NAVSEA staff at NSF Indian Head, with all building systems replaced, hazardous materials remediated, and the historic character of both buildings maintained in compliance with SHPO Section 106 requirements.
