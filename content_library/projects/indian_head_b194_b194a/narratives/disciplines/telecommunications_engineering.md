Telecommunications engineering for the Buildings 194/194A renovation at NSF Indian Head, Maryland designed structured cabling infrastructure for both renovated support buildings. The scope provided low-voltage cabling systems to support the communications requirements of Building 194 (approximately 1,560 SF, change house) and Building 194A (approximately 1,536 SF, lunch and locker room), serving NAVSEA staff at NSF Indian Head.

The telecommunications scope encompassed structured cabling design — voice and data distribution — within both buildings, coordinated with the new electrical infrastructure provided under the electrical engineering scope. Telecommunications systems were designed to meet the NAVFAC communications requirements and performance specifications in the Design-Build RFP. Low-voltage pathways and spaces were coordinated with the architectural layout and the electrical, mechanical, and fire protection disciplines within the constrained building footprints.

Telecommunications design deliverables were included in the Basis of Design Issued-for-Construction (IFC) submittal dated February 4, 2022, for NAVFAC Work Order No. 1650958 (BFA Project No. 20-081). The project is complete. Specific telecommunications system details beyond structured cabling (outlet counts, data room configuration, active equipment) are not captured in the project source record.
