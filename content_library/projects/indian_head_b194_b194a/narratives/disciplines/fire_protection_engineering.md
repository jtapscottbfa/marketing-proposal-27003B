Fire protection engineering for the Buildings 194/194A renovation at NSF Indian Head, Maryland provided design of fire alarm and clean-agent fire suppression systems for both renovated support buildings. The fire protection scope addressed Building 194 (approximately 1,560 SF, change house) and Building 194A (approximately 1,536 SF, lunch and locker room) as part of the comprehensive building-systems replacement under the NAVFAC Design-Build renovation.

The suppression scope specified a clean-agent fire suppression system, consistent with the NAVFAC engineering systems requirements and performance specifications in the Design-Build RFP. Fire alarm design addressed detection and notification for both buildings. Both the fire alarm and suppression systems were designed as complete new installations replacing the existing systems as part of the full renovation.

Fire protection design was coordinated with the architectural, mechanical, electrical, and plumbing disciplines within the constrained single-story footprints of both buildings — a coordination requirement common to small-building full-scope renovations where systems must be integrated within limited ceiling and wall cavities. Fire protection deliverables were included in the Basis of Design Issued-for-Construction (IFC) submittal dated February 4, 2022, for NAVFAC Work Order No. 1650958 (BFA Project No. 20-081). The project is complete.
