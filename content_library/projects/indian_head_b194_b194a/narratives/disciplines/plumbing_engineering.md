Plumbing engineering for the Buildings 194/194A renovation at NSF Indian Head, Maryland provided design of domestic water and sanitary drainage systems for both renovated support buildings. The plumbing scope addressed complete system replacement within Building 194 (approximately 1,560 SF, change house) and Building 194A (approximately 1,536 SF, lunch and locker room), consistent with the full building-systems replacement approach of the NAVFAC Design-Build renovation.

Domestic water supply design addressed distribution within both buildings to serve the fixture types appropriate to each building's use — change house, locker, lavatory, and lunch room functions. Sanitary and drain-waste-vent (DWV) design provided complete wastewater collection and venting for all plumbing fixtures in both buildings. Plumbing design was coordinated with the architectural floor plans and with the mechanical, electrical, and fire protection disciplines throughout the design process, meeting the space constraints inherent in small single-story buildings undergoing comprehensive renovation.

Plumbing deliverables were issued as part of the Basis of Design Issued-for-Construction (IFC) submittal on February 4, 2022, providing construction-ready plumbing design documents for the NAVFAC Design-Build contract (Work Order No. 1650958, BFA Project No. 20-081). The project is complete.
