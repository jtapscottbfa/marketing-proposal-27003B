The civil engineering scope for the Buildings 194/194A renovation at NSF Indian Head, Maryland addressed site design, grading, drainage, and exterior site improvements associated with the rehabilitation of both support facilities. Both buildings are located at the northeastern end of NSF Indian Head, on CAD PAD Drive, on the eastern portion of the installation in a cluster of support buildings. Civil design work was scoped to accommodate the renovation disturbance footprint at both buildings while meeting installation standards.

Civil deliverables encompassed site grading and drainage design supporting the exterior scope of work at both Building 194 (approximately 1,560 SF, change house) and Building 194A (approximately 1,536 SF, lunch and locker room). Exterior site improvement design addressed the areas around both buildings as part of the comprehensive renovation effort. Civil scope was coordinated with the architectural demolition and renovation sequencing and with the civil construction constraints of working within an active naval installation.

The civil team served as subconsultant to the design-build prime contractor alongside the full multi-discipline design team. Civil design deliverables were included in the Basis of Design Issued-for-Construction (IFC) submittal dated February 4, 2022, for NAVFAC Work Order No. 1650958 (BFA Project No. 20-081). The project is complete.
