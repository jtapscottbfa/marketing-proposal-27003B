Architecture served as the lead discipline for the Buildings 194/194A renovation at NSF Indian Head, Maryland, providing full architectural design across both one-story support buildings and serving as the point of coordination for Maryland State Historic Preservation Office (SHPO) Section 106 consultation. The architectural scope encompassed complete interior and exterior design for Building 194 (approximately 1,560 SF, change house) and Building 194A (approximately 1,536 SF, lunch and locker room), totaling approximately 3,096 square feet.

Exterior architectural scope included thermal roofing replacement, exterior wall rehabilitation, and replacement of windows and doors. Interior scope addressed complete finish replacement — drywall, flooring, painting and wallcovering, acoustical ceilings — along with new interior partitions and interior doors reconfigured to suit the renovated occupancies. Pre-renovation hazardous materials abatement of asbestos and lead-paint conditions was coordinated within the architectural demolition and renovation sequence.

Both buildings' historic character required SHPO Section 106 consultation under the National Historic Preservation Act. The architectural design balanced the functional scope required for code-compliant, fully renovated support facilities with the preservation requirements applicable to these historic structures. SHPO consultation was completed as part of the design process. The Basis of Design was authored and issued as an Issued-for-Construction (IFC) submittal on February 4, 2022 under NAVFAC Design-Build Work Order No. 1650958 (BFA Project No. 20-081). The project is complete.
