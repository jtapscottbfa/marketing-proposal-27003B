Electrical engineering for the Buildings 194/194A renovation at NSF Indian Head, Maryland provided electrical power distribution, interior lighting, and exterior lighting design for both renovated support buildings. The scope addressed complete electrical system replacement within Building 194 (approximately 1,560 SF, change house) and Building 194A (approximately 1,536 SF, lunch and locker room) as part of the comprehensive renovation under NAVFAC Work Order No. 1650958.

Electrical power distribution design addressed service entry, panelboards, and branch circuit distribution serving the full range of building loads across both occupancies — mechanical equipment, plumbing, lighting, and low-voltage systems. Interior lighting design provided illumination for the change house, locker, and support spaces in both buildings, designed to meet NAVFAC lighting requirements and the performance specifications in the Design-Build RFP. Exterior lighting was included in the electrical scope at both buildings, coordinated with the civil site improvements.

Electrical design was coordinated with the mechanical, plumbing, fire protection, and telecommunications disciplines to ensure complete systems integration within the small-footprint building envelopes. Electrical deliverables were issued as part of the Basis of Design Issued-for-Construction (IFC) submittal on February 4, 2022, providing construction-ready electrical design documents for NAVFAC Design-Build contract (BFA Project No. 20-081). The project is complete.
