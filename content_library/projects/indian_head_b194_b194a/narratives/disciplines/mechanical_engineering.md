Mechanical engineering for the Buildings 194/194A renovation at NSF Indian Head, Maryland encompassed HVAC design for both renovated support buildings. The scope provided packaged HVAC equipment selection and air distribution design for Building 194 (approximately 1,560 SF, change house) and Building 194A (approximately 1,536 SF, lunch and locker room), addressing space conditioning and ventilation for the NAVSEA support staff occupancies in both buildings.

HVAC systems for both buildings utilized packaged equipment — a configuration appropriate to the modest footprints and single-story construction of each building. Air distribution design addressed supply and return within each building's renovated floor plan, providing code-compliant ventilation and space conditioning for the occupancies. HVAC systems were sized and specified to meet NAVFAC engineering systems requirements and the performance specifications established in the Design-Build RFP.

Mechanical design was coordinated with the architectural layout, plumbing, electrical, and fire protection disciplines throughout the design process to integrate all building systems within the constrained building envelopes. Mechanical design deliverables were included in the Basis of Design Issued-for-Construction (IFC) submittal dated February 4, 2022, for NAVFAC Work Order No. 1650958 (BFA Project No. 20-081). The project is complete.
