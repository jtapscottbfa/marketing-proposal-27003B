The renovation of Buildings 194 and 194A at Naval Support Facility (NSF) Indian Head, Maryland involved the comprehensive rehabilitation of two one-story support facilities totaling approximately 3,096 square feet — Building 194 (approximately 1,560 SF), serving as a change house, and Building 194A (approximately 1,536 SF), serving as a lunch and locker room for Naval Sea Systems Command (NAVSEA) staff. Procured by the Naval Facilities Engineering Command (NAVFAC) via design-build delivery (Work Order No. 1650958), the design team served as Designer of Record — a licensed subconsultant to the design-build prime contractor — delivering full architectural and engineering services across seven disciplines.

The full-scope renovation addressed the complete building envelope (roofing, exterior walls, windows, and doors), interior finishes (drywall, flooring, painting, acoustical ceilings, partitions), and all building systems: packaged HVAC with new air distribution, plumbing (domestic water and sanitary/DWV), fire protection (fire alarm and clean-agent suppression), electrical power and lighting (interior and exterior), and telecommunications structured cabling. Civil site work encompassed grading, drainage, and exterior site improvements at both buildings. Pre-renovation hazardous materials abatement addressed asbestos and lead-paint conditions present in the existing building fabric. Both buildings' historic status required Maryland State Historic Preservation Office (SHPO) Section 106 consultation, which was completed as part of the design process. The Basis of Design was issued as an Issued-for-Construction (IFC) submittal on February 4, 2022. The project is complete.
