# Prepare Site for Cath Lab Equipment — Robert J. Dole VA Medical Center

## Project Overview

"Prepare Site for Cath Lab Equipment" (VA project 589A7-25-503, contract 36C25525R0048) is an interior renovation at the Robert J. Dole VA Medical Center, 5500 E Kellogg Drive, Wichita, Kansas 67218, for the Department of Veterans Affairs. The project renovates approximately 1,500 square feet of existing space in Building 2 to accommodate two image-guided catheterization laboratory suites and their supporting infrastructure. A-E design services were awarded to Moon Tree LLC (Oklahoma City, Oklahoma), an SDVOSB-certified prime architect-engineer, through a total SDVOSB set-aside procurement administered by VA Network Contracting Office (NCO) 15 under contract 36C25525R0048. MEP engineering design was performed by a subconsultant under the prime, carrying the work as project 25-042A.

## Scope of Work

The renovation converts seven rooms within Building 2 for catheterization laboratory use: Cath Lab 1 (room 218-2), Cath Lab 2 (room 216-2), Control Room (room 217-2), Clean Utility (room 217A-2), Equipment Room (room 216A-2), Sterile Supplies (room 215-2), and Corridor C-2-3. Per contract Supplement B, the design removes all existing image-guided catheterization laboratory equipment and prepares the site for installation of new imaging equipment by a separate equipment contractor — the A-E's responsibility ends at site readiness, not equipment commissioning.

Full-discipline design is required: mechanical (HVAC), electrical power and lighting, plumbing including medical gas and vacuum systems, fire protection, architectural renovation, structural assessment, and hazardous materials abatement design (asbestos, lead, and mold). The construction budget/design limitation established in the contract is $850,000. The contractual design period is 209 calendar days from notice to proceed, with design milestones at 30 days (35% schematic design plus hazmat testing reports), 74 days (65% construction documents), 95 days (95% CDs), and 125 days (100% CDs for bidding). The 100% construction document submittal was delivered March 17, 2026.

## Role on Project

The prime A-E, Moon Tree LLC, holds the design contract and leads the A-E team. The MEP subconsultant provided mechanical, electrical, and plumbing engineering design. The contract is firm-fixed-price, procured under NAICS code 541310 (Architectural Services) with a small business size standard of $12.5 million. The Contracting Officer for this procurement is Jennifer Hill (NCO 15, Leavenworth, Kansas); the Contracting Officer's Representative is Nick Howard (VAMC Wichita, phone 316-685-2221 x58005). ## Key Challenges and Resolutions

**Healthcare HVAC requirements for procedure rooms.** Catheterization laboratories are technically demanding HVAC spaces requiring precise temperature and humidity control, dedicated air handling, and minimum air change rates consistent with VA and ASHRAE 170 standards for interventional procedure rooms. The design provides a central air handler (AHU-1) sized for a peak supply of approximately 4,003 CFM with a sensible cooling coil load of approximately 50,098 Btu/h. Supplemental cooling is provided by a split-system configuration comprising a fan coil unit (FCU-1, 480V 3-phase) and air-cooled condensing unit (ACCU-1, 277V 1-phase), with booster cooling unit BCU-1 and refrigerant circuit RF-1. Air distribution follows a variable-air-volume design with terminal units per specification section 23 36 00. BACnet/IP DDC controls integrate new equipment with the VAMC's existing Energy Control Center, enabling centralized monitoring and alarm management per VA specification section 23 09 23.

**Electrical life-safety requirements for procedure rooms.** Catheterization laboratories require redundant, conditioned power with isolated grounding to protect patients and staff from electrical hazard — requirements that drive significant electrical infrastructure beyond a standard renovation. The design incorporates two 75 kVA UPS units, one per procedure room, fed on 480V 3-phase circuits from existing panel PNL 2Q3 using 4 AWG copper conductors. Downstream of each UPS, isolation panels IP-CL1 (Cath Lab 1) and IP-CL2 (Cath Lab 2) provide 30A, 208Y/120V 1-phase isolated-ground power within each procedure room. The existing panels were analyzed for capacity at 100% submittal: PNL 2Q3 (100A, 480Y/277V 3-phase) carries a total connected load of 36,134W (28.9 kVA demand) after new loads are added; PNL 2X (200A, 208Y/120V 3-phase) carries 49,083W (39.3 kVA demand). Voltage drop calculations confirmed compliance across all new circuit runs. Interior lighting is specified per VA master spec 265100.

**Hazardous materials abatement in an occupied VA medical facility.** Renovation of Building 2 requires full abatement of asbestos, lead, and mold within the renovation area prior to construction. Contract Supplement B, Section III.4.b states that "this project will remove all lead and asbestos in areas being renovated." A VA-qualified Professional Industrial Hygienist (VPIH) is required as part of the A-E team per Section B2. The design schedule front-loads hazmat work: testing reports are due with the 35% schematic design submittal (day 30 of the design period), enabling abatement scope to be fully characterized and designed into the construction document package before CDs begin. This sequencing is typical of VA healthcare renovation procurements in occupied facilities where the extent of hazardous materials must be documented before construction can proceed.

## Outcomes

The 100% construction document submittal was delivered March 17, 2026. The MEP engineering package — including HVAC load calculations (TRACE 700), electrical load analysis, voltage drop calculations, mechanical equipment catalog submittals, and lighting calculations — was completed and submitted as part of the 100% package. The design establishes site readiness for new imaging equipment installation within the $850,000 construction budget/design limitation and positions the VA to bid and award a construction contract for the catheterization laboratory renovation.
