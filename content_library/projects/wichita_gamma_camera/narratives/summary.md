"Prepare Site for Cath Lab Equipment" (VA project 589A7-25-503) is a renovation of approximately 1,500 square feet of existing space in Building 2 at the Robert J. Dole VA Medical Center, Wichita, Kansas, for the Department of Veterans Affairs. The project converts existing interior space into two image-guided catheterization laboratory suites — Cath Lab 1 (room 218-2) and Cath Lab 2 (room 216-2) — with associated Control Room, Equipment Room, Clean Utility, Sterile Supplies room, and Corridor support spaces. The principal scope item is removal of existing catheterization laboratory imaging equipment and site preparation for the installation of new image-guided equipment by a separate equipment contractor.

Procured under VA contract 36C25525R0048 as a firm-fixed-price, SDVOSB total set-aside through VA Network Contracting Office 15, the project carries a construction budget of $850,000 and a 209-calendar-day design period from notice to proceed. Prime architect-engineer is Moon Tree LLC (Oklahoma City, Oklahoma); MEP engineering was performed by a subconsultant under the prime. The full design team addressed mechanical, electrical, plumbing, fire protection, architectural, structural, and hazardous materials disciplines. HVAC design provides a central air handler (AHU-1) serving both procedure rooms via a variable-air-volume system with supplemental split-system cooling and BACnet/IP DDC integration with the existing VAMC building automation system. Electrical design incorporates two 75 kVA uninterruptible power supply units — one per procedure room — with dedicated isolation panels for patient safety compliance. Full asbestos and lead abatement of the renovation area is incorporated into the design scope, with a VA-qualified Professional Industrial Hygienist as a required member of the A-E team. The 100% construction document submittal was delivered March 17, 2026.
