Electrical engineering on this project covers power distribution, uninterruptible power supply systems, isolated-ground procedure room panels, and interior lighting for two image-guided catheterization laboratory suites and five supporting spaces in Building 2 of the Robert J. Dole VA Medical Center, Wichita, Kansas.

Two existing distribution panels serve the renovation area: panel PNL 2Q3 (100A, 480Y/277V 3-phase, 4-wire) with a total connected load of 36,134W (28.9 kVA demand) after new loads, and panel PNL 2X (200A, 208Y/120V 3-phase, 4-wire) with a total connected load of 49,083W (39.3 kVA demand). HVAC equipment loads — AHU-1, FCU-1, BCU-1, and RF-1 — connect to PNL 2Q3. Procedure room loads, lighting circuits, and the medical gas area alarm (300W, 120V 1-phase) connect to PNL 2X.

Each procedure room is served by a dedicated 75 kVA UPS unit (one per cath lab), fed from PNL 2Q3 on 480V 3-phase circuits with 4 AWG copper conductors. Downstream of each UPS, isolation panels IP-CL1 (Cath Lab 1) and IP-CL2 (Cath Lab 2) provide 30A, 208Y/120V 1-phase isolated-ground power within each procedure room, meeting NFPA 99 healthcare facility electrical requirements for patient safety. Interior lighting is specified per VA master specification section 265100. Voltage drop calculations and electrical load analysis were completed and submitted at 100% construction documents on March 17, 2026.

Applicable specification sections include: 260511 (requirements for electrical installations), 260519 (low-voltage conductors and cables), 260526 (grounding and bonding), 260533 (raceway and boxes), 262416 (panelboards), 262726 (wiring devices), 262921 (enclosed switches and circuit breakers), and 265100 (interior lighting).
