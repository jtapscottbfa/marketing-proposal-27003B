Plumbing engineering on this project includes medical gas systems, medical vacuum systems, and domestic plumbing for two image-guided catheterization laboratory suites and their supporting spaces at the Robert J. Dole VA Medical Center, Wichita, Kansas.

Medical gas systems — including piped oxygen, medical air, and related gases required at the point of care in interventional procedure rooms — are specified per VA master specification section 22 63 00 (Gas Systems for Healthcare Facilities). Medical vacuum systems serving the procedure rooms are specified per section 22 62 00 (Vacuum Systems for Healthcare Facilities). These systems require monitored alarm panels: low-pressure and status alarms for medical gas and compressed air, and high-pressure and status alarms for medical vacuum, per the DDC/BAS integration requirements of specification section 23 09 23. The medical gas area alarm load — 300W (120V 1-phase, 2.5A) — appears in the electrical panel PNL 2X load schedule, confirming the monitoring panel is in scope.

Domestic hot and cold water distribution and sanitary drainage and vent systems serve the clean utility, sterile supplies, and associated support spaces within the renovation, consistent with standard catheterization suite program requirements. Dedicated plumbing calculation submittals are not among the source documents staged in the project record; the plumbing discipline is confirmed in scope by the contract Supplement B scope narrative, the specification sections cited above, and the nature of the catheterization laboratory program.
