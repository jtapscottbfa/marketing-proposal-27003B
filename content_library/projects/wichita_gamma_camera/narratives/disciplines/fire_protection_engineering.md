Fire protection engineering on this project encompasses wet-pipe automatic sprinkler system modification and fire alarm system interface for the renovation of approximately 1,500 square feet in Building 2 of the Robert J. Dole VA Medical Center, Wichita, Kansas.

Renovation of existing catheterization laboratory space in Building 2 requires modification of the building's existing wet-pipe automatic sprinkler system to serve the new room configuration. Fire alarm system integration is established by the project's HVAC controls specification: VA master specification section 23 09 23 (Direct Digital Control for HVAC) requires smoke detector shutdown interlocks for the air handling unit serving the cath lab suite, a standard life-safety interface between the fire alarm system and HVAC equipment in healthcare occupancies. This requirement confirms that the fire alarm system has scope items within this design package.

Fire protection engineering is confirmed as an in-scope design discipline by the contract Supplement B scope narrative and by the project's design discipline taxonomy. Specific fire protection design documents — suppression head layout plans, hydraulic calculations, fire alarm device schedules and riser diagrams — are not among the source documents staged in the project record, which consists primarily of MEP calculation submittals and VA master specification sections. Those design documents would be found in the construction drawing and specification set. Source documentation for the detailed fire protection design extent is not available in the staged record.
