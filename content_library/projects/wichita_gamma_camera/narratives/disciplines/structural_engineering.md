Structural engineering on this project supports the renovation of approximately 1,500 square feet in Building 2 of the Robert J. Dole VA Medical Center, Wichita, Kansas, for image-guided catheterization laboratory use.

Structural design is listed as an in-scope discipline by the contract Supplement B scope narrative, which specifies that the A-E team's design services include structural engineering in connection with the catheterization laboratory renovation. The renovation scope — which involves removal of existing heavy imaging equipment, site preparation for new heavy imaging equipment, and modification of MEP systems within the procedure rooms and support spaces — typically requires structural assessment of existing floor slab capacity for equipment loads, review of structural framing at wall and ceiling penetrations, and structural detailing for any new equipment pads or supports.
