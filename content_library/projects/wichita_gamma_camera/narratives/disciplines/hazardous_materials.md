Hazardous materials services on this project address asbestos, lead, and mold assessment and abatement design for the renovation of approximately 1,500 square feet in Building 2 of the Robert J. Dole VA Medical Center, Wichita, Kansas.

Contract Supplement B, Section B2 explicitly confirms that "Hazardous materials (Asbestos, Lead, Mold, heavy metal and etc…) ARE included in this task order amendment." Section III.4.b states directly that "this project will remove all lead and asbestos in areas being renovated," establishing full abatement — not encapsulation or management-in-place — as the project approach. The contract requires a VA-qualified Professional Industrial Hygienist (VPIH) as a named member of the A-E team, reflecting the VA's specific qualification requirements for industrial hygiene personnel working in occupied healthcare facilities under VAAR and VA facility safety standards.

The design schedule reflects the priority of hazardous materials characterization: the 35% schematic design submittal — due 30 calendar days from notice to proceed, the earliest design milestone — is required to include asbestos, lead, and mold testing reports. Front-loading hazmat testing at day 30 ensures that the full extent of hazardous materials in Building 2's renovation area is documented before the construction document phases begin, allowing abatement scope, sequencing, and containment requirements to be fully incorporated into the 65% and subsequent CD submittals. This sequencing is characteristic of VA healthcare renovation procurements in occupied clinical facilities, where construction cannot proceed in areas with confirmed hazardous materials until abatement design and contractor qualifications are in place.
