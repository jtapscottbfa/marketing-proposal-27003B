Mechanical engineering on this project addressed HVAC system design for two image-guided catheterization laboratory procedure rooms and five supporting spaces within Building 2 of the Robert J. Dole VA Medical Center, Wichita, Kansas. The design is anchored by a central air handler (AHU-1) sized for a peak supply of approximately 4,003 CFM and a sensible cooling coil load of approximately 50,098 Btu/h. Supplemental cooling is provided by a split-system configuration: a fan coil unit (FCU-1, 480V 3-phase) paired with an air-cooled condensing unit (ACCU-1, 277V 1-phase), with a booster cooling unit (BCU-1, 480V 3-phase) and refrigerant circuit (RF-1, 480V 3-phase) completing the mechanical equipment inventory. Air distribution follows a variable-air-volume design with terminal units, delivering conditioned air to the procedure rooms and support spaces modeled in TRACE 700 at approximately 1,638 SF total served area.

Controls integration connects new mechanical equipment to the VAMC's existing building automation system via native BACnet/IP direct digital control, per VA master specification section 23 09 23. Medical gas and vacuum system alarms (low-pressure and high-pressure) are integrated into the DDC/BAS interface. Specification coverage includes: ductwork (23 31 00), air outlets and inlets (23 37 00), HVAC and boiler insulation (23 07 11), refrigerant piping (23 23 00), decentralized unitary HVAC equipment including fan coil and split systems (23 81 00), and testing, adjusting, and balancing (23 05 93). HVAC load calculations were produced using TRACE 700 and submitted at the 100% construction document milestone on March 17, 2026.
