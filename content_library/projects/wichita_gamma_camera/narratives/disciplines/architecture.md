Architecture on this project covers the renovation design for approximately 1,500 square feet of existing space in Building 2 of the Robert J. Dole VA Medical Center, Wichita, Kansas, converting the space for image-guided catheterization laboratory use.

The architectural scope, as defined in contract Supplement B, addresses a seven-room program: two procedure rooms — Cath Lab 1 (room 218-2) and Cath Lab 2 (room 216-2) — along with a shared Control Room (room 217-2), Clean Utility (room 217A-2), Equipment Room (room 216A-2), Sterile Supplies (room 215-2), and Corridor C-2-3. The renovation program involves removal of existing cath lab imaging equipment, site preparation for new imaging equipment, and architectural coordination of MEP systems within the reconfigured suite.

As-built reference drawings for adjacent buildings 24, 51, and 53 were provided to the A-E team per Supplement B Section VII, supporting existing conditions documentation for design development. The design period extends 209 calendar days from notice to proceed, with architectural design developed in parallel with MEP engineering through the schematic design, 65%, 95%, and 100% construction document phases.

Architectural specification sections, finish schedules, door and hardware schedules, and architectural drawing sheets are not among the source documents staged in the project record, which is dominated by MEP calculation submittals and VA master specification sections for mechanical, electrical, and plumbing systems. Architecture is confirmed as an in-scope design discipline by the contract Supplement B scope narrative, which explicitly lists architectural renovation among the required design services.
