The electrical engineering scope on the Canon Boulevard Laydown Yard Expansion covered site lighting design, underground electrical distribution, and site access control for an outdoor laydown yard at Thomas Jefferson National Accelerator Facility in Newport News, Virginia. The design team led the electrical engineering as prime consultant, with Richard Williams, PE serving as Electrical Engineer/Project Manager.

Site lighting consists of 17 LED light pole fixtures mounted on 20-foot aluminum poles at the yard perimeter, with luminaire ratings of 250–400 watts. Underground electrical distribution runs approximately 1,600 linear feet of conduit from an existing 60-amp, 208/120V, 3-phase panel (Panel PA) in adjacent Building 64 to serve the new site lighting. Site access control includes an automatic sliding gate with a 2-HP motor. The electrical design addressed both functional illumination of the laydown area and security/access control at the perimeter entry.

Electrical sub-disciplines included power distribution (extending the existing Building 64 panel to the expanded site) and exterior lighting design. The scope reflects a modest but complete site electrical package appropriate to the laydown yard's operational requirements: perimeter illumination, an automatic vehicle access gate, and all associated conduit and wiring infrastructure. The electrical design was delivered as part of the 100% Conceptual Design report on May 31, 2023, with definite design services available as a follow-on scope upon client authorization.
