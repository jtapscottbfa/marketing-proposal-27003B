The water resources engineering scope on the Canon Boulevard Laydown Yard Expansion focused on stormwater management design for a large impervious surface expansion at Thomas Jefferson National Accelerator Facility in Newport News, Virginia. VHB (Arash Niroomandi, PhD, PE) served as water resources subconsultant, responsible for stormwater analysis, hydraulic modeling, and Virginia Runoff Reduction Method (VRRM) compliance assessment.

The project site drains to the Brick Kiln Creek watershed. Three stormwater management options were evaluated at the conceptual level. Option 2 — retrofitting the existing East Pond as a Level 1 Wet Pond — was selected as the recommended approach, capable of serving the full 171,000 SF (3.92-acre) expansion footprint. Water quality compliance was demonstrated per VRRM (9VAC25-870-63) and the Virginia BMP Clearinghouse: the East Pond retrofit achieves approximately 19.41 lb/yr phosphorus load reduction, with the remaining 3.04 lb/yr addressed through nutrient credits, achieving full compliance without constructing a separate BMP facility.

Hydraulic capacity for the 100-year storm event was evaluated using EPA SWMM 5.1 (PCSWMM), with the model encompassing the Jefferson Lab property and the adjacent Oyster Point North Commerce Center watershed. Analysis confirmed a maximum hydraulic grade line at elevation 30.5 ft NAVD88, with emergency spillway at 30.5 ft and top of bank at 31.5 ft — providing one foot of freeboard. A storage-depth curve for the Level 1 Wet Pond was developed as part of the conceptual design. The water resources design was delivered at 100% Conceptual Design level on May 31, 2023.
