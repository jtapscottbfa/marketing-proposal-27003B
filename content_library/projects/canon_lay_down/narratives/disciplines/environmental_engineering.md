The environmental engineering scope on the Canon Boulevard Laydown Yard Expansion addressed jurisdictional wetlands impacts associated with the full build-out expansion scenario at Thomas Jefferson National Accelerator Facility in Newport News, Virginia. The project's full build-out configuration (Option 2, approximately 171,000 SF / 3.92 acres) encroaches on wetland areas adjacent to the Canon Boulevard site. At the conceptual design stage, the environmental scope identified that advancing to the full build-out footprint would require wetland permitting and mitigation as conditions of proceeding to definite design and construction.

The conceptual design evaluated wetland impact as a key constraint in the build-out decision: the full expansion offers larger usable surface area and improved resilience against flooding, while the base bid footprint of 90,000 SF avoids wetland encroachment. The conceptual report documented the permitting and mitigation pathway required for the full build-out extent, including the higher cost and regulatory effort associated with that option, and left the proceed/defer decision to the client.

Environmental sub-disciplines covered wetlands assessment and environmental permitting scoping. Specific wetland delineation data, permit application details, and mitigation requirements were scoped at the conceptual level; further environmental documentation would be expected as part of definite design deliverables. Environmental engineering was listed as a project discipline reflecting this wetlands assessment and regulatory compliance scope embedded in the conceptual design effort.
