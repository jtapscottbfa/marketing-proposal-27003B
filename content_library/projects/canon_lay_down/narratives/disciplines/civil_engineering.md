The civil engineering scope on the Canon Boulevard Laydown Yard Expansion encompassed site grading and earthwork, surface design, perimeter fencing, and site drainage for an outdoor material storage yard at Thomas Jefferson National Accelerator Facility in Newport News, Virginia. The design established a gravel-surfaced laydown area reaching approximately 171,000 square feet (3.92 acres) at full build-out, structured as a base bid of 90,000 SF with three bid alternates for phased expansion.

Earthwork for the full build-out was quantified at approximately 5,691 cubic yards of cut and 10,361 cubic yards of fill, yielding approximately 4,670 cubic yards of net import fill. The site surface design specified a compacted gravel surface throughout the laydown area. Perimeter security fencing (7-foot chain-link with barbed wire) and site drainage were integral civil elements common to all build-out options. Civil sub-disciplines covered site design, grading and drainage, site utilities coordination, and erosion and sediment control.

VHB (Nicholas Hadiaris, PE) served as civil subconsultant, leading the site civil design component of the conceptual design report. Three laydown yard expansion configurations were evaluated at the conceptual level, with Option 2 — supported by the East Pond stormwater retrofit — selected as the recommended approach for its ability to accommodate the maximum site footprint. The civil design was delivered as part of the 100% Conceptual Design report on May 31, 2023.
