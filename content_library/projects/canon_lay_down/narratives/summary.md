The Canon Boulevard Laydown Yard Expansion at Thomas Jefferson National Accelerator Facility (Jefferson Lab) in Newport News, Virginia was a conceptual design engagement for an expanded outdoor material storage and laydown yard adjacent to Canon Boulevard on the Jefferson Lab campus. Jefferson Science Associates, LLC (JSA) retained the design team as prime consultant under contract JSA 21-C0683, Task Order #6 ($38,862 design fee) to develop a 100% conceptual design for the laydown yard expansion.

The design evaluated a phased build-out reaching approximately 171,000 square feet (3.92 acres) of gravel-surfaced laydown area at full build-out, with a base bid footprint of 90,000 square feet of new impervious surface and three bid alternates extending coverage to full build-out. Site improvements included perimeter security fencing (7-foot chain-link with barbed wire), LED site lighting on 20-foot aluminum poles (17 fixtures), an automatic sliding gate with access control, and underground electrical distribution from an existing building panel. Stormwater management was achieved through a retrofit of the existing on-site East Pond as a Level 1 Wet Pond, achieving Virginia Runoff Reduction Method (VRRM) water-quality compliance through pond treatment and supplemental nutrient credits; hydraulic capacity for the 100-year storm event was confirmed with one foot of freeboard. Wetland impacts associated with the full build-out extent require permitting and mitigation planning at definite design. The Opinion of Probable Construction Cost for full build-out was $1,556,809. VHB served as civil and water resources subconsultant. Conceptual design was completed May 31, 2023.
