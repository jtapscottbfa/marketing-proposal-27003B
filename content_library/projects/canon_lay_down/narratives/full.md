## Project Overview

The Canon Boulevard Laydown Yard Expansion is a site civil conceptual design project for Thomas Jefferson National Accelerator Facility (Jefferson Lab), located at 12000 Jefferson Ave, Newport News, Virginia. Jefferson Lab is a DOE-funded national particle physics research facility operated by Jefferson Science Associates, LLC (JSA). JSA retained the design team as prime consultant under contract JSA 21-C0683, Task Order #6, with a design fee of $38,862. VHB served as civil engineering and water resources subconsultant ($21,550). Conceptual design was completed May 31, 2023.

## Scope

The project involved developing a 100% conceptual design for an expansion of the existing laydown and outdoor storage area to a site adjacent to Canon Boulevard on the Jefferson Lab campus. The design established a phased build-out strategy reaching approximately 171,000 square feet (3.92 acres) of gravel-surfaced laydown area at full build-out, structured as:

- **Base Bid:** 90,000 SF new impervious gravel surface
- **Year Out:** +31,000 SF
- **Bid Alternate 1:** +19,000 SF
- **Bid Alternate 2:** +31,000 SF (full build-out, 171,000 SF total)

Site improvements common to all options included a 7-foot chain-link perimeter fence with barbed wire, LED site lighting on 20-foot aluminum poles (17 fixtures, 250–400W), an automatic sliding gate (2-HP motor) with access control, and underground electrical service (approximately 1,600 LF conduit) from an existing 60-amp, 208/120V, 3-phase panel in adjacent Building 64. Earthwork for the full build-out was estimated at approximately 5,691 CY cut and 10,361 CY fill (4,670 CY net fill).

Stormwater management evaluated three options, recommending the retrofit of the existing East Pond as a Level 1 Wet Pond (Option 2). This configuration supports the full 171,000 SF expansion. Water quality compliance was demonstrated via the Virginia Runoff Reduction Method (VRRM); the East Pond retrofit reduces phosphorus loading by approximately 19.41 lb/yr, with the remaining 3.04 lb/yr addressed through nutrient credits. Hydraulic analysis using EPA SWMM 5.1 confirmed that the 100-year storm event produces a maximum hydraulic grade line at elevation 30.5 ft NAVD88, with emergency spillway at 30.5 ft and top of bank at 31.5 ft, providing one foot of freeboard. The hydraulic model encompassed both the Jefferson Lab property and the adjacent Oyster Point North Commerce Center watershed areas.

The full build-out (Bid Alternate 2) encroaches on jurisdictional wetlands, requiring permitting and mitigation planning as part of advancing to definite design. The Opinion of Probable Construction Cost for full build-out is $1,556,809.

## Role on Project

The design team served as prime consultant, responsible for overall project management, electrical engineering (site lighting and access control design), and delivery of the conceptual design report to JSA. VHB served as civil engineering and water resources subconsultant, providing site civil design, stormwater analysis, and wetland assessment. Bruce Bowman served as Client Manager; Richard Williams, PE served as Electrical Engineer/Project Manager. The design was delivered as a 100% Conceptual Design report on May 31, 2023, under a Federal IDIQ task order contract. Design-bid-build delivery is the intended method for subsequent construction phases.

## Key Challenges and Resolutions

**Stormwater compliance for a large impervious surface expansion.** Expanding a laydown yard by up to 171,000 SF on a 3.92-acre site generates significant new impervious area with associated runoff and nutrient loading obligations under Virginia's VRRM. Three stormwater management strategies were evaluated at the conceptual level. The selected approach — retrofitting the existing East Pond as a Level 1 Wet Pond — leverages existing infrastructure to serve the full build-out footprint without constructing a separate BMP facility. Residual phosphorus load not addressed by the pond retrofit is managed through purchased nutrient credits, achieving full VRRM compliance.

**Wetland impacts at full build-out.** The full 171,000 SF expansion encroaches on jurisdictional wetlands. The conceptual design documented the trade-offs: the full build-out offers larger usable surface area, greater accessibility, and improved resilience against flooding, while carrying higher cost and wetland permitting requirements. The conceptual design establishes the permitting and mitigation pathway for the full build-out extent, leaving the proceed/defer decision to the client.

**Phased build-out structuring.** The design organized the expansion into a base bid and three bid alternates, allowing JSA to stage construction investment, defer wetland-impact areas to later alternates, and competitively bid the work while retaining flexibility on ultimate scope.

## Outcomes

The project delivered a 100% conceptual design with an Opinion of Probable Construction Cost of $1,556,809 for the full build-out, including earthwork, gravel surfacing, fencing, electrical site lighting, access control, and stormwater improvements. The design established a stormwater compliance path via East Pond retrofit and nutrient credits satisfying Virginia VRRM requirements. The phased bid alternate structure provides a framework for advancing to definite design and construction. Definite Design services were offered as a follow-on scope upon client authorization.
