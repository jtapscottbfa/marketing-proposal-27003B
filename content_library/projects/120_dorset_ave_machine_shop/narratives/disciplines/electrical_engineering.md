# Electrical Engineering — 120 Dorset Avenue Interior Renovations

Bowman Foster & Associates provided electrical engineering design services for the interior renovation of an existing industrial facility at 120 Dorset Avenue, Virginia Beach, VA. The electrical scope was the most extensive discipline on the project, encompassing new critical power infrastructure, full LED lighting replacement, occupancy controls, and branch circuit modifications — all while retaining the existing multi-panel distribution backbone.

The existing distribution infrastructure was retained: a 400A MDP (480Y/277V, MLO), two Cutler-Hammer and Eaton 75 kVA dry-type step-down transformers, and five downstream panels (A at 150A/1-phase; B at 200A/3-phase; C at 225A/3-phase; Office Panel 1 at 200A/3-phase). These panels carry substantial industrial loads including 50A welder outlets, a powder coat booth, a sinter oven (125A circuit), and fabrication equipment.

New work centered on critical power for the clean work area: a new Emergency Panel D (100A, 208Y/120V, 3-phase, 22 KAIC) was installed, fed from Panel C under normal conditions and from a new 20 kW/25 kVA diesel generator via an automatic transfer switch (ATS) under emergency conditions. A new 25 kVA online double-conversion UPS (208Y/120V, 3-phase) provides continuous ride-through for sensitive process loads, including the SINTER-2 oven and clean work area receptacles.

All interior lighting was replaced with LED: high-bay LED fixtures on purlins in the industrial work area; 2×4 recessed direct/indirect LED troffers (36–50W, MVOLT) in office areas; surface LED wraparound fixtures (39W MVOLT) in support spaces; LED exit signs with 90-minute battery backup; and LED twin-head emergency units (33W, wall-mounted at 7'-6" AFF). Occupancy sensor switches (dual-technology PIR/ultrasonic, So designation) were installed in office and support areas. Arc flash warning labels per NEC 2020 110.16(A) and UFC 3-560-01 were required at all distribution equipment.
