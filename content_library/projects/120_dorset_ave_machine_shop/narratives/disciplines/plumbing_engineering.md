# Plumbing Engineering — 120 Dorset Avenue Interior Renovations

Bowman Foster & Associates provided plumbing engineering design services for the interior renovation of 120 Dorset Avenue, Virginia Beach, VA. The plumbing scope included complete demolition and replacement of all plumbing fixtures, domestic water and sanitary piping, and the water heater in the restroom and utility areas, as well as extension of the compressed air distribution system throughout the industrial floor.

Demolition work removed all existing plumbing fixtures, hangers, and piping in the affected areas, as well as the water heater. Existing 4-inch sanitary waste and 2-inch cold water mains were retained at the points of connection; the existing air compressor and its piping and drops were also retained.

New plumbing fixtures were provided per the fixture schedule: floor-mounted flush-tank water closets (P-1, 1.1 GPF, ADA-accessible); counter-mounted lavatories (P-2, 0.35 GPM, HC); floor-mounted mop sink (P-3); floor-mounted FRP shower (P-4); counter-mounted kitchen sink (P-5); stackable washer/dryer with utility box outlet (P-6); and wall-mounted urinal (P-7, 0.5 GPF). All fixtures were specified as handicap-accessible compliant with ICC/ANSI A117.1. Commercial fixture specifications (Sections 224213.13, 224213.16, 224216.13, 224216.16, 224223) were included in the project manual.

A new electric water heater (EWH-1) with dual 4500W elements, thermal expansion tank, T&P relief valve, and a 2-inch deep drain pan was installed in the restroom area. Domestic water piping is ASTM B88 Type L copper (Section 221116); sanitary waste and vent is cast iron or PVC (Section 221316). Piping insulation per Section 220719 (1-inch flexible elastomeric or mineral fiber) was specified for all domestic water piping and exposed accessible-fixture piping. New compressed air distribution from the existing compressor runs 1-inch mains throughout the industrial floor with 3/4-inch drops and quick-disconnect fittings at 12 inches AFF, per Section 221513.
