# Interior Renovations for 120 Dorset Avenue — Summary

Bowman Foster & Associates provided comprehensive mechanical, electrical, and plumbing (MEP) engineering services for the interior renovation of an existing single-story industrial building at 120 Dorset Avenue, Virginia Beach, Virginia. The project, issued for construction in September 2020, converted and upgraded an active industrial manufacturing and fabrication facility — evidenced throughout the drawings by powder coat booths, welding outlets, metal compressors, and a sinter oven — while establishing a dedicated clean work area with critical-power infrastructure.

Mechanical scope included full HVAC demolition and replacement: a new 840 CFM, 100% outside-air air handling unit (AHU-1) paired with an R-410a heat pump (HP-1) was installed to serve the clean work area, along with two inline exhaust fans (EF-1 and EF-2). Compressed air distribution was extended throughout the industrial floor from the existing compressor. Plumbing scope included complete demolition and replacement of all fixtures, with a new restroom suite (water closets, lavatories, shower, urinal, mop sink, kitchen sink, and washer/dryer), a new electric water heater with expansion tank, and new domestic water and sanitary piping.

Electrical scope was substantial: a new Emergency Panel D (100A, 208Y/120V, 3-phase) was installed to serve the clean work area, backed by a new 25 kVA UPS (208Y/120V, 3-phase) and a new 20 kW/25 kVA diesel generator with automatic transfer switch. All lighting was replaced with LED throughout, including LED high-bay fixtures in the industrial space, LED troffers in office areas, and occupancy sensors. The existing multi-panel distribution infrastructure — MDP, Panels A, B, C, Office Panel 1, and two 75 kVA transformers serving the 208Y/120V distribution system — was retained. The project also addressed fire alarm wire and cable infrastructure. Bowman Foster & Associates served as prime MEP consultant; Wylie R. Cooke Design, LLC served as engineer of record.
