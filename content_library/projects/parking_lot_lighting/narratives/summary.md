The project involved demolition of four single-floor NIH research buildings (Buildings 18, 18T, 32, and 32T — 30,400 GSF combined) on the NIH Bethesda Campus and construction of a surface parking lot on the cleared footprint. The design effort was executed under a Design-Build contract (NIH Contract C101656) for the NIH Office of Research Facilities, Division of Design and Construction Management.

The electrical engineer provided the Electrical Basis of Design for the new parking lot, encompassing LED site lighting, a new 200A exterior panelboard adjacent to the existing Transformer AT-3, and power supply to a new gate booth. The lighting system was designed for energy-efficient, continuous-use exterior operations. Distribution infrastructure included underground Schedule 40 PVC conduit (minimum 1½-inch), minimum #6 AWG copper conductors rated THHN/THWN at 600V, and equipment grounding conductors on all feeder and branch circuits with all lighting poles grounded. The Electrical Basis of Design final submittal was issued December 1, 2021, with construction targeted for completion by December 31, 2021. The project serves the southern end of the NIH Bethesda Campus and provides secure, energy-efficient surface parking within the approximately 0.75-acre available area.
