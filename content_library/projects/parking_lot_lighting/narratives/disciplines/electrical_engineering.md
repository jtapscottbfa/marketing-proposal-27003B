The electrical engineer provided the complete Electrical Basis of Design for the new surface parking lot constructed on the NIH Bethesda Campus following demolition of Buildings 18, 18T, 32, and 32T. The scope covered all exterior electrical systems required to bring the approximately 0.75-acre parking lot into service: new parking lot LED light fixtures, a new 200A exterior panelboard installed adjacent to the existing campus Transformer AT-3, and power supply to a new gate booth.

Distribution infrastructure was designed for durable exterior service: underground Schedule 40 PVC conduit (minimum 1½-inch), minimum #6 AWG copper conductors with THHN/THWN insulation rated at 600V, and equipment grounding conductors on all feeder and branch circuits with all lighting poles grounded. The LED fixture selection was documented in the project drawing set (Sheet E-501, Lighting Fixture Schedule). The electrical design satisfied NIH's directive to provide "energy-efficient utilities and lighting to support safe and secure continuous use of parking lot."

The electrical scope was the sole MEP discipline on this project — the surface parking lot required no HVAC, plumbing, or fire protection systems. The Electrical Basis of Design final submittal was issued December 1, 2021, on a Design-Build contract (NIH C101656) for the NIH Office of Research Facilities.
