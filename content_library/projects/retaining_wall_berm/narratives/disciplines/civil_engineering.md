Civil engineering on the Hall A Berm Retaining Wall Replacement addressed site design, access, grading, drainage, and regulatory compliance for the replacement of a deteriorated wooden retaining wall at Jefferson Lab's Hall A berm in Newport News, Virginia.

A central civil challenge was constructability: the existing wall is located at the lower end of the berm with no direct path for heavy construction equipment. The civil design included a temporary gravel access road off CEBAF Boulevard to facilitate machinery access to the wall during construction. Following construction, the road area was designed to be filled and restored to original grade and drainage capacity, limiting the permanent environmental footprint of the project.

Regulatory compliance encompassed erosion and sediment control and stormwater management per Virginia state requirements. The total area of disturbance is approximately 0.64 acres, engaging requirements of the Virginia Erosion and Sediment Control Handbook (1992) and the Virginia Stormwater Management Program (VSMP) Regulation (2023). VDOT Road and Bridge Standards (2016, rev. 2022) also governed applicable design elements. Civil engineering was provided by VHB; the VHB team members listed in the BOD are Jay Wheeler, PG and Matt Mader, PE. The design was delivered under Task Order #12 of contract JSA 21-C0683 via design-bid-build delivery.
