Geotechnical engineering on the Hall A Berm Retaining Wall Replacement provided subsurface investigation and soil analysis to underpin the structural retaining wall design at Jefferson Lab in Newport News, Virginia.

The geotechnical work is documented in the Geotechnical Engineering Report prepared by Terracon (Project No. K4235060, dated December 7, 2023). This report informed the design parameters for the reinforced concrete cantilevered retaining wall, including the continuous spread footing dimensions — 12 inches thick and 6 feet wide — and the drain board and corrugated pipe drainage system specified on the soil-side face of the wall to manage long-term hydrostatic pressure. The geotechnical conditions of the Hall A berm, including soil bearing capacity, lateral earth pressures, and subsurface drainage characteristics, were the primary design inputs to the structural wall and footing design.

Geotechnical engineering was provided by GET Solutions; Marcos Freeman, PE served as geotechnical engineer of record per the BOD team roster. The Terracon geotechnical report is the only geotechnical source document referenced in the project record; specific boring logs, soil classifications, or detailed foundation recommendations beyond the wall design parameters captured in the BOD are not available in the source record.
