Structural engineering on the Hall A Berm Retaining Wall Replacement at Jefferson Lab centered on the design of a reinforced concrete cantilevered retaining wall to replace a deteriorated wooden structure at the east side of the Hall A berm in Newport News, Virginia. The existing wooden wall, over 25 years old and degraded in several areas, required full replacement to continue protecting a critical exhaust vent and utility systems serving the DOE particle accelerator facility.

The structural design produced a 12-inch-wide reinforced concrete cantilevered wall on a continuous reinforced concrete spread footing — 12 inches thick and 6 feet wide. A drain board with corrugated pipe was specified on the back (soil) face of the wall to relieve hydrostatic pressure and reduce long-term lateral loading on the structure. Structural design parameters were developed in coordination with the geotechnical engineering findings from the Terracon geotechnical report (Project No. K4235060, dated December 7, 2023), which provided subsurface conditions, lateral earth pressure parameters, and soil bearing capacity data for footing and wall design. The structural engineer of record was Richard Williams, PE (McPherson Design). Final design was completed February 23, 2024, under Task Order #12 of contract JSA 21-C0683.
