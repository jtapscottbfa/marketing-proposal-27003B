# Electrical Engineering — Building 1475 HVAC Replacement, Phase II

BFA's electrical team provided power and lighting design supporting the HVAC system replacement at Building 1475, Norfolk Naval Shipyard, Portsmouth, Virginia.

The electrical scope began with demolition of all conduit, wiring, and disconnect switches associated with the four existing rooftop air handling units (AHU-1, AHU-2 Main, AHU-2 Back-up, AHU-3) and their associated remote-mounted condensing units, as well as the four existing roof-mounted return air fans and existing circulating pump circuits. Existing circuit breakers were retained in place and reused to serve new equipment circuits.

New electrical work included NEMA 4X stainless steel disconnect switches, wiring, and conduit for the three new rooftop AHUs and three new return air fans, along with circuits for the new VAV box controls. Power for the new DDC system was coordinated with the mechanical design. The existing 480Y/277V and 208Y/120V electrical services, along with the backup generator (currently offline), remain in place.

The administrative areas on the first and second floors received new LED lighting fixtures (4000K color temperature, minimum 80 CRI) with new lighting controls, replacing the demolished fluorescent fixtures. High-bay and mechanical room LED lighting — already modernized and in good condition — was retained. Lighting design was coordinated with the new reflected ceiling plans developed under the architectural scope. Design criteria followed UFC 3-530-01, UFC 3-501-01, UFC 3-520-01, NFPA 70, and IESNA standards.
