# Fire Protection Engineering — Building 1475 HVAC Replacement, Phase II

BFA's fire protection team provided design services coordinating the existing fire protection systems with the HVAC replacement and ceiling renovation at Building 1475, Norfolk Naval Shipyard, Portsmouth, Virginia.

Building 1475 contains mixed occupancies per IBC/LSC: Assembly (Group A-3), Business (Group B), and Industrial (Group F-1). The project scope was classified as a modification under LSC §43.2.2.1.3, requiring new construction to comply with UFC 3-600-01 and LSC for new construction, while the unaffected portions comply with LSC for existing occupancies.

The existing fire alarm/mass notification system — a Mircom FX-2000 Addressable Fire Alarm Control Panel with AES Intellinet Transmitter — was retained with modifications. New duct smoke detectors were added per NFPA 90A §11.4.2.1(1) for supply air systems exceeding 2,000 CFM. Ceiling-mounted notification appliances and initiating devices were removed when ceilings were demolished for the HVAC replacement and subsequently reinstalled to coordinate with the new reflected ceiling plans.

The building's existing sprinkler systems — a wet pipe system protecting the administration area and a pre-action system protecting the two-story industrial area — were retained and modified. Sprinklers throughout the affected ceiling areas were removed during demolition and reinstalled to align with the new ceiling layouts. The pre-action system air compressor was surveyed and resized as required. Fire protection design criteria were based on UFC 3-600-01, NFPA 13 (2022), NFPA 72 (2022), NFPA 90A (2024), NFPA 101 (2024), and IBC 2021.
