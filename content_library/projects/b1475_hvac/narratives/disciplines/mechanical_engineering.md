# Mechanical Engineering — Building 1475 HVAC Replacement, Phase II

BFA's mechanical team led the core scope of this project: full replacement of the HVAC systems serving Building 1475 at Norfolk Naval Shipyard (NNSY), Portsmouth, Virginia — a 12,552 SF Nuclear Refueling/Radcon Facility.

The existing pneumatic-controlled HVAC infrastructure was demolished in its entirety, including three rooftop air handling units (AHU-1, AHU-2 Main, AHU-3), four return air fans, and 100% of the interior ductwork on the first and second floors. The design replaced these systems with three new packaged DX rooftop air handling units with hot water heating coils and three new roof-mounted return air fans. New shut-off type VAV terminal boxes with hot water reheat coils serve the first and second floor office areas, supplied by rectangular medium-pressure insulated ductwork. Existing ductwork in the high bay was retained and reused.

The building heating hot water system was redesigned with new steam-to-hot-water heat exchangers, an air separator, expansion tank, shot feeder, and in-line circulation pumps. The domestic hot water heat exchanger and its circulation pump were likewise replaced. All heating hot water piping was replaced and insulated throughout the building. New motorized intake and exhaust louvers with DDC control were installed in the mechanical room. Bathroom, generator room, and mechanical room exhaust fans were replaced.

All pneumatic controls were demolished and replaced with a new DDC system integrated into the NNSY base-wide Siemens DDC network. HVAC design criteria were established per UFC 3-410-01, UFC 3-410-02, and ASHRAE 62.1/55 standards, with outdoor design conditions for Norfolk, Virginia.
