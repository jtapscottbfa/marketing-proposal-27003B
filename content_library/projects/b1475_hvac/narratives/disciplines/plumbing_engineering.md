# Plumbing Engineering — Building 1475 HVAC Replacement, Phase II

Plumbing engineering scope on the Building 1475 HVAC Replacement project at Norfolk Naval Shipyard was scoped to the heating hot water and domestic hot water systems directly tied to the HVAC replacement.

The domestic water heat exchanger was replaced along with its associated circulation pump. The building heating hot water distribution piping — which had served the existing steam-coil HVAC equipment — was replaced in full and insulated throughout. This scope was closely coordinated with the mechanical team's redesign of the heating hot water plant, which transitioned from a steam-condensate system to a new hot-water configuration using new steam-to-hot-water heat exchangers in the mechanical room.

New hydronic baseboard heaters replaced existing units on the first floor. New heating hot water piping was routed to all HVAC equipment with hot water coils — including the rooftop AHUs, VAV box reheat coils, and baseboard heaters.

Per the BOD, plumbing fixtures were not part of this project; water conservation measures and indoor plumbing systems beyond the domestic water heat exchanger replacement were not in scope. Plumbing work complied with the International Plumbing Code 2018 and applicable UFC criteria.
