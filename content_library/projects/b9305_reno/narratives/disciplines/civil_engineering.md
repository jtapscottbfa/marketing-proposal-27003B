Civil engineering scope on Building 9305 addressed exterior ADA accessibility improvements at two locations adjacent to the building. At the B-Wing entrance, the existing concrete stair and adjacent sidewalk were demolished and replaced with a new ADA-compliant stair and walkway combination at a reduced approach slope (1:20), improving accessible connectivity between the parking area and the B-Wing building entry. At the parking lot corner adjacent to the building, a new ADA-compliant curb ramp was constructed: demolition of existing 6-inch concrete curb and sidewalk, new curb ramp at 1:12 maximum slope, detectable warning surface (truncated dome mat) at the ramp landing, and new concrete curb return matching the existing site edge. Both exterior improvements are documented on civil sheet C-100 and demolition sheet D-104. The civil scope was entirely site-level and focused on achieving a compliant accessible path of travel from the parking area to the building — a threshold compliance element for a JCAHO-regulated healthcare-occupancy facility under ADA/ABA and Army installation standards. No grading, drainage, underground utility, or broader site civil work beyond the accessible path elements is documented in the source record for this project.
