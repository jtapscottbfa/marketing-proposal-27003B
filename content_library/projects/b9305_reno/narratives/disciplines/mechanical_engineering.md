Mechanical engineering scope on Building 9305 addressed HVAC in the renovated first-floor restroom areas. Existing mechanical equipment within the demolition zones was removed as part of the full gut-and-renovation; new baseboard heating units were provided in each renovated restroom, connected to the existing building hot-water supply system. New line sets were extended to serve the new baseboard units and were pressure-tested in accordance with SOW Section 23 05 02. The HVAC scope was intentionally limited to terminal-unit replacement within the renovated areas: the project did not include new air handling units, supply/return ductwork, ventilation fans, or modifications to the building's primary heating and ventilation infrastructure, which was existing-to-remain outside the renovation zones. No fire suppression or fire protection mechanical work is documented in the source record for this project. The scope reflects the targeted nature of the renovation — new heating at the point of service within the two restroom pods, coordinated with plumbing demolition and architectural finish sequencing to allow proper rough-in before tile and finish work. Mechanical design was performed as part of the design-build delivery, with PE-sealed submittals produced at 65%, 95%, and 100% IFC milestones under the construction contract.
