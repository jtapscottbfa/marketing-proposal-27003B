Architectural scope on Building 9305 encompassed design of a selective interior renovation across four primary work areas in the two-story masonry structure. At the first floor, the men's and women's gang restrooms were gutted to structural CMU and rebuilt as ADA-compliant single-sex restroom facilities. New construction included new toilet partitions, ceramic tile finishes floor-to-ceiling, ADA water closets with grab bars and full transfer clearances, standard water closets, wall-hung vitreous china lavatories, and accessible shower stalls with ADA-compliant grab bars, fold-down seats, and handheld showerheads. The former dining facility area received limited demolition — removal of a door, frame, and stud wall — to allow installation of an ADA-accessible interior ramp. At the second floor, a new metal stud partition wall (5/8" gypsum board, 3-5/8" studs, sound batt insulation carried 6" above ceiling grid) was constructed to subdivide the existing dayroom, with a new 3'-0"×6'-8" door providing a second means of egress and enabling conference room use, along with new acoustical ceiling tile as required. All work was documented in record drawings dated 28 May 2025. The renovation was governed by IBC, UFC 4.510.01, JCAHO occupancy requirements, and Army installation standards — a demanding code intersection for what is, in physical footprint, a targeted selective renovation of gang restroom and partition elements within an existing masonry building.
