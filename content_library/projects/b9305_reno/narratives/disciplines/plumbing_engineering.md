Plumbing engineering constituted one of the principal discipline scopes on Building 9305, encompassing full fixture replacement and new rough-in in both first-floor restrooms. In the men's restroom, new fixtures included wall-hung vitreous china lavatories (SOW Section 22 42 16), floor-mounted ADA and standard water closets (SOW Section 22 42 13), a wall-hung vitreous china urinal (SOW Section 22 42 13), and an ADA stainless steel panel with fixed handheld showerhead and fold-down seat (SOW Section 22 42 33). The women's restroom received the same complement of fixtures except without a urinal. ADA compliance requirements drove rough-in dimensions throughout: the ADA water closet stall in the men's restroom is approximately 5'-0" wide with grab bars and full transfer clearances; shower rough-in accommodates ADA-compliant grab bar placement and seat mounting. All shower locations received new floor drains; the existing concrete slab was saw-cut at new fixture locations to accommodate repositioned waste and supply rough-in, with concrete restoration per SOW Section 03 30 53. The slab saw-cut was a significant construction coordination item, requiring sequencing with architectural demolition and confirmation of slab thickness and reinforcing conditions prior to cutting. Plumbing design was produced as PE-sealed submittals under the design-build contract, with fixture selections and rough-in dimensions coordinated against the architectural partition layout and tile finish schedule.
