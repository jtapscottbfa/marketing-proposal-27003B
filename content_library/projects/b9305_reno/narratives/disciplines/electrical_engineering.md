Electrical engineering scope on Building 9305 provided new power distribution and lighting within the renovated first-floor men's and women's restrooms. Work included new surface-mounted light fixtures in both restrooms, exit signs at the new exterior door locations serving each restroom, and occupancy sensors for automatic lighting control. Lockout/tagout (LOTO) of existing electrical systems was required throughout the demolition phase to safely isolate wiring serving fixtures and accessories being removed. All electrical work was governed by NFPA 70 (National Electrical Code) and applicable Army installation electrical standards, per SOW Section 26 05 00. The electrical scope was bounded to the renovated restroom areas; the building's existing electrical distribution, panels, branch circuits, and systems serving non-renovation portions of the building were existing-to-remain. The source record does not document low-voltage, fire alarm, telecommunications, emergency power, or security system scope on this project — those systems either fall outside the project boundary or are documented in source materials not included in this record. Electrical design was produced as PE-sealed submittals under the design-build contract structure, coordinated with the architectural finish schedule to position rough-in ahead of tile and ceiling installation in each restroom.
