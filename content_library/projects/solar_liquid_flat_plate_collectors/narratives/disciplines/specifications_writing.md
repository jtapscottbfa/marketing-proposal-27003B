Specifications writing was the foundational discipline for this engagement — the project's entire deliverable was a federal guide specification. The firm, serving as prime consultant under NAVFAC IDIQ A-E contract N6247019D5013, was responsible for producing a merged, updated Unified Facilities Guide Specification (UFGS) governing solar water heating equipment for use across Department of Defense facilities.

The specifications writing scope encompassed all elements of UFGS criteria development: consolidating two existing UFGS sections (UFGS 48 14 13.00 20 and UFGS 22 33 30.00 10) into a single coherent document; updating technical requirements to reflect current technologies, industry standards, and energy and performance requirements; developing the comparison and coordination matrix used to track content between source specifications; reviewing and resolving outstanding Criteria Change Requests (CCRs) from the CMS system; and formatting deliverables in Word, SpecsIntact (.sec), and PDF formats as required by NAVFAC for guide specification publication.

The deliverable had to meet the UFGS program's standards for format, organization, and content — including mandatory coverage of accessibility, antiterrorism, security, sustainability, corrosion protection and control, and safety as applicable — and pass iterative technical review by five agencies (NAVFAC, USACE, AFCEC, USMC, and NASA) across five formal submittal phases. SpecsIntact is the GSA/DOD specification management platform used to maintain and publish UFGS sections; proficiency in SpecsIntact format (.sec files) was a direct technical requirement of this engagement. The completed specification serves as design and construction document criteria for federal facility designers and contractors government-wide.
