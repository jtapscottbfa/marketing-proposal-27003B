Plumbing engineering was engaged on this federal criteria development project as a distinct discipline alongside mechanical engineering, reflecting the direct integration of solar water heating systems with domestic hot water service in federal facilities. Solar water heating equipment — whether using flat plate or evacuated tube collectors — produces heated water that feeds directly into domestic hot water systems, placing plumbing system design criteria squarely within the specification's technical scope.

The plumbing engineering scope on this project was exercised through criteria writing: the discipline contributed technical knowledge of domestic water system integration, cold water makeup, hot water storage and distribution, tempering valve requirements, pressure and temperature relief provisions, and related plumbing system interfaces applicable to active solar water heating installations. The project record identifies plumbing_domestic_water as a sub-discipline and plumbing_systems as a building system within scope, consistent with the specification's coverage of solar thermal-to-domestic-water interface requirements.

The consolidated UFGS section — merging UFGS 48 14 13.00 20 (Solar Liquid Flat Plate and Evacuated Tube Collectors) with UFGS 22 33 30.00 10 (Solar Water Heating Equipment) — required plumbing engineering expertise to address current standards, material requirements, testing provisions, and installation criteria governing the plumbing system interfaces for solar water heating equipment. Updating these requirements to reflect current technologies and applicable industry standards was a component of the criteria development scope.

Plumbing engineering contributed through the five-phase iterative submittal process, with coordinated review across Tri-Service agencies (NAVFAC, USACE, AFCEC, USMC) and NASA.
