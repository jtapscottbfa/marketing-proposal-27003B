This project was a federal A-E professional services engagement under NAVFAC IDIQ contract N6247019D5013 to develop a merged Unified Facilities Guide Specification (UFGS) for solar water heating systems. The firm served as prime consultant to the Naval Facilities Engineering Systems Command (NAVFAC), merging UFGS 48 14 13.00 20 (Solar Liquid Flat Plate and Evacuated Tube Collectors) with UFGS 22 33 30.00 10 (Solar Water Heating Equipment) into a single, consolidated criteria document updated to reflect current technologies, industry standards, and energy and performance requirements. The engagement included developing a comparison and coordination matrix between the two source specifications and reviewing and resolving outstanding Criteria Change Requests (CCRs) from the Criteria Management System (CMS). Deliverables were produced in Word, SpecsIntact (.sec), and PDF formats for use as design and construction criteria by federal facility designers and contractors.

The work was executed through a structured five-phase submittal process — Kickoff, 35% Concept Outline, 65% Progress, Pre-Final Draft, and Final — with formal technical review and coordination by Tri-Service agencies (NAVFAC, USACE, AFCEC, USMC) and NASA at each phase. The engagement drew on expertise across mechanical engineering, plumbing engineering, fire protection engineering, and specifications writing to produce standards-quality federal criteria governing solar thermal collector systems, including requirements for accessibility, antiterrorism, security, sustainability, corrosion protection, and safety.
