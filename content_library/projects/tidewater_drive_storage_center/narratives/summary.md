Tidewater Drive Storage Center Phase II is a commercial self-storage facility located on Tidewater Drive in Norfolk, Virginia. The engineer of record provided plumbing engineering services for Phase II of the project, which encompassed new work associated with elevator pit drainage and below-grade waste infrastructure on a multi-level self-storage building.

The plumbing scope for Phase II centered on two elevator pit sump pump systems — SP-1 serving Elevator 1 and SP-2 serving Elevator 2 — each sized at 55 gallons per minute with 5 horsepower, 208-volt, three-phase motors. Both systems discharge via sanitary waste piping routed through the building interior to the existing main waste connection. An oil/water separator (OWS-1) was incorporated to treat elevator pit drainage prior to discharge, consistent with best practice for below-grade elevator pit applications where hydraulic fluids may be present. New underground sanitary piping was coordinated with the oil/water separator connection to the building's main waste piping.

The plumbing drawings (sheets P-001 and P-101), plotted April 2020, document the full extent of new plumbing work for Phase II. Restroom fixtures visible in the service area were identified as Phase I work and were not part of the Phase II plumbing scope. The two-elevator configuration and building scale indicate a substantial multi-story facility. The engineer of record performed this scope as a standalone plumbing engineering engagement, circa 2020.
