Riverport Welding is a private industrial welding facility in Newport News, Virginia (project no. 24-053). The design firm provided electrical engineering services as engineer of record, producing construction documents for the facility's complete electrical power distribution system.

The project required designing a new electrical service fed from an existing private 3.4/6kV, three-phase, four-wire overhead utility. A bank of three pole-mounted 167 kVA step-down transformers — totaling 501 kVA — converts the medium-voltage supply to 120/208V for building distribution. An exterior-mounted 1,200-ampere, NEMA 3R main service breaker (1,000A continuous, 100,000 AIC) serves as the service entrance, feeding a wire trough backbone that distributes power to four 600-ampere, 120/208-volt, 30-circuit panels dedicated to welding machine power. Two of the four panels (E and F) were new construction; two were existing. Each panel provides fifteen two-pole, 60-ampere circuit positions for individual welding machines, with dedicated conduit feeders per machine. Surge protection (three 6kV arresters) and fused cut-outs (three 100A cut-outs, fused at 20A) protect the medium-voltage interface at the transformer bank. The construction documents were sealed by a Virginia-licensed professional engineer and consist of a single electrical one-line diagram.
