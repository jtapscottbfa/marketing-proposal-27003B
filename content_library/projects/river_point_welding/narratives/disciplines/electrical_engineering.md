Electrical engineering was the sole design discipline on the Riverport Welding project in Newport News, Virginia. The design firm served as engineer of record, delivering a complete electrical power distribution system design for a private industrial welding facility.

The electrical scope extended from the medium-voltage utility interface through to individual welding machine circuit positions. The existing private 3.4/6kV, three-phase, four-wire overhead utility feeds a bank of three pole-mounted 167 kVA step-down transformers (501 kVA aggregate), each protected by fused cut-outs and 6kV surge arresters at the medium-voltage tap. The transformer bank steps the supply down to 120/208V, entering the building through a 1,200-ampere NEMA 3R exterior main service breaker rated for 100,000 AIC. Primary distribution routes from the service entrance via a wire trough backbone to four 600-ampere, 120/208-volt, 30-circuit MLO panelboards dedicated to welding machine power — two new (E and F) and two existing. Each panel provides fifteen two-pole, 60-ampere circuit positions with a dedicated feeder per machine.

Sub-disciplines applied include electrical power distribution (service entrance, transformer bank, and primary distribution design) and electrical motor control (welding machine circuit design and load-side coordination). Construction documents were sealed by a Virginia-licensed professional engineer (PE no. 0998004) and consist of a single-sheet electrical one-line diagram (Sheet E-501).
