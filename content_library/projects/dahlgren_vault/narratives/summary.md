Building 1470 2nd Floor Vault Expansion is a new approximately 4,200-square-foot second-floor addition to an existing building at Naval Support Facility (NSF) Dahlgren, Virginia, designed for the Department of the Navy under NAVFAC Washington, South Potomac (NAVFAC Washington, South Potomac, Dahlgren). The project delivers a Sensitive Compartmented Information Facility (SCIF) accredited under ICD/ICS 705 standards, housing a 2,005-SF High Performance Laboratory (HPL), four dedicated workrooms, a server room, electrical room, entry vestibule, and interconnecting corridor — all constructed to classified facility requirements.

Executed under a design-build contract (NAVFAC Document No. N0017819PX00007; Construction Contract No. N40080-12-C-2542), the design team served as prime consultant with Joel Andre as Architect of Record. The scope encompassed new structural steel and masonry construction atop the existing building footprint; SCIF-compliant wall and ceiling assemblies per ICD/ICS 705 Wall A specifications (STC 50/1-hour rated partitions, triple-layer Type X gypsum board at SCIF perimeters, no exterior glazing); a DX VAV rooftop unit and dedicated server room split systems; a 500 kVA electrical distribution upgrade with twelve new panelboards; a multi-network structured cabling infrastructure supporting NMCI, RDT&E, CNET, and JWICS networks; an electronic security access control system integrated with the existing Lenel platform; and a complete fire alarm and wet-pipe sprinkler system extension. The 100% Final Design Submittal was completed April 23, 2021.
