The architecture scope for the Building 1470 2nd Floor Vault Expansion encompassed design of a new approximately 4,200-square-foot second-floor addition to an existing building at Naval Support Facility Dahlgren, Virginia. Joel Andre served as Architect of Record. The addition is a masonry and structural steel structure rising above the existing Building 1470 first-floor footprint and providing classified workspace to ICD/ICS 705 SCIF standards for the Department of the Navy.

The building envelope consists of CMU cavity wall construction with 4-inch economy brick veneer selected to match the existing facility's exterior character, a fluid-applied vapor barrier, rigid insulation, and metal stud interior framing. No exterior glazing was provided anywhere in the addition — a fundamental ICD/ICS 705 SCIF requirement. The roof is EPDM single-ply over tapered rigid insulation with metal scuppers and downspouts, parapet walls, and two roof access ladders from the existing lower roofs. A 10-foot by 24-foot canopy at the north entry and vehicular bollards provide arrival-side protection and orientation.

Interior spaces are arranged around a central corridor (321 SF) serving the HPL room (2,005 SF), four workrooms, an electrical room, and a vestibule. Workrooms feature 9-foot acoustical tile ceilings; the HPL room has an exposed ceiling. All SCIF perimeter and workroom partition walls are constructed per ICD/ICS 705 Wall A specifications — STC 50/1-hour rated assemblies with sound attenuation batt insulation and multiple layers of Type X gypsum board. A 14-foot by 14-foot roll-up door accesses the HPL space from the north. First-floor selective demolition in the janitor and kitchenette area created the new stair connection and entry vestibule to the existing building.
