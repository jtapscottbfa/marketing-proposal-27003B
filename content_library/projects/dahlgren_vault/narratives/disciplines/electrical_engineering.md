Electrical engineering for the Building 1470 2nd Floor Vault Expansion at Naval Support Facility Dahlgren, Virginia provided full power distribution, lighting, grounding, and lightning protection design for the new approximately 4,200-square-foot second-floor SCIF addition. The electrical scope extends existing building infrastructure significantly to support a high-density classified laboratory and server room environment.

A new 500 kVA dry-type transformer (480V to 208/120V, 3-phase, 4-wire) is fed from a new 600A circuit breaker installed in the existing 3,000A 480V MTM switchgear. The transformer serves a new 800A main panelboard, which distributes power to twelve new panelboards: a 400A server room panel with emergency power-off (EPO) capability via a shunt-trip breaker, and ten 125A, 42-space sub-panels serving the individual workrooms and labs. All conductors are copper THHN/THWN-2 in EMT conduit throughout.

The lighting design provides 4,000K/80 CRI LED sources throughout, with 50 footcandles at 3 feet AFF in workrooms and the server room, 30 footcandles in the conference and electrical rooms, and 5 footcandles in corridors. Dimmable LED fixtures serve the conference room; dual-technology (PIR + ultrasonic) occupancy sensors control all spaces. Emergency and exit lighting is provided throughout. Surface-mounted wireway systems in all labs and workrooms accommodate both power and data/IT drops.

The grounding system employs ICD/ICS 705-compliant conduit grounding clamps and bare copper conductors looped to building steel at all SCIF perimeter penetrations, providing a continuous ground path required by SCIF construction standards. A new lightning protection system using nickel-tipped copper air terminals connects to the existing building lightning protection, with UL 96A certification required for the complete system.
