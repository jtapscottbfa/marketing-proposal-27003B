Mechanical engineering for the Building 1470 2nd Floor Vault Expansion at Naval Support Facility Dahlgren, Virginia provided HVAC design and building automation for the new approximately 4,200-square-foot second-floor SCIF addition. The mechanical scope serves a mix of occupied classified workspace, a server room with dedicated cooling requirements, and a high-performance laboratory, all within a facility that imposes strict requirements on every penetration of the SCIF perimeter.

The primary HVAC system for the main second-floor addition is a DX VAV rooftop unit with variable-speed EC motor, serving individual VAV boxes with electric reheat at each zone. This zoned approach provides independent temperature control for the HPL room and each of the classified workrooms while managing reheat loads from the high-sensible SCIF environment. Medium-pressure supply ductwork distributes conditioned air from the RTU to low-pressure distribution at each zone; main return ductwork routes back to the RTU. The server room receives dedicated cooling via two mini-split systems with seismic bracing, independent from the main HVAC system to provide cooling redundancy for critical IT infrastructure. Existing heat pump units were relocated from the lower roof to the new high roof as part of the addition work.

Design conditions are 90°F DB/81°F WB for cooling and 12°F DB for heating. Emergency shutdown buttons are located at all egress points. All HVAC duct and pipe penetrations through the SCIF perimeter are detailed with Z-duct acoustic treatment and ICD/ICS 705-compliant grounding and sealing. Building automation uses DDC controls with BACnet MSTP, integrating new equipment into the existing facility JACE panel.
