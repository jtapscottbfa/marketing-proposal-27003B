Fire protection engineering for the Building 1470 2nd Floor Vault Expansion at Naval Support Facility Dahlgren, Virginia provided fire suppression, fire alarm, and life safety design for the new approximately 4,200-square-foot second-floor SCIF addition. The fire protection scope integrates new systems into the existing Building 1470 infrastructure while meeting IBC requirements for the classified occupancy.

The new wet-pipe sprinkler system taps the existing 4-inch main serving the existing second floor of Building 1470. A light-hazard design (0.10 gpm/1,500 SF) applies to offices and corridors; ordinary-hazard design (0.20 gpm/2,500 SF) applies to the server room and electrical room. Quick-response recessed chrome pendent sprinklers are installed in finished ceiling areas; quick-response brass upright sprinklers serve unfinished areas. Hydrant flow test data established: 57 PSI static, 54 PSI residual, and 1,015 GPM flow. SCIF wall pipe penetrations are detailed with ICD/ICS 705-compliant grounding.

The fire alarm system extends the existing Gamewell FCI E3 addressable fire alarm panel. New devices — including strobes and speakers in each workroom and corridor, pull stations at egress doors, and duct smoke detectors at the air handling unit — connect via extension of the existing IDC (Class B circuits). An existing NAC booster panel in the adjacent HPL space provides notification appliance circuit power for the new addition. All circuits include 48-hour standby and 10-minute alarm battery backup capacity.

Life safety analysis classifies the addition as IBC Group B (Business) occupancy, Type IIB construction, in an unlimited area building by virtue of its fully sprinklered, two-story configuration. Two required exits are provided via a new stair and a fire door to the existing building corridor. Travel distance, common path, and dead-end corridor dimensions were verified to comply with IBC limits. One-hour fire barriers at the corridor comply with ICS 705 requirements.
