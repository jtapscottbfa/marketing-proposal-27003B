The ORF Parking Administration Building is a new, single-story, approximately 3,895-square-foot facility for the Norfolk Airport Authority at 2200 Norview Ave on the campus of Norfolk International Airport (ORF), Norfolk, Virginia. The building provides dedicated operational space for the airport's parking management program, housing administrative offices, a parking operations suite, a break room, restrooms, an IT closet, and a mechanical/electrical room. The project is delivered as design-bid-build, with MEP engineering services provided as a subconsultant to the architect of record.

Mechanical, electrical, and plumbing engineering services span the complete MEP scope for this new structure. The mechanical system is organized around a central built-up air handling unit with variable-air-volume distribution and electric heating, serving the primary occupied spaces, supplemented by dedicated mini-split systems for the IT closet and MEP room and restroom exhaust. Total installed cooling capacity is approximately 9.5 tons across three HVAC subsystems. The building is entirely all-electric — no natural gas service. Electrical scope includes LED interior and site lighting, three distribution panelboards, emergency and exit lighting with battery backup, occupancy sensor controls, and pole-mounted site area lighting. The plumbing scope covers domestic cold and hot water distribution with electric water heating and recirculation, a thermostatic mixing valve, backflow prevention, and a full accessible fixture complement including water closets, lavatories, kitchen sink, ice maker, bi-level drinking fountain, urinal, and mop sink. Design documentation was advanced from a July 2025 concept through 65% submittal drawings for electrical and plumbing (October 2025) and 100% submittal-stage mechanical work (October 2025), with equipment selections finalized across all MEP disciplines.
