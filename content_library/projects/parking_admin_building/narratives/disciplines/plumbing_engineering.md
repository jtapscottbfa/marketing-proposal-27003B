Plumbing engineering scope for the ORF Parking Administration Building covers domestic water, sanitary, and associated plumbing systems for a new single-story, approximately 3,895-square-foot facility at Norfolk International Airport. Domestic water service enters the building through a 2½-inch main and distributes cold water throughout. Hot water is generated by a 30-gallon, 4,500-watt, 208V/3-phase electric water heater (EWH-1). A Bell & Gossett ECOICIRC XL recirculation pump (RCP-1, 3 GPM, 15 ft head) maintains hot water availability at fixtures throughout the building. A master thermostatic mixing valve (TMV-1) tempers the 140°F storage temperature to 105°F at distribution, and a 2-inch reduced-pressure zone backflow preventer (BFP-1) protects the potable water supply at the service entrance.

The plumbing fixture complement is sized for the building's mixed occupancy and includes flush-valve water closets (standard and accessible), lavatories, a two-compartment kitchen sink, an ice maker connection, a bi-level drinking fountain, a flush-valve urinal, and a mop sink. Exterior frost-proof wall hydrants are provided at multiple building perimeter locations. All fixtures are selected to meet ADA/ABAAG accessibility requirements.

The building is all-electric with no natural gas service; all domestic water heating is provided electrically. Plumbing design documents were produced at the 65% submittal stage as of October 2025, with plumbing equipment selections finalized at the 100% submittal stage.
