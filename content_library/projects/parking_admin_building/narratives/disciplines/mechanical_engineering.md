Mechanical engineering scope for the ORF Parking Administration Building covers HVAC design for a new single-story, approximately 3,895-square-foot facility on the campus of Norfolk International Airport. The mechanical system is organized around a built-up air handling unit (AHU-1) with an electric heating coil and variable-air-volume distribution, serving the building's primary occupied spaces — administrative offices, a parking operations suite, a break room, sign storage, and corridor areas, representing approximately 3,272 square feet of conditioned floor area per thermal load modeling. Six or more VAV terminal boxes with baseboard electric heating provide zone-level temperature control throughout these spaces.

Two dedicated mini-split systems supplement the primary air handling unit: a 0.6-ton unit (MSI-1/MSO-1) for the IT closet and a 1.2-ton unit (MSI-2/MSO-2, supported by condensing unit ACCU-1) for the MEP room, each providing independent control for heat-gain-sensitive equipment environments. An exhaust fan (EF-1) serves the restroom zone. Total installed cooling capacity is approximately 9.5 tons across the three HVAC subsystems.

Thermal load analysis was performed using TRACE 700 v6.3.5 against Norfolk, Virginia outdoor design conditions: 22°F heating design dry-bulb and 90–91°F cooling design dry-bulb. The building is entirely all-electric — no natural gas service — with the AHU electric heating coil and electric baseboard heat at VAV terminals covering all space heating loads. Mechanical design documents were advanced to the 65% submittal stage for HVAC drawings, with mechanical equipment selections finalized at the 100% submittal stage.
