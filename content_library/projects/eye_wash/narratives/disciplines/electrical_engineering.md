## Electrical Engineering — Site Eyewash and Safety Shower Stations, Jefferson Lab

Electrical engineering was a participating discipline on this site-wide safety infrastructure upgrade at Jefferson Laboratory, Newport News, Virginia. The sub-discipline scope included electrical power distribution, recorded in the project taxonomy as the electrical discipline's contribution to the eyewash and safety shower station installations across approximately 20-plus campus locations.
