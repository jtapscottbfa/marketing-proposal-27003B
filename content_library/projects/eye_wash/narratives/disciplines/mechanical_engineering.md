## Mechanical Engineering — Site Eyewash and Safety Shower Stations, Jefferson Lab

Mechanical engineering services on this project addressed the HVAC and tepid-water system requirements imposed by ANSI Z358.1-2014 for the site-wide eyewash and safety shower station upgrade at Jefferson Laboratory, Newport News, Virginia. The standard mandates that emergency fixture water be delivered within a specific temperature range, which requires mechanical system involvement beyond the plumbing rough-in — particularly in a multi-building campus environment where supply-water temperatures and available building mechanical infrastructure vary across locations.

The project's sub-discipline scope included HVAC design, and HVAC systems are recorded as a relevant building system for the upgrade. Work was performed under ANSI/ASHRAE/IESNA Standard 90.1-2019, DOE Order 436.1, and 10 CFR Part 433/434 energy requirements, as well as Jefferson Lab's Design Standard. The mechanical scope spanned approximately 20-plus locations across the Jefferson Lab campus. The Basis of Design (BOD) issued as a Final Submittal in March 2024 incorporated the mechanical design basis for the tepid-water systems across all affected buildings.
