The design team provided architectural-engineering services for the Site Eyewash and Safety Shower Stations project at Jefferson Laboratory (Jefferson Lab) in Newport News, Virginia — a Department of Energy national research facility operated by Jefferson Science Associates, LLC. Working as prime consultant under Task Order 7 of on-call IDIQ contract JSA 21-C0683, the team delivered a site-wide safety infrastructure upgrade spanning approximately 20-plus building locations across the Jefferson Lab campus, including multiple experimental halls (Hall C, Hall D, LERF) and support buildings.

The scope encompassed plumbing, mechanical, and electrical engineering design for new and replacement eyewash and safety shower stations compliant with ANSI Z358.1-2014. Applicable codes and standards included the International Plumbing Code, NFPA 70 (National Electrical Code), Jefferson Lab's own Design Standard, and DOE energy and sustainability requirements (ANSI/ASHRAE/IESNA 90.1-2019, DOE O 436.1, 10 CFR Parts 433 and 434). Mechanical engineering services addressed tepid-water system requirements mandated by ANSI Z358. Electrical engineering addressed power distribution for the associated systems. The project was a design-only engagement; the owner held and managed the construction contract independently.

The design fee was $37,211 against a total owner program budget of $260,000. The project was executed under a design-bid-build delivery method. The Basis of Design (BOD) was issued as a Final Submittal in March 2024.
