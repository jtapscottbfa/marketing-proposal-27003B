The design team — operating as the Hartigan, Bowman, Foster & Associates (HBFA) joint venture — provided architectural and engineering design services for a new central emergency power distribution system at the Perry Point VA Medical Center, part of the VA Maryland Health Care System. The design installed four 2 MW EPA Tier IV final dual-fuel diesel generators with integral belly tanks in a new central generator facility behind the Chiller Plant, with new paralleling switchgear sized for current and future emergency loads. New main switchgear was specified at approximately 2,000A per feeder at 5kV. The existing 5kV emergency distribution was intercepted, spliced, and rerouted to the new paralleling switchgear via underground ductbanks and new manholes. The design also included a fuel tank farm sized for 96-hour full-load generator run time plus 10 January days of boiler-plant fuel, quick-connect boxes for approximately 13 critical buildings, and demolition of approximately 16 obsolete generators and 23 obsolete fuel tanks. The design was developed to comply with the Mission Critical Physical Design Manual for VA Facilities and Maryland regulatory requirements (Clean Air Act / MDE-ARMA Air Quality Permit, PSC CPCN exemption for Type I generators, MDE-approved Storm Water and Erosion & Sediment Control plans, and tank, waste, and lead/asbestos compliance). The A/E design contract (VA245-17-D-0138 / task order 36C24518N3701, VA Project No. 512A5-18-302) was accepted September 10, 2018 at a total design fee of $798,566.18 across two purchase orders. The 100% Final Basis of Design was issued March 11, 2022.
