The plumbing engineering scope for LR-167 and LR-168 provides domestic water supply and sanitary drainage service for both two-story relocatable office buildings at Naval Air Station Patuxent River, Maryland.

Domestic hot water is generated by electric tankless (instantaneous) water heaters serving restrooms, kitchen/break areas, and janitorial mop sinks throughout each building. Flush-valve toilets and urinals are specified throughout the restroom cores in lieu of tank-type fixtures, consistent with commercial office occupancy and UFC standards. Restrooms include ADA-accessible showers with fold-down seats. ADA-compliant drinking fountains with bottle-refill capability are provided on each floor.

The domestic water service enters each building as a 1.5-inch copper line from a government-furnished 6-inch stub-up at the building pad. A water meter is installed on the domestic water supply line. Sanitary drainage is routed to a 4-inch PVC connection at the existing site manhole. A sump pump is installed in the elevator shaft to manage drainage in that confined pit.

Specific pipe sizing, fixture unit totals, water pressure calculations, and isometric drawings were not captured in the source records available at the time of extraction.
