The electrical engineering scope for LR-167 and LR-168 provides complete power distribution, lighting, emergency power, and low-voltage infrastructure for both two-story relocatable office buildings at Naval Air Station Patuxent River, Maryland.

Electrical service is 208Y/120V, three-phase, four-wire, supplied through an exterior CT cabinet connecting to SMECO (Southern Maryland Electric Cooperative) utility. A main distribution panel (MDP) with transient voltage surge suppression (TVSS/SPD) distributes power to multiple subpanels throughout each building.

Interior lighting is provided by recessed 2×4 LED fixtures, designed to deliver 60 footcandles at desk height in general office areas and 75 footcandles in the POP room and IDF room, which have higher task-lighting requirements. Exterior lighting includes a 400-watt metal halide floodlight at the main entrance. Lighting controls include dimming switches in conference rooms and occupancy/motion sensors in kitchens, storage areas, and restrooms, meeting UFC energy efficiency requirements.

Emergency lighting with 30-minute battery backup is provided throughout egress paths. A backup generator with a minimum 24-hour fuel capacity provides standby power per the J-1501001-05 technical specification. Grounding and lightning protection systems are included in the electrical scope.

The buildings also incorporate structured cabling infrastructure, electronic access control (ACS), and CCTV provisions consistent with ICD-705 secure facility requirements, as reflected in the telecommunications and security building systems on the project.
