The fire protection engineering scope for LR-167 and LR-168 provides fire suppression and life safety systems for both two-story relocatable office buildings at Naval Air Station Patuxent River, Maryland, in compliance with UFC 3-600-01, NFPA 13, NFPA 72, and UFC 4-021-01.

Fire suppression is provided by a wet pipe automatic sprinkler system designed per NFPA 13. The system is governed by UFC 3-600-01 (DoD standard for fire protection of facilities), which is the applicable NAVFAC design standard for this project.

The fire alarm system is a combined Fire Alarm and Mass Notification System (FMCU/FAMNS) designed per NFPA 72 and UFC 4-021-01. Notification throughout both buildings is by combination voice/strobe appliances. Initiating devices include manual pull stations at all exterior egress doors, system smoke detectors above electrical panels and at code-required locations, and duct detectors on supply and return ductwork serving systems larger than 2,000 CFM. ADT/DMP automatic dialers provide reporting to the base Regional Dispatch Center.

Additional life safety provisions include a government-furnished Knox Box near the main entrance (contractor installed), AED units with wall cabinets at one unit per floor per J-1501001-05 specification, and fire extinguishers in recessed or semi-recessed wall cabinets distributed throughout both buildings.
