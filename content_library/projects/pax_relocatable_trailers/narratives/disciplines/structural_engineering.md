The structural engineering scope for LR-167 and LR-168 addressed foundation design for both two-story modular/relocatable office buildings at Naval Air Station Patuxent River, Maryland. Each building is approximately 37,586 gross square feet across two floors.

Foundation design for both structures consists of continuous CMU (concrete masonry unit) foundation walls bearing on reinforced concrete spread footings. This foundation system supports the modular building superstructure and is appropriate for the site conditions at a Tidewater Maryland coastal installation. The reinforced spread footing configuration distributes the building loads while meeting the site preparation requirements of a leased, relocatable facility.

The structural scope is focused on the subgrade and foundation elements. The building superstructure is a factory-manufactured modular/pre-engineered system provided by the relocatable vendor; it is not a traditional site-built structural design. The structural engineering deliverable defines the foundation system required to receive and support the modular units in place on site. Specific structural calculations, bearing pressure analysis, reinforcing schedules, and geotechnical input data were not captured in the source records available at the time of extraction.
