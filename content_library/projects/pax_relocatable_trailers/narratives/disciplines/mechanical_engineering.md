The mechanical (HVAC) scope for LR-167 and LR-168 provides climate control for both two-story relocatable office buildings at Naval Air Station Patuxent River, Maryland.

Primary space conditioning is provided by air handling units: ground-mounted units on steel stands serve the first floor, and roof-mounted units serve the second floor. Conference rooms receive supplemental cooling from dedicated ductless split systems. Additional ductless split systems are provided for the elevator machine room, POP room, and server room, which have distinct 24/7 heat loads and reliability requirements independent of the main AHU systems. Electric baseboard heaters serve stairwells, the mechanical room, and restrooms where primary AHU distribution does not extend.

Exhaust ventilation is provided by a roof-mounted centrifugal exhaust fan serving the restroom cores, and a sidewall direct-drive exhaust fan with a motorized louver at the mechanical and electrical rooms. The exhaust strategy is configured to meet ASHRAE and UFC ventilation requirements for each space type.

Controls are implemented via stand-alone DDC (direct digital control) with 7-day programmable thermostats. The stand-alone DDC configuration — rather than a centralized building automation system — is consistent with the maintainability and vendor-independence requirements of a relocatable leased facility.
