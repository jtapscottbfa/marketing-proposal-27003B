The architectural scope covered the design of two two-story relocatable modular office buildings — LR-167 and LR-168 — at Naval Air Station Patuxent River, Maryland. Each building is approximately 37,586 gross square feet across two stories, with an overall footprint of approximately 156 feet by 138 feet.

The program on each building combines general open-plan office areas with cubicle layouts, private enclosed offices, multiple conference rooms (the largest at 814 SF on the second floor), dedicated telecommunications rooms (a main POP room, IDF room, and server rooms), reception, break/vending areas, restrooms with ADA-compliant showers and drinking fountains, and dedicated mechanical and electrical rooms. A passenger/cargo elevator serves both floors.

A defining feature of both buildings is the integration of ICD-705-compliant secure space construction within the modular building envelope. Wall assemblies in secure zones are rated STC-45 to STC-50, with electromagnetic shielding at selected locations. Secure vestibule entries with electronic access control are provided at all classified areas. Wall types throughout are consistent with ICD-705 Wall Type A specifications. The floor plan layout is organized to separate secure from non-secure circulation, which drove room adjacency decisions, wall thickness requirements, and door configurations. Both buildings are pre-engineered modular/relocatable construction, classified as special construction under the project's construction scope.
