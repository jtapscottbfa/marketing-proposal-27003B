## Project Overview

This project called for design engineering services for two two-story relocatable modular office buildings at Naval Air Station Patuxent River, Maryland (commonly known as Pax River). Designated LR-167 and LR-168, the buildings were procured by Naval Air Systems Command (NAVAIR) to support Naval Air Warfare Center Aircraft Division (NAWCAD) personnel. The Authority Having Jurisdiction (AHJ) is NAVFAC (Naval Facilities Engineering Command). The design team completed a 65% Basis of Design submittal in May 2024 under BFA Project #21-042J, against base IDIQ contract N40080-11-D-3019.

## Scope

Each building is approximately 37,586 gross square feet across two stories. The program on each building includes general open-plan and private office space, multiple conference rooms (the largest at 814 SF on the second floor), dedicated telecommunications infrastructure spaces (main POP room, IDF room, and server rooms), reception, break/vending areas, restrooms with ADA-compliant showers and drinking fountains, and dedicated mechanical and electrical rooms. A passenger/cargo elevator serves both floors of each building.

A distinguishing program element is the integration of ICD-705-compliant secure spaces within the modular building envelope. Wall assemblies throughout the secure zones are rated STC-45 to STC-50, with electromagnetic shielding at selected locations. Secure door vestibule entries are provided at all classified areas, and the floor plan layout is organized to segregate secure from non-secure circulation. Electronic access control (ACS), closed-circuit video (CCTV), intrusion detection, and noise generation provisions are incorporated throughout the secure zones per ICD-705 requirements.

The contract vehicle is a lease-based IDIQ (contract N40080-11-D-3019). Under this structure, the design team engineered the facility to performance specifications; a relocatable vendor delivers, installs, and leases the completed structures to NAVFAC. The completed, installed facility was required to be ready for occupancy within 240 calendar days of award, with liquidated damages of $800 per calendar day of delay.

Full-building MEP systems were designed across all disciplines:

**HVAC:** Ground-mounted air handling units on steel stands serve the first floor; roof-mounted air handling units serve the second floor. Conference rooms receive supplemental cooling via ductless split systems. Additional dedicated ductless split systems are provided for the elevator machine room, POP room, and server room, which carry distinct 24/7 heat loads. Electric baseboard heaters serve stairwells, the mechanical room, and restrooms. Exhaust ventilation is provided by a roof-mounted centrifugal fan for restroom cores and a sidewall direct-drive exhaust fan with motorized louver at mechanical and electrical rooms. Controls are stand-alone DDC with 7-day programmable thermostats.

**Plumbing:** Electric tankless water heaters serve restrooms, kitchen/break areas, and janitorial mop sinks. Flush-valve toilets and urinals are specified throughout. ADA-compliant drinking fountains with bottle-refill capability are provided on each floor. The 1.5-inch copper domestic water service connects to a government-furnished 6-inch stub-up at the building pad, with a water meter on the domestic water line. Sanitary drainage terminates at a 4-inch PVC connection to the existing site manhole. A sump pump is installed in the elevator shaft.

**Electrical:** Service is 208Y/120V, three-phase, four-wire via an exterior CT cabinet (SMECO utility). A main distribution panel with TVSS/SPD feeds multiple subpanels throughout the building. Interior lighting is recessed 2×4 LED fixtures at 60 footcandles at desk height (75 fc in POP/IDF rooms). A 400-watt metal halide exterior floodlight is provided at the main entrance. Lighting controls include dimming switches in conference rooms and occupancy/motion sensors in kitchens, storage, and restrooms. Emergency lighting with 30-minute battery backup is provided throughout egress paths. A backup generator with minimum 24-hour fuel capacity provides standby power per J-1501001-05 technical specification. Grounding and lightning protection are included.

**Fire Protection:** A wet pipe automatic sprinkler system is designed per NFPA 13 and UFC 3-600-01. A combined Fire Alarm and Mass Notification System (FMCU/FAMNS) per NFPA 72 and UFC 4-021-01 provides voice/strobe notification throughout, with ADT/DMP dialers reporting to the base Regional Dispatch Center. Initiating devices include manual pull stations at all exterior egress doors, system smoke detectors at panels and code-required locations, and duct detectors on supply/return ductwork exceeding 2,000 CFM. A government-furnished Knox Box (contractor installed), AED units with wall cabinets (one per floor), and recessed fire extinguisher cabinets complete the life safety package.

**Structural:** Foundation design consists of continuous CMU walls on reinforced concrete spread footings, sized to support the modular building superstructure.

## Role

The design team served as prime consultant, responsible for all engineering disciplines for both buildings under BFA Project #21-042J. Deliverables at the 65% Basis of Design stage covered architectural, structural, mechanical, plumbing, electrical, and fire protection disciplines.

## Key Challenges and Resolutions

**Secure facility requirements within a relocatable building envelope.** Both buildings serve NAWCAD operational functions that include classified spaces, requiring ICD-705-compliant construction features — STC-rated wall assemblies, electromagnetic shielding, secure vestibule entries, ACS, and IDS provisions — within the constraints of modular/pre-engineered relocatable construction. Space planning carefully balanced the secure-zone adjacency requirements (POP rooms, server rooms, classified conference areas) against standard office program elements and the modular structural grid.

**Dual-building parallel design tracks.** LR-167 and LR-168 are distinct structures with separate government-furnished floor plan attachments — the LR-167 plans date from 2012 (NAWCAD Infrastructure Business Operations Department), while the LR-168 plans were revised in 2021. The BOD documentation is organized both by building and by discipline, requiring consistent design criteria and MEP system sizing to be maintained across two parallel facility programs.

**Square footage measurement basis.** The BOD General section reports approximately 37,586 gross SF (including wall thickness), while the SOW and Electrical BOD state 36,000 SF (net interior, explicitly excluding wall thickness). The ~4.5% difference is explained by the SOW's explicit measurement basis language; both figures are internally consistent. The 37,586 GSF figure from the BOD General section is used as the standard gross area reference throughout the design.

## Outcomes

The design team produced a complete 65% Basis of Design submittal in May 2024 covering all engineering disciplines for both LR-167 and LR-168. The design documents provide a full engineering basis for the two buildings, including MEP system sizing, ICD-705-compliant space planning, structural foundation design, and UFC-compliant fire protection systems. Financial and schedule close-out data — including the design fee, awarded dates, and construction completion dates — were not available in the source documents at the time of extraction.
