This project involved design engineering services for two two-story relocatable modular office buildings — LR-167 and LR-168 — at Naval Air Station Patuxent River, Maryland, for Naval Air Systems Command (NAVAIR) under the authority of NAVFAC. Each building is approximately 37,586 gross square feet across two floors and was designed to serve as administrative and operational office space for Naval Air Warfare Center Aircraft Division (NAWCAD) personnel.

Both buildings were designed to support general office occupancy alongside ICD-705-compliant secure spaces, including dedicated rooms for classified telecommunications infrastructure (POP rooms, server rooms, IDF rooms) with enhanced wall construction rated STC-45 to STC-50, electromagnetic shielding at selected locations, and secure vestibule entries throughout. Both structures are two-story, pre-engineered modular construction with reinforced concrete spread footing foundations. MEP systems included packaged and split-system HVAC with stand-alone DDC controls, electric tankless domestic hot water, a wet pipe automatic fire suppression system per NFPA 13 and UFC 3-600-01, and a combined Fire Alarm and Mass Notification System (FMCU/FAMNS) per UFC 4-021-01. Electrical service is 208Y/120V three-phase, with LED interior lighting, occupancy controls, and a backup generator sized for a minimum 24-hour run capacity.

The design team served as prime consultant, producing a 65% Basis of Design submittal in May 2024 under BFA Project #21-042J against base IDIQ contract N40080-11-D-3019. The contract required the completed, installed facility to be ready for occupancy within 240 days of award.
