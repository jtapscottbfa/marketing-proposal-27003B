Civil engineering on this project centered on site preparation, earthwork, and the design of a compliant secondary containment system for the new three-tank fuel storage installation at the Perry Point VA Medical Center boiler plant.

The primary civil scope was demolition of the existing 150,000-gallon single-wall aboveground fuel tank and its earthen containment berm, followed by site grading to receive the new tank pad and containment structure. A reinforced concrete secondary containment pad was designed to retain 110 percent of the volume of one 35,000-gallon tank plus the volume generated by a seven-day, 25-year storm event — the threshold required by Maryland Department of the Environment (MDE) regulations for aboveground petroleum storage facilities. A site retention and containment wall completes the secondary containment boundary.

Civil utilities work included layout and coordination of new fuel fill piping routing across the site and tie-in of the oil/water separator for containment drainage. Erosion and sediment control measures were designed as part of the SWPPP for the construction disturbance area.

Site civil design was constrained by the active medical center campus setting: the tank farm footprint had to accommodate three tanks, blast mitigation screen walls, secondary containment geometry, and fuel delivery vehicle access within a limited site area adjacent to occupied facility buildings. Coordination with structural engineering for the blast wall layout and with environmental engineering for drainage and containment drainage treatment was integral to the civil design package.
