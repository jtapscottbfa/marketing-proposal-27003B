The design team served as prime A/E consultant for replacement of a 150,000-gallon single-wall fuel storage tank at the boiler plant of the Perry Point VA Medical Center in Perry Point, Maryland. Procured as an SDVOSB set-aside task order under VA IDIQ contract 36C24520R0091 (NRM Project No. 512A5-20-302), the project replaced an aging aboveground fuel system with three prefabricated, double-wall, 35,000-gallon horizontal aboveground storage tanks — a configuration that met contemporary environmental containment requirements while sustaining uninterrupted fuel supply to the facility's four boilers.

The design encompassed full demolition of the existing tank and its earthen containment berm; a reinforced concrete secondary containment pad engineered to retain 110 percent of the volume of one tank plus a seven-day, 25-year storm event; new double-wall fuel fill piping (ASTM 53 steel with insulation and aluminum jacket); dedicated supply and return lines to each boiler; a permanent fuel polishing system; an MDE-approved automatic tank gauge and leak detection system; tank flow meters with remote readout to the boiler plant control room; cathodic protection and tank grounding; and site lighting. Structural engineering addressed physical security requirements through design of 12-inch CMU blast mitigation screen walls on the north and west sides of the tank farm. Environmental services included a SWPPP, industrial hygiene assessment of existing soil contamination, and lead paint sampling and disposal planning. The construction budget was $800,000 with a 120-calendar-day design performance period.
