The design team provided electrical engineering services for a commercial-scale solar photovoltaic system at a private agricultural property in Bloxom, Virginia, on the Eastern Shore. The project scope centered on the design of a 278-module, three-phase grid-tied PV system for Tai Dat, LLC, with a rated capacity of 112.5 kWDC and 133.05 kWAC. The module complement consists of 278 Hyperion GH108PB-400W panels, each paired with an Enphase IQ8P-3P-72-E-US microinverter — a one-to-one inverter-per-module architecture that maximizes energy harvest and provides module-level monitoring. Modules are organized across three sub-arrays that feed three Solar Load Center Distribution Panels, converging through an 800-amp safety switch positioned adjacent to the utility-owned transformer as the solar disconnect.

The primary project deliverable was a single-line electrical diagram (S.L.D.) prepared for permitting and utility interconnection, dated March 2024. The design addresses NEC compliance for grid-tied distributed generation, grounding and bonding scheme, revenue-grade utility metering (meter 164368573, account 2880800), conduit requirements, and back-feed protection at the utility interface. The design team served as prime consultant on the project. Contract value, construction-phase details, and personnel records are not captured in the available source record.
