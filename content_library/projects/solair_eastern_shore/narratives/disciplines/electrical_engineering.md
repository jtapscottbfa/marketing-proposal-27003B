Electrical engineering was the sole design discipline on this project. The scope encompassed the complete electrical design for a 278-module, three-phase, grid-tied solar photovoltaic system at a private property in Bloxom, Virginia, rated at 112.5 kWDC and 133.05 kWAC. Work included module and microinverter selection (Hyperion GH108PB-400W modules; Enphase IQ8P-3P-72-E-US microinverters, one per module), array string configuration across 27 strings organized into three sub-arrays, and the design of the three-phase AC collection and distribution system connecting to the utility-owned transformer through an 800-ampere solar safety disconnect.

The electrical design addressed NEC-compliant grounding and bonding, 10/3 copper feeder and distribution panel design for each sub-array, revenue-grade utility metering, back-feed protection, and utility coordination documentation for the interconnection application. The primary deliverable was a single-line electrical diagram (S.L.D.) prepared for permit and interconnection, dated March 2024.

Sub-disciplines engaged were electrical power distribution and electrical renewable systems. The design team served as prime consultant.
