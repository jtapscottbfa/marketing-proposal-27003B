Electrical engineering services on this project were focused on coordination and protection of existing electrical and mechanical systems during the retaining wall replacement and access road construction at an active particle accelerator research facility. The primary electrical scope involved a 480V, 3-phase circuit serving an air-cooled condensing unit (ACCU) near the construction area: the circuit was designed to be temporarily removed and capped at defined limits to allow excavation for the new retaining wall, then reinstalled upon completion of the retaining wall and access road. The circuit power source and circuit name were identified as to be determined at the concept design phase.

Two mission-critical mechanical systems required electrical coordination to ensure uninterrupted facility operations. An existing air-cooled chiller identified as mission-critical was to receive a temporary generator to maintain operation during construction activities. An existing exhaust fan was identified as mission-critical and required to remain operational continuously throughout the construction sequence.

The electrical discipline also provided documentation of the extensive underground utility network crossing the site on sheet EC-100 — a network characteristic of an active accelerator campus including chilled water (CHW), sanitary (SAN), gas (G), communications (COMM), cryogenic (CRYO), cooling water (CW), and deionized water (DIWP) distribution systems. This documentation informed site coordination requirements for the civil and structural construction phases and provided contractors a comprehensive picture of the utility conflicts and protection requirements within the construction corridor.
