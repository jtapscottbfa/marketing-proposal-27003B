Electrical engineering scope on the Dental Clinic Exhaust Fan Installation at Shaw Air Force Base was focused on providing power infrastructure for the new crawl space exhaust fan system at Building 1046. The electrical design provided a new power connection for exhaust fan EF-1, using THHN/THWN copper conductors sized for the fan load, with grounding specified to match existing facility systems in conformance with NFPA 70 (National Electrical Code).

Design was governed by UFC 3-501-01 (Electrical Engineering) and UFC 3-520-01 (Interior Electrical Systems), within the broader military medical facility framework established by UFC 4-510-01. The electrical scope was coordinated with the mechanical scope: the exhaust fan controller integration with the existing building automation system (BAS) was carried under the mechanical design, with the electrical scope providing the power supply infrastructure to support the fan unit. No additional electrical systems, lighting design, low-voltage, or other electrical scope is recorded in available source documents.

The project's electrical scope was narrow and defined — a single new power feed for a single new piece of mechanical equipment — consistent with the targeted, corrective nature of the overall project, which addressed a specific crawl space pressurization deficiency identified in the Defense Health Agency (DHA) Infrastructure Condition Assessment Survey rather than a broader facility renovation.
