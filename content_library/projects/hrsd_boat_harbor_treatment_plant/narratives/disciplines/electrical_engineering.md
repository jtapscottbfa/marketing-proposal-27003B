The electrical engineering scope on the HRSD Boat Harbor Treatment Plant encompassed the complete power distribution design for this wastewater treatment facility in Norfolk, Virginia. The design team produced an electrical one-line diagram (Sheet E-601) documenting a new 1,250-ampere, 480-volt, three-phase main service from Dominion Energy — engineered at 65 kAIC fault interrupting capacity — feeding a fully coordinated multi-branch distribution system.

The distribution design branches into six dedicated 125A/480V pump feeders (Pumps 1–6), a 400A/480V dewatering feeder, two 300A/480V crane circuits, a 125A NSDL feeder, and a 300A/480V North WLSI feeder. Two 225 kVA step-down transformers (480-to-208/120V) serve a 900-ampere inside facility panel and a 600-ampere north panel for building-level loads. Conductor sizing is specified throughout: the main service runs three sets of 600 KCMIL conductors with #1/0 AWG ground in four-inch conduit; branch circuits range from 350 KCMIL to #1/0 AWG. Sub-disciplines engaged include electrical power distribution and electrical motor control. The design firm served as prime consultant to Hampton Roads Sanitation District, with the engineer of record's professional seal on the drawing issued September 2023 (project 23-011A).
