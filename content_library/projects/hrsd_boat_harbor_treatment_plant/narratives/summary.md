The HRSD Boat Harbor Treatment Plant project provided electrical engineering design services for Hampton Roads Sanitation District's wastewater treatment facility in Norfolk, Virginia. The design firm served as prime consultant, delivering a complete electrical power distribution design documented as project 23-011A, with the engineer of record's drawings issued in September 2023.

The project's primary electrical scope centered on a new 1,250-ampere, 480-volt, three-phase main service from Dominion Energy, engineered at 65 kAIC fault interrupting capacity, to support the treatment plant's full operational load. The power distribution system branches into dedicated circuits serving six wastewater pumps (each at 125A/480V), a dewatering system (400A/480V), two crane hoist circuits (each 300A/480V), and a North WLSI feeder (300A/480V). Facility lighting and convenience loads are served downstream of two 225 kVA step-down transformers providing 208/120-volt power to a 900-ampere inside load panel and a 600-ampere north panel. Conductor sizing is fully specified throughout, with the main service running three sets of 600 KCMIL conductors with #1/0 AWG ground in four-inch conduit.

This was a single-discipline electrical engagement delivered via design-bid-build procurement. Source documentation captured in this record consists of the issued electrical one-line diagram (Sheet E-601). Financial and schedule details are not available in the source record.
