The firm served as prime consultant to the National Institute of Building Sciences (NIBS), administering on behalf of Naval Facilities Engineering Systems Command (NAVFAC) Atlantic, to develop an updated tri-service Unified Facilities Guide Specification (UFGS) for Terminal Heating Units under IDIQ contract N6247019D5013. The engagement consolidated three previously independent service-branch specifications — UFGS-23 80 20.00 10 (Navy, Gas-Fired Heating Equipment), UFGS-23 82 00.00 20 (Army, Terminal Heating Units), and UFGS-23 83 00.00 20 (Army, Electric Space Heating Equipment) — into a single unified document, UFGS 23 82 00.

Deliverables included the revised 28-page tri-service specification, an 8-page cross-reference comparison matrix cataloging the alignment of all source documents, Criteria Change Request (CCR) resolutions addressing outstanding technical issues including updates to reference standards for radiant heating equipment, and facilitated multi-service review meetings. The final specification was delivered in September 2025 and serves as a design and construction reference for facility designers and contractors across all three military services. The project was completed under a qualifications-based, federally procured A-E task order with the firm acting as prime consultant.
