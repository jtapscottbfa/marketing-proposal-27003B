Specifications writing was the primary and defining discipline for this engagement. The task required developing, updating, and unifying three existing DoD Unified Facilities Guide Specifications into a single tri-service document — UFGS 23 82 00 for Terminal Heating Units — suitable for use by facility designers and contractors across Army, Navy, and Air Force projects.

Core deliverables included authoring the revised 28-page unified specification in Word/SpecsIntact format; preparing an 8-page cross-reference comparison matrix cataloging the source and disposition of every provision across the four specification versions under consideration (UFGS 23 82 00, UFGS-23 80 20.00 10, UFGS-23 82 00.00 20, and UFGS-23 83 00.00 20); preparing Criteria Change Request (CCR) resolutions addressing outstanding technical issues in the existing criteria; and supporting facilitated review meetings to coordinate multi-service approval of the revised document.

This work required both technical knowledge of the subject matter — terminal heating units and the HVAC and controls systems they integrate with — and detailed familiarity with the UFGS specification format and the DoD criteria development and CCR process. The final specification was delivered in September 2025 under NAVFAC IDIQ contract N6247019D5013, administered through the National Institute of Building Sciences (NIBS) on behalf of NAVFAC Atlantic.
