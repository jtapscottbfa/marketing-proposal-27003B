The architectural scope covered interior renewal of the 1,863-square-foot ER waiting room within Building 2 of Naval Medical Center Portsmouth, and design criteria for three priced options — none of which were exercised.

Demolition in the base scope included removal of existing ceramic tile and carpet flooring, the full acoustic tile ceiling system (including grid, tile, and 6-inch fiberglass batt insulation above the grid), three existing doors (one 3-foot single-leaf and two 9-foot automatic sliding units), and all wall-mounted items. New construction included vinyl composition tile (VCT) resilient flooring over approximately 1,863 square feet with 4-inch resilient base; a new 2×2 acoustic ceiling tile grid with new insulation; 5/8-inch Type X gypsum board on 20-gauge steel studs at 16 inches on center for any new partitions; paint (prime plus two coats); one 4-foot 0-inch by 7-foot 0-inch wood interior door at triage; two trackless automatic aluminum sliding glass doors serving the corridor-to-waiting and waiting-to-ER transitions; and new seating furniture.

The three priced options specified in the design criteria were not exercised: Option 1 (Milk Technology Room) included new partition, sheet rubber flooring, ACT ceiling, wood door, sliding glass window, and copper-infused solid surface countertops and casework. Option 2 (GME Building 3) addressed approximately 1,385 square yards of carpet and 167 square yards of VCT replacement, 22,548 square feet of drywall refinish and paint, and 2,503 linear feet of cove base. Option 3 (ENT Clinic, Building 2 second deck) covered approximately 9,733 square feet of patient room refresh including patch/paint, Acrovyn wall protection, 25 door refinishes, ceiling replacement, and flooring replacement.
