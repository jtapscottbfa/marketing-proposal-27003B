Structural engineering for the ERC Inspection Station #2 Replacement was performed by subconsultant Broyles and Associates, PC, coordinating under the prime A/E team. The structural scope addressed foundation design for a pre-manufactured Porta-King Model RR-PC167SW building — a single-story, approximately 304 square-foot structure. The foundation consists of a cast-in-place concrete slab-on-grade measuring 24 feet by 15 feet (out-to-out), 6 inches thick, reinforced with #4 bars at 12 inches on center each way over a 15-mil vapor barrier and 4 inches of porous fill. The slab includes a turned-down perimeter edge footing 12 inches wide by 30 inches deep with two #5 continuous bars. All concrete is specified at 4,500 PSI. Design was performed in accordance with the 2021 Virginia Construction Code and IBC 2021, with a floor live load of 60 PSF, roof live load of 20 PSF, ground snow load of 35 PSF, and an ultimate wind speed of 120 MPH (Exposure C, Risk Category II). Allowable soil bearing pressure was set at 1,500 PSF; structural fill was specified to be compacted to 95% per ASTM D698. The interface between the Porta-King manufacturer's C4x5.4 steel channel base frame and the concrete slab was resolved through manufacturer-supplied anchor tabs and half-inch galvanized adhesive anchors (Hilti HY200 or equal) at 4-inch embedment. Special inspections were required per IBC §1704/1705 for earthwork, concrete reinforcing and placement, and post-installed adhesive anchors. Sub-disciplines represented include structural foundation design.
