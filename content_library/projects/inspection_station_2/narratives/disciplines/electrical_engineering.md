Electrical engineering scope for the ERC Inspection Station #2 Replacement encompassed demolition of the existing inspection station's electrical systems and full fit-out of the replacement pre-manufactured building. The new building is served by a 100-ampere, three-phase, four-wire electrical panel (Panel A, 120/208V) fed from the existing ERC tunnel electrical infrastructure at Cabinet 264-13 via two new 2-inch conduits — one carrying power and control wiring, one spare for future use. Interior electrical systems include fluorescent and emergency lighting fixtures, 20-ampere duplex convenience receptacles, single-pole switches (120/277V), and LAN system outlets. CCTV cameras previously located at the existing ERC field station were relocated and integrated into the new building, with weatherproof junction boxes for exterior-mounted devices; all camera and technology wiring was coordinated with the existing ERC control infrastructure. VDOT signage was relocated to the new building exterior as part of the electrical and site work scope. A separate 480V circuit feeds a 1-HP, 120/240V single-phase submersible pump for the new sanitary lift station, run below grade from the existing electrical manhole. The demolition scope included removal of the existing 100A load center, all lighting fixtures, control station, mechanical equipment connections, and existing conduit and wiring. A key operational constraint — that the existing inspection station remain energized and active until all utilities were transferred to the new building — sequenced the electrical transition work and governed the timing of demolition activities. Sub-disciplines represented include electrical power distribution and electrical lighting.
