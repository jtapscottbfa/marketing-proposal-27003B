Plumbing engineering for the ERC Inspection Station #2 Replacement introduced domestic water and sanitary drainage systems to a facility that previously had no plumbing service. The existing inspection station lacked any plumbing infrastructure; the replacement building includes a water heater and domestic water supply, representing a new programmatic capability for the facility. Sanitary drainage from the building is handled by a 1-HP submersible pump lift station (120/240V, single-phase), with a below-grade pump circuit routed from the existing ERC electrical manhole (Cabinet 264-13) to serve the lift station. The addition of a plumbing lift station required cross-discipline coordination between plumbing and electrical engineering to route and size the pump circuit, specify the junction box configuration, and ensure the circuit was incorporated into the site electrical plan. Domestic water supply and the lift station represent the full plumbing scope for this small, single-story facility. Sub-disciplines represented include plumbing domestic water supply and plumbing sanitary/DWV design. The introduction of plumbing where none previously existed is a meaningful scope upgrade for the replacement facility, enabling improved operational functionality for ERC inspection personnel at this tunnel access point.
