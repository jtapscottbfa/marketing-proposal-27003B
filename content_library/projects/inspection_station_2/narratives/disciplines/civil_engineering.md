Civil engineering scope on the ERC Inspection Station #2 Replacement covered site design, grading, drainage, and utility coordination for the replacement facility at the Portsmouth entrance of the Downtown Tunnel in Portsmouth, Virginia. The civil design addressed demolition of existing site utilities associated with the aging inspection station and relocation of utilities serving the new pre-manufactured building footprint. Site paving and grading work was integrated with the replacement building's foundation plan, including surface drainage designed to accommodate the new slab and surrounding site improvements. Utility scope included coordination with the existing ERC tunnel electrical infrastructure for service relocation and new conduit routing — two 2-inch conduits routed from the existing electrical manhole (Cabinet 264-13) to the new building. Survey services for the project were provided by subconsultant Hoggard/Eure Associates, P.C. Civil drawings were produced as part of the 100% design submittal package supporting the design-bid-build construction procurement issued by Elizabeth River Crossings OpCo, LLC. Sub-disciplines represented on this project include civil site design, civil grading and drainage, and civil utilities.
