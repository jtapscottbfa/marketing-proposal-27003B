Mechanical engineering scope on the ERC Inspection Station #2 Replacement addressed HVAC design for the replacement pre-manufactured building. The Porta-King Model RR-PC167SW building incorporates factory-integrated mechanical components; the mechanical scope encompassed specification and coordination of the HVAC systems provided as part of the pre-manufactured building package. The replacement facility is equipped with a wall-mounted HVAC unit, a chase heater, and a wall-mounted heater to provide year-round climate control within the approximately 304 square-foot single-story building. The pre-manufactured building's factory-supplied HVAC package required coordination with the site electrical service for power connections and with the building specifications to establish performance requirements. No dedicated mechanical drawing sheets appear in the issued drawing set; mechanical systems are addressed through the architectural floor plan (sheet A-101) in conjunction with the pre-manufactured building manufacturer's documentation. The building HVAC system is classified as a packaged system, appropriate to the small footprint and operational nature of the inspection facility. Sub-disciplines represented include HVAC design; the installed building system is a packaged HVAC configuration. Where scope delineation between BFA-designed MEP and manufacturer-supplied MEP was not fully explicit in the drawing set, this reflects the nature of pre-engineered building procurement for a project of this scale.
