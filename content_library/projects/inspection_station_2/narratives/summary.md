Bowman, Foster & Associates, Inc. provided full A/E design services as prime consultant and engineer of record for Elizabeth River Crossings OpCo, LLC (ERC) on the replacement of Inspection Station #2, a vehicle inspection facility at the Portsmouth entrance of the Downtown Tunnel in Portsmouth, Virginia. The project involved demolishing the aging existing station and replacing it with a pre-manufactured Porta-King Model RR-PC167SW building — approximately 304 square feet, single story — on a new cast-in-place concrete slab-on-grade foundation. Design scope spanned civil site work, structural foundation engineering, and full MEP systems fit-out, including a new 100-ampere three-phase electrical service tapped from the existing tunnel electrical infrastructure, HVAC equipment, and a new plumbing system (domestic water and submersible pump lift station) not present in the previous facility. Existing ERC CCTV cameras and VDOT signage were relocated to the new building as part of the scope. A critical project constraint required the existing inspection station to remain fully operational throughout construction until utilities were transferred and commissioned in the new building. Bowman, Foster & Associates led the multi-disciplinary design team, coordinating with subconsultants Broyles and Associates, PC (structural engineering) and Hoggard/Eure Associates, P.C. (surveying). The design was delivered as a complete construction bid package for design-bid-build procurement, with final drawings issued March 2026.
