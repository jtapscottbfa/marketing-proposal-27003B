Civil engineering scope on this project supported the installation of two diesel backup generators at the Southwest Virginia RRC campus in Abingdon, Virginia, spanning the two adjacent parcels at 26460 and 26502 Newbanks Road.

The civil scope encompassed site design and utility work associated with routing underground electrical ductbank and conduit from each generator to the existing electrical service infrastructure. This work required trenching, earthwork, and site grading coordination across the campus, with subsurface utility conditions informing conduit routing decisions. GPRS utility scan maps covering the 26502 Newbanks Road parcel (dated June 2021) were included as reference documents in the project package, providing pre-existing underground utility context for the civil site design.
