Plumbing engineering was the primary engineering discipline on the Lynnhaven Apartments renovation, a three-story multifamily residential building renewal in Virginia Beach, Virginia. The design team delivered a complete new domestic water, sanitary, and waste-and-vent system for the full building, with construction documents developed to the pre-final submittal level representing the second round of review responses.

Domestic cold water (¾-inch distribution throughout units, ½-inch branch to kitchen) and hot water piping were designed for all units. Electric water heaters were selected, sized, and scheduled by unit type: 47-gallon, 4,500W/240V units (EWH-1) for one-bedroom apartments and 66-gallon, 1,500W/240V units (EWH-2) for two-bedroom apartments. Each installation included expansion tank provisions (diaphragm type, 100 PSIG maximum), dielectric unions, T&P relief valve, and heat trap connections. Sanitary waste and vent (DWV) systems were designed as new throughout all units, connecting to existing underslab piping. Vent-through-roof details addressed shingle-integrated flashing with stainless-steel draw band connections.

Per-unit fixture connections included water closets, lavatories, kitchen sinks, shower and tub combinations with mixing valves, washer connection boxes (¾-inch CW/HW supply with 2-inch standpipe drain), ice maker connections, and garbage disposals (1/3 HP, 120V). The plumbing design was developed with dedicated typical unit plans for each unit type, providing construction-ready documentation for both one-bedroom and two-bedroom configurations. Design was compliant with the International Plumbing Code (IPC) and applicable state building codes.
