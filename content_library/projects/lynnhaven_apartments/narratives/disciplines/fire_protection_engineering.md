Fire protection engineering on the Lynnhaven Apartments renovation addressed two distinct scopes for the three-story multifamily residential building in Virginia Beach, Virginia: wet fire suppression system design and fire-resistive penetration sealing for all rated assembly penetrations.

For fire suppression, a wet sprinkler system was designed for the full three-floor building. Dedicated sprinkler rooms were proposed at each end of the building per the architectural floor plans, providing the system infrastructure to serve the multi-unit residential occupancy across all three floors.

For penetration sealing, the engineering team produced a comprehensive suite of UL-listed through-penetration firestop details for all plumbing penetrations through rated floor-ceiling and wall assemblies. Six UL-listed assemblies were detailed and specified: floor-ceiling assemblies F-C-1010, F-C-1074, and F-C-2032 addressing metallic pipe and conduit penetrations through 1- to 2-hour rated floor-ceiling constructions; and wall assemblies F-C-2157, W-L-1040, and W-L-2241 addressing PVC, CPVC, ABS, flexible metal conduit, and metallic pipe penetrations through rated wall constructions. Through-penetrant materials, T-ratings, F-ratings, and approved sealing products were tabulated for each assembly and delivered as dedicated construction document sheets (P5.1 and P5.2), providing contractors with clear installation guidance for every penetration condition in the building.

The fire protection scope reflects experience integrating wet suppression design with comprehensive penetration sealing documentation in multifamily residential renovation construction.
