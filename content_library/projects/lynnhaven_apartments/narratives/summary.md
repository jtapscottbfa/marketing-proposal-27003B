Lynnhaven Apartments is a three-story multifamily residential building renovation in Virginia Beach, Virginia. The engineering team served as MEP subconsultant to the prime architectural consultant, delivering plumbing, mechanical HVAC coordination, and fire protection engineering for the full building renewal. Scope encompassed complete new domestic water distribution — sized separately for one-bedroom and two-bedroom unit types — new sanitary waste and vent systems, and wet fire suppression design for the three-floor structure. Per-unit electric water heaters were specified and scheduled by unit type (47-gallon units for one-bedroom apartments, 66-gallon units for two-bedroom apartments), and HVAC air handling unit coordination was incorporated into the plumbing design, including condensate drainage routing to the sanitary system. The design was delivered in 2021 as a pre-final submittal responding to a second round of review comments, reflecting a thorough design review process.

Fire-resistive penetration sealing was detailed for all plumbing penetrations through rated floor-ceiling and wall assemblies, using six UL-listed through-penetration firestop assemblies covering metallic pipe, copper pipe, plastic pipe, and conduit penetrations. The project demonstrates experience in unit-by-unit MEP coordination for multifamily residential renovation under an architect-led team structure, with design compliant with the International Plumbing Code, ASHRAE standards, and applicable Virginia building codes.
