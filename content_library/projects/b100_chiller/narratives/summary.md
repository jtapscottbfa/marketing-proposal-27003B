# Building 100 Chiller Replacement — NAS Oceana

The firm provided full architect-engineer design services for the replacement of two aging rooftop air-cooled chillers at Building 100, Naval Air Station Oceana, Virginia Beach, Virginia. The project was executed under Contract No. N40085-20-D-0005, a NAVFAC Indefinite Quantity A-E contract for mechanical and electrical projects, with an estimated construction cost of $424,707.

The central challenge of the project was mission criticality: Building 100 houses radar equipment whose uninterrupted cooling is operationally essential. The existing two 30-ton backup chillers had been identified as unreliable through facility operator interviews; the design replaced both with a single, properly sized air-cooled chiller configured with absolute priority to protect the radar system whenever the primary chilled water supply from the Building 119 central plant is unavailable. The new chiller's controls were engineered for full integration with the facility's existing Trane Energy Savings Performance Contract system, and a crane access plan was developed to accommodate equipment placement adjacent to an active flight line.

Multi-discipline design scope spanned mechanical (chiller sizing, piping, controls integration), electrical (panel assessment, circuit demolition and replacement from Panel "PJ," emergency power coordination), structural (load verification of existing roof support framing and concrete piers, replacement of an out-of-compliance guardrail), and architectural (rooftop membrane repair, wall penetration re-sealing). The project was procured as a Design-Bid-Build package under an SBA 8(a) set-aside and was completed within a 150-day design schedule following task order award.
