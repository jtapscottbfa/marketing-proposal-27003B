# Electrical Engineering — Building 100 Chiller Replacement, NAS Oceana

The electrical scope required a thorough site investigation of the existing power distribution system serving the two backup chillers, followed by design of the demolition and replacement circuits for the new single chiller. Both existing Trane chillers (146 MCA, 200V, 3-phase) were fed from Panel "PJ" — a 400A MLO, 208Y/120V, 3-phase, 4-wire GE panelboard in mechanical room 207 — via 175A/3P circuit breakers and exterior-mounted Square D 200A/3P NEMA 3R disconnect switches.

The electrical design demolished all conductors, circuit breakers, and disconnect switches associated with both existing chillers back to Panel "PJ," and provided a new circuit, circuit breaker, disconnect switch, and conductors for the replacement chiller. A critical design judgment was required regarding Panel "PJ" itself: given its age, the design assessed whether the existing panelboard remained capable of safely accepting new circuit breakers, or whether full replacement was warranted.

Coordination with the government was required for any work intersecting the first-floor main electrical distribution room, where the building's service entrance switchgear, an Onan Bypass Transfer Switch, and an adjacent emergency generator provide building-wide emergency power backup — systems involved in a concurrent government project. The chilled water pumps (Pump #11 and #12) in the second-floor mechanical room, fed from Panel "LV4" (100A MLO, 208Y/120V, Cutler Hammer), were also documented as part of the electrical survey.
