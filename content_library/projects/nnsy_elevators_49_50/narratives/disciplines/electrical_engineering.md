Electrical engineering services provided new power distribution, lighting, and communications infrastructure for two elevator machine rooms and associated hoistways in Building 276 at the Norfolk Naval Shipyard.

For Elevator #49, a new 3-phase/200-ampere circuit breaker was installed in existing Switchboard CL (480Y/277V), feeding a new 30 kVA, 480-208Y/120V dry-type transformer and a new 100A, 208Y/120V, three-phase, four-wire panelboard installed in the machine room to provide dedicated power for the elevator system and associated equipment. For Elevator #50, the existing 3-phase/200-ampere breaker in Switchboard CL was reused for the new elevator feeder, reducing scope while meeting the power requirements of the replacement equipment. Fused elevator control switches with integral shunt trip relays were provided for both elevators, with actuation coordinated with the fire protection waterflow switches to ensure automatic power disconnection upon sprinkler system flow.

Lighting throughout each machine room, hoistway, and elevator cab was upgraded to LED, providing energy-efficient, low-maintenance illumination. GFCI duplex receptacles were installed at machine room, pit, and top-of-hoistway locations for maintenance access. Emergency communications — a two-way intercom system with a master station in each machine room and a lobby station at the first floor — were provided to meet code requirements for elevator emergency communications in both elevators.
