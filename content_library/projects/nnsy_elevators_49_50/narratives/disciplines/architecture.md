Architecture services on this project encompassed elevator lobby alterations, interior finishes, and overall document coordination for the replacement of two freight elevators in Building 276 at the Norfolk Naval Shipyard. Building 276 is an approximately 544,000-square-foot industrial warehouse originally constructed in 1942 and designated a contributing historic property within the NNSY historic district — a classification that imposed coordination requirements beyond those of a standard elevator replacement project.

For Elevator #49, SHPO consultation was required in connection with exterior alterations (a stair addition and window replacement) associated with the replacement scope. Design solutions were developed to satisfy NAVFAC operational requirements while addressing the Secretary of the Interior's standards applicable to work on a contributing historic property. No SHPO coordination was required for Elevator #50, which involved no exterior alterations.

Architectural scope across both elevators included elevator lobby modifications, interior finishes within the lobbies and machine rooms, miscellaneous metals, and coordination of hazardous materials abatement sequencing with demolition and construction activities. Architecture led multi-discipline document coordination to produce separate Final Design Submittals for Elevator #49 and Elevator #50 in August 2022 under a NAVFAC IDIQ task order (Contract N40085-20-D0005).
