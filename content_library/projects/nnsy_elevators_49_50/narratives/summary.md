The design team provided architect-engineer services for the replacement of two freight elevators — Elevator #49 and Elevator #50 — in Building 276 at the Norfolk Naval Shipyard (NNSY) in Portsmouth, Virginia. The firm served as prime consultant under a NAVFAC IDIQ task order (Contract N40085-20-D0005), administered by NAVFAC PWD Portsmouth, delivering Final Design Submittals for both elevators in August 2022. The combined estimated construction cost is approximately $3.2 million ($1,660,000 for Elevator #49; $1,560,000 for Elevator #50), with estimated construction completion in December 2023.

Building 276 is an approximately 544,000-square-foot industrial warehouse originally constructed in 1942 and listed as a contributing historic property within the NNSY historic district. The project replaced both four-stop freight elevators and all associated systems — mechanical, electrical, plumbing, fire protection, and finishes — within each hoistway, machine room, and elevator lobby. A 3D laser scan survey of the elevator hoistways documented as-built dimensional conditions prior to design, addressing the out-of-plumb masonry walls typical of 1940s-era concrete construction. Hazardous materials assessment and abatement design were incorporated into the scope in advance of demolition activities. State Historic Preservation Office (SHPO) coordination was required for Elevator #49 in connection with exterior alterations on the contributing historic property. HVAC and fire alarm systems were designed for integration with the NNSY base-wide building automation and fire alarm infrastructure.
