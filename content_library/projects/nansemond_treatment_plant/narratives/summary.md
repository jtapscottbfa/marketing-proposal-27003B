The HRSD Nansemond Treatment Plant Struvite Recovery Facility Improvements and Digester Capacity Upgrade is a wastewater treatment infrastructure project for Hampton Roads Sanitation District (HRSD) at the Nansemond Treatment Plant in Suffolk, Virginia. The project addressed two interrelated facility modernization goals: improvements to the struvite recovery facility and an upgrade to the plant's digester capacity — work characteristic of a regional wastewater utility investing in resource recovery and treatment throughput.

The documented scope includes electrical engineering design for power distribution serving six scrubber exhaust fan motors associated with the struvite recovery facility improvements. Each fan feeder — designated Fans 1-A, 1-B, 2-A, 2-B, 3-A, and 3-B — was designed for 150-ampere service at 480 volts, three-phase, using 1/0 AWG copper THHN conductors in 2-inch PVC Schedule 80 conduit in accordance with specification section 26 05 19. Feeder run lengths range from 260 to 320 feet. The two longest circuits — 305 feet for Fan 2-B and 320 feet for Fan 3-B — required dedicated voltage-drop analysis, which confirmed compliant results of 1.93% and 2.02%, both below the NEC 3% threshold. All six circuits were designed at the same conductor size without upsizing. Engineering calculations were produced using Southwire SIMpull tools, sealed, and submitted as design deliverables.
