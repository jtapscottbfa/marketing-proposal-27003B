Electrical engineering services on the HRSD Nansemond Treatment Plant Struvite Recovery Facility Improvements and Digester Capacity Upgrade project encompassed the design of power distribution feeders for six scrubber exhaust fan motors associated with the struvite recovery facility improvements. The electrical scope covered motor feeder design — conductor sizing, conduit system, voltage drop compliance, and specification — for all six fans (Fans 1-A, 1-B, 2-A, 2-B, 3-A, and 3-B).

Each feeder was designed for 150-ampere service at 480 volts, three-phase. Conductors were sized as 1/0 AWG copper THHN in 2-inch PVC Schedule 80 conduit, conforming to specification section 26 05 19 (low-voltage cable). Feeder lengths range from 260 to 320 feet. For the two longest circuits — 305 feet for Fan 2-B and 320 feet for Fan 3-B — dedicated voltage-drop analyses were performed using Southwire SIMpull tools, confirming compliant drops of 1.93% and 2.02% against the NEC 3% limit. The standard conductor size was maintained across all six feeders without upsizing. Cable-pull and voltage-drop calculations were sealed and submitted as design deliverables.
