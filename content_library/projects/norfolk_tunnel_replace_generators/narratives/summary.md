The ERC Downtown Tunnel (DTT) Norfolk East Vent Building Emergency Generator Replacement is a multi-discipline engineering design project for Elizabeth River Crossings (ERC), the private concessionaire operating the Downtown Tunnel under Norfolk Harbor. The project replaces an aging 400 kW generator inside the East Vent Building's first floor with a new Caterpillar 3512C 1500 ekW / 1875 kVA diesel generator set — mounted on a custom elevated structural steel support frame and reinforced concrete mat foundation immediately adjacent to the vent building at 114 Ligon St, Norfolk, Virginia. The generator is elevated to +15.30 ft to satisfy FEMA Flood Zone AE requirements and the City of Norfolk's Flood Plain and Coastal Hazard Overlay, which sets a required design flood elevation of 12.0 ft on this sea-level-rise-vulnerable site.

The electrical scope includes a new 4000A main switchboard, an emergency distribution panel, and two ASCO Series 300 automatic transfer switches (800A and 400A) that restore emergency power to eight 100 HP tunnel jet fans — four per bore — ventilating the Downtown Tunnel. The structural scope provides a new elevated steel support frame bearing on a 2-ft thick mat foundation (~400 sf, penetrating to -12 ft below grade) designed to ASCE 7-2022 and ACI 318-2019 on a seismic site class D site. The civil scope includes site layout, underground electrical routing, survey coordination, and floodplain permitting. The design team served as prime consultant, coordinating structural and civil/survey subconsultants, and navigated the project through Virginia DGS/DEB multi-round review to a REFINAL submittal in March 2026.
