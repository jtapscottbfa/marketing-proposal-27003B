The electrical engineering scope for the ERC DTT Norfolk East Vent Building Emergency Generator Replacement centered on a near-4× capacity increase: replacing an aging 400 kW generator with a new Caterpillar 3512C diesel generator set rated at 1500 ekW / 1875 kVA (480Y/277V, 3-phase, 60 Hz, standby) and rebuilding the downstream emergency distribution architecture to match. The generator includes a sub-base diesel fuel tank providing 48 hours of runtime at 100% load — sufficient to sustain tunnel ventilation through an extended grid outage.

New distribution infrastructure includes a 4000A main switchboard, an emergency distribution panel (EMDP) inside the East Vent Building, and two ASCO Series 300 automatic transfer switches — one 800A/4P unit and one 400A/4P unit. Each tunnel jet fan circuit is served by an ATS with bypass-isolation capability, allowing maintenance access without interrupting emergency power availability. Eight 100 HP tunnel jet fans are served by the new system (four per bore, eastbound and westbound), providing ventilation redundancy for both bores of the Downtown Tunnel. New EMDP panels also feed the existing EMDP panel arrangement, preserving compatibility with the existing distribution architecture. The existing 400 kW generators are disconnected but remain in place. The electrical design was submitted through Virginia DGS/DEB review and reached REFINAL submittal status in March 2026, with electrical sheet coverage across the 12-sheet drawing set.
