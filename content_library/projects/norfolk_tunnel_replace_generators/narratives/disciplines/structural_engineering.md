The structural engineering scope for the ERC DTT Norfolk East Vent Building Emergency Generator Replacement addressed a concentrated design challenge: supporting a 115,000 lb Caterpillar 3512C generator set at an elevation of +15.30 ft on a FEMA Flood Zone AE site with a required design flood elevation of 12.0 ft. The elevated floor requirement — 7.3 ft above base flood elevation — drove the entire structural approach.

The solution is a purpose-designed elevated steel support frame comprising W8 wide-flange columns and beams, HSS 4×4 columns, and C12×20.7 channel stringers, with lateral stability provided by X-bracing in two bays. The frame bears on a 2-ft thick reinforced concrete mat foundation approximately 32 ft × 12.5 ft in plan (~400 sf), with the foundation bottom at -12.0 ft below existing grade. Geotechnical investigation (GeoEnvironmental Resources, Inc., project 110-7766) informed the foundation design. A galvanized steel bar-grating stair and landing provides code-compliant access to the generator platform, with handrails designed to 2021 VCC requirements. Seismic restraint vibration isolators are specified at the generator mounting points.

The design complies with ASCE 7-2022, ACI 318-2019, and AISC 360-2016 on a seismic site class D site. The structural engineer of record was Don Broyles, P.E. of Broyles and Associates, PC, serving as subconsultant to the prime consultant. The structural design was reviewed through multiple DGS/DEB cycles; the REFINAL submittal — including resolution of structural observation requirements and stair detailing per DEB reviewer comments — was accepted in March 2026.
