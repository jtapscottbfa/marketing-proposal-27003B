The civil engineering scope for the ERC DTT Norfolk East Vent Building Emergency Generator Replacement was primarily defined by the project site's flood hazard conditions. The site at 114 Ligon St, Norfolk, Virginia is located in FEMA Flood Zone AE with a base flood elevation (BFE) of 8.0 ft and is designated as a sea-level rise inundation area. The City of Norfolk's Flood Plain and Coastal Hazard Overlay (FPCH-O) imposes a required design flood elevation of 12.0 ft under Section 3.9.7 — setting the controlling elevation criterion for all new equipment and structures on the site.

The civil scope includes site planning for the new outdoor generator enclosure located adjacent to the existing vent building, routing of new underground electrical conduit, and coordination of a topographic site survey performed by Hoggard/Eure Associates. The generator platform elevation at +15.30 ft — 7.3 ft above BFE and 3.3 ft above the FPCH-O required elevation — is the central civil compliance strategy. The site plan (sheet C-101) documents the National Flood Hazard Layer FIRMette, identifies flood zone boundaries, and establishes the relationship between the new generator slab, the existing building footprint, and the underground electrical routing.

Floodplain compliance documentation was submitted to and approved by the City of Norfolk Floodplain Administrator prior to the REFINAL submittal. The Administrator's compliance letter (Tristian Barnes, March 5, 2026) confirms the project satisfies FPCH-O requirements. The civil design was reviewed through the DGS/DEB process by structural and civil reviewer John R. Swecker, P.E., with all floodplain and civil comments resolved in the REFINAL submittal.
