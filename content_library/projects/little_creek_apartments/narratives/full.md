## Scope

**Demolition.** The demolition scope directed complete removal of all existing electrical infrastructure at each building: the meter center and all associated conduit, wiring, and devices were to be disconnected and removed back to the utility source; all existing distribution panels and all associated branch circuit wiring and devices were similarly removed. **New electrical service and distribution.** The new work design provided a utility-connected meter center at each building, connected to the existing Dominion Power service. Across both buildings, the design provides four panels total.
