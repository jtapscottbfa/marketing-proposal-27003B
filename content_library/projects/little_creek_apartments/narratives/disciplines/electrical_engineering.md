The electrical engineering scope for 1301 & 1307 W Little Creek Apartments encompassed a complete demolition-and-replacement of all electrical infrastructure across two 2-story residential apartment buildings in Norfolk, Virginia, for owner Coluzos Properties. The design firm served as prime consultant, and the construction documents are electrical discipline only.

The demolition design directed full removal of each building's existing meter center and all associated conduit, wiring, and devices back to the utility source, as well as complete removal of all existing distribution panels and branch circuit wiring. New work provided a utility-connected meter center at each building (fed from the existing Dominion Power service), with wire-chase feeder distribution — two #1/0 conductors and #6 ground, typical — to four new 125-ampere, 120/240-volt single-phase panels (two per building, one per floor). Each panel is a recessed-mounted Homeline HOM2448M125-PC-HOM INGR unit with a 125-ampere main circuit breaker and 10 kAIC minimum interrupting capacity. Branch circuit design addresses standard residential unit loads including convenience and GFCI receptacles, switches, flush-mount lighting, and dedicated appliance circuits for dryer (30A/240V), electric range (50A), dishwasher (10A), washing machine (10A), and microwave (8A). Existing exterior lighting was retained without modification. The design is governed by NEC, NFPA, and Virginia Uniform Statewide Building Code requirements. Construction documents were issued June 2020.
