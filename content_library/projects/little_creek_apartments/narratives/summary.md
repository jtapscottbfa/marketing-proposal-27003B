1301 & 1307 W Little Creek Apartments is a residential multifamily electrical renovation project located in Norfolk, Virginia, completed for private owner Coluzos Properties. The project encompassed two 2-story apartment buildings at separate addresses on W Little Creek Road and involved a complete replacement of each building's electrical systems — from the utility service point through all distribution panels, feeder wiring, branch circuits, and unit-level devices.

The demolition scope directed full removal of the existing meter centers, all associated conduit, wiring, and devices back to the utility source, and all existing distribution panels and branch circuit wiring at both buildings. The new design provided a utility-connected meter center at each building fed from the existing Dominion Power service, with wire-chase feeder distribution to four new 125-ampere, 120/240-volt single-phase panels — one per floor per building. Each dwelling unit received new convenience and GFCI receptacles, switches, and flush-mount lighting, with dedicated appliance circuits for standard residential loads including dryer (30A/240V), electric range (50A), dishwasher, washing machine, and microwave. Existing exterior lighting at both buildings was retained. The design conforms to NEC, NFPA, and Virginia Uniform Statewide Building Code requirements. Construction documents were issued in June 2020 under BFA project number 20-049, with the design firm serving as prime consultant for the electrical engineering scope.
