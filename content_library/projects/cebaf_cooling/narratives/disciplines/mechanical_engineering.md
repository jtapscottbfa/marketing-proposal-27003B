Mechanical engineering scope for the CEBAF Center F-Wing supplemental cooling project encompassed the complete HVAC design for two electronics-intensive laboratory rooms (F109 and F110) at Jefferson Lab, Newport News, Virginia.

The mechanical design specifies two Daikin 18 MBH wall-mounted indoor split-system air handlers (model FTXM18VVJU) — one per room — paired with two outdoor air-cooled condensing units (model RXM18VVJU) mounted on new concrete pads at the exterior of the F-Wing. Equipment was sized to the owner's defined cooling loads with an additional 10 percent capacity margin. Condensate drainage is provided via 3/4-inch PVC condensate drain piping from each indoor unit.

As part of the installation, the mechanical design includes removal of an existing portable cooling unit and associated ductwork in Room F110, with ceiling tile restoration at the penetration location.

The new split systems are integrated into the facility's existing BACnet DDC network (Johnson Controls platform). New thermostats for each room are coordinated 2°F above the existing base-HVAC thermostat set points — new units set at 75°F against existing at 73°F — establishing control hierarchy to prevent contention between supplemental and primary HVAC. The design includes full equipment schedules, refrigerant piping (suction and liquid line sets routed through exterior wall penetrations), and DDC controls interface specifications. Mechanical drawings cover demolition, new work plan, details, and equipment/controls schedules across five sheets of the eight-sheet construction document set.
