Under Task Order #5 of master contract JSA 21-C0683, the design team provided supplemental cooling engineering for Rooms 109 and 110 of the F-Wing, CEBAF Center (Building 12), Thomas Jefferson National Accelerator Facility (Jefferson Lab), Newport News, Virginia — a DOE Office of Science national laboratory operated by Jefferson Science Associates (JSA). The rooms house sensitive electronics requiring climate control beyond what the existing building HVAC could sustain.

The scope of work comprised full design services — mechanical and electrical — for the installation of two Daikin 18 MBH wall-mounted split-system air handlers (one per room) paired with two outdoor air-cooled condensing units mounted on new concrete pads outside the F-Wing. Equipment was sized to the owner's scope-of-work with an additional 10 percent capacity margin. An existing portable cooling unit in Room 110 was removed as part of the work. New split systems were integrated into the facility's existing BACnet DDC network (Johnson Controls), with thermostats coordinated 2°F above the existing set points to maintain room hierarchy. The electrical design added two new circuits to existing 225A panelboard RP1G and provided NEMA 4X outdoor disconnect switches at each condensing unit.

The design team served as prime consultant, delivering 60 percent design and 100 percent/final construction documents, a construction cost estimate totaling approximately $40,361, and full project specifications. The A-E fee was $7,915 against a $50,000 program budget. Final design was submitted April 10, 2023, with construction projected over an approximately eight-calendar-day window in May 2023.
