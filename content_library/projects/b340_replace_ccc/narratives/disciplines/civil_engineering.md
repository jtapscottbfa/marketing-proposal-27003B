# Civil Engineering — Building 340 Replace Closed Circuit Evaporative Cooler

BFA's civil engineering scope for the Building 340 project at Naval Air Station Oceana addressed site preparation, grading, and new site improvements within a tightly constrained 750 SF limit of disturbance (LOD) on the northeast side of the building, at the intersection of Tomcat Blvd and E Avenue.

The existing site consisted of a paved area bordered on two sides by building walls, with a grass area, a gated wood fence enclosure, and a sidewalk — with the existing deteriorated cooling unit installed on the pavement. The civil demolition scope included removal of the existing closed-circuit evaporative cooler equipment pad, existing concrete walk pavement, and the surrounding gated wood fence in its entirety. All demolished materials were required to be disposed of off-base.

New civil work was designed in accordance with UFC 3-201-01. The primary deliverable was a new elevated concrete utility pad/foundation designed to be a minimum of 6 inches above finish grade and a minimum of 6 inches larger than the cooler footprint on all sides, including the galvanized steel I-beam supports and all piping supports. Site grading was designed to provide positive drainage away from the building and equipment area, tying into existing drainage patterns, with a minimum concrete pad elevation of 18.00 NAVD 88 per the RFP. A new 50 LF, 7-foot high pressure-treated wood fence enclosure was specified for the equipment yard perimeter.

Areas disturbed by construction were specified for restoration with Bermuda grass sod to match existing turf species. Erosion and sediment control measures, including silt fence, were provided per the Virginia Erosion and Sediment Control Handbook (1992). The land disturbance of approximately 750 SF did not exceed 10,000 SF required for DEQ permit review, and additional impervious surface did not exceed 5,000 SF, so neither DEQ VPDES permit nor LID calculations were required.
