# Architecture — Building 340 Replace Closed Circuit Evaporative Cooler

Architecture discipline was on the design team for this project in a support role to the primary mechanical, structural, civil, and electrical scopes. BFA's architectural scope was specifically described in the Basis of Design as "in support of the mechanical scope of work."

The architectural work encompassed two primary tasks. First, the existing acoustical ceiling system in the affected mechanical equipment room areas was required to be removed to provide access to the ceiling space for execution of the mechanical and electrical scopes of work. BFA specified that the ceiling system be stored, cleaned, and reinstalled following completion of the work above — a sequencing requirement driven by the occupied status of Building 340 throughout construction.

Second, all existing wall openings remaining from Plumbing, Mechanical, and Electrical work that were not reused for new work were specified to be filled, patched, and painted to match adjacent construction and finishes. All new and reused pipe and conduit wall penetrations through exterior and interior walls were required to be sealed and flashed.

No new exterior construction was anticipated by the architectural scope. The applicable design criteria included UFC 1-200-01, UFC 3-101-01, UFC 3-190-06 (protective coatings and paints), IBC 2018, and ICC A117.1-2009 for accessibility. The architectural scope is modest in extent relative to the project's MEP and structural content; the primary project driver was equipment replacement and systems integration rather than building modification.
