# Building 340 Replace Closed Circuit Evaporative Cooler — Project Summary

Naval Air Station Oceana, Virginia Beach, Virginia | NAVFAC Mid-Atlantic | BFA Project No. 22-044

Bowman Foster & Associates (BFA) served as Designer of Record (DOR) under SYNCON LLC, the design-build prime contractor, for the replacement of a deteriorated closed-circuit evaporative cooler (CC-1) at Building 340 on Naval Air Station Oceana. The existing EVAPCO unit had deteriorated to an unusable state and required full replacement to restore the condenser water system that serves the building's water-source heat pump (WSHP) geothermal cooling system.

BFA provided complete multi-discipline engineering design services across mechanical, electrical, structural, civil, and architectural disciplines in compliance with NAVFAC Unified Facilities Criteria (UFC) and the design-build RFP issued by NAVFAC Mid-Atlantic (eProjects WO# 1655654). The new EVAPCO ESW4 9-43G18-SP-R closed-circuit cooler — rated at approximately 168 tons (2,524 MBH) at 524.8 GPM with 25% propylene glycol — was selected and designed to integrate seamlessly with the existing Trane DDC control system via BACnet. A custom two-level W12 structural steel support frame, supported on a new 2'-6" thick reinforced concrete mat foundation, elevated the cooler above the pad grade while addressing wind (124 mph Vult) and seismic loading. Civil site work encompassed demolition of the existing concrete pad and wood fence enclosure, installation of a new elevated equipment pad, site grading, and Bermuda grass restoration within a 750 SF limit of disturbance. Electrical scope included new power circuitry from the existing panelboard in Room 165 and re-lamping of mechanical room lighting fixtures. BFA completed the Final Interim design submittal in April 2023.
