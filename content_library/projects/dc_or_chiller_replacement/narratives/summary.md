The OR Chiller Replacement project at VA Medical Center, Washington D.C. (BFA Project No. 22-001 / VA Project No. 688-22-004) was a multi-scope design engagement performed as a Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small Business (SDVOSB) prime consultant for the Department of Veterans Affairs. The project combined two related scopes under one project number: replacement of an operating room chiller at the medical facility, and a comprehensive Roof Fall Protection Design Analysis covering Buildings 1 and 6 of the VAMC campus. The Final Design Analysis was submitted March 10, 2025.

The substantive documented scope — the Roof Fall Protection Design Analysis — identified all locations along the Building 1 and Building 6 roof areas where fall protection was required, evaluated three roof membrane systems present across the campus (ballasted aggregate, TPO single-ply, and EPDM synthetic rubber), and confirmed through structural assessment that existing roof structures consist of 10-inch reinforced concrete slabs with calculated allowable live loads exceeding 100 pounds per square foot. The analysis quantified approximately 7,300 linear feet of required guardrail and produced general fall protection construction documents for use by subsequent designers and contractors. The work was performed in accordance with IBC 2024, OSHA Standard 1926 / 29 CFR, VA Technical Information Library standards, and ASCE 7-22. The OR chiller replacement scope was carried under the same project number; detailed technical documentation for that scope is not reflected in the source documents available for this record.
