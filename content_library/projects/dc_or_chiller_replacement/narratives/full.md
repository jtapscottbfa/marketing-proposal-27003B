## Project Overview

The OR Chiller Replacement project at VA Medical Center, Washington D.C. (BFA Project No. 22-001; VA Project No. 688-22-004) was a multi-scope design engagement executed for the Department of Veterans Affairs under a Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small Business (SDVOSB) procurement. The project encompassed two related scopes under a single project number: replacement of an operating room chiller at the VAMC Washington D.C. facility, and a comprehensive Roof Fall Protection Design Analysis covering Buildings 1 and 6 of the medical campus. The Final Design Analysis was submitted March 10, 2025. The firm served as prime consultant throughout.

## Scope

**Roof Fall Protection Design Analysis.** The fall protection analysis identified all locations along the Building 1 and Building 6 roof areas requiring guardrail installation, consistent with IBC Section 1015 guardrail requirements, OSHA Standard 1926 / 29 CFR, VA Technical Information Library (TIL) criteria, and ASCE 7-22 minimum design loads. The scope also produced general fall protection construction document sheets for use by subsequent designers and contractors performing work near fall-risk areas within the Building 1 and Building 6 roof extents. Three distinct roof membrane systems were documented and evaluated across the scope area: ballasted aggregate systems (weighted rock or pebble on felt fabric), TPO (thermoplastic polyolefin) single-ply white membrane, and EPDM (ethylene propylene diene terpolymer) synthetic rubber membrane. The total linear length of required railing was determined to be approximately 7,300 linear feet, distributed across a complex, multi-wing hospital roof plan with approximately 15 or more distinct numbered railing zones and two identified crane staging areas for installation access.

**OR Chiller Replacement.** The operating room chiller replacement scope, which is the primary named scope of this project, was included under the same project number and VA project number. This scope is classified under hvac_central_plant and hvac_chilled_water building systems, consistent with an operating room cooling plant replacement at a major medical facility. Detailed technical content for this scope — equipment selection, system capacity, existing plant description, piping configuration — is not captured in the source documents available for this record.

## Role

The firm served as prime consultant for both scopes of the engagement, providing structural engineering services for the fall protection design analysis and mechanical engineering services for the OR chiller replacement scope. The project was procured as an SDVOSB set-aside by the Department of Veterans Affairs under VA Project No. 688-22-004.

## Key Challenges and Resolutions

**Existing structural assessment across a complex, multi-system roof.** Building 1 and Building 6 at VAMC Washington D.C. present an extensive, irregular, multi-wing hospital roof plan with three different membrane system types coexisting across the scope area. A key deliverable of the fall protection analysis was confirming whether the existing roof structure could support railing installation loads. Structural assessment found that all roof structures within the scope area are constructed using 10-inch thick reinforced concrete slabs — likely originally designed for future vertical expansion — with calculated allowable live loads exceeding 100 pounds per square foot. This finding established that the structural system could support required fall protection railing installations without modification across the full approximately 7,300 linear feet of scope.

**Systematic fall protection mapping across a large hospital campus.** The VAMC Washington D.C. campus presents a large, irregular, multi-wing complex with extensive flat roofing across Buildings 1 and 6 and varied existing conditions including rooftop mechanical equipment platforms and active roof edge exposures. The analysis systematically mapped the full scope area into numbered railing zones, identified crane staging locations for railing installation access, and produced construction document sheets suitable for use by subsequent designers and contractors. The work documented existing fall hazards at roof edges — including multi-story drop conditions — that existing conditions had addressed only with temporary measures.

## Outcomes

The project delivered a Final Design Analysis (submitted March 10, 2025) establishing the complete fall protection requirements for Buildings 1 and 6 at VAMC Washington D.C., quantifying approximately 7,300 linear feet of required guardrail and documenting the structural basis for railing system installation across three distinct roof membrane system types. The fall protection construction documents provide a procurement and installation basis for subsequent contractor work. Design was complete at the time of record capture. The companion OR chiller replacement design scope was progressed under the same project number; detailed outcomes for that scope are not reflected in the source documents available for this record.
