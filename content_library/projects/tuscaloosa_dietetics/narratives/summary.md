The design team provided architect-engineer (A/E) design services for replacement of the chilled water system in Building 143 (Dietetics) at the Tuscaloosa VA Medical Center (VAMC), Tuscaloosa, Alabama. The project scope encompassed replacement of the building's failing cooling towers, associated mechanical equipment, and the existing R-22 refrigerant chiller — a unit employing a phased-out refrigerant requiring regulatory compliance as part of the replacement effort. The design team served as prime consultant on this 100% Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small Business (SDVOSB) set-aside contract (VA Contract No. 36C24722R0026, VA Project No. 679-22-104), procured by VA Network Contracting Office (NCO) 7 under NAICS 541330.

Design deliverables included 35%, 65%, 95%, and 100% submission packages comprising specifications and drawings, construction cost estimates at each milestone, site investigation services, and construction period services (CPS) encompassing four site visits, one final inspection, and submittal reviews. The project was executed under a 120-calendar-day design period with a target A/E completion in FY2022, and the design was required to remain within a construction cost limitation of $900,000. Disciplines engaged on the project included mechanical engineering (HVAC and central plant), plumbing engineering (domestic water), fire protection engineering (wet sprinkler and fire alarm), and electrical engineering (power distribution), all coordinated under the prime consultant's management on a design-bid-build delivery.
