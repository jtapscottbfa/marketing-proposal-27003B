Fire protection engineering on this project provided life safety system integration for the commercial range hood installation in the Demo Kitchen (room 115) of ICC Building 7, a behavioral health facility in Norfolk, Virginia.

The fire protection scope had two elements. First, the Greenheck Model GRR5 range hood was specified with its factory-supplied self-contained fire suppression system, which is integral to the hood unit and compliant with NFPA 101. The self-contained suppression system activates automatically in response to heat or flame in the cooking zone; no separate suppression agent distribution or piping was required beyond what the manufacturer provides as part of the listed assembly.

Second, a fire alarm pull station was provided and connected to the range hood per the manufacturer's installation instructions. This pull station allows manual actuation of the suppression system and ties the hood's suppression event into the building's fire alarm infrastructure. The fire alarm scope falls under the fire alarm sub-discipline (electronic safety and security). No sprinkler system modifications or separate suppression agent systems were within the project scope; the self-contained hood assembly satisfied the life safety requirement for this small kitchen installation in a healthcare occupancy. The project is documented as complete.
