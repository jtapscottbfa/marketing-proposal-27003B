Electrical engineering on this project provided power distribution design to support the new commercial range hood installation in the Demo Kitchen (room 115) of ICC Building 7 in Norfolk, Virginia.

The electrical scope included a new NEMA 5-15R receptacle (115V, 1A) for the range hood control panel and coordination of an existing NEMA 14-50R receptacle serving the existing range. Because the new Greenheck GRR5 range hood also required a NEMA 14-50R connection, relocation of the existing receptacle may have been required; the drawings noted this condition for contractor resolution in the field. A new 15A/1P circuit breaker was added at space 22 of the existing Panel 2 to serve the new range control circuit. Panel 2 is a 225A, 208/120V, three-phase, three-wire surface-mounted panelboard (Square D Type NQOB, catalog number NQ0B-19128-2C1). The panel schedule for Panel 2 was documented on the MEP plan sheet. Existing Panel 1 and Panel 2 were to remain in service without modification beyond the added breaker.

The electrical scope also included the fire alarm pull station connected to the range hood system (documented in coordination with fire protection discipline). Sub-disciplines engaged were electrical power distribution and electrical lighting (the existing lighting in the affected area was accounted for in the circuit schedule). The project is documented as complete.
