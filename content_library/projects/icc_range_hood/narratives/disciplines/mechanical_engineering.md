Mechanical engineering on this project delivered the exhaust system design for a commercial range hood installation in the Demo Kitchen (room 115) of ICC Building 7, a behavioral health facility in Norfolk, Virginia.

The primary mechanical element was a Greenheck Model GRR5 fire-ready range hood — a 30-inch-wide unit with a minimum exhaust capacity of 500 CFM and a factory-supplied self-contained fire suppression system compliant with NFPA 101. This model was selected for its suitability in a healthcare occupancy setting where life safety compliance is required. The exhaust system consisted of a 12-inch diameter duct routed from the hood through the kitchen cabinet space and through the exterior building wall. The exterior termination was designed as a spun aluminum wall cap assembly incorporating a backdraft damper and bird screen to prevent reverse airflow and pest infiltration.

A wall cap detail was prepared at no-scale to document the exterior penetration configuration. Construction notes on sheet ME101 addressed installation sequence and coordination requirements for the duct-through-wall condition. The mechanical scope was limited to the range hood and associated exhaust system; no other HVAC systems were added or modified. The project is documented as complete.
