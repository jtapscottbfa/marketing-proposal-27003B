The engineering team provided MEP design services for the installation of a commercial range hood in the Demo Kitchen (room 115) of ICC Building 7, a behavioral health and wellness facility in Norfolk, Virginia. The project served the Prosperity Eating Disorders and Wellness Center, a tenant occupying the building's first floor, and was executed as a targeted tenant improvement within an existing healthcare building.

The mechanical scope centered on a Greenheck Model GRR5 fire-ready range hood — a 30-inch-wide unit with integral self-contained fire suppression, NFPA 101 compliant, with a minimum exhaust capacity of 500 CFM. A 12-inch diameter exhaust duct was routed through the kitchen cabinets and exterior wall, terminating at a spun aluminum wall cap with backdraft damper and bird screen. Electrical work included a new NEMA 5-15R receptacle for the range control panel and a new 15A/1P circuit breaker in the existing 225A, 208/120V, three-phase Panel 2; existing panels remained in service. Fire protection integration consisted of the hood's factory-supplied suppression system and a new fire alarm pull station connected to the hood per manufacturer requirements. The design was issued for construction on February 3, 2022. The project was completed under the prime consultant role, with Romdhya Kumar serving as engineer of record.
