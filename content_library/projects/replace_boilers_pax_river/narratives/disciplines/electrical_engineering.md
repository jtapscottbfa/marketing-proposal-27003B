Electrical engineering on this project provided power distribution modifications to support the new mechanical equipment installed in all three buildings at NAS Patuxent River. Each boiler room received updated electrical service tailored to the new boiler plant configuration and motor loads.

The scope included modifications to existing three-phase electrical distribution panels — serving either 480Y/277V or 208Y/120V systems depending on the building — to accommodate the new boilers, pumps, and ancillary mechanical equipment. Individual motor starters with separate disconnect switches were provided for each motor in the new plant, meeting the isolation and safety requirements of the installation. New branch circuit wiring was run in IMC or rigid metallic conduit, using copper THHN/THWN conductors, consistent with NAVFAC standards for mechanical room installations.

Coordination with the mechanical scope was integral to the electrical design, as the boiler control panels interface with each building's existing direct digital control (DDC) system. The electrical scope ensured that control wiring, power feeds to control panels, and service disconnects were coordinated with the mechanical DDC integration requirements.
