Mechanical engineering was the primary discipline on this three-building boiler replacement project at NAS Patuxent River. The work centered on central plant replacement across Buildings B2641, B2865, and B101A, each housing a distinct existing boiler configuration and requiring a tailored replacement strategy.

In Building B2641, two Smith 19A-51W-03 cast-iron fuel oil boilers (each 810 MBH) were replaced with new oil-fired packaged non-condensing hot water boilers — a minimum of two units, each sized at 67% of building load — along with new hydronic pumps, piping, and a shot-type chemical feeder. Crane access was required for equipment removal and installation.

In Building B2865, a single Fulton Gas-Fired Pulse Combustion Boiler (Model PHw-1000, 1,000,000 Btu/hr) was replaced with two new gas-fired cast-iron modular condensing boilers rated at 650 MBH each, meeting a minimum thermal efficiency of 92.7% — a significant efficiency improvement over the pulse combustion predecessor.

In Building B101A, two existing Weil McLain BGL-988-SF-PF-LO-UL gas-fired units, associated hot water pumps, and a steam converter were replaced with two new high-efficiency gas-fired condensing hot water boilers and all associated equipment. This building required coordination with the NAS Patuxent River Title V Operating Permit and a Maryland MDE Permit to Construct.

Across all three buildings, mechanical scope included replacement of hydronic accessories (air separators, make-up water assemblies, expansion tanks), controls integration with existing building DDC systems, and temporary heating provisions to maintain building operations during construction.
