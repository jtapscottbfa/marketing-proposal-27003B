The design team served as Designer of Record (DOR) on a design-build contract to replace aging boiler plants in three separate buildings — B2641, B2865, and B101A — at Naval Air Station Patuxent River, Maryland (NAWC/NAS PAX River), under NAVFAC acquisition ACQR#5768527 / Work Order 1639149. The project was procured as a single solicitation with three corresponding CLINs, each addressing a distinct building, existing equipment configuration, and fuel type.

Scope across all three buildings included removal of existing boiler and associated equipment, installation of new high-efficiency hot water boilers, replacement of hydronic pumps, piping, and expansion system components, and integration of new control panels with each building's existing direct digital control (DDC) system. B2865 and B101A required new Permits to Construct from the Maryland Department of the Environment (MDE). Building B101A — the boiler room serving Hangar 101 — introduced additional complexities: potential asbestos abatement, a Title V air operating permit associated with the existing equipment, and Foreign Object Debris (FOD) protocols due to proximity to an active taxiway. All three buildings remained occupied or in active use during construction, requiring temporary heating provisions throughout the work. Electrical power distribution in each building was modified to serve the new mechanical equipment.
