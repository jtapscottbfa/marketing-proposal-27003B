# B520 Renovate A3Q Offices — Summary Narrative

BFA served as prime consultant and Designer of Record for the Design-Build renovation of the A3Q office suites in Building 520 at Joint Base Anacostia-Bolling (JBAB), Washington, DC, under contract to NAVFAC Washington. The project was procured as a task order competition under the Small Business Multiple Award Construction Contract (SB MACC), with a construction value estimated in the range of $1,000,000 to $5,000,000 and a 240-calendar-day performance period.

The renovation encompassed a full complement of design disciplines — architecture, mechanical, electrical, fire protection, telecommunications, and security engineering — within an existing large-footprint building classified as an unlimited-area structure under IBC Section 507.5. A defining element of the project was the Phase 1 renovation of Suite 220 as an ICD/ICS 705-compliant Sensitive Compartmented Information Facility (SCIF). That scope required perimeter walls extending true floor to true ceiling, STC-45 acoustical isolation, and upgrade of the existing Advantor security system to satisfy new occupancy requirements. The broader renovation included demolition, approximately 5,000 square feet of new drywall, interior finishes, acoustical ceilings, doors and hardware, HVAC systems, electrical power and lighting, wet fire suppression, fire alarm, structured cabling, and access control and intrusion detection systems.

BFA developed the Basis of Design, submitted as a Final Submittal in December 2020, documenting the design approach across all disciplines and serving as the authoritative design record for the Design-Build delivery.
