# B520 Renovate A3Q Offices — Full Narrative

## Project Overview

Building 520 at Joint Base Anacostia-Bolling (JBAB) in Washington, DC is a large-footprint structure classified as an unlimited-area building under IBC Section 507.5 — a designation that applies to fully sprinklered facilities that exceed the code's standard floor-area thresholds for multi-story Type VB construction. The A3Q office suites within Building 520 required comprehensive renovation to meet current mission, occupancy, and security requirements. NAVFAC Washington issued a Design-Build solicitation (N40080-20-R-6921) under the NAVFAC Washington Small Business Multiple Award Construction Contract (SB MACC), with a performance period of 240 calendar days from award and a FAR 36.204 estimated price range of $1,000,000 to $5,000,000. BFA was engaged as the Design-Build team's Designer of Record (prime consultant) under NAVFAC Washington Work Order 1666195, BFA Project #20-080.

## Scope of Work

The project was organized around two phases. Phase 1 focused on the renovation of Suite 220 as a fully compliant Sensitive Compartmented Information Facility (SCIF) per ICD and ICS 705 standards. Phase 1 SCIF scope included extending all perimeter walls of the newly created SCIF from true floor to true ceiling, modifying existing wall partitions to achieve an STC-45 acoustical rating, and upgrading the existing Advantor security system to meet new occupancy requirements.

The broader renovation scope spanned the full building systems complement:

- **Architecture:** Selective demolition of existing office partitions; installation of approximately 5,000 square feet of new drywall; interior finishes including flooring, acoustical ceiling tile, painting, and wall coverings; new doors, frames, and hardware throughout the renovation area; furnishings.
- **Mechanical:** HVAC air distribution and controls/building automation system (BAS) serving the renovated office suites.
- **Electrical:** Electrical power distribution and branch circuit work; interior lighting with controls.
- **Fire Protection:** Modification and extension of the existing wet-pipe sprinkler system; fire alarm system work.
- **Telecommunications:** Structured cabling (voice/data) for the renovated suites.
- **Security:** Access control system (ACS) and intrusion detection; SCIF-grade electronic security for Suite 220 including Advantor system upgrades.

## Role on the Project

BFA served as the prime consultant and Designer of Record for the Design-Build team. In the Design-Build delivery model, BFA held full responsibility for developing the design from the RFP performance requirements through the Basis of Design submittal. BFA provided architectural, mechanical engineering, electrical engineering, fire protection engineering, telecommunications engineering, and security engineering services. The Basis of Design was submitted as a Final Submittal in December 2020, documenting design decisions across all disciplines and providing the authoritative design record for Government review and construction.

## Key Challenges and Resolutions

**ICD/ICS 705 SCIF compliance within an existing building.** Renovating an existing office suite to full SCIF standards required reconciling the existing construction with strict ICD 705 physical security and acoustic requirements. The design extended all perimeter walls to true slab-to-slab conditions and upgraded partitions to STC-45, integrating security construction requirements with ongoing building operations.

**Unlimited-area building fire protection.** Building 520's classification as an unlimited-area structure under IBC 507.5 required fire protection analysis confirming that the building's sprinkler coverage and occupancy classification met the code requirements that substitute for prescriptive area limits. The design addressed this by documenting the existing sprinkler system status and scope of modification needed for the renovated suites.

**Proprietary security system integration.** The RFP identified the Advantor security system as the required platform for the SCIF electronic security scope, including a Government-specified budget allowance for Advantor services. The design team coordinated system upgrade requirements directly with the Advantor system configuration to meet new occupancy requirements.

**Design-Build scope definition.** As the Design-Build DOR, BFA developed design scope and design criteria from a performance-specification RFP, without Government-furnished drawings. The Basis of Design document served as BFA's mechanism for recording, and obtaining Government concurrence on, all key design decisions before construction documents were developed.

## Outcomes

The Basis of Design was completed and submitted as a Final Submittal in December 2020. The project is recorded as complete. The Design-Build renovation delivered a fully compliant ICD/ICS 705 SCIF in Suite 220 alongside a comprehensive multi-system office renovation, executed within the 240-day performance period framework established by the NAVFAC SB MACC task order.
