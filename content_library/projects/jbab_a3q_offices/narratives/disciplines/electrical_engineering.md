# Electrical Engineering — B520 Renovate A3Q Offices

Electrical engineering services on the B520 A3Q Offices renovation at Joint Base Anacostia-Bolling included electrical power distribution and interior lighting design for the renovated office suites in Building 520. The electrical scope encompassed branch circuit work serving the reconfigured spaces, interior lighting fixtures, and lighting controls throughout the renovation area.

The electrical design addressed the full suite of renovated office spaces, including the Phase 1 SCIF renovation of Suite 220. Electrical work within the SCIF envelope required coordination with ICD 705 physical security requirements, including proper treatment of electrical penetrations through the SCIF perimeter construction. Lighting and power layouts were developed to suit the new interior partition configuration resulting from the renovation.

Electrical system specifics — including panel schedules, circuit counts, fixture types, and lighting control system details — are not captured in the source record; those details reside in the construction documents developed under the Design-Build delivery. BFA contributed the electrical engineering scope to the Basis of Design submitted as a Final Submittal to NAVFAC Washington in December 2020, establishing the design approach and system criteria for Government review.
