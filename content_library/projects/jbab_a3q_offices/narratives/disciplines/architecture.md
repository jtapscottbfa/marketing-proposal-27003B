# Architecture — B520 Renovate A3Q Offices

The architectural scope of the B520 A3Q Offices renovation at Joint Base Anacostia-Bolling encompassed the full interior renovation of office suites within Building 520, an unlimited-area structure under IBC Section 507.5. Architectural work spanned selective demolition of existing partitions, installation of approximately 5,000 square feet of new drywall, and a complete interior finishes package including flooring, acoustical ceiling tile systems, painting, and wall coverings. New interior doors, frames, and hardware were specified and integrated throughout the renovation area.

A primary architectural challenge was the Phase 1 renovation of Suite 220 to satisfy ICD/ICS 705 Sensitive Compartmented Information Facility (SCIF) standards. The architectural design extended all perimeter walls of the Suite 220 SCIF from true floor to true ceiling and modified existing wall partitions to achieve the required STC-45 acoustical rating. These measures addressed ICD 705's physical security construction criteria for a classified space within an existing occupied building. The architectural documentation also addressed Building 520's IBC unlimited-area classification, confirming that the renovation scope and occupancy configuration remained consistent with the conditions that sustain that classification.

BFA developed the architectural portions of the Basis of Design as part of the Design-Build team's submission to NAVFAC Washington, recording design decisions for Government review prior to construction document development.
