# Mechanical Engineering — B520 Renovate A3Q Offices

Mechanical engineering services on the B520 A3Q Offices renovation at Joint Base Anacostia-Bolling covered HVAC system design and controls integration for the renovated office suites in Building 520. The mechanical scope encompassed HVAC air distribution serving the reconfigured interior spaces and integration with the building automation system (BAS) for environmental controls.

The renovation of existing office suites required adapting HVAC distribution to the new partition layout, including Suite 220's SCIF configuration with its stringent acoustic and physical security construction requirements. HVAC penetrations through SCIF perimeter walls required coordination with ICD 705 construction standards to maintain the acoustic and physical security integrity of the classified space. The mechanical design documented system configurations and equipment selections in the Basis of Design, which BFA submitted as a Final Submittal to NAVFAC Washington in December 2020.

Specific mechanical equipment quantities, capacities, and system configuration details are not captured in the source record for this project; the BOD and SOW establish the scope and design criteria but detailed system specifics are contained in the construction documents not available in the project record.
