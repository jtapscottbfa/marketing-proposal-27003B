On behalf of Naval Facilities Engineering Command – Washington (NAVFAC Washington), the design team served as prime consultant to design a permanent open pitched-roof shelter over three sludge drying beds at the existing Wastewater Treatment Plant on Webster Field Annex, St. Inigoes, Maryland. The project — formally designated B8175 Modify Wastewater Treatment Plant — provided weather protection for active treatment infrastructure without enclosing the beds, preserving access and ventilation for ongoing wastewater operations.

The structure spans approximately 78'-7.5" × 38'-0" in plan and carries a 4:12-pitch prefabricated wood truss roof at a minimum eave height of 14 feet above the existing concrete slab. Pressure-treated southern pine framing, laminated veneer lumber (LVL) ridge beams, and new concrete piers and spread footings on the existing slab-on-grade were designed to NAVFAC Unified Facilities Criteria (UFC), IBC 2018, and ASCE 7, with a 105 MPH ultimate design wind speed and Risk Category I seismic criteria. Asphalt shingle roofing and continuous aluminum gutters complete the envelope. The project was delivered under NAVFAC MACC contract N40080-19-D-0003 using a design-bid-build delivery method. Final construction documents were issued in March 2022, and the construction contract — modified via bilateral scope addition from $395,612 to $750,487 — was substantially complete by June 2022.
