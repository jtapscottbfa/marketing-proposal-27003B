The CBP Advanced Training Center Mezzanine Expansion is a Design-Build renovation and expansion at the U.S. Customs and Border Protection (CBP) Advanced Training Center in Harpers Ferry, West Virginia. The project delivered two parallel spatial improvements within Building 6, the campus Tactical Training Building — a pre-engineered metal building on a reinforced concrete slab on grade. On the first floor, the existing gym was enlarged by reclaiming the adjacent restroom and locker room area; the restroom facility was removed in its entirety, and a new laundry room with domestic water and sanitary plumbing was constructed on the first floor to relocate the displaced function. On the second floor, the mezzanine was extended with new structural steel framing and a lightweight concrete floor system to create additional office and storage capacity.

The multi-discipline design team provided architecture, structural engineering, mechanical engineering, electrical engineering, plumbing engineering, and fire protection engineering under a single Design-Build contract. Scope included new HVAC systems serving the expanded areas, electrical power distribution and interior lighting, domestic water and sanitary plumbing for the relocated laundry, new interior partitions and finishes throughout affected spaces, and extension of wet-pipe fire suppression and fire alarm coverage to the new mezzanine and gym footprints. The contractual period of performance was 180 days following Notice to Proceed. All contractor personnel were required to hold a favorably adjudicated Background Investigation, reflecting the federal law enforcement training mission of the CBP ATC campus.
