Plumbing engineering addressed the domestic water supply and sanitary drainage systems affected by the first-floor restroom removal and laundry function relocation. The scope was driven by the architectural decision to convert the existing restroom and locker room area into expanded gym space: existing domestic water supply lines and sanitary drain-waste-vent (DWV) connections in that area were removed, and a new laundry room with fresh domestic water supply and sanitary drainage was designed on the first floor to accommodate the displaced laundry function.

Sub-disciplines addressed were plumbing domestic water and plumbing sanitary. The primary building systems in scope were domestic water supply and the sanitary DWV system serving the new laundry room. The plumbing scope was concentrated in the first-floor modification zone and was coordinated with the architectural re-layout and the mechanical and electrical trades working in the same areas. Plumbing design was developed as part of the multi-discipline Basis of Design and provided the technical basis for the Design-Build contractor's plumbing construction documents.
