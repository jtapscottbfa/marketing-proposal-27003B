Architecture led the spatial reconfiguration of the Tactical Training Building, addressing both the first-floor gym expansion and the second-floor mezzanine extension. On the first floor, the existing restroom and locker room area was removed to expand the gym footprint, with new interior partitions and finishes establishing the enlarged gym space. A new laundry room was designed on the first floor to relocate the displaced laundry function from the demolished restroom area. On the second floor, the mezzanine was extended outward to create additional office and storage area, with new interior partitions defining the occupied space within the expanded mezzanine deck.

Architectural scope encompassed interior finish systems throughout the affected areas: drywall, flooring, acoustical ceilings, painting, and new doors and frames. The host structure — a pre-engineered metal building on a reinforced concrete slab on grade — set the geometric and structural constraints for the expansion design, with the mezzanine extension following the existing column grid and building bay geometry. The architectural program was documented in the Basis of Design and served as the framework for the Design-Build contractor's construction document development across all six project disciplines.
