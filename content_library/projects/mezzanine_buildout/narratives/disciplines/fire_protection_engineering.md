Fire protection engineering designed the extension of fire suppression and fire alarm coverage to all areas created or reconfigured by the project. The expanded first-floor gym and the new second-floor mezzanine office and storage spaces each required new wet-pipe sprinkler branches to provide code-compliant coverage within the additional occupied area. The fire alarm system was extended to include detection and notification devices in the new and modified spaces, ensuring continuous coverage across the expanded building footprint.

Both wet-pipe fire suppression and fire alarm systems are recorded as building systems in scope for the project. The fire protection design was coordinated with the architectural and structural layout to establish correct sprinkler spacing, head placement, and branch routing compatible with the new mezzanine deck and the reconfigured first-floor geometry. Fire alarm device placement was coordinated with the architectural ceiling layout and with the mechanical discipline for HVAC equipment interfacing. The fire protection scope was developed as part of the multi-discipline Basis of Design and provided the technical basis for the Design-Build contractor's fire protection construction documents.
