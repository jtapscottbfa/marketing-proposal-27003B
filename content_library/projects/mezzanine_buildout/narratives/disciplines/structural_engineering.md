Structural engineering designed the new mezzanine framing system that is the physical basis of the second-floor expansion. New structural steel members were introduced to extend the mezzanine deck beyond its existing footprint, with a lightweight concrete floor system applied over the steel frame to provide the finished floor for the new office and storage area. Shallow foundations were designed to transfer the new mezzanine loads to the existing reinforced concrete slab on grade.

The work required careful integration with the existing pre-engineered metal building (PEMB) frame. PEMB structures carry their loads through a factory-engineered primary and secondary framing system with fixed bay spacing and connection geometry; adding a new mezzanine level requires connecting new steel members to the existing primary frame in a manner consistent with the original design basis. The structural scope also included cast-in-place concrete for new foundation elements supporting the mezzanine extension. Sub-disciplines addressed were structural steel and structural foundation design, with the primary building systems being the superstructure steel frame and shallow foundation elements. The structural design was documented in the Basis of Design as the technical basis for the Design-Build contractor's construction document development.
