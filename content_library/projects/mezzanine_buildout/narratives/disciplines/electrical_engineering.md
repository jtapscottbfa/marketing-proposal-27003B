Electrical engineering provided power distribution and interior lighting design for the expanded and reconfigured areas, including the enlarged first-floor gym and the new second-floor mezzanine office and storage spaces. New electrical distribution circuits and interior lighting were designed to serve the expanded footprints, with systems developed to connect to the existing building's electrical infrastructure.

An electrical subcontract base bid of $78,068 (I.B. Abel Inc., dated September 2020) is documented in the project record, referencing drawings from the design package as the basis for the subcontractor's scope of work. That bid encompassed new fixtures, circuits, and connections to existing equipment. Electronic safety and security systems were also included in the overall construction scope for the project. Sub-disciplines addressed were electrical power distribution and electrical lighting, with electrical distribution and interior lighting systems as the primary building systems in scope. The electrical design was developed as part of the multi-discipline Basis of Design and established power and lighting criteria for the Design-Build contractor's construction document development.
