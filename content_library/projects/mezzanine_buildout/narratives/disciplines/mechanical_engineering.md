Mechanical engineering provided HVAC design for the expanded first-floor gym and second-floor mezzanine office and storage areas. Scope addressed air distribution to the new and reconfigured occupied spaces, packaged HVAC equipment serving those spaces, and controls integration. The Basis of Design documented design conditions, equipment parameters, and the approach for routing mechanical systems within the Tactical Training Building's pre-engineered metal building structure and the new mezzanine deck above.

Sub-disciplines addressed included HVAC design, HVAC controls, and HVAC air distribution. The primary building systems in mechanical scope were packaged HVAC equipment and the air distribution system serving the affected areas. The pre-engineered metal building configuration required mechanical routing solutions compatible with the existing framing and the new mezzanine construction above; coordination with the structural discipline was integral to establishing duct routing paths and equipment placement within the expanded spaces. Mechanical design was developed as part of the multi-discipline Basis of Design and provided the technical basis for the Design-Build contractor's HVAC construction document development.
