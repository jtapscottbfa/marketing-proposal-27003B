The design team provided mechanical and electrical engineering services for the replacement of Boiler 108 at Portsmouth Naval Medical Center (PNMC) Building 20 — the Central Steam Plant — in Portsmouth, Virginia. Working under a NAVFAC Mid-Atlantic A-E IDIQ task order (Contract N40085-19-D-9049, Task Order 9E41P0), the project removed the aging Nebraska "D" type water tube boiler (Cleaver Brooks/Nebraska Model 2D 2056, 20,000 lb/hr) and replaced it with a new 30,000 lb/hr package water tube boiler operating at 110 psi delivery pressure and 347°F. The new boiler was designed to operate in conjunction with the existing Boilers 109 and 110, establishing true n+1 redundancy for the Central Steam Plant serving the Naval Medical Center campus.

The project was classified as Maintenance/Repair under General Fund, with a program amount of $2,800,000 and an estimated construction cost of $2,000,000. A 240-calendar-day construction duration was specified. Associated mechanical and electrical work included all modifications to the existing 208Y/120V, 3-phase, 4-wire electrical distribution system and motor control provisions required to support the new equipment. The 100% Final Basis of Design was submitted in April 2021 under a Design-Bid-Build delivery method, providing NAVFAC with a complete design package for competitive procurement. The project increased steam generation capacity by 50% over the replaced unit while addressing the long-term reliability and efficiency objectives of a mission-critical military healthcare facility.
