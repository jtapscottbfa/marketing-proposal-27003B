The electrical engineering scope for this project addressed the power distribution modifications required to support the replacement of Boiler 108 at Portsmouth Naval Medical Center Building 20. The existing electrical distribution service at the building is 208Y/120V, 3-phase, 4-wire. The electrical design provided new and modified circuits, conduit systems (IMC and RMC, minimum 3/4-inch interior), and THHN/THWN copper conductors to serve the new 30,000 lb/hr package water tube boiler and its associated ancillary equipment.

Motor control provisions included individual motor starters with separate disconnect switches for all motors rated 1/2 HP and larger, consistent with the operational demands of an industrial boiler plant environment. The electrical scope was coordinated with the mechanical design to ensure complete integration of the new boiler package within the existing Central Steam Plant electrical infrastructure. Sub-disciplines engaged included electrical power distribution and electrical motor control, addressing both the service connection requirements for the new boiler and the control interface provisions needed for parallel operation with the existing Boilers 109 and 110.
