The mechanical engineering scope for this project centered on the full removal and replacement of Boiler 108 — an aging Nebraska "D" type water tube boiler (Cleaver Brooks/Nebraska Model 2D 2056, rated at 20,000 lb/hr steam output) — within the Central Steam Plant at Portsmouth Naval Medical Center Building 20. The replacement unit is a 30,000 lb/hr package water tube boiler, operating at 110 psi delivery pressure and 347°F, representing a 50% increase in steam generation capacity over the unit it replaces.

The mechanical design encompassed the complete removal of the existing boiler and its ancillary connections, preparation and repair of the boiler room systems and structures to receive the new equipment, and all mechanical work required to integrate the new boiler with the existing steam plant. The new boiler was designed to operate in parallel with the existing Boilers 109 and 110 to achieve a true n+1 redundancy configuration for the Central Steam Plant — a mission-critical reliability objective for a continuously operated military medical facility. The sub-disciplines engaged included central plant HVAC systems and steam plant controls coordination, addressing the operational interface requirements necessary for the new and existing boilers to function as an integrated system.
