The electrical engineering scope for Building 8221 Addition at NAWCAD Webster Field was the most system-wide of any discipline on the project, requiring reconfiguration of the existing facility's electrical service to accommodate the expanded load of the combined original building and new addition. The existing service — a 208Y/120V, 3-phase, 4-wire system supplied from a 150 KVA transformer with a 600A service fused at 500A — was assessed as insufficient for the combined facility and was replaced with a new 800A main circuit breaker panel and an 800A 3R current transformer cabinet. The electrical service entrance was relocated to the north wall of the new addition, establishing it as the combined facility's new service point.

A central design constraint was compliance with ICD-705 technical specifications and CNSSAM TEMPEST/1-13 standards, which govern electromagnetic security requirements for the facility's Open Storage Secret classification. These requirements shaped conduit selection (EMT and rigid metallic conduit throughout), cable routing, and service configuration. Conductors are THHN/THWN in metallic raceway systems. An existing cable tray from the original building was extended into the addition to support structured cabling.

Interior lighting consists of 1×4 LED fixtures with occupancy sensors; four exterior lighting fixtures were provided at the addition perimeter. Fourteen tele/data outlets were installed, each with a 1-inch empty conduit stub for future cable pull flexibility. The electrical design was coordinated with the electrical subcontractor Brown Electric, Inc., whose bid scope confirmed existing service parameters and addition load requirements.
