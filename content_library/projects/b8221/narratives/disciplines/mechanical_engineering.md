The mechanical engineering scope for Building 8221 Addition at NAWCAD Webster Field covered HVAC design for the new approximately 2,400 SF pre-engineered metal building addition attached to the east endwall of the existing industrial laboratory. The design provided two primary equipment items: a new packaged outdoor air conditioning unit to serve the addition's cooling load, and a packaged outside air handling unit with hot gas bypass to manage ventilation and humidity control for the expanded laboratory space.

The hot gas bypass provision reflects the laboratory occupancy's need to maintain stable temperature and humidity conditions independent of cooling load variation — a configuration appropriate for industrial lab environments where equipment heat loads and occupancy patterns may not track one another closely. Design criteria were governed by UFC 3-410-02N and the applicable NAVFAC mechanical standards referenced in the Basis of Design.

The existing building's mechanical systems were not modified as part of this scope; the design was limited to new equipment serving the addition. Mechanical equipment for the new addition was sized and specified as standalone packaged units, avoiding modifications to any existing HVAC distribution in the original building. The resulting system provides independent environmental control for the addition while keeping the existing building's mechanical infrastructure intact.
