The fire protection engineering scope for Building 8221 Addition at NAWCAD Webster Field extended whole-building fire suppression and fire alarm coverage from the existing 4,800 SF industrial laboratory into the new 2,400 SF addition. Rather than tapping the existing sprinkler riser, the design introduced a dedicated new 6-inch wet-pipe sprinkler riser for the addition, complete with a backflow preventer, flow switch, and tamper switches, providing hydraulic independence from the original building's suppression system while achieving continuous coverage across the combined facility footprint.

The fire alarm scope integrated new notification and detection appliances into the facility's existing Notifier NFS2-640 addressable fire alarm control unit. New devices were added to serve the addition, with provision for a new notification appliance circuit panel if the existing FACU's NAC capacity was insufficient for the expanded device count. The design also incorporated mass notification system integration consistent with the NAVFAC facility's requirements under UFC 4-021-01.

Design was governed by UFC 3-600-01, UFC 4-021-01, UFC 1-200-01, IBC 2021, NFPA 101 (2021), NFPA 72 (2019), and NFPA 13. The existing building's fire protection systems remained in service throughout, and the addition's riser and alarm devices were designed for integration without disrupting operational continuity of the existing facility systems.
