Building 8221 Addition at NAWCAD Webster Field, St. Inigoes, Maryland is an approximately 2,400 SF pre-engineered metal building addition to an existing 4,800 SF industrial laboratory. The facility operates under Open Storage Secret classification and is required to conform to ICD-705 technical specifications and CNSSAM TEMPEST/1-13 standards, making this a secured accredited facility addition with demanding engineering constraints across all disciplines.

The project was delivered as an 8(a) direct award (contract N0042123C0029) to The Sanchez Group of Companies, LLC, with the engineering design team serving as subconsultant to the general contractor. The design scope covered mechanical, electrical, plumbing, fire protection, and structural engineering for the new single-story addition, with several systems extended to serve the combined existing-plus-addition facility. The mechanical design incorporated new packaged HVAC equipment sized for the addition. The electrical scope required full service reconfiguration — including a new 800A main panel and service relocation to the addition's north wall — while maintaining compliance with ICD-705 and TEMPEST electromagnetic security standards for the classified environment. The fire protection scope extended whole-building wet-pipe sprinkler coverage from the existing building into the addition and integrated new fire alarm devices into the facility's existing addressable fire alarm control unit. Plumbing work focused on compressed air distribution to support the industrial laboratory workstations. Construction was contracted for delivery by September 30, 2024, under a 476-calendar-day performance period.
