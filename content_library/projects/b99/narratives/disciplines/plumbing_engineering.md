The plumbing engineering scope for Building 99 addressed domestic water service, domestic hot water production and distribution, sanitary drainage, and all plumbing fixtures for 54 apartment modules — designed throughout for reliable, freeze-proof operation at Pituffik Space Base, Greenland.

Domestic cold water is supplied from the 8-inch base utility main via a 3-inch service line, metered per DoDI 4170.11, and protected by a reduced-pressure zone backflow preventer in the first-floor mechanical room. Domestic hot water is produced by three SWEP B85 plate heat exchangers (102 kW combined capacity) using the base hot water supply as the primary heat source, and stored in three 500-liter tanks maintained at 62°C. A dedicated recirculation pump circuit (duty/standby pair) routes hot water to the third floor and back to sustain loop temperature throughout the building.

Each of the 54 apartment modules is served by a vitreous china lavatory (Hansgrohe), a stainless steel shower, and a vitreous china water closet (Duravit). Three laundry rooms contain 12 washing machines and 3 dishwashers; three kitchen areas contain kitchen sinks and utility sinks. Sanitary drainage from each apartment bathroom routes through 110 mm stacks to 160 mm main drain lines in the building's utility crawl space, discharging to two connections to the base sewer at the building's north face.

All crawl-space and exterior sanitary piping is pre-insulated PE pressure pipe with polyurethane foam insulation and a spiral hot-galvanized outer casing, incorporating dual heat trace circuits — one thermostatically controlled and one manual — per UFC 3-130-05. Minimum drain slope in the crawl space is 2% for exterior runs, with yard cleanouts provided at each drain run.
