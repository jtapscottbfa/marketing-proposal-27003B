Electrical engineering scope on this project addresses the power distribution requirements for the existing and replacement domestic hot water system equipment. The existing electrical infrastructure includes legacy electric service to the DHW storage tank—originally the primary heating source before conversion to natural gas in 1981—and a 600V main electrical switchgear serving the building's mechanical systems. These components establish the electrical baseline against which new replacement equipment demands are assessed.

The sub-discipline scope is electrical power distribution (electrical_power_distribution), focused on evaluating existing electrical service adequacy for replacement DHW equipment options, identifying any panel, branch circuit, or switchgear modifications required to support new equipment configurations, and coordinating electrical rough-in requirements with mechanical and plumbing equipment selections developed during the alternatives analysis (RFP #2). The 600V main switchgear capacity and existing branch circuit configurations in the mechanical rooms are relevant inputs to the option comparison work.

Detailed electrical design—panel schedules, load calculations, wiring diagrams, conduit routing—is not yet developed; this work is scoped to RFP #3 (final design), following selection of the preferred replacement option. The source record captures the electrical systems as documented in client presentations and scope documents; specific design calculations and equipment schedules are not present in the current record and will be developed in subsequent project phases. Electrical engineering is on the design team for this project; discipline-specific scope detail beyond power distribution assessment is a noted content gap in the source record.
