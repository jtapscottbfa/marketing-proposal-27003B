The C2F NH95 Renovations project delivers a comprehensive MEP systems renovation of portions of Building NH-95 at Naval Station Norfolk (NSA Hampton Roads) in Norfolk, Virginia, for the US Second Fleet (C2F) Command. Executed under a NAVFAC MIDLANT Design-Bid-Build task order (FY24 Special Project RM24-2031, Contract N4008520D005), the design team served as prime consultant providing full MEP, telecommunications, and security engineering across the first and second floors of the command facility. The $39 million program encompasses reconfiguration of existing dual-duct VAV HVAC systems, conversion of a former Data Center from CRAC-cooled to VAV-served office use, new DDC controls, complete demolition and replacement of all electrical distribution and lighting systems with energy-efficient LED fixtures and occupancy controls, new restroom and galley plumbing, and a fully renewed telecommunications infrastructure. Security requirements define the project: the design includes an EF/POP room meeting ICD 705 Type A wall standards for the secure telecom environment, an Electronic Security System (ESS) with perimeter sensors, CCTV, and card reader access control, and controlled-area construction for a US-persons-only operational facility. Hazardous material abatement — ACM floor tiles, pipe insulation, lead paint, and PCB ballasts — was required throughout the renovation zones prior to construction. The project was delivered Design-Bid-Build with sole-source construction procurement and is classified as design complete.
