# Fire Protection Engineering — C2F NH95 Renovations

Fire protection engineering was part of the design team scope for the C2F NH95 Renovations at Building NH-95, Naval Station Norfolk. The source record captures the fire protection scope as it was defined in the SAES task order requirements, which structured the discipline around survey work and design package deliverables.

The scope requires a survey of the existing fire suppression system, including water flow testing of the available water supply serving the renovation areas, to establish the capacity baseline for the updated system design. A separate survey of the existing mass notification system is required to assess integration requirements with the renovated building. Where clean agent suppression systems exist in the areas of work, those systems are also to be surveyed prior to design.

Fire suppression plans and fire alarm/mass notification system plans are identified as required components of the construction design package. The specific system types, coverage extents, and design standards for the renovated areas are not detailed in the available source documents beyond these survey and deliverable requirements.
