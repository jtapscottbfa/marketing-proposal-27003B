# Plumbing Engineering — C2F NH95 Renovations

The plumbing engineering scope for the C2F NH95 Renovations introduces new plumbing infrastructure in areas of Building NH-95 previously lacking restroom and service facilities, in support of the Second Fleet (C2F) Command's renovated occupancy. New restroom facilities are designed to include water closets, lavatories, and bi-level water coolers meeting federal accessibility requirements. The cook staff galley serving the Admiral's staff receives a two-compartment sink and a mop sink with complete domestic water supply and sanitary drain-waste-vent connections.

Domestic water distribution is designed in copper tubing per ASTM B88, Type K, L, or M as appropriate by application and pressure zone. Sanitary drainage and vent piping is cast iron throughout the above-grade renovation areas. Domestic hot water is generated by electric water heaters meeting UL 174 with 4.5 kW dual-element heating.

Pipe insulation is specified by service type: mineral fiber insulation is used on domestic hot water piping; cellular glass insulation is used on domestic cold water piping to prevent condensation in the conditioned space. All plumbing work is coordinated with the concurrent mechanical, electrical, and finishes renovation to maintain construction sequencing within the active military facility.
