# Telecommunications Engineering — C2F NH95 Renovations

The telecommunications engineering scope for the C2F NH95 Renovations delivers a complete replacement of all secure and non-secure communications infrastructure in Building NH-95, supporting the US Second Fleet (C2F) Command's operational and information technology requirements at Naval Station Norfolk. All existing telecommunications systems — secure and non-secure — are demolished and replaced with new fiber and copper infrastructure supporting data, voice, and video services.

A defining feature of the telecommunications design is the Equipment Facility/Point-of-Presence (EF/POP) room, designed per Protected Distribution System (PDS) guidelines. The EF/POP room walls are constructed to ICD 705 Type A standards, providing the physical security classification boundary required for the secure telecom environment serving a classified command facility. This requirement drives construction coordination with the security engineering and general construction scopes, including dielectric sleeve penetrations and controlled access during installation.

The telecommunications scope also encompasses the LR-167 and LR-168 relocatable facilities associated with the project, covering approximately 36,000 square feet of facilities area. New structured cabling infrastructure — fiber and copper — is provided throughout, serving the command's operational layout including workstations, data services, and the secure/non-secure network separation required for a US Second Fleet command environment. The scope is designed in accordance with NAVFAC telecommunications standards and the applicable PDS and ICD 705 requirements for the facility's security classification level.
