The security engineering scope for the DLA Secure Area Renovation delivered the complete electronic security system (ESS) required for ICS 705-1 SCIF accreditation at Building 45, Naval Support Activity Philadelphia.

The integrated ESS comprised three coordinated subsystems. The access control system (ACS) managed and recorded personnel entry into the Secure Suite, enforcing the controlled-access requirements of the ICS 705-1 physical security standard and coordinating with the architectural door and partition design at suite entry points. The intrusion detection system (IDS) provided detection of unauthorized access attempts during periods when the Secure Suite was unoccupied, satisfying the after-hours physical security requirements of the accreditation standard. The closed-circuit television (CCTV) system provided visual surveillance coverage at entry points and designated interior locations as required by the facility's security design.

Cybersecurity requirements applicable to classified facility electronic security infrastructure were addressed in the ESS design, reflecting the heightened system-level requirements for networks and devices operating within an ICS 705-1 boundary. The security engineering scope encompasses a sub-discipline specifically designated for SCIF/classified facility security design, reflecting the coordination requirements between the ESS design team, the NAVFAC contracting authority, and the cognizant accrediting authority responsible for ICS 705-1 compliance review.

ESS design was developed in close coordination with the electrical discipline's Red/Black separation strategy, ensuring classified-signal circuits within the ACS, IDS, and CCTV infrastructure were properly isolated from unclassified electrical loads. The scope was delivered under the full Design-Build Designer of Record engagement.
