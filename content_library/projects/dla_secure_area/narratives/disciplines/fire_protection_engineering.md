The fire protection engineering scope for the DLA Secure Area Renovation addressed modifications to Building 45's existing fire suppression and fire alarm systems to serve the newly constructed SCIF Secure Suite. Building 45 opened in November 2019, meaning the base-building fire protection infrastructure was current-generation at the time of the SCIF renovation.

The wet sprinkler system was modified to extend coverage into the SCIF interior buildout, providing suppression protection to the VTC Room, IT/Server Room, and Common Open Office areas in compliance with applicable life safety codes. The fire alarm system was extended to integrate detection, notification, and annunciation appropriate for the new Secure Suite spaces, supporting the continuous 24/7 occupancy requirement and the controlled-access nature of the facility.

Penetrations of sprinkler piping, fire alarm conduit, and associated infrastructure through the SCIF perimeter required careful coordination with the architectural and mechanical disciplines. Each penetration at the Secure Suite boundary was designed to maintain the acoustic attenuation (STC 50 minimum) and RF shielding integrity required for ICS 705-1 accreditation, ensuring that life-safety system infrastructure did not create code-compliant but accreditation-compromising pathways through the SCIF envelope. The fire protection scope was delivered as part of the full multi-discipline Design-Build package under the Designer of Record role.
