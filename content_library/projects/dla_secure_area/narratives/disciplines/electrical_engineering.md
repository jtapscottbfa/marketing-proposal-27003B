The electrical engineering scope for the DLA Secure Area Renovation addressed the specialized power, lighting, and TEMPEST countermeasure requirements of an ICS 705-1 SCIF Secure Suite operating on a 24/7 continuous basis.

A dedicated electrical service panel (Panel EML1A) was designed to serve the Secure Suite independently from the building's common electrical distribution. Power line RF filters were specified on supply conductors at the SCIF boundary to prevent conducted emanation of classified signal information — a TEMPEST countermeasure required under UFC 4-010-05 and the associated IC technical specifications for ICD/ICS 705. Red/Black electrical separation was implemented to isolate circuits carrying classified-system signals (Black) from circuits serving unclassified loads (Red), a foundational SCIF electrical design practice that must be maintained from source panel to termination throughout the accredited space.

Interior lighting was designed for the VTC Room, IT/Server Room, and Common Open Office areas. Emergency power provisions were incorporated to support the continuous operation requirement, ensuring the Secure Suite's critical systems — security, communications, and environmental — remain available without interruption.

All electrical design was developed in close coordination with the telecommunications and electronic security disciplines to maintain proper Red/Black topology through all classified-system circuits, and with the architectural discipline to ensure RF filter and conduit penetrations preserved the RF shielding integrity of the Secure Suite perimeter. The electrical scope was delivered under the full Design-Build Designer of Record engagement.
