The mechanical engineering scope for the DLA Secure Area Renovation provided a standalone HVAC system to serve the SCIF Secure Suite independently from Building 45's shared mechanical infrastructure. Isolation from the building's general HVAC distribution is a critical ICS 705-1 accreditation requirement: shared ductwork or air-handling pathways create potential acoustic and signal-leakage vulnerabilities at the SCIF perimeter boundary.

The design delivered a packaged HVAC system with dedicated air distribution serving all three functional spaces — VTC Room, IT/Server Room, and Common Open Office — sized for continuous 24/7 operating conditions. The IT/Server Room load profile, driven by continuous server and communications equipment operation, was addressed within the standalone system capacity. Building automation system (BAS) controls were incorporated to support monitoring and operation of the dedicated mechanical plant.

The system design was coordinated with the architectural partition layout and ceiling scope to route supply and return air within the SCIF perimeter, treating each penetration of the Secure Suite boundary in a manner that maintained the acoustic and RF shielding integrity required for accreditation. Equipment selection and placement within the unfinished space — originally featuring exposed metal deck ceiling and exposed structural steel — was coordinated with the architectural finish scope and the electrical power supply from the dedicated panel. The mechanical scope was delivered as part of the full multi-discipline Design-Build engagement under the Designer of Record role.
