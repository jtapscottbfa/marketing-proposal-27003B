The telecommunications engineering scope for the DLA Secure Area Renovation provided the structured cabling infrastructure required to support the SCIF Secure Suite's communications and data functions within an ICS 705-1 accreditation boundary.

The design specified CAT6 copper cabling and fiber optic cabling to serve the VTC Room and IT/Server Room — the two primary technology-intensive spaces within the 650-square-foot suite. The VTC Room required both high-bandwidth data connectivity and cabling coordination with the audiovisual and video teleconference equipment it was designed to support. The IT/Server Room cabling infrastructure supported the suite's server and network equipment operating under continuous 24/7 conditions.

All cabling infrastructure was routed and terminated within the SCIF accreditation boundary, with cable management designed to comply with ICS 705-1 physical security requirements governing data pathways that enter or exit the Secure Suite. Conduit and cable penetrations of the Secure Suite perimeter were treated in coordination with the architectural RF shielding requirements to ensure TEMPEST countermeasure integrity was maintained at each penetration point. The telecommunications design was developed in coordination with the electrical Red/Black separation strategy, ensuring cabling carrying classified signals was handled within the classified (Black) side of the electrical topology. The scope was delivered as part of the full multi-discipline Design-Build package under the Designer of Record role.
