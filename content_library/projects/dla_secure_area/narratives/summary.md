The Defense Logistics Agency (DLA) Secure Area Renovation converted approximately 650 square feet of unfinished interior space in Building 45 at Naval Support Activity Philadelphia, Pennsylvania into a fully accredited Sensitive Compartmented Information Facility (SCIF) Secure Suite. The facility was designed to meet Intelligence Community Standard ICS 705-1 and Unified Facilities Criteria UFC 4-010-05, and configured as a Continuous Operation (24/7) type encompassing a Video Teleconference (VTC) Room, an IT/Server Room, and a Common Open Office area. The design team served as Designer of Record (DOR) under the prime Design-Build contractor, delivering all engineering and architectural design services across seven disciplines.

The renovation transformed a raw space — concrete floor, exposed metal deck ceiling, and exposed structural steel — into a hardened, accredited facility. Scope spanned architectural interior construction to Sound Group 4 (STC 50 minimum) acoustic standards; a standalone packaged HVAC system isolated from the building's shared infrastructure; dedicated electrical service with power line RF filters and Red/Black separation as TEMPEST countermeasures; structured telecommunications cabling (CAT6 and fiber) within the accreditation boundary; fire suppression and fire alarm modifications; plumbing connections; and an integrated electronic security system incorporating access control, intrusion detection, and CCTV. RF foil shielding was incorporated throughout the Secure Suite perimeter to satisfy TEMPEST requirements. Work was performed within a newly constructed building (opened November 2019) in a restricted fenced compound, with construction sequenced to protect continuous operations of the adjacent Command Control Center.
