# Building 111 Renovations — Summary

Bowman Foster & Associates (BFA) provided mechanical engineering design services for the renovation of Building 111 at Naval Support Facility (NSF) Indian Head, Maryland — a historic 1899 structure originally built as the installation's powerhouse and now serving as the primary shop and administrative space for NAVFAC Public Works Facilities' in-house workforce. The project addressed decades of deferred maintenance and failed building systems through a phased, multi-option construction scope executed under a 212-day period of performance.

The renovation was structured as a base bid plus four options delivered in order of precedence: (Base) building repair and HAZMAT remediation including asbestos and lead-based paint abatement; (Option 1) full electrical service upgrade and new LED lighting throughout; (Option 2) complete HVAC replacement with six new split-system heat pump air handlers and condensing units; (Option 3) plumbing fixture replacement and construction of a new Women's restroom; and (Option 4) comprehensive room finishes including new LVT flooring, ceiling grid systems, interior doors, and paint.

BFA's design scope centered on Option 2, delivering a mechanical basis of design at the Prefinal submittal stage (February 24, 2025) in accordance with NAVFAC Planning & Design Policy, UFC 3-410-02N, ASHRAE 62.1-2007, and IMC-2010. The new HVAC systems fully replaced all existing equipment — including failed units causing recurring flooding — with zoned heat pump systems sized for current and future occupancy needs across the basement, main floor, and second floor.
