# Mechanical Engineering — Building 111 Renovations

BFA provided full mechanical engineering design services for the HVAC renovation of Building 111 at Naval Support Facility Indian Head, Maryland. The scope encompassed complete removal of all existing failed HVAC systems and replacement with a new six-zone split-system heat pump configuration covering all occupied spaces across the basement, main floor, and second floor.

The design specified six new heat pump air handlers paired with six condensing units, complemented by ductless mini-split systems for individual offices, conference rooms, the second-floor training room, and basement spaces. Four exhaust systems were designed for the Men's restroom, Private restroom, future Women's restroom, and basement bathroom. Ductwork was galvanized sheet metal throughout, with aluminum specified for restroom exhaust and high-humidity areas to address corrosion in a 125-year-old building envelope with known moisture intrusion history.

The Prefinal Basis of Design submittal (February 24, 2025) was developed in compliance with NAVFAC Planning & Design Policy, UFC 3-410-02N, ASHRAE 62.1-2007, ASHRAE 55-2008, and IMC-2010, establishing outdoor design conditions for Indian Head, Maryland and interior comfort targets of 78°F / 50% RH cooling and 68°F heating across admin and shop areas. Standalone programmable thermostat controls per zone were specified — appropriate for a small facility without a central BAS. The design included a complete testing and balancing (TAB) scope, and noise levels were limited to NC 30 in occupied office and conference spaces.
