Fire protection engineering was included in the prime consultant's scope of services for Building 3400 Suite 117 at Fort Gregg-Adams, Virginia. The project involved interior reconfiguration of approximately 3,700 SF of first-floor space, which required review and modification of existing fire protection systems — including sprinkler coverage and fire alarm devices — to reflect the new partition layout and reconfigured occupancy.

Specific fire protection scope details are not captured in the source records available for this project.
