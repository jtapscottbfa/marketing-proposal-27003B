Electrical engineering scope on Building 3400 Suite 117 covered the design of a complete new interior lighting system, emergency lighting, branch circuit power distribution, and data/communications outlet infrastructure to serve the reconfigured administrative office layout at Fort Gregg-Adams, Virginia.

New LED lighting was specified throughout the approximately 3,700 SF suite, with color temperatures of 4000K and 5000K and a minimum 80 CRI — quality levels appropriate for the legal and communications office environments occupied by Trial Defense Services (TDS) and the Public Affairs Office (PAO). All LED fixtures include integral emergency capabilities, providing code-compliant egress illumination without requiring separate emergency-only fixtures. Conduit throughout is EMT, IMC, or RMC, minimum 3/4" trade size.

New power and data outlets were distributed across all reconfigured occupancies: open office and cubicle areas, private attorney and director offices, conference room, recording/photo studio, paralegal workstations, reception, break rooms, and copy areas. The outlet layout was coordinated with the architectural partition plan to ensure coverage consistent with the new room configuration.

Electrical design connected to the existing building service infrastructure, which provides both 480Y/277V three-phase four-wire and 208Y/120V three-phase four-wire service to the building. No modifications to the main building electrical service or distribution were required; scope was limited to suite-level branch circuitry and lighting within Suite 117. The electrical package was delivered as part of the multi-discipline 95% construction document submittal in April 2025.
