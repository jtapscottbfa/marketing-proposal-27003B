Plumbing engineering scope on Building 3400 Suite 117 managed the transition from a heavily plumbed medical/clinical suite to a straightforward administrative office plumbing layout at Fort Gregg-Adams, Virginia. The former clinical configuration had included a mop sink, multiple wall-mounted clinical sinks, restroom fixtures and accessories, toilet partitions, and associated supply and drain/waste/vent piping — all of which were removed as part of the selective demolition scope.

New plumbing construction serves two break room locations, each equipped with two sinks for a total of four new sinks within the reconfigured suite. New domestic cold and hot water supply piping is Type L copper above grade and Type K copper below grade, per project specifications. Sanitary waste and vent piping is Schedule 40 PVC. The existing building hot water system was retained and extended to serve the new break room fixtures, avoiding replacement of the central domestic hot water infrastructure. Floor drains are provided at mechanical equipment areas and locations with condensate drainage requirements.

The overall plumbing scope on this project reflects the program change from clinical to administrative occupancy: fixture count and system complexity are substantially reduced from the demolished configuration. The primary design challenge was coordinating the tie-in of new break room supply and drain lines with the existing building plumbing infrastructure after removal of the clinical piping that had previously occupied the same chases and areas. The plumbing design was delivered as part of the multi-discipline 95% construction document submittal in April 2025.
