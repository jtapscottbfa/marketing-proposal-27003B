Mechanical engineering scope on Building 3400 Suite 117 addressed the HVAC reconfiguration required by the complete interior re-partitioning of the approximately 3,700 SF space at Fort Gregg-Adams, Virginia. The suite's prior medical/clinical configuration had established an air distribution layout tied to the former room arrangement; the new administrative office design required a fully reassessed distribution system to serve the reconfigured partition layout and varied room types.

New single-duct variable air volume (VAV) boxes replaced the demolished suite distribution system. Controls for the new VAV boxes were coordinated with the installation's existing building automation system (BAS), maintaining compatibility with the central building controls infrastructure. New low-pressure supply air ductwork was routed through the reconfigured space to serve the new room arrangement, with new supply air diffusers and return air grilles selected, located, and sized for the reconfigured office, conference, studio, and support spaces.

The existing central air handling and chiller plant infrastructure serving Building 3400 was retained and remained in service throughout; the mechanical scope was explicitly limited to the suite-level air distribution system. This boundary-of-scope discipline — modifying only the terminal distribution while maintaining the central plant — was a key design coordination requirement given that soldiers continued training in the building during construction, requiring conditioned air to be maintained in occupied portions of the building at all times.

The mechanical design was delivered as part of the multi-discipline 95% construction document submittal in April 2025.
