Building 3400 Suite 117 is an approximately 3,700 SF interior renovation and reconfiguration at Fort Gregg-Adams, Virginia, executed for the US Army / Fort Gregg-Adams Directorate of Public Works (project number PB00002-4J). The design team served as prime consultant, providing full multi-discipline design services covering architecture, mechanical, electrical, plumbing, and fire protection engineering under a design-bid-build delivery framework.

The project represents a significant program change: Suite 117 previously housed a medical and clinical suite — with exam rooms, optometry, audiology, immunization, and soiled linen facilities — and was completely reconfigured to serve the Public Affairs Office (PAO) and Trial Defense Services (TDS) as contemporary administrative office space. Selective demolition removed all existing clinical finishes, partitions, plumbing fixtures, and casework. New construction introduced metal stud partitions with gypsum wallboard and sound batt insulation (extended to deck above for acoustically sensitive rooms), 2×2 acoustical ceiling tile at 9'-0" finished height, carpet tile flooring, solid core wood doors, break room casework and sinks, LED lighting with integral emergency capability, new VAV boxes and reconfigured air distribution connected to the existing building automation system, and new power and data outlets throughout. A defining constraint was active military occupancy: soldiers continued training in Building 3400 throughout construction, requiring careful phasing and operational coordination. Design was completed through 95% submittal in April 2025.
