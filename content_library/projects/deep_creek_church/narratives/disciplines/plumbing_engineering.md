The plumbing engineering scope for the partial renovation of The Way, The Truth, and The Life Church at 4608 Deep Creek Blvd, Portsmouth, Virginia encompassed a complete replacement of the domestic water and sanitary systems within the renovated restroom suites and adjacent office area. Existing plumbing fixtures, supply connections, and sanitary piping were demolished to the points indicated on the plumbing demolition plans (sheet PD101). The existing 40-gallon gas-fired domestic water heater was designated to remain in service.

New domestic cold and hot water distribution was designed with 1/2-inch CW and HW branches to the counter-mounted lavatories and 3/4-inch CW/HW branches to the floor-mounted shower. Top-mounted centerset faucets with a 0.5 GPM flow rate were specified at lavatories. To ensure reliable hot water delivery without excessive draw-off time, a hot water recirculation loop was incorporated — RCP-1, an inline pump rated at 5 GPM and 0.5 HP, 120V/1-phase. New fixtures include flush-tank water closets (one ADA-accessible floor-mounted unit and one standard floor-mounted unit), a flush-valve wall-mounted urinal, counter-mounted lavatories, and a floor shower. The new sanitary waste and vent system used 2-inch to 4-inch piping tied into the existing building sanitary lines, with 2-inch floor drains, 4-inch floor clean-outs, and a new vent through roof (VTR). All new sanitary rough-in required below-slab trenching in the existing slab-on-grade construction, with saw-cut and patched slabs per the coordinated architectural construction details. Plumbing design was documented on BFA progress drawings PD101 (demolition) and P-101 (new work), issued July 25, 2024.
