The mechanical engineering scope for the partial renovation of The Way, The Truth, and The Life Church at 4608 Deep Creek Blvd, Portsmouth, Virginia covered the design of restroom exhaust ventilation systems for two new restroom suites — mens and womens — within the renovated tenant space. The overall building's heating and cooling was not within the mechanical scope; the existing building systems were presumed to serve general space conditioning, and the mechanical design addressed only the code-required exhaust ventilation for the new restroom suites.

Two Greenheck inline cabinet exhaust fans were selected and designed for the project. EF-1 (Greenheck CSP-A390) serves the mens restroom at 220 CFM, 0.5 ESP, 1,035 RPM, 115V/1-phase, with 1.42 FLA. EF-2 (Greenheck CSP-A250) serves the womens restroom at 200 CFM, 0.47 ESP, 1,000 RPM, 115V/1-phase, with 0.56 FLA. Ductwork at both suites was routed at the ceiling level, with supply grilles and exhaust grilles scheduled on the mechanical new work plan. Wall-mounted gravity dampers were specified at each exhaust discharge point to prevent back-drafting when fans are off, and manual balancing dampers were included within the duct runs to facilitate field-balancing of airflow. Each exhaust fan is provided with a dedicated disconnect switch and is controlled by an occupancy sensor, ensuring ventilation operates only when the restrooms are occupied. Pre-submittal equipment cut sheets for both fans were produced as part of the design documentation.
