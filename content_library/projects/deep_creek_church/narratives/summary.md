The Way, The Truth, and The Life Church engaged a design team to partially renovate its existing tenant space at 4608 Deep Creek Blvd, Portsmouth, Virginia — replacing aging restroom facilities with new, code-compliant mens and womens restroom suites and an adjacent office area. The scope involved full demolition of existing partitions, plumbing rough-in, and fixtures, followed by construction of new restroom suites with ADA-accessible fixtures, new interior finishes, new doors and hardware, and a new suspended ceiling system. The mechanical engineering firm delivered exhaust ventilation for both restroom suites via two Greenheck inline cabinet exhaust fans (EF-1 at 220 CFM serving the mens restroom; EF-2 at 200 CFM serving the womens restroom), with occupancy-sensor controls and wall-mounted gravity dampers at each discharge point. The plumbing engineering scope encompassed a complete new domestic cold and hot water distribution system, a hot water recirculation loop (RCP-1 inline pump, 5 GPM), and a new sanitary waste and vent system with below-slab rough-in — requiring coordinated slab saw-cutting and patching within the existing slab-on-grade structure. The existing 40-gallon gas-fired water heater was retained. Fixtures include flush-tank water closets (ADA-accessible and standard), a flush-valve urinal, low-flow lavatories (0.5 GPM faucets), and a shower. The project was delivered as a design-bid-build standalone engagement, with the MEP engineer serving as subconsultant under the architect of record, Bondurant Associates Engineering & Architecture. The project is complete.
