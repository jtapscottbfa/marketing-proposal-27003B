Structural engineering scope on this project focused on the design of new concrete equipment pads and foundation systems for the replacement medium-voltage electrical equipment. Broyles & Associates, PC served as the structural subconsultant.

New reinforced concrete equipment pads — 8 inches thick, positioned 2 feet above grade — were designed to support the SF6 gas switches and junction boxes at each field site. Anchor bolt systems, using 3/4-inch diameter anchors, were designed to provide resistance to lateral overturning forces acting on the switch equipment. Structural scope also included an assessment of existing structural conditions relevant to the equipment installation sites.

Transformer pad construction — reinforced concrete with dimensions coordinated to match the new pad-mounted transformer footprints — was developed in coordination with the civil and electrical disciplines to ensure correct pad geometry, embedment locations, and orientation relative to electrical service entries. Equipment pad elevations and drainage were coordinated with the civil grading and ductbank design to ensure compatibility across each of the six field sites at the installation.

Geotechnical findings from GeoEnvironmental Resources informed the foundation design approach, including bearing assumptions and subgrade conditions, for all new concrete pad elements.
