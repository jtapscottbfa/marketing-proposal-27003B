Civil engineering scope covered the site design and infrastructure work required to support the medium-voltage electrical replacement across six field sites within the north-central portion of Naval Station Norfolk. Hoggard-Eure Associates, PC served as the civil subconsultant, working under the prime consultant's coordination.

Site work began with topographic survey and underground utility marking to define existing conditions prior to excavation. New concrete-encased ductbanks were designed to route replacement medium-voltage cabling between switch sites, junction boxes, and building transformers, with pavement patching at road and sidewalk crossings to restore base infrastructure after trench construction. Transformer pads were designed in reinforced concrete, positioned 2 feet above grade, to accommodate new pad-mounted transformers and the structural anchor requirements for the replacement switch equipment.

Erosion and sediment control measures — including silt fence and inlet protection — were designed to meet NAVFAC environmental requirements during active construction. Tree replacement per the request for proposal was also included in the civil scope. Civil design was coordinated closely with the electrical discipline for ductbank routing and with the structural discipline for equipment pad elevations and drainage compatibility across each of the six discrete field sites within the installation.
