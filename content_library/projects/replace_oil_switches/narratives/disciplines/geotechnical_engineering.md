Geotechnical engineering services were provided by GeoEnvironmental Resources, Inc. as subconsultant to the prime design team. The geotechnical scope supported the foundation and subgrade design for the project's new concrete equipment pads and ductbank construction across the six field sites within the north-central portion of Naval Station Norfolk.

Subsurface investigation findings informed the structural design of reinforced concrete equipment pads — 8-inch-thick slabs positioned 2 feet above grade — and the anchor bolt systems designed to resist lateral overturning loads on the SF6 gas switches and junction boxes. Geotechnical data also informed the civil discipline's ductbank trench design and pavement subgrade conditions at road and sidewalk crossing locations.

Specific details of the subsurface investigation — including boring locations, soil classifications, bearing capacity values, and groundwater conditions — are not captured in the available source record and reside in the geotechnical report produced by GeoEnvironmental Resources. Coordination with the structural and civil disciplines translated geotechnical findings into foundation design parameters and trench support requirements for the project's below-grade scope elements.
