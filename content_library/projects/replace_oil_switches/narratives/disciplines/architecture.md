Architectural scope on this project was limited to a single building element: replacement of a damaged exterior door on Building SP-62. The replacement door is a 3 feet 4 inches by 7 feet 2.5 inches steel hollow metal unit with new hardware. Building SP-62 is one of the base facilities served by the replacement pad-mounted transformer included in the electrical scope.

The door replacement was included in the project package to address a deteriorated building enclosure condition at the utility access point and to meet NAVFAC facility standards. Architecture served as a listed discipline on the design team; the scope was incidental to the primary utility infrastructure focus of the project, and no broader architectural design, space planning, or building systems work was within the contract. The architectural work was coordinated with the electrical transformer replacement scope for Building SP-62 to ensure construction access and sequencing compatibility during the broader utility replacement effort.
