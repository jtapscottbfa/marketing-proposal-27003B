This project involved complete design services for the replacement of three hazardous oil-filled medium-voltage switches at Naval Station Norfolk, Virginia — Switches 117, 129, and 143 — with modern SF6 gas-insulated switches, under a NAVFAC Mid-Atlantic (NAVFAC MIDLANT) indefinite-delivery, indefinite-quantity design contract (Contract N40085-20-D-0005, eProject WO 1582361). Additional scope encompassed demolition of a fourth oil switch (Switch 196) at the intersection of Bellinger Boulevard and Fifth Avenue; replacement of deteriorated pad-mounted transformers serving Buildings V-66, SP-368, and SP-62 with new units providing 11.5 kV primary and 208V three-phase secondary service; sectionalizing of loads to vacuum fault interrupter (VFI) protected switch ways for improved fault isolation; replacement of failing medium-voltage cabling in the SP and V areas (11.5 kV and 4160V feeders); new concrete-encased ductbank construction; and pavement restoration. Hazardous material abatement addressed lead-jacketed cables, oil-filled switch equipment, and asbestos fireproofing in underground manholes. The program budget was approximately $3.85 million, with an estimated construction cost of $3.5 million; the 100% design submittal was delivered in February 2023, with construction estimated to complete in September 2023.

As prime consultant, the design team coordinated a multi-discipline subconsultant team covering civil site engineering, structural foundation design, geotechnical investigation, and construction cost estimating. The project required maintaining base utility continuity during an active-installation construction sequence while meeting NAVFAC environmental and safety requirements for hazardous material handling across six discrete field sites within the installation.
