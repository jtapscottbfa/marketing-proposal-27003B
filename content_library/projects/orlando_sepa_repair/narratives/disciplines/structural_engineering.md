Structural engineering on the NOSC Orlando SEPA Repairs project addressed the foundation and anchoring requirements for the new K-rated vehicle barrier systems installed at the facility perimeter. The structural scope was concentrated in the structural foundation sub-discipline, supporting the civil and security engineering work at the entry control points and along the north perimeter fence.

K-rated vehicle barrier systems — including cantilevered sliding vehicle gates and anti-ram cable reinforcement — impose specific foundation and anchor loading requirements derived from vehicle crash-test rating standards. The structural engineering effort on this project was directed at designing gate foundation systems and cable anchor assemblies capable of meeting the required K-rating under vehicle impact loading, integrated into the existing site conditions at the Armed Forces Reserve Center perimeter.

Structural engineering was included as one of the disciplines covered in the December 2022 site survey conducted by the design team, alongside architectural, civil, security, and electrical survey work. The site survey documented existing conditions at the perimeter and within the facility to inform structural design decisions for the barrier foundation systems.

Specific structural calculations, foundation configurations, and anchor system details are not captured in depth in the available source record beyond the identification of structural foundation as the relevant sub-discipline scope. The structural effort was a supporting discipline on a project where security engineering and civil site work defined the primary scope.
