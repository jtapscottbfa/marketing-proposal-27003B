Civil engineering scope on the NOSC Orlando SEPA Repairs project addressed site improvements at the perimeter of the Armed Forces Reserve Center, with primary work at the two vehicle entry control points (ECPs) off Dowden Road, along the north perimeter fence, and at the rear loading dock.

At the west ECP, the existing cantilevered sliding vehicle gate and gate pedestals were demolished and replaced with a new 24-foot K-rated cantilevered sliding gate. At the east ECP, the existing gate assembly was similarly removed and replaced with a 20-foot K-rated cantilevered sliding gate. Both new gates satisfy vehicle barrier rating requirements consistent with ATFP criteria for NOSC-class facilities. At the rear loading dock, a K-rated manual drop-arm gate was added at the vehicle access point.

Along the north perimeter fence, K-rated anti-ram cable reinforcement was installed in two runs: approximately 100 linear feet along the west portion and approximately 420 linear feet along the east portion, providing approximately 520 total linear feet of cable barrier coverage along the north perimeter.

At the primary entry, two ingress/egress pedestrian turnstiles replaced the existing man gate, with associated foundations and site improvements to accommodate the new turnstile structures and their security hardware.

Site utility work supported the routing of electrical conduit for the security system infrastructure from the building to the perimeter gate and turnstile locations. Civil site design was documented at 1-inch-equals-20-foot and 1-inch-equals-30-foot scales. A civil site survey was conducted in December 2022 to document existing conditions prior to design-build execution.
