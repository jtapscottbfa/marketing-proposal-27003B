The architecture discipline scope on the NOSC Orlando SEPA Repairs project addressed security-driven modifications to the existing multi-story, multi-tenant facility — a three-floor complex comprising the Wilson, Taft, and Smedley Buildings within the Armed Forces Reserve Center at NSA Orlando. Architectural work concentrated on two primary categories: glazing security and interior door hardware.

All exterior windows and glazed doors throughout the facility received fragmentation retention film — 3M Ultra Night Vision S25, an 8-mil film applied using mechanical attachment — to mitigate glass fragmentation hazard in a blast or ballistic event. This measure addressed a documented ATFP vulnerability identified under the Navy's programmatic security evaluation of NOSC facilities following the 2015 Chattanooga attack.

Interior door hardware was upgraded at selected locations throughout the facility to add deadbolts and security hardware, supporting a shelter-in-place capability for occupants. Door and hardware work was documented in a door schedule encompassing approximately 30 or more door locations, including restroom and examination room doors identified for deadbolt additions.

The architectural survey conducted in December 2022 documented the floor plan configuration of the occupied portions of the facility — Navy and USMC spaces on the first and second floors, organized across designated building areas — as well as the third floor, which was unoccupied at the time of survey and identified as slated for demolition under a separate contract. The survey established the existing conditions baseline for the design-build team's final architectural design and construction documentation.
