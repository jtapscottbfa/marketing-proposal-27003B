Electrical engineering scope on the NOSC Orlando SEPA Repairs project provided the power distribution infrastructure for the new vehicle barrier, pedestrian access control, and electronic security systems. Work was concentrated at the existing electrical panels serving the facility and extended to the perimeter gate and turnstile locations via new conduit runs.

New branch circuits were added to the existing normal power panel L1-11 (208Y/120V, 225A bus, Section 2 of 2, located in Room S105) to serve the new security infrastructure: 30-ampere circuits at Circuits 68 and 72 for the two vehicle gate operators, 20-ampere circuits at Circuits 66 and 70 for the two pedestrian turnstile locations, and an additional 20-ampere circuit (Circuits 74/76) for the security system workstation. A dedicated 20-ampere circuit was also provided for the new security headend equipment rack installed in Room S109 Telecom. The existing emergency panel EL1-11 (208Y/120V, 125A main circuit breaker, located in Room W120) served the existing cameras and gate circuits and was retained.

Conduit infrastructure for the security systems required long runs from the electrical room to the perimeter gate and turnstile locations: approximately 556 feet to the west turnstile, approximately 566 feet to the west gate, approximately 442 feet to the east turnstile, and approximately 416 feet to the east gate. Voltage drop calculations were performed and confirmed within the 3% threshold, with conductor sizing of #1/0 copper for the west gate run and #1 copper for the east gate run to maintain acceptable voltage levels over the extended distances.

The electrical discipline was included in the December 2022 field site survey to document existing panel configurations, available capacity, and conduit routing prior to design-build execution.
