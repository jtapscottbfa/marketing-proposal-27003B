The NOSC Orlando SEPA (Security Engineering Program Assistance) Repairs project delivered a comprehensive set of physical security upgrades to the Navy Operational Support Center (NOSC) located within the Armed Forces Reserve Center at Naval Support Activity (NSA) Orlando, Florida. Following the July 2015 terrorist attack on the Chattanooga, Tennessee, Navy Operational Support Center, the Department of the Navy commissioned security evaluations and programmatic upgrades at 21 NOSCs nationwide; this project addresses the Orlando installation.

Work encompassed anti-terrorism/force protection (ATFP) improvements across the existing multi-story, multi-tenant facility and its perimeter: two new K-rated cantilevered sliding vehicle gates at the primary entry control points (ECPs) off Dowden Road, two ingress/egress pedestrian turnstiles with CAC card readers, a K-rated drop-arm gate at the rear loading dock, and K-rated anti-ram cable reinforcement along the north perimeter fence. The electronic security system was fully modernized — a Hirsch/Identiv Velocity access control system with CAC-compatible readers at all exterior access doors, a CCTV upgrade to six IP cameras with a 16-channel hybrid DVR, and Aiphone intercom/speakerphone stations at all gates and turnstiles. Fragmentation retention film was applied to all exterior windows and glazed doors, and interior door hardware was upgraded to support shelter-in-place capability. Project delivery was design-build, procured as a Section 8(a) direct award by NAVFAC Southeast, with a construction magnitude between $1,000,000 and $5,000,000 and a contractual performance period of 180 days. A licensed design team served as designer of record under the design-build prime contractor, performing a field site survey in December 2022 across architectural, structural, civil, security, and electrical disciplines to document current conditions before design execution.
