The NIT Pier 3 South – Power Feeder Serving Pier project delivered a new medium-voltage electrical power feeder at Norfolk International Terminals (NIT), Pier 3 South, in Norfolk, Virginia, for Talton Marine Terminals, LLC. Serving as prime consultant, the design team provided complete electrical engineering for a new distribution circuit extending approximately 635 linear feet along the pier from the Dominion Energy 34.5 kV overhead utility service to a new pad-mounted transformer at the pier end. The distribution system includes a utility tap using #1/0 ACSR aluminum conductor, a 600-ampere/35 kV S&C Alduti-Rupter gang-operated sectionalizing switch, 60-ampere fused cutouts with 27 kV surge arrestors, and a 2,500/2,800-kVA (OA/FA) pad-mounted transformer stepping the 34,500-volt primary supply down to 480Y/277-volt, three-phase, 60-hertz secondary power for the pier's electrical loads. The conduit system routes 6-inch steel conduit in concrete encasement from the utility poles to the pier edge, transitioning to 6-inch rigid pipe conduit surface-mounted on the pier deck face with stainless steel banding and treated timber blocking at four-foot intervals for the pier run. Design coordination with Dominion Energy addressed the utility pole connection approach. The project was completed as a final submittal in August 2021 (BFA project no. 21-037) under a design-bid-build delivery structure.
