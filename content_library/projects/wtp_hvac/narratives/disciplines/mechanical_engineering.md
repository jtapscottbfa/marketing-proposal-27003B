Mechanical engineering scope on the Gloucester SWTP HVAC project encompassed the complete HVAC design and specification for two systems at Gloucester County's Surface Water Treatment Plant complex. BFA led all mechanical engineering work as prime consultant, producing the full 100% HVAC specification issued in Addendum No. 3 to the county's Invitation for Bids.

For the Reverse Osmosis (RO) Plant Server Room, mechanical engineering scope included equipment selection and sizing for a replacement 10-ton split system (Trane TWA120044 outdoor heat pump / Trane TWE12044 air handler), specification of 3-phase 460V electrical integration requirements, and removal and disposal protocols for the existing unit.

For the Water Treatment Plant (WTP) Lab, mechanical engineering scope included design of a new 4-ton multi-port mini-split heat pump system (Trane/Mitsubishi MXZ-SM48) with two 2-ton ceiling cassette indoor air handling units (PLA-A24EA8) and branch selector box (PAC-MKA32BC). Outside makeup air was designed to meet ASHRAE 62.1 requirements: 6-inch intake ducts with 16×8 flanged storm blade wall louvers and volume dampers, balanced to 150 CFM. Abandonment of the existing lab HVAC system and electrical rerouting requirements were included in the mechanical scope. Pre-construction coordination requirements with the County Engineer for duct routing and makeup air discharge location were specified as part of the installation protocol.

BFA's mechanical engineering deliverable covered all equipment specifications, installation requirements, testing and balancing criteria, and contractor coordination protocols for both scopes.
