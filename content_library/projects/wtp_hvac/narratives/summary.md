The Gloucester SWTP HVAC project addressed failing environmental control systems at two process buildings within Gloucester County's Surface Water Treatment Plant complex at 8929 Reservoir Ridge Road, Gloucester County, Virginia. BFA served as prime consultant, providing complete HVAC design and procurement specification services for both scopes.

Scope 1 covered the Reverse Osmosis (RO) Plant Server Room: removal and disposal of the existing HVAC unit and installation of a replacement 10-ton split system consisting of a Trane TWA120044 heat pump and Trane TWE12044 air handler, served by 3-phase 460V electrical. Scope 2 covered the Water Treatment Plant (WTP) Lab: abandonment of the existing lab HVAC system and installation of a new 4-ton multi-port mini-split heat pump (Trane/Mitsubishi MXZ-SM48) with two 2-ton ceiling cassette indoor air handlers (PLA-A24EA8) and a branch selector box (PAC-MKA32BC). The Lab scope also included outside makeup air introduced through 6-inch intake ducts fitted with 16×8 flanged storm blade wall louvers and volume dampers, balanced to 150 CFM per ASHRAE 62.1. High-voltage wiring was rerouted from the abandoned lab system to serve the new equipment.

BFA issued the complete HVAC specification as Addendum No. 3 to Gloucester County's Invitation for Bids — a 100% design submittal dated June 28, 2024 — with construction desired to complete by June 30, 2024. The project was procured as a standalone design-bid-build contract under local competitive bidding and is assessed as complete.
