# Mechanical Engineering — Renovate for Human Performance Program, Building 3855E, JEBLCFS

The mechanical engineering scope for this project covered HVAC design for the conversion of Building 3855E at JEBLCFS from warehouse to an active military training and human performance facility. The central challenge is thermal load: warehouse buildings are conditioned minimally, while high-occupancy athletic training spaces generate substantial sensible and latent cooling loads driven by physical activity and occupant density. A gymnasium or human performance training room requires conditioning systems designed for peak occupancy and activity levels that differ significantly from office, storage, or standard assembly occupancy profiles.

The project scope — warehouse reconfiguration to spillover training and gym space — required new HVAC systems capable of maintaining appropriate thermal conditions and ventilation rates for the intended training population. Applicable NAVFAC UFC standards govern HVAC design criteria for this facility type on a military installation. The mechanical design was developed as part of a design-build task order under the NAVFAC MIDLANT 8(a) MACC, with the design team serving as subconsultant to the design-build contractor.
