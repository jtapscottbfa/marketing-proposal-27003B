# Summary — Renovate for Human Performance Program, Building 3855E, JEBLCFS

The project renovated Building 3855E at Joint Expeditionary Base Little Creek – Fort Story (JEBLCFS), Virginia Beach, Virginia, converting an existing warehouse into spillover training and gymnasium space for the Navy's Human Performance Program. The facility expands physical training capacity on the installation, addressing demand generated by the primary Human Performance Program building (Building 3855A). The project was procured by Naval Facilities Engineering Command, Mid-Atlantic (NAVFAC MIDLANT) as a firm fixed-price task order under an 8(a) design-build Multiple Award Construction Contract (MACC), RFP N40085-21-R-8721, issued July 2021. The estimated construction value was between $500,000 and $1,000,000, with a contractual duration of 200 calendar days from award.

The design team served as mechanical and electrical engineering subconsultant to the design-build contractor. Scope included HVAC design, electrical power distribution, and lighting for the renovated training facility, along with selective demolition and finishes appropriate for a high-activity athletic training environment. The conversion from warehouse to training space required significant MEP systems upgrades to meet the occupancy loads and functional requirements of a human performance facility. The project is assessed as complete based on elapsed time since the 2021 procurement.
