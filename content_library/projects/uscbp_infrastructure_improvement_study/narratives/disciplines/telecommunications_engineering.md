The telecommunications engineering scope for the CBP Advanced Training Center Infrastructure Improvements Analysis covered analysis and documentation of the campus's communications infrastructure, including both external service topology and internal distribution. The campus was served by two providers: Comcast (delivering coaxial and fiber service) and Frontier/Verizon (copper-based, providing analog telephone service). Potomac Hall was documented as the primary hub for campus communications distribution.

The underground communications infrastructure was documented in detail, including a network of communications manholes (A through S, with variants) and handholes (CP1 through CP24 and additional units), captured on drawing sheets E-503, E-504, and E-505 at 1/4"=1' and 3/8"=1' scales respectively. Each manhole detail confirmed conduit stub-out connections to specific campus buildings, mapping the full structured cabling distribution topology across dormitories, the dining facility, administrative buildings, and training facilities. The minimum manhole size for communications infrastructure was 12' L × 8' W × 8' H. One handhole on the E-502 drawing set was noted as a mixed-use unit carrying both power and communications designation, flagging a potential labeling discrepancy in the existing infrastructure. Communications improvements were incorporated into the study's phased capital improvement program spanning FY2022 through FY2042.
