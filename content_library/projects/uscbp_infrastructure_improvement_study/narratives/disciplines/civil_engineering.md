Civil engineering disciplines contributed to the CBP Advanced Training Center Infrastructure Improvements Analysis through assessment of campus-wide site utility infrastructure — principally the underground utility distribution network, site stormwater management, and the spatial organization of utility corridors across the 116-acre campus. The study evaluated the condition and capacity of potable water mains, sanitary sewer gravity mains and the associated pump station, irrigation mains, and related site civil infrastructure in the context of the campus's planned 25-year build-out.

The study was structured around the campus development program established in the 2017 Area Development Execution Plan (ADEP), which defined new facility projects in short-, mid-, and long-range segments. Civil utility analysis established baseline existing-conditions data and identified infrastructure improvements needed to support each new facility addition across the five ADEP fiscal year windows (FY2022 through FY2042). Of the 26 infrastructure-affecting capital projects identified, civil utility work — water, sewer, and site distribution — constituted a portion of the recommended capital program. The source record does not separately attribute the civil utilities analysis scope to specific team members; the documented team attribution assigns electrical and communications analysis to the design firm as the primary subconsultant discipline, with civil utility scope performed as part of the broader team effort under the prime A/E.
