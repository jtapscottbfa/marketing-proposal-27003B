The electrical engineering scope for the CBP Advanced Training Center Infrastructure Improvements Analysis constituted the design team's primary documented discipline contribution to the study. The existing campus electrical system was served by a 12.5kV, 3-phase overhead distribution circuit from Potomac Edison (First Energy) entering the 116-acre campus at its northern boundary along US Route 340. A critical finding of the analysis was that this circuit was at or near its rated capacity, representing a significant constraint on the campus's ability to accommodate the planned 25-year build-out defined in the 2017 Area Development Execution Plan.

The electrical scope included field verification and documentation of the full underground ductbank infrastructure serving the campus — more than 63 power manholes and handholes (documented on drawing sheets E-501 and E-502) connecting campus buildings via an extensive underground distribution network. Electrical site plan sheets ES-101 and ES-102 provide a complete existing-conditions map of power manhole and handhole locations at 1"=80' scale across both the upper (north) and lower (south/training) campus areas. Findings were translated into a phased capital improvement program with electrical infrastructure upgrades distributed across five fiscal year windows from FY2022 through FY2042, as part of a projected $4,502,466 total campus infrastructure investment.
