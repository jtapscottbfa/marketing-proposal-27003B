The design team performed an Infrastructure Improvements Analysis (IIA) for the U.S. Customs and Border Protection (CBP) Advanced Training Center (ATC) in Harpers Ferry, West Virginia — a 116-acre campus supporting federal law enforcement training operations including dormitories, dining facilities, a tactical training center, administrative buildings, firearms ranges, and associated support infrastructure. The engagement was delivered as a subconsultant to GORDON Engineering under a contract held by IntegriWard, LLC, with the IIA 95% submission completed in May 2021. The study was scoped under the framework of CBP's 2017 Area Development Execution Plan (ADEP) and evaluated all major campus utility systems — potable water, fire suppression, irrigation, sanitary sewer, electrical distribution, and communications — against the campus's projected 25-year build-out through FY2042.

The study produced a phased infrastructure improvement program comprising 26 capital projects scheduled across five fiscal year windows (FY2022, FY2027, FY2032, FY2037, and FY2042), with a total projected construction cost of approximately $4,502,466. A critical finding was that the existing 12.5kV Potomac Edison electrical service circuit was at or near capacity, necessitating phased electrical infrastructure upgrades to support planned campus expansion. The electrical and communications scope included field-verified documentation of more than 63 power manholes and handholes and an extensive communications ductbank network serving the full campus. Deliverables included the IIA report, a detailed cost estimate, and a seven-sheet electrical and communications site plan and infrastructure detail drawing set.
