The Gloucester County Well No. 1 Electrical Coordination Study was performed for Gloucester County, Virginia, on the electrical distribution system serving Well Pump No. 1 at the Raw Water No. 2 Treatment Plant in Gloucester, VA. The engagement encompassed a field survey to generate a system model in SKM Power Tools, a short circuit study, equipment evaluation against ANSI fault analysis criteria, time current curve (TCC) development, and formulation of protective device setting recommendations in accordance with ANSI/NEMA MTS-2019 and NEC 2023.

The distribution system under study includes a 1,500 kVA utility transformer stepping down from 34,500 V to 480Y/277 V, a 3,000-ampere main switchboard, a 600-ampere distribution panel (PNL DP-1) serving the well pump starter, a 100 HP / 460 V well pump motor, and emergency generator backup through a transfer switch. The short circuit study established a utility three-phase fault contribution of 2,528 amperes at the service entrance.

Equipment evaluation confirmed that all protective devices in the system passed the ANSI Fault Analysis criteria — no equipment was found to have insufficient interrupting or withstand ratings relative to available fault currents. The coordination analysis determined that full selective coordination of the overcurrent protection devices is not achievable for this system configuration; however, compromised coordination is attainable by applying the recommended instantaneous trip settings. NEC 2023 provisions provide regulatory grounding for the two 600-ampere circuit breakers in series that are not required to be selectively coordinated. The study was completed June 2024.
