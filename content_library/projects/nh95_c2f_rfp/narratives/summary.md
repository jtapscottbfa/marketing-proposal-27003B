The design team served as prime architect-engineer consultant to Naval Facilities Engineering Systems Command Mid-Atlantic (NAVFAC MIDLANT) under single-award IDIQ contract N4008520D005, delivering a Design-Build RFP package for the conversion of approximately 20,810 square feet within Building NH-95 at Naval Support Activity Hampton Roads, Norfolk, Virginia. Building NH-95 is the installation's Headquarters Operational Control Center — a 275,409 SF, two-story structure constructed in 1959 — and the project repurposed interior space on both floors to accommodate Commander, U.S. Second Fleet (C2F) and Defense Information Systems Agency (DISA) personnel.

The scope encompassed a four-day design charrette producing the final preferred concept (Concept K), DD Form 1391 in both Design-Build and Design-Bid-Build versions, a parametric cost estimate, a Value Engineering study yielding approximately $5.16 million in net savings, and a complete Design-Build RFP package (Parts 1–6). Programming addressed two primary tenants: C2F (18,241 SF, including a Flag Suite with senior leadership offices, executive kitchenette, conference and dining, and open-office operational space) and DISA (2,583 SF of secure offices and work rooms). The post-charrette estimated construction cost was $23,242,000; the total project cost per the DD Form 1391 Design-Build version was $27,800,000. The Final Charrette Report was completed September 2024, with the corrected DB RFP scheduled for submission January 2025. The design team included full A-E disciplines: architecture, interior design, mechanical, electrical, plumbing, fire protection, structural, telecommunications, security, and cost estimating.
