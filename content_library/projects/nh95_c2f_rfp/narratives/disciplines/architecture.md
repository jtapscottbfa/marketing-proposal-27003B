The architecture team led schematic design and Design-Build RFP criteria development for the interior renovation of approximately 20,810 SF across two floors of Building NH-95 at NSA Hampton Roads. Working through a four-day design charrette, the team developed ten iterative concept plans (Concepts A through K) before arriving at the final preferred solution. The architectural program organized the first floor into two C2F zones: Area 6 (9 private offices, 70 workstations plus 11 existing workstations in rooms 132A–C, a large conference room, open conference area, and break area) and Area 11 (Flag Suite with private offices for the COM at 302 SF, VCOM, DCOM, RVCOM, COS, RCOS, and FMC, plus executive kitchenette at 276 SF, conference and dining room at 669 SF, storage at 134 SF, private and shared toilet/shower facilities, and a new secure exterior entrance with vestibule). The second floor provided C2F Area 8/10 (2 private offices, 34 workstations, open conference) and DISA Area 10 (3 private offices, 12 workstations, work room, secure comm/electrical room, and ramp). Schematic concept plans were produced at 1/32" = 1'-0" and 1/8" = 1'-0" scales. The architecture team compiled the full room schedule tabulating net areas for each space, produced the architectural DB RFP criteria documents, and coordinated demolition scope for a 1959-vintage building with potential hazardous materials in renovation areas.
