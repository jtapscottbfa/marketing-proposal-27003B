Electrical engineering scope addressed power distribution, lighting, and low-voltage infrastructure throughout the approximately 20,810 SF renovation. All existing lighting was removed and replaced with new LED fixtures specified at 4000K color temperature and 80+ CRI, controlled by vacancy sensors throughout. New wiring devices and branch circuits were provided from existing panelboards, avoiding the cost of new motor control centers. Communications distribution systems throughout the renovation areas were removed and replaced entirely. Emergency lighting was provided via standalone LED fixture types. The scope also included Electronic Security System (ESS) pathway provisions: the design-builder provides conduit, pull boxes, and infrastructure, while the government separately furnishes and installs security devices. Government-furnished fiber-optic cable trunks were routed through contractor-provided pathways. Telecommunications cabling provisions for NIPR (Non-classified Internet Protocol Router), SIPR (Secret Internet Protocol Router), and NMCI (Navy Marine Corps Intranet) networks were specified within the electrical and low-voltage criteria documents. Electrical engineering produced power, lighting, and low-voltage RFP criteria for the Design-Build package.
