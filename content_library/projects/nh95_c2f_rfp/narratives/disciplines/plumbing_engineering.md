Plumbing engineering scope was driven primarily by the Area 11 Flag Suite program requirements and the executive kitchenette. New restroom facilities in Area 11 were designed as a ladies' restroom (three fixtures) and an accessible unisex restroom (one fixture) — a two-unit configuration accepted as Value Engineering item VE A-1.0, which reduced the scope and cost relative to the originally programmed restroom arrangement. The executive kitchenette included a three-compartment sink, garbage disposal, and domestic hot and cold water service. Flush valve water closets compliant with ASME A112.19.2 were specified. Domestic water piping was specified as copper (ASTM B88, Type K, L, or M); drainage, waste, and vent (DWV) piping was specified as cast iron. Bi-level water coolers and electric water heaters (dual element, 4.5 kW each) were included in the plumbing scope. The plumbing engineer produced performance specifications and fixture criteria for inclusion in the Design-Build RFP package.
