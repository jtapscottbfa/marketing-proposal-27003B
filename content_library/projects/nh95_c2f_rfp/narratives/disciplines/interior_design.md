Interior design scope encompassed space planning, finish specifications, and furnishings criteria for approximately 20,810 SF of federal office and support space within a 1959 headquarters building. The program included open-plan workstation areas, private enclosed offices, a Flag Suite for senior naval leadership, an executive kitchenette, conference and dining facilities, and restroom and break areas. Interior finishes in scope included flooring, acoustical ceiling systems, drywall and plaster partitions, painting, and wallcovering. Toilet partition specialties and building signage were also included in the construction scope. Interior design contributed criteria documents to the Design-Build RFP package, establishing finish, furniture, and space planning performance requirements for the design-build contractor to meet during final design and construction execution.
