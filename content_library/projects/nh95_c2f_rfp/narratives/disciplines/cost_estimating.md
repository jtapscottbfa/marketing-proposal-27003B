Cost estimating was an integral discipline in the Design-Build RFP development process, directly supporting the four-day design charrette and the government's program documentation requirements. The cost estimating team produced a parametric cost estimate for the final preferred concept (Concept K) and supported the preparation of DD Form 1391 in both Design-Build and Design-Bid-Build versions for government review. A formal Value Engineering study, conducted as part of the charrette, evaluated multiple scope and system alternatives; accepted VE items yielded a net savings of approximately $5,162,000, reducing the post-charrette Estimated Construction Cost to $23,242,000. The DD Form 1391 Design-Build version total project cost was $27,800,000 (inclusive of contingency and supervision, inspection, and overhead); the DBB version was $27,000,000. The SAES-established program budget was $39,000,000. The cost estimate assumed a 360-calendar-day construction duration with an assumed start of January 2026 and completion of January 2027, used as the basis for escalation and midpoint cost calculations.
