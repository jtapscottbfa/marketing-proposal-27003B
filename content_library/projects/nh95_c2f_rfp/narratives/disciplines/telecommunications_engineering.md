Telecommunications engineering addressed structured cabling, audiovisual systems, and network infrastructure for the approximately 20,810 SF renovation. Communications distribution systems throughout the renovation areas were removed and replaced entirely. New telecommunications cabling provisions were specified for NIPR (Non-classified Internet Protocol Router), SIPR (Secret Internet Protocol Router), and NMCI (Navy Marine Corps Intranet) networks. Government-furnished fiber-optic cable trunks were accommodated through contractor-provided conduit pathways. Audiovisual systems were included in the scope. The telecommunications discipline produced conduit and pathway infrastructure criteria, cabling performance specifications, and AV system criteria for the Design-Build RFP package — establishing the requirements for the contractor's final telecommunications design while accounting for the government-furnished/contractor-installed (GFCI) nature of certain network and security-related equipment.
