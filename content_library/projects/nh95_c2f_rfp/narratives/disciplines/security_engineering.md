Security engineering addressed physical and electronic security system infrastructure for the renovation, including access control and video surveillance provisions. The Electronic Security System (ESS) scope followed a government-furnished/contractor-installed split: the design-builder provides conduit, pull boxes, and pathway infrastructure, while the government separately furnishes and installs the electronic security devices. The Flag Suite (Area 11) included a new secure exterior entrance with vestibule and waiting area, providing a controlled access point for the C2F senior leadership area. The DISA space on the second floor (Area 10) included a dedicated secure communications and electrical room. Access control system (ACS) and video surveillance (CCTV) pathway criteria were included in the Design-Build RFP package as part of the Electronic Safety and Security (ESS) scope documents.
