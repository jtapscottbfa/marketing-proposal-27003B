Fire protection engineering required close coordination with a concurrent fire protection system repairs project already under construction in Building NH-95 (contract N4008522R2634, NAVFAC project ST19-0641). That concurrent project was replacing the building's existing dry and pre-action sprinkler systems with wet pipe systems; the NH-95 renovation project reconfigured the resulting wet pipe sprinkler coverage to serve the new floor plan layouts throughout the approximately 20,810 SF renovation areas. A clean agent suppression system originally programmed for Area 11 Room 044 was deleted from scope as Value Engineering accepted item VE M-2.0. Fire alarm and mass notification system devices were relocated to serve renovated space layouts, with new device locations coordinated with the architectural and mechanical disciplines. All fire protection work was specified per UFC 3-600-01, NFPA 13, and NFPA 72. Fire protection engineering produced suppression system and fire alarm criteria for the Design-Build RFP package, establishing layout performance requirements and system standards for the contractor's final design.
