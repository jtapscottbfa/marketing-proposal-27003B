Mechanical engineering addressed HVAC systems throughout approximately 20,810 SF of renovation space in Building NH-95, a two-story 1959 headquarters building. Existing variable air volume (VAV) systems serving administrative areas were retained and reconfigured to match new floor plan layouts. Existing computer room air conditioning (CRAC) units in former data spaces — particularly in Area 11, which had been used for data center functions — were demolished and replaced with new VAV distribution. Existing air handlers serving Areas 6, 11, and 8/10 were retained as a Value Engineering accepted item (VE M-1.0), avoiding replacement costs while meeting performance requirements. New direct digital controls (DDC) were specified throughout all renovation areas. An executive kitchenette cooking exhaust system — a centrifugal kitchen exhaust fan with kitchen hood — was added for the Flag Suite kitchenette in Area 11. Ductless split systems were provided in select areas requiring supplemental conditioning independent of the primary VAV distribution. Mechanical engineering produced the HVAC performance criteria and equipment specifications for the Design-Build RFP package, establishing the basis of design for the contractor's final engineering.
