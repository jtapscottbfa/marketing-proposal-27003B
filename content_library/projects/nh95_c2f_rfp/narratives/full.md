# Convert Space in NH-95 for Commander, U.S. Second Fleet

## Project Overview

Building NH-95 is the Headquarters Operational Control Center at Naval Support Activity (NSA) Hampton Roads in Norfolk, Virginia — a 275,409 SF, two-story federal facility constructed in 1959 with a Plant Replacement Value of approximately $177.3 million. The project, designated NAVFAC project number RM24-2031 (FY24 Special Project), converted approximately 20,810 square feet of existing interior space within NH-95 to house Commander, U.S. Second Fleet (C2F) headquarters personnel and a Defense Information Systems Agency (DISA) footprint on the same floors.

The project was procured as a Design-Build Request for Proposals (DB RFP) under a sole-source modification to an existing construction contract. The design team operated under NAVFAC MIDLANT IDIQ contract N4008520D005 (eProjects Work Order 1797165), with the A-E task order initiated in March 2024 and the final corrected DB RFP package targeted for submission January 2025.

## Scope of Work

The renovation encompassed 20,810 SF across two floors of Building NH-95:

**First Floor — C2F Area 6** (9 private offices, 70 new workstations plus 11 existing workstations in rooms 132A–C, large conference room, open conference area, break area, and existing communications rooms to remain). Departmental groups housed include CMC, MOC Director, Reserve Director, Chaplain, Medical, Public Affairs, N1, Culinary Team, FMC Staff, Protocol Officer, and others.

**First Floor — C2F Area 11 (Flag Suite)** (approximately 18,241 SF total C2F scope). The Flag Suite accommodated the senior leadership of C2F: Commanding Officer (COM) office at 302 SF, Vice COM and Deputy COM offices at approximately 302 SF and 297 SF respectively, Reserve Vice COM and Chief of Staff suites, executive kitchenette at 276 SF, storage at 134 SF, conference and dining room at 669 SF, private toilet and shower facilities for the COM and shared facilities for the suite, and a new secure exterior entrance with vestibule and waiting area.

**Second Floor — C2F Area 8/10** (2 private offices totaling approximately 255 SF, open office area of approximately 2,714 SF with 34 workstations, and open conference area at 291 SF; N4 and N7 directorates).

**Second Floor — DISA Area 10** (2,583 SF; 3 private offices, 12 workstations, work room, storage, secure communications/electrical room, and ramp access to expansion joint).

Systems scope encompassed full interior demolition and reconstruction of non-structural partitions, all new MEP systems (HVAC reconfiguration, LED lighting replacement, plumbing for new restrooms and kitchenette, fire sprinkler reconfiguration), telecommunications and data cabling, electronic security system pathways, and hazardous materials abatement coordination.

## Role on Project

The design team held the prime A-E contract with NAVFAC MIDLANT and led all aspects of the DB RFP development. Responsibilities included organizing and facilitating a four-day design charrette with government stakeholders, developing ten iterative concept plans (Concepts A through K) to arrive at the preferred solution, preparing the full Design-Build RFP package (Parts 1–6), producing the DD Form 1391 in both Design-Build and Design-Bid-Build versions, conducting the Value Engineering study, preparing the parametric cost estimate, and compiling meeting minutes and scheduling analysis. Post-Construction Award Services (PCAS) were included as a contract option.

## Key Challenges and Resolutions

**Hazardous materials in a 1959 building.** Building NH-95 was constructed in 1959 and contains hazardous materials (wall finishes, insulation, paints) in the renovation areas. A prior hazmat investigation was performed for the concurrent Fire Protection Repairs project (ST19-0641); that project was expected to abate the majority of hazmat in the building, but residual hazmat remained a coordination and scheduling factor for this renovation.

**Concurrent construction project coordination.** Building NH-95 was simultaneously under construction for a fire protection system repairs project (contract N4008522R2634). The renovation project was designed to be issued as a sole-source modification to that same contractor (WMJ), requiring deep coordination on phasing, swing space, and systems integration — particularly the transition from dry/pre-action sprinkler systems being replaced under the FP Repairs project to the wet pipe reconfiguration in scope for this renovation.

**Scope refinement from programming through charrette.** The initial SAES scope estimate of approximately 30,000 SF was refined during the charrette process to a final contract quantity of 20,810 SF (C2F 18,241 SF + DISA 2,583 SF). This approximately 30% reduction reflected programming decisions made during the four-day charrette, including the elimination of a watch floor, removal of a SCIF requirement, and relocation of the Flag Suite from Area 6 to Area 11.

**Value Engineering.** A formal VE study conducted during the charrette process identified and evaluated multiple alternatives. Accepted VE items with the greatest cost impact included: retaining existing air handlers serving all three renovation areas rather than replacing them (VE M-1.0); reducing plumbing scope through a simplified restroom configuration in Area 11 (VE A-1.0); and deleting a clean agent suppression system from Area 11 Room 044 (VE M-2.0). The net result of accepted VE items was approximately $5,162,000 in savings.

**Dual acquisition strategy documentation.** NAVFAC MIDLANT required parallel preparation of DD Form 1391 packages for both Design-Build and Design-Bid-Build acquisition strategies, supporting an internal government decision on procurement method. The DB version (recommended) totaled $27,800,000; the DBB version totaled $27,000,000.

## Outcomes

The four-day design charrette was completed and the Final Charrette Report was delivered September 20, 2024. The post-charrette Estimated Construction Cost was $23,242,000. The total project cost per the DD Form 1391 Design-Build version was $27,800,000 against a SAES program budget of $39,000,000. The final corrected Design-Build RFP was scheduled for submission January 21, 2025. The assumed construction schedule was 360 calendar days (12 months), with an assumed start of January 2026 and completion January 2027. The project status at the close of the A-E contract scope was design-complete / RFP issued.
