Plumbing engineering services for Pump Station #290 in the Springton at Grassfield neighborhood of Chesapeake, Virginia addressed the sanitary piping systems that form the functional core of the sewage lift station: the influent gravity sewer connection to the wet well, the pump discharge piping, the force main connection, and the ancillary plumbing within the pump room.

The influent system receives sanitary flow through a 10-inch PVC gravity sewer at an invert elevation of -6.30, discharging into the circular wet well (bottom elevation -15.30, finish floor elevation +12.90). The wet well provides storage volume across the pump operating range: lead pump activation at elevation -9.30, lag pump activation at -8.30, high water alarm at -7.30, and pump-off at -12.30. The approximately 28-foot wet well depth — from bottom to pump room finish floor — establishes the suction lift conditions for the station's self-priming pumps (Gorman Rupp VS6A60-B, 8-inch suction connection).

Pump discharge routing conveys flow from the 8-inch ductile iron flanged pump discharge connections through the pump room, past gate valves and check valves on each pump, to the 10-inch ductile iron force main (discharge centerline elevation +8.20). A PVC bubbler tube and bell serve the air-purging bubbler-type level control system, housed in the 8-inch Schedule 80 PVC stilling well assembly.

Ancillary pump room plumbing includes a 3/4-inch hose bib with reduced-pressure zone backflow preventer for maintenance water supply, a 4-inch floor drain with P-trap for drainage, and a sump pump (Zoeller Model 147, 1/2 HP, 115V, submersible) where indicated by plans for pump room drainage. All materials and work conform to City of Chesapeake Department of Public Utilities construction specifications and standards for sanitary sewer facilities.
