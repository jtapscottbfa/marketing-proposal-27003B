Electrical engineering services for Pump Station #290 in the Springton at Grassfield neighborhood of Chesapeake, Virginia covered power distribution, lighting, emergency power, pump controls, and instrumentation for the municipal sanitary sewage station. All work was designed to meet City of Chesapeake Department of Public Utilities construction specifications, City Public Facilities Manual requirements, and the service rules and regulations of Dominion Energy Virginia.

The power distribution system centers on Main Distribution Panel MDP-5, serving two 60-horsepower, three-phase pump motors (Gorman Rupp VS6A60-B) through variable frequency drives for speed-modulated pump operation. Emergency power continuity is provided through a portable generator receptacle (42 ampere, 3-pole/4-wire) connected to a manual 4-pole double-throw safety switch (lockable in all switch positions), with a dedicated generator pad south of the building and underground conduit routing coordinated with Dominion Energy Virginia for the utility service point. A surge protective device is included in the distribution system.

The pump control scheme uses a bubbler-type liquid level control system (air-purging type, NEMA 12 panel) with a submersible level transducer in a 6-inch Schedule 80 PVC stilling well providing continuous wet well level measurement, supplemented by three high-level float switches (high level, backup high level, and hi-hi) for alarm redundancy. Check valve limit switches confirm valve position on each pump discharge. A phase monitor protects against phase loss, reversal, and low-voltage conditions. A remote terminal unit provides telemetry and alarm transmission from the station.

Interior LED lighting (Phoenix Lighting, 17W, 5600K, 120V, surface ceiling) serves the pump room and control room. Exterior LED wall packs (Hubbell Outdoor Geopak TRP1, 28.2W, 3000K, integral photocell) serve the building exterior. An arc flash hazard analysis — time-current coordination study and short-circuit equipment evaluation based on actual installed equipment — is specified as a contractor deliverable. Electrical drawings (sheets E-1 through E-4) cover legend, site plan, lighting floor plans, and power floor plans for both station levels.
