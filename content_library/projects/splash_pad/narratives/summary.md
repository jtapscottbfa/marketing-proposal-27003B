The Melwood Splashpad (BFA project 24-002) is an electrical engineering design project for a new outdoor interactive splash pad at Melwood's facility in Nanjemoy, Maryland. The design team served as prime consultant and engineer of record for Phase II electrical work — Phase I having addressed the existing in-ground pool complex, with Phase II introducing the new splash pad and its associated water feature equipment. The 100% construction documents, issued February 2024, cover site electrical routing from the existing pool equipment building to the splash pad, new equipment connections within the pool equipment building (chemical room, filter room, pump pit), and demolition and renovation of corroded electrical distribution infrastructure.

New loads include a Feature Filtration Pump (480V, 3Ø, 7.5HP), a Feature Pump (208V, 3Ø, 10HP), a feature timer panel, UV sterilizer and ozone feeder circuits, motor starters, and GFCI protection throughout the pool equipment room. Existing infrastructure — a 750 kVA pad-mounted utility transformer and a Cutler-Hammer POW-R-LINE panelboard — was retained and integrated with the new work. All new equipment enclosures were specified in 304 stainless steel for durability in the aquatic chemical environment. Design is complete at 100%; construction status is not documented in available source records.
