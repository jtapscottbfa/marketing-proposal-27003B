The electrical engineering scope for the Melwood Splashpad covered all power distribution design and equipment connections for Phase II of a two-phase aquatic facility expansion in Nanjemoy, Maryland. The design team served as engineer of record, producing a four-sheet 100% construction drawing package issued February 2024.

Work within the pool equipment building addressed new circuit connections to the splash pad's water treatment and feature equipment: a Feature Filtration Pump (480V, 3Ø, 7.5HP), a Feature Pump (208V, 3Ø, 10HP), a feature timer panel for splash pad activation control (120V), and a UV sterilizer and ozone feeder (120V). Motor starters were added for the new pumps, and an external emergency stop switch was specified. GFCI and SPGFCI receptacle protection was provided throughout the pool equipment room. All new enclosures were specified in 304 stainless steel, and corroded existing equipment was directed for replacement with LMI or PVC materials to address the harsh aquatic chemical environment.

On site, a 1-inch empty conduit was designed from the equipment building to the new splash pad area for feature bollard power and controls, with final connection requirements designated for manufacturer coordination by the electrical contractor.

The existing electrical infrastructure — a 750 kVA pad-mounted transformer, a 208/120V, 3Ø service disconnect, and a Cutler-Hammer POW-R-LINE Panelboard 'A' (100A MCB) — was retained and integrated with the Phase II work, with new loads connected to available panel circuits without requiring a service upgrade.
