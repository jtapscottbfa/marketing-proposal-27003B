The project encompassed A/E design services for the renovation of Cardiac Catheterization Laboratory Room 1 at the Ralph H. Johnson VA Medical Center in Charleston, South Carolina. The scope covered approximately 1,500 square feet of second-floor space, including the interventional procedure room and all associated support areas, against a programmed construction budget of $1,500,000 and a 271-calendar-day design period of performance. The contract was procured as a Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small Business (SDVOSB) set-aside under the VA Veterans First Contracting Program, with Moon Tree LLC serving as the prime A/E consultant and the MEP engineering team engaged as subconsultant providing mechanical, electrical, and plumbing engineering services representing approximately 40 percent of the contract scope.

The technical program required full renovation of the HVAC system serving the cath lab suite — including replacement and modification of air handling unit AHU-18, chilled and hot water piping connections, VAV air distribution, supply fan VFD, and DDC/BAS integration via Johnson Controls — alongside medical gas system design (oxygen, medical air, medical vacuum, and nitrogen delivered from a procedure table boom per NFPA 99 and the VA Surgical and Endovascular Services Design Guide, Section 4.16), electrical distribution and isolated power for the interventional suite, and fire protection systems coordination. Field investigations conducted in October 2023 documented existing electrical distribution equipment and mechanical system conditions across the cath lab suite and adjacent mechanical and electrical rooms, providing the factual basis for design decisions in an occupied, operational federal medical facility.
