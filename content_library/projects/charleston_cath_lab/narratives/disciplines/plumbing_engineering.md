Plumbing engineering services for the Cath Lab Room 1 renovation at the Ralph H. Johnson VA Medical Center focused on medical gas system design for the interventional procedure suite. The scope addressed oxygen, medical air, medical vacuum, and nitrogen distributed to the procedure room via a boom assembly at the cath lab table — the delivery configuration prescribed by the VA Surgical and Endovascular Services Design Guide, Section 4.16 for the Cardiac Catheterization Laboratory room type.

Medical gas zone valve boxes were required in the semi-restricted corridor outside the cath lab per NFPA 99 healthcare facility requirements, providing isolation capability for each gas type at the zone boundary. Medical gas shutoff boxes were located in the corridor outside the procedure room. The design was required to comply with both NFPA 99 and the VA design guide equipment list and room data sheet, which specifies medical gas outlet types and quantities for the XCCE1 / XACR1 room template.

The medical gas scope required close coordination with the equipment interface for the Siemens ARTIS Icono cath lab system, which delivers gases through the table boom — a manufacturer-defined connection point whose location and service requirements were established by the vendor reference drawings provided in the solicitation package. Richard O'Berry, CPD, GPD (Senior Mechanical Designer and Certified Plumbing Designer) led the plumbing and medical gas scope within the MEP subconsultant team. Medical gas system design for occupied VA healthcare facilities carries particular compliance weight given NFPA 99 inspection and commissioning requirements; the design approach was informed by the October 2023 mechanical field investigation, which documented the existing medical gas distribution serving the cath lab area.
