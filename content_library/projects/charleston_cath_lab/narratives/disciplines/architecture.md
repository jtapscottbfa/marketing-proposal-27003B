Architecture was among the disciplines on the design team for the Cath Lab Room 1 renovation at the Ralph H. Johnson VA Medical Center. The renovation encompassed approximately 1,500 square feet of second-floor space — the procedure room (Cath Lab 1, room AA205), control room (AA219), cardiology computer room (AA219A), equipment rooms, and semi-restricted corridor areas — within an occupied federal medical facility. Architectural scope included demolition of existing finishes, hazardous materials abatement coordination, drywall and finish renovation, and layout coordination for the Siemens ARTIS Icono cardiac catheterization equipment installation.

The architectural design was required to comply with the VA Surgical and Endovascular Services Design Guide, Section 4.16, which prescribes the room dimensions, adjacency requirements, finish standards, and equipment list (by JSN) for the Cardiac Catheterization Laboratory room type. The 850-NSF procedure room template in the design guide governed the spatial organization of the cath lab room itself; the total 1,500-SF project scope incorporated the procedure room and all associated support spaces.
