Electrical engineering services for the Cath Lab Room 1 renovation at the Ralph H. Johnson VA Medical Center involved detailed field investigation of the existing electrical distribution infrastructure, followed by design of the electrical systems serving the renovated interventional suite. Two separate electrical field investigations were conducted — on October 4 and October 5, 2023 — covering the mechanical and electrical rooms housing distribution equipment (rooms AA203, AA204, AA221, AA223, AA229, AA230, AA231, AA237, AA237A, AA240, AA241, and corridor rooms COR2AA1 through COR2AA3) as well as the active cath lab suite rooms (AA205, AA219, AA219A, AA243).

The existing electrical infrastructure serving the floor included automatic transfer switches (ATS-EE, ATS-LS, ATS-CC) at 480/277V, motor control centers (MCC-NA, MCC-EA), a 480/277V panelboard (2HEDP1), VFDs for air handling units AHU-16 through AHU-19, and a Gamewell E-3 Series Fire Alarm Control Panel — all concentrated in rooms AA229 through AA231. Isolated power infrastructure in the cath lab corridor and suite included ISOTROL-2 (120V isolated power panelboard) and 277V isolated power panelboards (2INP1, 2ICP1, 2ICP2). The October 5 investigation of the active cath lab rooms found no electrical deficiencies in the equipment serving the procedure space. Decommissioned equipment in room AA203 was documented as offline.

The electrical scope also covered coordination with the Siemens ARTIS Icono equipment electrical reference drawings (sheets E-101, E-102, E-501), which established the power service and isolated power requirements for the cath lab system. Named electrical personnel included Richard Williams, PE (Electrical Engineer) and Blair Fisher, EIT (Electrical Designer and Project Manager).
