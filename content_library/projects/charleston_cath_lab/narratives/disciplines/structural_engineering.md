Structural engineering was among the disciplines on the design team for the Cath Lab Room 1 renovation at the Ralph H. Johnson VA Medical Center. The structural scope for this project centered on coordinating the floor loading and ceiling suspension requirements for the Siemens ARTIS Icono cardiac catheterization system — a biplane interventional imaging platform with demanding structural interface requirements. Manufacturer reference drawings included two structural sheets (S-101 and S-102), labeled "ARTIS ICONO FLOOR — REFERENCE DOCUMENT — NOT FOR CONSTRUCTION," which established the equipment's floor load distribution and service access clearances for use by the structural engineer of record in developing construction documents.

Structural engineering services on this project were led by the prime A/E consultant, Moon Tree LLC. David R. Walton, PE, SE, CWI served as VP Engineering, Project Manager, and Structural/Civil/Blast Engineer for the prime firm. The source record does not contain structural scope details attributable to the MEP subconsultant; the discipline appears on the project's taxonomy because structural engineering was part of the full design team scope required to address the equipment interface and any structural modifications within the existing second-floor space.
