Fire protection engineering services for the Cath Lab Room 1 renovation at the Ralph H. Johnson VA Medical Center addressed both fire suppression and fire alarm systems serving the procedure room and associated support spaces. Fire protection design was provided by Poole Fire Protection (Olathe, KS), a Woman-Owned subconsultant engaged within the broader MEP team.

The existing fire alarm system at the facility was a Gamewell E-3 Series installation. The scope required that any new fire alarm work — including devices serving the renovated cath lab suite — be designed and installed by a Gamewell-authorized contractor to maintain system integrity and VA-required compatibility. Existing duct smoke detectors on supply and return ductwork for air handling units AHU-16, AHU-17, AHU-18, and AHU-19 were documented during field investigations and accounted for in the HVAC replacement and modification scope.

The wet fire suppression system serving the cath lab suite was classified as Ordinary Hazard Group 1, consistent with the VA Surgical and Endovascular Services Design Guide requirements for interventional procedure rooms. The fire protection scope was coordinated with the HVAC replacement — the AHU-18 modification affected duct smoke detector locations and smoke control configurations — and with the construction sequencing requirements for the occupied facility. Fire alarm and suppression system design followed VA standard requirements and NFPA codes, with the Gamewell system constraint representing the key facility-specific coordination requirement for the design team.
