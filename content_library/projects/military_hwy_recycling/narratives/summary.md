The Military Highway Recycling project involved the design of a 5kV medium-voltage electrical distribution system for a private industrial recycling facility in Chesapeake, Virginia, owned and operated by Gutterman's. The engineering team was engaged as an electrical subconsultant to Virginia Industrial Controls, the prime contractor and industrial systems integrator, and produced a single-sheet 5kV one-line diagram (Sheet E-601) documenting the facility's medium-voltage power distribution from a Dominion Power 13.2 kV underground service through a utility-furnished step-down transformer to the facility's high-horsepower motor loads.

The electrical scope covers the complete medium-voltage service entrance and distribution system: Dominion Power underground cable feeders entering a metering cabinet, a utility-provided step-down transformer, BENSHAW medium-voltage fused switch and motor starter equipment, and a Westinghouse Lifeline D-type LAC induction motor rated 800HP, 4160V, 3-phase (98 FLA). The design documents the utility/owner interface, demarcates Dominion Power-furnished versus owner-furnished equipment, and coordinates medium-voltage motor starting and protection for the facility's heavy industrial load. The project was completed in May 2021 under a commercial subconsultant agreement (project no. 21-011).
