# Military Highway Recycling — Electrical Engineering Subconsultant Services

## Project Overview

The Military Highway Recycling project is an electrical engineering subconsultant engagement for an industrial recycling facility in Chesapeake, Virginia. The facility, owned and operated by Gutterman's, is a materials-processing and recycling operation requiring high-horsepower medium-voltage power distribution. The engineering team was engaged by Virginia Industrial Controls — the prime contractor and industrial systems integrator holding the agreement with the facility owner — to produce the facility's 5kV electrical one-line diagram. The engagement was completed in May 2021 under project number 21-011, with a subcontract fee of $2,000.

## Scope of Work

The scope consisted of electrical engineering design services, specifically the production of Sheet E-601: a 5kV ONE-LINE DIAGRAM documenting the facility's medium-voltage power distribution system. The diagram captures the complete electrical service pathway from the utility service entrance to the facility's primary motor load:

- **Utility service:** Dominion Power 13.2 kV underground cable feeders entering via a metering cabinet, routed through Dominion Power-furnished conduit and equipment to a utility-provided step-down transformer
- **Medium-voltage distribution:** 5kV class system downstream of the transformer, feeding two main branches via three No. 70 5kV conductors in conduit:
  - A BENSHAW 2200VAC fused switch (400A application load)
  - A BENSHAW 4200VAC motor starter (1000HP application load)
- **Primary load:** Westinghouse Lifeline D-type LAC induction motor, rated 800HP, 4160V, 3-phase, 98A full-load amperes

The final one-line diagram constitutes the complete scope of design services for this engagement. No BOD, SOW, or multi-discipline design work is recorded for this project.

## Role on Project

The engineering team served as electrical engineering subconsultant to Virginia Industrial Controls, the prime contractor. The engagement was structured as a commercial professional services agreement; Gutterman's, as facility owner, sits above Virginia Industrial Controls in the contracting chain. The team's role was limited to electrical engineering design services (drawing production); no construction administration, field services, or multi-discipline design responsibilities are recorded for this engagement.

## Key Challenges and Resolutions

**Medium-voltage design for a heavy industrial load.** The facility's primary electrical load — an 800HP induction motor operating on a 5kV class system — is characteristic of heavy industrial recycling and materials-processing equipment. Medium-voltage motor starting and protection requires careful coordination between utility service characteristics, transformer sizing, and medium-voltage switching and starter equipment. The design integrates BENSHAW medium-voltage fused switch and motor starter equipment with the Westinghouse motor, coordinated against the Dominion Power service infrastructure.

**Utility/owner interface documentation.** The one-line diagram correctly demarcates Dominion Power-furnished equipment (underground cable service, conduit, metering cabinet, and step-down transformer) from the owner's downstream distribution system. Clear delineation of the utility/owner boundary is essential for permitting, interconnection agreements, and construction coordination on industrial medium-voltage projects.

## Outcomes

The final one-line diagram was issued in May 2021. The drawing documents the medium-voltage distribution system serving the facility's 800HP motor load from a Dominion Power 13.2 kV underground service. The design supports the facility's heavy industrial recycling operations and provides a clear engineering record of the service entrance, metering, transformer, medium-voltage switching, motor starting, and primary load configuration. No construction phase or construction administration services are recorded for the engineering team on this project.
