The electrical engineering scope for the Military Highway Recycling project consisted of the design of a 5kV medium-voltage power distribution system for an industrial recycling facility in Chesapeake, Virginia. Working as an electrical subconsultant to Virginia Industrial Controls, the team produced Sheet E-601 — a 5kV ONE-LINE DIAGRAM documenting the complete medium-voltage electrical system from the Dominion Power utility service entrance to the facility's primary motor load.

The system originates at a Dominion Power 13.2 kV underground cable service entering a metering cabinet, then routes through utility-furnished conduit and a Dominion Power-provided step-down transformer. Downstream of the transformer, the 5kV class distribution system feeds two BENSHAW medium-voltage branches: a 2200VAC fused switch rated for 400A application load and a 4200VAC motor starter rated for 1000HP application load. Both branches supply a Westinghouse Lifeline D-type LAC induction motor rated 800HP, 4160V, 3-phase, 98A full-load amperes via three No. 70 5kV conductors in conduit. The one-line diagram clearly demarcates Dominion Power-furnished equipment from the owner's downstream system, providing a complete engineering record of the service entrance, metering, transformer, medium-voltage switching, motor starting, and primary load configuration.

This engagement represents electrical engineering competency in medium-voltage industrial power distribution, heavy-load motor starter coordination, and utility service documentation for large-scale industrial operations. The project was completed in May 2021.
