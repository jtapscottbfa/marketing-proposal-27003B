The electrical engineering scope for Building 301 involved modifications to the building's existing electrical service to accommodate the new boiler plant equipment. Power distribution work included provision of power connections for the variable frequency drives (VFDs) serving the two new boiler feed pumps, with motors rated at one-half horsepower or greater connected at 208V or 480V, 3-phase, 60 Hz per design standards. The electrical scope was focused on the power distribution modifications necessary for the mechanical equipment replacement program rather than broader electrical system upgrades.
