Electrical engineering scope on this project supports two interlocking systems: the exhaust system variant and the fire alarm/suppression control integration. Per the Electrical Basis of Design, new circuits provide power to the inline exhaust fans installed to clear discharged clean agent from the protected server room space following a suppression event. A dedicated disconnect switch replaces the existing disconnect serving the ventilation equipment. Control wiring connects the clean agent release panel to fan activation and damper sequencing. Air sampling detector circuits and power and control wiring for motorized damper and louver actuators round out the exhaust system electrical package.

Fire alarm integration elements within the electrical scope include new circuits connecting the Fire Alarm Releasing Control Panel to above-floor and under-floor detection devices, the Emergency Power Off switch, and CRAC unit relay interfaces. Radio transmitter power and data circuits supporting the AES mesh network link from the server room to the Emergency Operations Center in Building D327 are also within scope.

These electrical elements are closely coordinated with the fire protection engineering discipline; the exhaust system electrical scope is documented in a separate Basis of Design from the primary fire suppression SOW, reflecting the exhaust system's supplemental design track within the ECMP 1204 project package. Specific load calculations, circuit schedules, and panel assignments are not further detailed in the source record.
