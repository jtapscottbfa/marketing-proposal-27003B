Mechanical engineering scope on this project is focused on the exhaust system variant designed to clear discharged clean agent from the protected server room space following a suppression event, enabling safe re-entry by fire services. Per the Mechanical Basis of Design, the scope includes new inline exhaust fans sized to evacuate both the under-raised-floor plenum and the above-floor volume of Room 003; dedicated exhaust ductwork routing to the exterior; intake and exhaust air louvers with integrated motorized dampers controlling airflow during normal operation and agent discharge events; and underfloor grilles facilitating agent evacuation from the raised-floor plenum.

The exhaust system is a companion system to the clean agent fire suppression replacement — it does not alter the suppression agent selection or design concentration, but provides the required post-discharge ventilation path that NFPA 2001 and life safety requirements for fire service access demand. A key interdisciplinary interface exists between the mechanical exhaust system and the fire protection releasing panel: fan activation, damper sequencing, and louver operation must coordinate with agent discharge sequencing. This coordination is reflected in the companion Electrical Basis of Design, which addresses the actuator and control circuits.

The mechanical exhaust scope is documented as a supplemental Basis of Design within the ECMP 1204 project package, distinct from the primary fire suppression SOW. Specific equipment performance data, fan selections, duct sizing calculations, and louver/damper specifications are not further detailed in the source record.
