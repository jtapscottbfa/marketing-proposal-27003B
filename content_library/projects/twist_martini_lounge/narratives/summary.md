The Twist Martini Lounge patio heater installation was a small commercial tenant-improvement project in Virginia Beach, Virginia. The scope of work involved mechanical and electrical engineering design for the installation of three gas-fired infrared tube unit heaters in an outdoor patio space. Each heater — SunStar Eclipse SIR .35 Series — is rated at 35 MBH high and 25 MBH low input, suspended at approximately 10 feet 5 inches above the finished floor and fueled by natural gas tapped from the building's existing 5 PSIG above-ceiling supply. Natural gas piping design included a pressure regulator, emergency gas shut-off valve (EGSOV), ball valves, 6-inch dirt leg, and a 24-inch flexible gas connector per heater. Each heater is Type B vented with a draft inducer.

The electrical design extended power from the existing Panel A to serve each heater at 120V single-phase, with individual three-position (Hi/Lo/Off) manual switches providing independent two-stage heating control. An emergency shut-off system integrated a 120V relay and transformer at each gas valve, enabling single-point wall-switch activation to close the gas supply across all heaters. An existing ceiling fan was demolished and its associated circuit was repurposed to serve the new heating equipment. The design firm served as prime consultant, delivering complete mechanical and electrical engineering construction documents sealed November 30, 2020, under project number 20-095.
