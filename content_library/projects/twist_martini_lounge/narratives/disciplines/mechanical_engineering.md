Mechanical engineering scope for the Twist Martini Lounge patio heater installation centered on the selection, layout, gas systems design, venting, and controls integration for three gas-fired infrared tube unit heaters (UH-1, UH-2, UH-3) in an existing outdoor patio space in Virginia Beach, Virginia.

The specified equipment is the SunStar Eclipse SIR .35 Series, each unit rated at 35 MBH high and 25 MBH low input. Heaters are suspended with the bottom edge at approximately 10 feet 5 inches above the finished floor, positioned along the patio adjacent to Commerce Street.

Natural gas distribution was designed to tap the building's existing 5 PSIG above-ceiling supply. The piping system includes a pressure regulator stepping supply down to heater operating range (minimum 5" WC, maximum 14" WC; burner pressure 3.5" WC), a 6-inch dirt leg at each branch, ball valves, Y-connections for branch takeoffs, and a 24-inch flexible gas connector at each heater. An emergency gas shut-off valve (EGSOV), mounted at 48 inches above the finished floor, closes via a 24V/120V relay and transformer on wall-switch activation.

Each heater is Type B vented with a draft inducer. Vent supports are specified at each structural column and at the storefront, with a Type B vent manifold at 9 feet 11 inches. The M-501 detail sheet documents the complete unit heater section, vent cap arrangement, and draft inducer assembly hardware.

Controls provide two-stage (high/low) manual operation per heater, independently adjustable by venue staff, with a separately configured emergency shut-off sequence.
