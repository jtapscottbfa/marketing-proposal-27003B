This professional services engagement tasked the prime A&E consultant with researching and revising Unified Facilities Guide Specification (UFGS) 26 31 00, Solar Photovoltaic (PV) Components, for Naval Facilities Engineering Systems Command (NAVFAC Atlantic) under IDIQ contract N62470-14-D-3006. The revised specification serves as a tri-service planning, design, construction, and maintenance document for facility planners, designers, contractors, and maintenance technicians across USACE, NAVFAC, AFCEC, and NASA programs.

Work commenced with Notice to Proceed on September 22, 2020 and concluded with a publication submittal on October 6, 2021, within the contractually scheduled 405-day performance period. The 50-page deliverable — produced in SpecsIntact, PDF, and Word formats — was developed through an iterative six-submittal review process (Kick-off, 35%, 65%, Pre-Final, Final, Final Corrected) with the NAVFAC Atlantic project team and tri-service working group. The revised specification updates UFGS 26 31 00 against current Unified Facilities Criteria (UFC) and recent industry product improvements, incorporating updated standards including NFPA 70 (2020) and IEEE 1547 (2018). The final document was retitled "Facility-Scale Solar Photovoltaic (PV) Systems Components" to reflect its expanded scope and published under joint USACE/NAVFAC/AFCEC/NASA authority as the current federal construction specification for facility-scale solar PV installations at DoD installations nationwide.
