# UFGS Research and Revision of Solar Specs — UFGS 26 31 00 Solar Photovoltaic (PV) Components

## Project Overview

This engagement was a professional services task order issued by Naval Facilities Engineering Systems Command (NAVFAC Atlantic) under IDIQ A&E contract N62470-14-D-3006. The task directed the prime A&E consultant to research and revise Unified Facilities Guide Specification (UFGS) 26 31 00, Solar Photovoltaic (PV) Components, updating the existing May 2015 edition to conform to current Unified Facilities Criteria (UFC) and incorporate recent industry product improvements. The resulting specification is a tri-service document prepared under joint USACE, NAVFAC, AFCEC, and NASA authority and serves as the federal standard for planning, designing, procuring, constructing, and maintaining facility-scale solar PV systems at DoD installations.

This project is distinct from facility design and construction engagements: no physical facility was designed or built. The scope was entirely professional services — research, standards analysis, technical writing, and structured review coordination — with the deliverable being the specification document itself.

## Scope

The scope covered all work necessary to produce a revised UFGS from the existing May 2015 baseline through a six-submittal iterative review process:

- **Kick-off submittal** — initial project orientation and scope confirmation with NAVFAC Atlantic PM/COR and tri-service working group
- **35% submittal** — preliminary draft with initial research findings and proposed revisions
- **65% submittal** — intermediate draft incorporating first-round review comments
- **Pre-Final submittal** — near-complete draft resolving outstanding technical and editorial comments
- **Final submittal** — complete revised specification
- **Final Corrected submittal** — publication-ready version incorporating last-round corrections

Each submittal was reviewed by NAVFAC Atlantic and by the tri-service working group representatives from AFCESA (Air Force) and USACE (Army). The revised UFGS was delivered in three formats: SpecsIntact (the federal specification editing and management platform), PDF, and Microsoft Word. The final revised document is 50 pages and was retitled "Facility-Scale Solar Photovoltaic (PV) Systems Components" to reflect the updated scope of coverage. Tracked-changes edits against the May 2015 base document were incorporated throughout, with updated references to current standards including NFPA 70 (2020 edition) and IEEE 1547-2018.

## Key Challenges and Resolutions

**Technical currency across a rapidly evolving domain.** Solar PV technology, codes, and standards were actively evolving during the 2020–2021 performance period. The specification needed to reflect the current state of facility-scale PV system components, inverter technology, interconnection requirements, and applicable electrical and renewable energy standards. The research phase identified changes in NFPA 70 (National Electrical Code) and IEEE 1547 (interconnection and interoperability of distributed energy resources) that had material implications for federal PV specifications, and the revised document incorporated those updates in tracked-changes form.

**Multi-audience specification clarity.** UFGS 26 31 00 serves a broad audience — facility planners, designers, contractors, and maintenance technicians — with different technical backgrounds and different points of interaction with the specification. The revision required writing that met the needs of each audience without introducing ambiguity or contradiction between the planning, design, construction, and maintenance use contexts.

**Tri-service coordination across four agencies.** Reaching a consensus document under USACE, NAVFAC, AFCEC, and NASA authority required managing multi-agency comment cycles across six formal submittal rounds. The iterative submittal structure provided a disciplined mechanism for collecting and resolving differing agency perspectives and ensuring the final publication reflected tri-service consensus.

## Outcomes

The prime consultant delivered the publication-ready revised UFGS 26 31 00 within the contractually scheduled performance period. Notice to Proceed was September 22, 2020; the publication submittal was delivered October 6, 2021, representing approximately 380 calendar days of actual performance against 405 contractually scheduled days. The 50-page revised specification was submitted for publication under joint USACE/NAVFAC/AFCEC/NASA authority and is the current federal construction specification for facility-scale solar PV system components at DoD installations.
