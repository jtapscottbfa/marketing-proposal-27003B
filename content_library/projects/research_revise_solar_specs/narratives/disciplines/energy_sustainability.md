The energy and sustainability discipline provided subject matter expertise in the solar photovoltaic technology domain that underpins UFGS 26 31 00. The specification covers facility-scale solar PV components — a renewable energy technology with actively evolving products, standards, and performance characteristics during the 2020–2021 performance period.

The energy/sustainability role was to ensure the revised specification accurately reflected the current state of solar PV technology and industry practice, updating the May 2015 specification baseline against product improvements and new installation methods that had emerged in the intervening years. This required tracking changes in PV module technology, inverter architectures, racking systems, monitoring equipment, and the standards governing their application in facility-scale installations.

The revised specification is intended for use by DoD facility designers and contractors specifying facility-scale solar PV systems at installations across USACE, NAVFAC, AFCEC, and NASA programs. The building system classification for this project is electrical_renewable, reflecting the renewable energy generation context of the specification. The engagement required familiarity with federal renewable energy policy, UFC requirements applicable to solar PV at military installations, and the technical standards environment governing distributed energy resource integration — particularly NFPA 70 (2020) and IEEE 1547-2018 — as the basis for identifying which elements of the May 2015 specification required updating and what the updated requirements should be.
