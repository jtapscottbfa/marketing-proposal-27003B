Specifications writing was the primary discipline and the core deliverable of this engagement. The entire scope consisted of producing a revised Unified Facilities Guide Specification (UFGS) — a federal construction specification used across USACE, NAVFAC, AFCEC, and NASA programs — updated from the existing May 2015 edition of UFGS 26 31 00.

The specifier worked from the baseline document to research current industry practices and standards, identify revisions required for technical currency, and draft tracked-changes edits through a structured six-submittal review process (Kick-off, 35%, 65%, Pre-Final, Final, Final Corrected). Deliverables were produced in SpecsIntact format — the federal specification editing and management platform — as well as PDF and Word. The final revised specification is 50 pages.

A key writing challenge was serving four distinct user communities within a single document: facility planners, designers, contractors, and maintenance technicians, each with different technical backgrounds and different points of use. The revision required clear delineation of requirements across planning, design, construction, and maintenance contexts without introducing ambiguity.

Coordination with the tri-service working group (USACE, NAVFAC, AFCEC, NASA) required managing multi-agency review comments across six rounds of formal submittals. Comment resolution cycles across agencies with differing technical standards and institutional preferences required disciplined document management and clear editorial decision-making to produce a consensus publication-ready specification.
