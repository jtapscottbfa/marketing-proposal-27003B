The electrical engineering scope on the B2829 Pave Gravel Parking Lot project at Naval Air Station Patuxent River covered the full design of a new exterior parking lot lighting system, including luminaire selection, photometric analysis, pole and foundation design, conduit routing, panelboard integration, and lighting controls.

Eight pole-mounted LED area luminaires were specified: Lithonia RSX1 LED P3 40K R4 fixtures operating at 277V, 110 watts each (875.5W total installed load), on 20-foot anchor-base steel/aluminum poles. NEMA Type V distribution was selected to provide broad, uniform coverage across the 19,630-square-foot parking lot. A photometric analysis confirmed adequate illumination levels across the lot area.

All lighting power was drawn from the building's existing electrical infrastructure in Building 2829 Electrical Room 117, avoiding new service installation. Two 20-amp, 1-pole lighting circuits were added to existing Panel L1 (480/277V, 3-phase, 4-wire, 400A MCB) — one circuit per lighting relay zone. Conduit from the building to the poles is direct-buried 1-inch Schedule 40 PVC, transitioning to galvanized rigid steel conduit (GRC) above grade at each pole, with underground warning ribbon at the conduit transition.

Lighting controls were designed as a two-zone, two-relay system via Lighting Control Panel LCP1, located in Electrical Room 117 and powered from a spare circuit in Panel R1 (208Y/120V, 3-phase). Each zone provides photocell-based automatic on/off control with manual switch override. The photocell sensor is mounted in a NEMA 3R junction box on the Building 2829 roof. The design was stamped and sealed by a Maryland-licensed professional engineer.
