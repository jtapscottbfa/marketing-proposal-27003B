The civil engineering scope on the B2829 Pave Gravel Parking Lot project at Naval Air Station Patuxent River encompassed site design for conversion of the existing gravel parking area north of Building 2829 into a paved asphalt parking lot, together with associated grading, drainage, stormwater management, and exterior site improvements.

The civil design addressed asphalt pavement construction over the previously unpaved gravel surface, site grading to establish positive drainage, and on-site drainage infrastructure to collect and convey stormwater runoff from the new impervious surface. Because the project site disturbance exceeded 5,000 square feet — as noted explicitly in the RFP project program — the design was subject to Maryland Department of the Environment (MDE) stormwater management requirements. Stormwater best management practices (BMPs) were incorporated to achieve compliance with applicable MDE regulations, including control of runoff quantity and quality from the new paved lot.

The parking lot was designed to accommodate at least 46 private motor vehicles including the required number of ADA-compliant handicap-accessible spaces, consistent with the NAVFAC project program minimum. Exterior improvements beyond the paved surface included landscaping to restore or stabilize areas disturbed during construction outside the pavement boundary.
