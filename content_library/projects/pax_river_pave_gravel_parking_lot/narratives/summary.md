The design team served as subconsultant Designer of Record for the B2829 Pave Gravel Parking Lot project at Naval Air Station Patuxent River, Maryland. Awarded under a NAVFAC design-build contract (Work Order 1577286), the project converted an existing gravel parking area north of Building 2829 into a paved asphalt parking lot accommodating at least 46 private motor vehicles across approximately 19,630 square feet of paved surface.

Scope of design services encompassed electrical engineering — including site lighting and lighting controls — and civil engineering for site paving, grading, drainage, and stormwater management. The electrical design produced eight pole-mounted LED area luminaires (Lithonia RSX1, 277V, 110W, 20-foot poles) distributed across the lot, controlled by a two-zone photocell/relay lighting control panel tied to existing electrical panels inside Building 2829. The civil design addressed pavement construction over the previously gravel lot, site grading, drainage, and stormwater best management practices required by the Maryland Department of the Environment, given that site disturbance exceeded 5,000 square feet.

The 100% electrical design drawings were completed in May 2021, approximately one year after the May 2020 RFP. As a design-build project under NAVFAC, the design team operated as a licensed subconsultant to the prime design-build contractor, with a Maryland-licensed engineer of record stamping the electrical drawing set.
