Civil engineering scope on the FY22 Fire Protection Repairs project supported two primary project elements at Naval Station Norfolk. The first was site civil and utility design for the temporary SCIF-capable modular office installation on the adjacent NH5 site. Establishing a fully operational temporary facility with classified workspace required site preparation, grading, utility connections (water, sanitary, electrical, and communications), and access coordination — all within the installation boundary and consistent with the security requirements of a classified facility environment. This site scope was necessary to provide displaced building users with compliant workspace during Building NH95's phased construction period.

The second civil scope element encompassed general site utilities work (Division 33) associated with the broader project, including utility connections and any site-level work driven by the fire protection system upgrades and building renovation. The 35% Basis of Design included civil and site notes describing existing site conditions and project-related site scope.

All civil work was executed within the Naval Station Norfolk installation boundary, requiring coordination with installation security, operations, and infrastructure requirements. The temporary NH5 facility in particular required integration of NIPR, SIPR, and other classified network access at the site level, coordinated with the electrical and telecommunications disciplines. Specific grading, drainage, utility design details, and site layout for the NH5 installation are not captured in detail in the source record at this extraction stage.
