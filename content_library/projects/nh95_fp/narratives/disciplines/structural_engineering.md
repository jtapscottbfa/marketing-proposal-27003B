The renovation scope — which required invasive construction throughout the building for fire protection system replacement, hazardous materials abatement, and interior finish repairs — necessitated structural evaluation of the existing building fabric to confirm adequacy for the proposed work and to identify any conditions requiring remediation before or during construction.

The structural scope on this project was limited to assessment and renovation support rather than new structural construction.
