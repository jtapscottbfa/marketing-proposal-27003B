Building NH95 is a 275,400 SF, three-story headquarters operations center at Naval Station Norfolk (NSA Norfolk), Virginia, constructed in 1959. With a Mission Dependency Index of 99 — among the highest rated facilities in service to the Atlantic Fleet — the building required comprehensive fire protection system repairs and full life-safety modernization. The project was delivered under a NAVFAC MIDLANT A-E services task order as part of the AEDC IDIQ contract vehicle, funded by FY22 O&MN appropriations with a program budget of $31,645,000 and an estimated construction cost of $26,637,000.

The design scope encompassed repair and replacement of the existing wet-pipe sprinkler system throughout the building, conversion of dry-pipe and preaction systems to wet-pipe construction, installation of a new fire alarm and mass notification system integrated with the base-wide Giant Voice network, and extension of fire suppression coverage into previously unsprinklered areas. Supporting scope included hazardous materials abatement (asbestos-containing materials, lead-based paint, and PCBs), interior finish repairs and replacements in common spaces affected by the construction work, emergency lighting replacement, and design of temporary SCIF-capable modular office facilities on the adjacent NH5 site to maintain mission continuity during construction. Because the building remained fully occupied by active Navy personnel throughout construction, all work was designed for phased execution outside normal working hours. The multi-discipline design team — prime consultant with fire protection, mechanical, electrical, plumbing, structural, civil, architectural, and hazardous materials expertise — managed the full scope under design-bid-build delivery.
