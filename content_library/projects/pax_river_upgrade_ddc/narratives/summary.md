# Summary — B2185, B2187, B2855 Replace DDC Controls, NAS Patuxent River

This design-build project replaced and upgraded the Direct Digital Control (DDC) / Building Automation System (BAS) across three occupied facilities at Naval Air Station Patuxent River, Maryland, under contract to the Naval Facilities Engineering Command (NAVFAC). The three buildings — Building 2187, a 467,530 SF research and development facility; Building 2185, a 268,000 SF office building; and Building 2855, a 49,464 SF office building — together represent approximately 785,000 SF of occupied federal facility space served by aging or obsolete controls infrastructure.

Work was structured as three separate line items covering distinct building systems: B2187 required full DDC replacement of a complex JCI Metasys installation serving 15 variable-speed and 27 constant-volume air handling units, 56 computer room air conditioning units, and 442 VAV terminal units, along with energy savings controls and integration to the base-wide UMCS network via Niagara 4.x. B2185 required replacement of an Automated Logic DDC system serving 24 AHUs, 225 VAV terminals, and 90 computer room units. B2855 required upgrade of an existing JCI Metasys installation, sensor replacement, testing and balancing of the HVAC system, and full commissioning.

All three buildings remained occupied throughout construction. The project required full BACnet compliance per ASHRAE 135 and UFC 3-410-02, cybersecurity implementation per UFC 4-010-06 and the DoD Risk Management Framework, and government-witnessed Performance Verification Testing per UFGS 23 09 00.
