# Electrical Engineering — B2185, B2187, B2855 Replace DDC Controls, NAS Patuxent River

Electrical engineering on this project supported the replacement DDC control systems across three occupied facilities at NAS Patuxent River, focusing on power distribution to new DDC panel assemblies, integration of government-furnished Facility Point of Connection (FPOC) panels, and the low-voltage electrical requirements of the controls infrastructure.

For Buildings 2187 and 2855, the project required installation of government-furnished FPOC panels — the electrical interface between the base-wide UMCS network and each building's new DDC system. Electrical engineering coordinated the installation of these panels within the existing electrical infrastructure of each facility, including identification of panel locations, circuit provisions, and conduit routing compatible with the occupied-facility constraint.

The broader controls replacement scope involved electrical power provisions for hundreds of new DDC controllers, field control panels, and associated sensors and actuators across the three buildings. Existing field wiring was reused where conditions allowed, requiring electrical review of wiring adequacy and documentation of reuse versus replacement decisions.

Cybersecurity requirements under UFC 4-010-06 and UFGS 25 05 11 imposed electrical discipline considerations including physical security of IP network components, network wiring isolation requirements, and IEEE 802.1x network access control implementation at the panel level. These requirements were coordinated across the electrical, controls, and telecommunications disciplines.
