# Commissioning — B2185, B2187, B2855 Replace DDC Controls, NAS Patuxent River

Commissioning on this project encompassed Total Building Commissioning per UFGS 01 91 00.15 for the HVAC and Building Automation System (BAS) scopes across all three buildings at NAS Patuxent River, with an independent Commissioning Authority (CxA) required by the contract.

The commissioning scope was significant given the technical complexity of the systems being replaced. In Building 2187, commissioning covered a large central plant — three 650-ton chillers, three cooling towers, four boilers, fifteen variable-speed AHUs, twenty-seven constant-volume AHUs, fifty-six CRAC units, and 442 VAV terminal units — all transitioning from an existing JCI Metasys platform to a new open-protocol BACnet DDC system. In Building 2185, commissioning addressed the replacement of an Automated Logic DDC system serving a similarly scaled installation. In Building 2855, commissioning was integrated with testing, adjusting, and balancing (TAB) of the full HVAC system per NEBB/AABC standards, providing a combined performance baseline for both the mechanical and controls systems.

All three buildings were required to complete Performance Verification Testing (PVT) per UFGS 23 09 00, conducted under government witness. PVT included endurance testing to demonstrate sustained DDC performance before system acceptance. Commissioning coordination extended to the cybersecurity scope — verifying that network access controls, password management, and security configurations met UFC 4-010-06 and DoD RMF requirements as part of overall system acceptance. Integration to the base-wide Niagara 4.x UMCS was also verified through the commissioning process, confirming that each building's DDC data points were correctly registered and accessible from the centralized government monitoring platform.
