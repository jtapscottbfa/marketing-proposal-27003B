Plumbing engineering scope for Building 1791 at Joint Base Andrews provided entirely new domestic water, sanitary drainage, natural gas, compressed air, and specialty plumbing systems throughout the 23,773 SF industrial and administrative facility.

Domestic cold and hot water distribution was designed in copper tubing — Type L above grade and Type K below grade — serving fixtures throughout the building. Sanitary waste, vent, and drain systems were designed in Schedule 40 PVC below the floor slab and cast iron no-hub above, with trench drains provided for service shop areas. Hot water is generated by two natural gas-fired instantaneous (tankless) water heaters mounted outdoors. Natural gas distribution piping was designed in black carbon steel Schedule 40, serving the building's gas-fired HVAC equipment and water heaters.

The compressed air system — central to the building's maintenance shop operations — was designed as a duplex rotary-screw compressor installation serving 18 service bays, three tire-changing machines, and a tool room. Two separate low-pressure breathing air compressors, each with a minimum capacity of 5 cfm and sized for dual 3M supplied-air face masks, were provided for operations requiring respiratory protection. An emergency eyewash and shower station was installed per applicable safety codes. The plumbing design coordinated closely with the mechanical discipline for equipment drain connections and with the civil discipline for the new underground water service and sanitary sewer connections at the building.
