Electrical engineering scope for Building 1791 at Joint Base Andrews comprised a complete replacement of the electrical service infrastructure and all associated distribution, lighting, and power systems throughout the 23,773 SF facility. The existing pad-mounted transformers, primary switch gear, and all wiring were demolished and replaced.

The new electrical service is anchored by a 1.5 MVA transformer rated at 13,200–480Y/277V, feeding a 1,600A 480/277V service-rated switchboard. Step-down transformers — a 225 KVA unit at 480V:120/240V and a 150 KVA unit at 480V:120/208V — serve lower-voltage loads throughout the building. A full complement of 480/277V, 208/120V, and 240/120V panelboards distributes power to building systems and equipment. Surge protection devices (SPDs) were installed throughout the service and distribution infrastructure.

Interior lighting was replaced entirely with LED fixtures compliant with ASHRAE 90.1 and UFC 3-530-01, with occupancy sensors provided throughout to meet energy performance requirements. Dedicated branch circuits were provided for motorized roll-up doors serving the shop areas. An arc flash hazard analysis was conducted per NFPA 70E to document incident energy levels and establish required personal protective equipment boundaries for the new electrical infrastructure. The electrical design coordinated with the mechanical discipline for power feeds to the six air handling units, two chillers, dust and fume collection systems, paint booth equipment, and compressed air compressors, as well as with the fire protection discipline for fire alarm and mass notification system power.
