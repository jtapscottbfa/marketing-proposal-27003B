The architectural scope for Building 1791 at Joint Base Andrews covered the full interior renovation of an approximately 23,773 SF single-story industrial and administrative facility originally constructed in 1966 with a 1993 addition. The building houses a mix of industrial operations — welding and fabrication, paint booth, non-destructive inspection — alongside offices, conference rooms, and support spaces, resulting in a mixed Group B (Business) and Group F-1 (Moderate Hazard Industrial) occupancy under IBC with Type IIB construction.

Architectural work encompassed completing demolition that had been left unfinished by a prior contractor whose contract was terminated mid-renovation, establishing the baseline from which new construction could proceed. New interior partitions, masonry CMU construction, roofing, door and frame replacement, window replacement, and a full scope of interior finishes were designed and documented: drywall, ceramic tile, acoustical ceilings, resilient and hard flooring, painting, and wall coverings. Toilet partitions and accessories were provided in restroom areas. The architectural design coordinated closely with the mechanical and fire protection disciplines to address the distinct ventilation and life safety requirements of the industrial zones — particularly the paint booth and welding areas — within the building's overall layout. Code compliance addressed current IBC requirements for the mixed occupancy, construction type, egress, and accessibility.
