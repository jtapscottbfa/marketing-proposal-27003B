Civil engineering scope for Building 1791 at Joint Base Andrews addressed site improvements and underground utility connections required to support the building renovation, including new fire protection infrastructure, utility service upgrades, and site restoration at utility installation areas.

A new fire hydrant was installed on G Street tied into the existing 8-inch watermain, increasing on-site fire protection coverage consistent with the new sprinkler system demands. A new fireline connection was established from Arnold Avenue, with a new Post Indicator Valve (PIV) installed 40 feet from the building to allow independent control of the building's fire suppression water supply. A new 2-inch domestic water service was connected to the building. On the sanitary side, a new 6-inch sanitary sewer main connection was made on the west side of the building, and the existing sanitary connection on the southwest side was abandoned in place. Curb and gutter disturbed by utility installation operations were replaced. The existing concrete drive at the northwest corner of the building was repaired. A new stoop was constructed at the southwest rear of the building to support revised egress and access requirements introduced by the architectural renovation.

Civil design coordinated with the plumbing discipline for service sizing and connection details and with the fire protection discipline for fireline routing, PIV placement, and hydrant location to meet UFC 3-600-01 hose stream and flow requirements.
