Fire protection engineering scope for Building 1791 at Joint Base Andrews provided a complete new wet-pipe automatic sprinkler system and a combined addressable fire alarm and mass notification system throughout the 23,773 SF industrial and administrative facility, designed in accordance with UFC 3-600-01, NFPA 13, UFC 4-021-01, and NFPA 72.

The sprinkler system was designed across multiple hazard classifications corresponding to the building's mixed occupancies: light hazard in office and administrative areas, ordinary hazard in general industrial zones, and extra hazard in the paint booth. The paint booth sprinkler design complied with NFPA 33 requirements for spray finishing operations and was provided with a dedicated isolation valve. Site fire protection infrastructure included a new Post Indicator Valve placed 40 feet from the building, a new fire department connection, and a new fire hydrant on G Street tied into the existing 8-inch watermain on the site. Hydrant flow testing conducted by Jensen Hughes on October 2, 2024 established the available water supply: static pressure 63 psi, residual pressure 60 psi, and flow of 1,840 gpm.

The fire alarm system is a fully addressable intelligent system providing both voice evacuation and mass notification functionality, with an RF transceiver connection to the Monaco D21 Central Monitoring Center. Duct smoke detectors were provided throughout the air distribution system per NFPA 90A. Carbon monoxide detection was installed in all rooms containing fuel-fired equipment. The fire protection design coordinated with the mechanical discipline for duct detector locations and with the civil discipline for the new fireline connection from Arnold Avenue.
