Building 1791 at Joint Base Andrews, Maryland is a 23,773 SF single-story industrial and administrative facility serving the Air Force Maintenance Group (MXG). Originally constructed in 1966 with a 1993 addition, the building houses welding and fabrication shops, a paint booth, non-destructive inspection operations, office and conference space, and associated support areas. The project was procured as a Design-Build contract (AJXF 16-1507) for the 316th Civil Engineering Squadron and carried a 465-calendar-day performance period — 100 days for design and 365 days for construction.

A prior renovation contract had been awarded and subsequently terminated before completion, leaving the building in a partially demolished and unfinished state. This contract assumed full responsibility for completing the renovation scope, including unfinished demolition left by the prior contractor, while delivering a fully functional, code-compliant facility. The design team served as prime consultant across all engineering disciplines, providing a comprehensive basis of design covering architecture, structural, mechanical, electrical, plumbing, fire protection, civil, environmental, and hazardous materials. Principal scope items included complete HVAC replacement with specialized industrial ventilation for welding and paint booth operations; full electrical service upgrade anchored by a 1.5 MVA transformer and 1,600A switchboard; new domestic water, sanitary, natural gas, and compressed air systems; a new wet-pipe fire suppression system; a combined addressable fire alarm and mass notification system; civil site improvements including a new fire hydrant and utility connections; and hazardous materials abatement covering asbestos, lead paint, mercury lamps, and PCB-containing equipment. The building is classified as mixed Group B and Group F-1 occupancy under IBC with Type IIB construction.
