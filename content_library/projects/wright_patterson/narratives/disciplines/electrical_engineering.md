# Electrical Engineering — Room Renovation, Bldg. 20840, Wright-Patterson AFB, OH

Electrical engineering services on this project covered power distribution, lighting, and circuitry modifications for three rooms (W133L, W133K, and W133G) in Building 20840 at Wright-Patterson Air Force Base, Ohio — an active research and aerospace medicine facility operated by the 711th Human Performance Wing.

New electrical work included the installation of a 208V emergency power outlet connected to panel L1E-A, providing critical power availability within the renovated laboratory space. A desktop electric raceway was installed along the south and east walls, providing seven receptacle positions to support benchtop laboratory equipment. GFCI duplex receptacles were provided at multiple wall locations throughout the rooms in compliance with laboratory safety requirements and applicable codes.

Lighting modifications included the relocation of an existing fixture in the W133K/W133L vestibule area and the rewiring of all lighting within that space to a single vestibule switch, consolidating switching control and supporting the renovated room configuration.

The electrical design was coordinated with the government-furnished laboratory snorkel equipment in W133G, the fire alarm and mass notification system relocation, and the overall MEP systems package across all three rooms. Work was performed under the design-build delivery method with UFC 4-510-01 and NFPA 99 governing design requirements. The occupied-facility setting required electrical design documentation to support sequenced installation coordinated with the active Wing operations schedule.
