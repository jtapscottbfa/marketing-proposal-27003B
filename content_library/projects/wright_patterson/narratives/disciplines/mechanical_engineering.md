# Mechanical Engineering — Room Renovation, Bldg. 20840, Wright-Patterson AFB, OH

Mechanical engineering services on this project covered HVAC system modifications for three laboratory and support rooms (W133L, W133K, and W133G) within Building 20840 at Wright-Patterson Air Force Base, Ohio. The building is an active 711th Human Performance Wing research facility subject to UFC 4-510-01 Design: Military Medical Facilities and NFPA 99 Health Care Facilities Code.

The existing HVAC system was designed to remain in service, with modifications limited to the specific rooms under renovation. In W133L and W133K, new air grilles and diffusers were provided, and the air valve serving W133L was upgraded to deliver 385 CFM to match the renovated laboratory's ventilation requirements. HVAC controls and air distribution systems were updated accordingly.

The most technically demanding mechanical scope element was the new laboratory snorkel exhaust system in W133G. A 3-inch stainless steel duct was installed to serve a government-furnished snorkel unit — model MOVE-X ME Type 1500-75 (also identified as FUMEX MET 1500-75) — providing point-source capture ventilation for laboratory fume control. An 8-inch Phoenix Controls air control valve was incorporated into the snorkel ductwork to provide airflow regulation. The snorkel system design was developed in coordination with the government-furnished equipment requirements and the overall building ventilation balance.

All mechanical work was performed under the design-build delivery method, with the design team responsible for full construction document production, specifications, and coordination with architectural and other MEP disciplines across the three-room scope.
