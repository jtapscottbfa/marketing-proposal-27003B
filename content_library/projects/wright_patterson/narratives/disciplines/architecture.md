# Architecture — Room Renovation, Bldg. 20840, Wright-Patterson AFB, OH

Architectural services on this project encompassed interior design and documentation for the renovation of three rooms (W133L, W133K, and W133G) in Area B of Building 20840, a 457,182 SF, four-story research and medical support facility at Wright-Patterson Air Force Base, Ohio. The facility serves the 711th Human Performance Wing and the United States Air Force School of Aerospace Medicine, and the renovation was required to comply with UFC 4-510-01 Design: Military Medical Facilities.

The architectural scope began with full interior demolition of the existing rooms, including removal of partitions, finishes, casework, and associated equipment, along with hazardous materials abatement. Reconstruction included new interior partitions, drywall, ceilings, and door and frame assemblies, as well as specification and coordination of laboratory-grade flooring, painting, and wall coverings. Signage for the renovated spaces was also within the architectural package.

Casework and laboratory furniture coordination was required to accommodate government-furnished laboratory equipment, including the snorkel exhaust system in W133G. The occupied-facility setting — an active Air Force research wing operating on a 0700–1600 schedule — necessitated phasing and sequencing considerations embedded in the design documentation to support contractor coordination with Wing personnel. Design was delivered under the design-build project delivery method with compliance to UFC 4-510-01 and NFPA 99 governing the finished environment.
