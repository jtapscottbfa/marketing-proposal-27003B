# Fire Protection Engineering — Room Renovation, Bldg. 20840, Wright-Patterson AFB, OH

Fire protection engineering services on this project addressed fire alarm and mass notification system modifications required by the interior renovation of three rooms (W133L, W133K, and W133G) in Building 20840 at Wright-Patterson Air Force Base, Ohio. The facility is an active research and aerospace medicine building serving the 711th Human Performance Wing, governed by UFC 4-510-01 Design: Military Medical Facilities and NFPA 99 Health Care Facilities Code.

The primary fire protection scope element was the relocation of existing fire alarm and mass notification system devices off a wall in W133G that was scheduled for demolition as part of the room renovation. The relocation required the production of NICET Level 4-stamped fire alarm drawings — a specialty certification requirement for fire alarm design on military installations — which the design team incorporated into the overall design-build documentation package.

No fire suppression system modifications were identified in the source record for this scope. The fire alarm relocation was coordinated with the architectural demolition sequence, the electrical systems package, and the overall room reconfiguration to ensure continuous life safety coverage within the occupied building throughout the construction phase.

Fire protection engineering discipline was on the design team for this project; the scope as documented in source records is limited to the fire alarm and mass notification relocation described above. Additional system-level details beyond what is captured in the project source documents are not available in this record.
