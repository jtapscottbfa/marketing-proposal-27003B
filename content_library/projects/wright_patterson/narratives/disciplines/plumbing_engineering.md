# Plumbing Engineering — Room Renovation, Bldg. 20840, Wright-Patterson AFB, OH

Plumbing engineering services on this project covered domestic water, laboratory plumbing fixtures, and utility system modifications for three rooms (W133L, W133K, and W133G) within Building 20840 at Wright-Patterson Air Force Base, Ohio. The project was subject to UFC 4-510-01 Design: Military Medical Facilities and NFPA 99 Health Care Facilities Code, both of which impose specific requirements on laboratory and healthcare-adjacent plumbing systems.

Existing domestic cold and hot water piping within all three rooms was evaluated and retained, with new connections made to serve the renovated laboratory fixtures. In W133L and W133K, a new stainless steel laboratory sink with a gooseneck faucet was provided, along with an eye wash wand with a tempered water connection — meeting emergency eye wash requirements for a laboratory environment. In W133G, a chemical-resistant epoxy/resin sink was installed with a hand-held eye wash station, selected for compatibility with the chemical research activities anticipated in that room.

Existing utility piping for nitrogen and compressed air, present from the prior use of the rooms, was abandoned in place and capped as part of the demolition and systems coordination scope.

The plumbing design was coordinated with the architectural casework, MEP systems rough-in, and the government-furnished laboratory equipment to ensure fixture placement, drainage, and tempered water supply aligned with the laboratory layout. All work was delivered under the design-build project delivery method.
