The Naval Air Systems Command (NAVAIR) Building 2272 engagement is a sustained, multi-phase interior renovation and workplace modernization program at Naval Air Station Patuxent River, Maryland. Building 2272 — the five-story Moffett Building, a cast-in-place concrete-framed headquarters office structure originally constructed in 1996 — houses multiple NAVAIR programmatic user groups that the command sought to consolidate and modernize into contemporary, activity-based workplace environments. The design team provided prime MEP consulting services across Phases 2b through at least Phase 6, encompassing mechanical, electrical, plumbing, fire protection, and telecommunications engineering alongside architectural interior design.

Each phase targeted specific suites distributed across the building's five floors, upgrading the existing 1996 VAV HVAC infrastructure, installing LED lighting and new electrical power distribution, modifying the wet-pipe sprinkler and Simplex 4100ES fire alarm systems, and providing structured cabling to support new partition layouts and modern office configurations. Suite 345 on the third floor was separately procured and designed to ICD-705 SCIF standards. The program spans multiple contract actions and BFA project numbers under a task-order-based delivery structure typical of sustained NAVFAC on-call engagements. Active design work remained ongoing as of April 2026, with additional suites in 35–65% design phases.
