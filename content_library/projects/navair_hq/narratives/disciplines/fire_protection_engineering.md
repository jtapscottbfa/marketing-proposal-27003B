Fire protection engineering scope across all Building 2272 renovation phases addressed modifications to the building's existing combined wet-pipe sprinkler and standpipe system. The system features 4-inch vertical risers, a 2.5-inch main floor loop serving all project areas, and a 750 gpm / 100 psi sprinkler pump with 61 psi static pressure observed — infrastructure original to the 1996 building and confirmed consistent across all phase Basis of Design documents. Each suite phase required sprinkler head relocations and additions to serve new partition layouts, coordinated with the architectural floor plans to maintain code-compliant coverage under reconfigured ceiling planes.

The building's fire alarm system — a Simplex 4100ES addressable fire alarm control panel with a combined fire alarm and mass notification system — was addressed in each phase for strobe and speaker appliance relocations and additions to serve new occupant configurations. Fire alarm scope was coordinated with the base Simplex system's addressable zone structure.

Suite 345 involved materially different fire protection requirements as part of a separate ICD-705 compliant Sensitive Compartmented Information Facility (SCIF) construction procurement (Solicitation N4008024R2402). The SCIF construction requirements and security construction standards applicable to Suite 345 governed the fire protection design for that space, distinct from the standard office renovation approach applied to other suites.
