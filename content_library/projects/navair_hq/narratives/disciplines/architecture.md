Architectural scope on the Building 2272 renovation program covered the full interior fit-out of multiple office suites across all five floors of the Moffett Building. Each phase transformed existing federal office space — traditional closed-office layouts typical of 1990s government buildings — into contemporary activity-based workplace environments for NAVAIR programmatic user groups including procurement groups, weapon systems program executive offices, and related headquarters functions.

Per-phase interior construction scope included new demountable and fixed partition systems, interior doors with frames and hardware, acoustical ceiling tile systems, flooring (carpet and resilient tile), painting and wall coverings, architectural woodwork for reception areas and work cafes, and furnishings. Building 2272's Type IB cast-in-place concrete framed structure with a clear floor-to-floor height established the physical envelope for each suite renovation.

Suite 345 was designed to Intelligence Community Directive (ICD) 705 standards, governing the construction of a Sensitive Compartmented Information Facility (SCIF). ICD-705 SCIF construction requirements — acoustic attenuation, penetration sealing, access control infrastructure — defined the architectural and construction scope for that space, distinguishing it from the standard open-office renovation work in other suites. Suite 345 was procured through a separate solicitation from the general office renovation phases.

Construction scope across the program also included wood architectural woodwork elements and furnishings provisions, consistent with the activity-based workplace program established in the Phase 2b schematic design.
