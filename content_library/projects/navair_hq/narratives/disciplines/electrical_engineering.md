Electrical engineering scope across all Building 2272 renovation phases covered lighting, power distribution, and low-voltage specialty systems for each suite. LED 2×2 troffer luminaires were installed throughout renovated areas in place of existing fixtures, with occupancy sensors specified for enclosed private offices to support energy performance requirements. Branch circuit wiring used EMT conduit throughout with THHN/THWN copper conductors, consistent across all phases.

Power distribution scope provided dedicated receptacles for printers and copiers, workstation monitors, and breakroom equipment including ground-fault circuit interrupter (GFCI) outlets. Furniture-integrated power systems using whips and floor boxes served open workstation clusters. Sound masking systems were installed throughout renovated suites to support acoustic privacy in the activity-based workplace layouts.

All new electrical work was served from the building's existing distribution service — no new service entrance equipment was required across the standard office renovation phases. Each phase delivered new circuits within the existing panel capacity, coordinated with the building's existing electrical infrastructure dating from the 1996 original construction. Electrical design was produced at 95% Basis of Design submittal level per phase, with construction documents aligned to each suite's scope of work.
