## Architecture — Building 285 Demolish Three Smoke Fans, NAS Oceana

Architectural scope on this project addressed the building envelope openings and interior finishes affected by the smoke fan demolition in Building 285 at Naval Air Station Oceana. Each of the three fans was connected to the exterior of the building through a 72-inch by 30-inch exterior louver. Following louver removal, the three openings in the exterior wall required infill construction to restore the building envelope to a weathertight, finished condition.

The louver infill design specified 8-inch CMU masonry construction to fill each opening structurally, with 1-inch EPS insulation board applied to the exterior CMU face. A vinyl copolymer aggregate finish system applied over reinforcing fabric was selected to match the appearance and texture of the building's existing EIFS exterior, so that the infilled locations would coordinate visually with the surrounding facade. Metal flashing at each infill was detailed to integrate with the existing wall assembly and prevent water infiltration.

Interior work included removal of the mechanical ceiling grilles at each fan location and replacement of all acoustical ceiling tiles disturbed by the demolition activity. The ACT replacement scope was sized to address tiles directly displaced or damaged during the fan, duct, and support removal work. Architectural coordination with the mechanical and structural scopes was required to align the ceiling work with the duct demolition phasing and to ensure the floor infills and ceiling replacements were addressed as a complete, finished package.
