Building 285 Demolish Three Smoke Fans is a design-build project performed for NAVFAC Mid-Atlantic at Naval Air Station Oceana in Virginia Beach, Virginia. The project required selective demolition of three 20,000-CFM axial smoke exhaust fans (F-14, F-15, and F-16) from second-floor mechanical rooms, together with all associated ductwork, duct silencers, hangers, exterior louvers, and electrical circuits back to source panels. In place of the removed louvers, the design called for masonry infill using 8-inch CMU with an EIFS exterior finish matched to the existing building facade; disturbed acoustical ceiling tile was similarly replaced to restore interior finishes.

The design scope was multi-discipline, spanning mechanical, electrical, architectural, and structural engineering. Structural design addressed the concrete floor openings left by the 42-inch ductwork demolition, producing reinforced concrete slab infills on light-gauge metal plate and HSS tube framing, designed for 50 PSF live load or a 1,000-pound point load and finished flush with the existing floor. The project was executed under a design-build contract vehicle with the team serving as Designer of Record, delivering an Interim Final Basis of Design in February 2025. Project BFA #24-004, NAVFAC eProjects WO# 1703410.
