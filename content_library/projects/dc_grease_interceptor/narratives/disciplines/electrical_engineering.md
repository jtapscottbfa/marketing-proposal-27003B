Electrical engineering for the NSF Kitchen Grease Interceptor project at Washington VA Medical Center provided power circuits to serve three newly installed grease interceptors in the First Floor B Wing kitchen. The design specified three 20A single-pole circuit breakers in the existing Panel "K" located in Room 1C-116, which carried both 480Y/277V and 208Y/120V three-phase, four-wire services. Three 20A GFI receptacles in cast boxes were specified at each interceptor location, with branch circuit wiring run in EMT conduit using copper conductors with THHN/THWN insulation.

The GFI protection requirement reflected both NEC code compliance and the wet environment conditions typical of commercial kitchen equipment areas. Panel "K" was the designated power source for the new circuits, and the design coordinated circuit breaker sizing and slot assignments with the existing panel configuration to confirm available capacity.

Design criteria included ANSI C2 (National Electrical Safety Code), NFPA 70 (National Electrical Code), the Department of Veterans Affairs Electrical Design Manual, and applicable OSHA regulations. The electrical scope was documented in the Basis of Design submitted on October 13, 2022, and carried through construction documents, construction administration, and post-construction record drawings within the contracted performance period.
