Plumbing engineering for the NSF Kitchen Grease Interceptor project at Washington VA Medical Center involved the design of three 30-GPM-capacity grease interceptors for the First Floor B Wing kitchen, with Type "L" copper piping connecting each interceptor to its corresponding kitchen equipment and to the existing floor sinks. One grease interceptor was connected to the kitchen pulper; two were connected to commercial sinks equipped with garbage grinders. The sizing and piping configuration were designed to handle the grease and waste loading from each distinct equipment type independently, preserving operational flexibility and simplifying maintenance access.

Design criteria included the VA Plumbing Design Manual (May 2021), DC Water GI Regulation, IPC, applicable UFC and ASPE publications, OSHA regulations, NFPA, and the District of Columbia Building Code. The 30-GPM interceptor sizing was established in compliance with DC Water requirements for kitchen grease interceptor installations. The Basis of Design, submitted as a final deliverable on October 13, 2022, documented the plumbing system design in full and served as the basis for construction documents and subsequent construction administration. Post-construction services included the preparation of record drawings reflecting installed conditions.
