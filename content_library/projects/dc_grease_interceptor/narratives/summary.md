The NSF Kitchen Grease Interceptor project at the Washington VA Medical Center (50 Irving Street NW, Washington, DC) involved complete A/E engineering design and construction administration services for the installation of three grease interceptors in the First Floor B Wing kitchen. The work was procured under a federal VOSB set-aside task order (solicitation 36C24522R022, Project 688-22-003) with an estimated construction cost in the range of $100,000 to $250,000.

Plumbing design addressed three 30-GPM-capacity grease interceptors connected via Type "L" copper piping — one serving the kitchen pulper and two serving commercial sinks equipped with garbage grinders — in compliance with the VA Plumbing Design Manual (May 2021), DC Water GI Regulation, IPC, and applicable UFC standards. Electrical design provided three new 20A single-pole circuits from existing Panel "K" (Room 1C-116) and three 20A GFI receptacles mounted in cast boxes at the interceptor locations, utilizing EMT conduit with copper THHN/THWN conductors and tying into the facility's existing 208Y/120V and 480Y/277V three-phase services. The A/E scope covered construction documents, construction administration, and post-construction record drawings, executed within a 30-calendar-day performance period from notice to proceed. The Basis of Design was submitted as a final deliverable on October 13, 2022.
