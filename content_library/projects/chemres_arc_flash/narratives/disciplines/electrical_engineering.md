BFA's electrical engineering team served as the engineer of record for a comprehensive power systems study at the CHEMRES Chemical Plant in Chesapeake, Virginia (August 2022). The work encompassed short circuit analysis, protective device coordination, equipment interrupting-capacity evaluation, and arc flash hazard analysis for the facility's entire 34.5 kV–480V electrical distribution system.

The team modeled the distribution network in SKM Power Tools, capturing the full impedance hierarchy from the Dominion Power utility connection—via a Westinghouse 345GS1500 main substation switch rated 1,200 A continuous and 22,000 A short circuit—through five step-down transformers (four at 1,151 kVA and one at 1,500 kVA), multiple 480V bus systems (including 3,200 A and 1,600 A bus ducts), more than thirteen motor control centers, and the facility's downstream distribution panels. All analyses were performed in accordance with OSHA, IEEE Std 1584-2018, NFPA 70-2022, and NFPA 70E-2021.

The study confirmed that all evaluated equipment met applicable interrupting-capacity requirements. Arc flash incident energy calculations were completed for 35 labeled equipment locations; the highest recorded value was 64.8 cal/cm² at E/W Sub A under maximum trip time conditions. BFA delivered the final study report, correlated one-line diagram drawings, and a complete arc flash label set—providing the plant with the quantitative hazard documentation required to maintain a compliant electrical safety program.
