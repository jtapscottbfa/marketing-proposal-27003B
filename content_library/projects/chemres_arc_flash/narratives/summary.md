Bowman, Foster & Associates, Inc. (BFA) performed a comprehensive power systems study for the CHEMRES Chemical Plant in Chesapeake, Virginia, completing the final report in August 2022 (BFA Project No. 22-005). The study encompassed short circuit analysis, protective device coordination, equipment interrupting-capacity evaluation, and arc flash hazard analysis for the plant's entire electrical distribution system. The facility receives 34.5 kV utility service from Dominion Power through a Westinghouse power circuit breaker and distributes power to production areas through five step-down transformers—four rated 1,151 kVA and one rated 1,500 kVA—operating at 480V across multiple bus systems and more than thirteen motor control centers.

BFA modeled the full electrical system using SKM Power Tools and conducted the arc flash evaluation in accordance with OSHA regulations, IEEE Std 1584-2018, NFPA 70-2022, and NFPA 70E-2021. The study produced arc flash labels for 35 equipment locations throughout the plant, with the highest recorded incident energy of 64.8 cal/cm² at E/W Sub A under maximum trip time conditions. All equipment was found to meet applicable interrupting-capacity requirements; no equipment failed the evaluation. The study delivered the documentation and labeled equipment data the plant needed to establish and maintain a compliant arc flash safety program.
