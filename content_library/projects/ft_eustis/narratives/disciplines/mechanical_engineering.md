Mechanical engineering scope on this project addressed HVAC system modifications and controls work throughout the 23 renovated restrooms of McDonald Army Health Center Building 576 — a 134,984 square foot three-story military outpatient clinic on Fort Eustis Army Base. The work was delivered as part of a complete multi-discipline Basis of Design under a design-build task order, with the AE team subconsulting to the construction prime.

HVAC systems serving the renovation area were replaced, modified, or augmented as required to serve the reconfigured restroom spaces. Thermostats and temperature sensors were relocated per revised floor plans to maintain proper environmental monitoring and control within the updated configurations. Upon completion of all HVAC work, testing, adjusting, and balancing (TAB) was required, to be performed by a firm certified by AABC, NEBB, or TABB — consistent with the standards applicable to work in an occupied DoD healthcare facility.

Mechanical scope was sequenced and coordinated with plumbing, electrical, and architectural work across the active facility. The project's active-facility constraint — a continuously operating outpatient clinic with DHA oversight of utility disruptions — required that HVAC system outages be planned and staged collaboratively with facility management to avoid interrupting climate control in areas of the building outside the immediate construction zone.
