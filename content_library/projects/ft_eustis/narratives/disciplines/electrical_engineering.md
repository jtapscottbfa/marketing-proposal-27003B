Electrical engineering scope on this project covered interior lighting replacement and controls installation throughout the 23 renovated restrooms of McDonald Army Health Center Building 576 — a 134,984 square foot three-story military outpatient clinic at Fort Eustis, Virginia. Work was delivered as part of a multi-discipline design-build package, with the AE team subconsulting to the construction prime.

LED lighting fixtures replaced the existing lighting inventory throughout the renovation scope, with fixture types including wall-mounted, recessed, and ceiling-mounted luminaires selected per the requirements of each restroom configuration. Occupancy-sensor controls — both wall-switch-type and ceiling-mounted types — were installed to provide automatic shutoff in unoccupied spaces, improving energy performance in the active healthcare facility. Existing electrical conduit and conductors were evaluated and retained where serviceable, limiting new rough-in to locations where fixture placement changes required it. No new emergency power infrastructure was required within this scope.

The electrical design was coordinated with concurrent architectural and plumbing work, as well as with HVAC modifications in the same spaces. Sequencing of electrical outages across the 23 restrooms was managed in coordination with the Defense Health Agency and facility management to maintain continuous clinic operations in the occupied outpatient building throughout construction.
