Plumbing engineering scope on this project replaced all plumbing fixtures in the 23 renovated restrooms of McDonald Army Health Center Building 576 with motion-activated, battery-operated models meeting current standards for infection control and water efficiency in a healthcare facility. Building 576 is a 134,984 square foot three-story military outpatient clinic at Fort Eustis, Virginia, and the fixture replacement was a central driver of the renovation's programmatic justification. Work was delivered under a design-build task order with the AE team subconsulting to the construction prime.

Motion-activated battery-operated faucets were installed at all lavatories, and motion-activated battery-operated flush valves were installed on all toilets and urinals. The battery-operated configuration eliminated the need to rough in hardwired solenoid valve power infrastructure, simplifying installation within the existing plumbing layout. Existing domestic water supply and sanitary drain piping were retained in place as serviceable; new piping connections were provided only where fixture replacements required adjustments to rough-in dimensions or where condition of existing piping necessitated replacement.

Plumbing design and construction were sequenced across the 23 restrooms in coordination with the Defense Health Agency and facility management, planning domestic water shutoffs to maintain uninterrupted access to restroom facilities across the active outpatient clinic throughout the project duration.
