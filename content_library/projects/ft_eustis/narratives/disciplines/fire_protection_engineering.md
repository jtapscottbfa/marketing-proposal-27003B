Fire protection engineering scope on this project addressed fire alarm device coordination and fire suppression compliance for the 23 renovated restrooms of McDonald Army Health Center Building 576 — a 134,984 square foot three-story military outpatient clinic at Fort Eustis, Virginia. Work was performed as part of a multi-discipline design-build package, with the AE team subconsulting to the construction prime.

Fire alarm horn/strobe devices in the affected restrooms were evaluated for installation as part of the renovation; the scope of work identified this as a code question requiring resolution during design, indicating that final device configuration was subject to code analysis and coordination with the authority having jurisdiction. Fire suppression systems were addressed per UFC 3-600-01, the Unified Facilities Criteria that governs fire protection design for Department of Defense facilities, ensuring that the restroom renovation complied with the applicable DoD-specific fire protection standard.

Fire protection scope was integrated with the broader multi-discipline design and required coordination with the active facility to manage any required fire alarm system impairments during construction. As with all work in this occupied outpatient clinic, impairment planning and notification procedures were coordinated with facility management and the Defense Health Agency consistent with the project's active-facility operational constraints.
