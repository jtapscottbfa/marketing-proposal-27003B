McDonald Army Health Center (MAHC) Building 576 at Fort Eustis, Virginia is a 134,984 square foot three-story outpatient clinic constructed in 1962 that serves as the primary ambulatory care facility on post. This project upgraded restroom finishes and fixtures across 23 restrooms — twenty-two on the first floor and one on the second floor — under FY22 funding as CLIN 0003 of the Eastern Region Miscellaneous Projects task order (solicitation W912DY22R0073), procured as an 8(a) sole-source set-aside by USACE Huntsville Engineering and Support Center. The design team participated as architect-engineer of record subconsulting to a design-build prime contractor.

Work encompassed full demolition and replacement of deteriorated interior finishes: ceramic tile flooring and wall finishes, acoustical ceiling systems, toilet partitions, and interior doors with frames and hardware. Mechanical, electrical, plumbing, and fire protection systems were upgraded in parallel — HVAC systems were modified and balanced by a certified TAB firm, LED lighting fixtures with occupancy-sensor controls replaced the existing inventory, motion-activated battery-operated faucets and flush valves replaced all plumbing fixtures, and fire alarm devices were coordinated per applicable code. Existing water piping and electrical conduit were retained where serviceable, limiting demolition and new rough-in to what the fixture replacements required. Throughout design and construction, work was coordinated with the Defense Health Agency and facility management to stage utility disruptions and maintain continuous clinic operations across the active outpatient facility.
