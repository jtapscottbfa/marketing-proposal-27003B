The plumbing engineering scope on this project was a single focused improvement: installation of a digital water meter on an existing 2-inch domestic cold water supply line at Coastal Precast Systems' wood working shop in Chesapeake, Virginia (project no. 21-006, final submittal April 2021).

The existing plumbing configuration — a 2-inch CW main entering the wood working shop through the exterior wall, with an existing isolation valve — was documented on the existing site plan (PS001) and the enlarged plan (P-101). New plumbing work comprised the digital water meter inserted into the vertical supply piping, a Y-strainer on the 2-inch CW line, and union connections to facilitate future meter maintenance and replacement. The existing isolation valve was retained in place. No other plumbing systems were within scope; HVAC, electrical, and fire protection disciplines had no presence on this project.

The plumbing design was documented on a single enlarged plan at 1/4"=1'-0" with an accompanying no-scale Water Meter Piping Detail (Detail 1/P-101) illustrating the complete assembly: 2-inch CW supply, union, digital water meter, Y-strainer, and 2-inch CW downstream continuation. The design team sealed and submitted the complete plumbing documentation as a final submittal. Contract value, construction schedule, and construction-phase outcomes are not captured in the source record for this project.
