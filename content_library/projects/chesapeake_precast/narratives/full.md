## Project Overview

This project involved the design and documentation for installation of a digital water meter at the Coastal Precast Systems industrial facility in Chesapeake, Virginia. Coastal Precast Systems operates a precast concrete manufacturing plant at 1316 Yacht Drive, with associated batch plant, wood working, and concrete runway infrastructure on the site. The project — assigned internal number 21-006 — was a minor capital plumbing improvement: the addition of a single digital water meter on an existing 2-inch domestic cold water supply line feeding the wood working shop.

Final construction drawings were issued as a final submittal in April 2021. The source record for this project consists solely of the three-sheet drawing set; no basis of design, specifications, or contract documents are captured in the record.

## Scope of Work

The scope was limited to a single targeted modification to the existing plumbing system at an active industrial facility. The existing 2-inch domestic cold water (CW) main enters the wood working shop through the exterior wall. New work, as documented on Drawing P-101 (Enlarged Plan – New Work, scale 1/4"=1'-0") and the accompanying Water Meter Piping Detail (no scale), comprised:

- A new digital water meter inserted into the vertical 2-inch CW supply piping
- A Y-strainer installed on the 2-inch CW line upstream of downstream distribution
- Union connections to facilitate future meter maintenance and replacement
- The existing isolation valve retained in place

No other building systems — HVAC, electrical, fire protection — were within the project scope. No modifications to site infrastructure or the building shell were included beyond the pipe work at the meter location. The existing site plan (PS001, scale 1"=60'-0") established the location of the new work, with a circle-1 symbol locating the water meter installation area at the southwest entry of the wood working area.

## Role on Project

The design team served as prime consultant, preparing the complete three-sheet final drawing set — cover sheet, existing site plan, and enlarged plan with piping detail. The drawing set was stamped and sealed by the project engineer and submitted as a final submittal in April 2021. The project was executed as a standalone single-trade engagement. No subconsultants are documented in the source record.

## Key Challenges and Resolutions

The source record does not contain a basis of design, project narrative, or specifications document, so specific engineering challenges are not directly documented. At the scale of this project — a single digital water meter installation on an existing 2-inch line — the design challenge was primarily one of coordination and configuration: confirming the existing pipe layout, determining the appropriate placement in the vertical run, and selecting and specifying the Y-strainer and union connections necessary to support future meter serviceability and replacement without full system shutdown.

The site context introduces one characteristic relevant to construction: the facility is an active heavy-industrial precast concrete manufacturing plant with a batch plant, concrete runways, a railroad right-of-way along the eastern boundary, and active yard operations. Installation of a meter on an active domestic water main at such a facility requires coordination with plant operations for service interruption sequencing. The extent of this coordination is not documented in the drawing set, but the design addresses it through the retained isolation valve and union connections that enable future maintenance access.
