The project involved the design and documentation for installation of a digital water meter on an existing 2-inch domestic cold water supply line at Coastal Precast Systems' industrial precast concrete manufacturing facility at 1316 Yacht Drive, Chesapeake, Virginia. The scope was a focused single-trade plumbing improvement: a new digital water meter was inserted into the vertical supply piping at the entry to the wood working shop, accompanied by a Y-strainer and union connections to support future serviceability, while the existing isolation valve was retained in place. Final construction drawings were issued in April 2021 under internal project number 21-006.

The design team served as prime consultant, preparing a three-sheet final drawing set — a cover sheet, an existing site plan, and an enlarged plan of the new work with an accompanying piping detail — that fully documented the installation requirements for the single meter location. The project was executed as a standalone single-trade engagement at an active heavy-industrial facility, with the meter installation scoped to a single location on the 2-inch cold water main. Source documents for this project consist solely of the final drawing set; contract value, schedule, and construction-phase outcomes are not captured in the available project record.
