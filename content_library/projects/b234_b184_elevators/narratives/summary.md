# Summary — Building 234 / Building 184 Freight Elevator Replacement, Norfolk Naval Shipyard

This project encompassed architect-engineer design services for the design-bid-build replacement of two aging freight elevators at Norfolk Naval Shipyard (NNSY) in Portsmouth, Virginia. Under NAVFAC contract N40085-17-D-0347 (FY 2020), the work addressed two separate but concurrently designed construction packages: Building 234 Elevator 34, a hydraulic freight elevator serving three floors at 10,000-lb capacity, and Building 184 Elevator 60, a geared traction freight elevator serving four floors at 16,000-lb capacity. Both existing units were replaced with new Class C1 geared electric traction freight elevators rated at 200–250 fpm, compliant with UFC 3-490-06 and ASME A17.1/A17.2.

The design scope extended well beyond the elevator mechanisms themselves. It encompassed full MEP analysis and modification, structural assessment and repair of cracked and spalled shaft walls and machine rooms, HVAC replacement with new DDC controls integrated into the NNSY base-wide building automation system, fire protection upgrades (wet sprinkler, fire alarm interface, shunt-trip interlocks), hazardous materials survey and abatement (including hydraulic oil removal and underground piston casing backfill at Building 234), and State Historic Preservation Office (SHPO) window replacement coordination at both buildings to achieve a finding of No Adverse Effect. Post-construction award services were included. The combined estimated construction cost across both buildings was approximately $2.68 million, with an estimated construction completion date of August 30, 2023.
