# Plumbing Engineering — Building 234 / Building 184 Freight Elevator Replacement, NNSY

Plumbing engineering scope on this project addressed pit drainage at both elevator locations. At Building 234 and Building 184, site investigation revealed that the existing elevator pit sump pumps — single 120V fractional-horsepower submersible units — were submerged in mud and inoperable at both locations. New sump pumps were designed for both pits, with discharge piping routed to approved drainage points as directed by NAVFAC.

At Building 234, an additional and more complex plumbing/hazmat scope element arose from the elevator type change. The existing hydraulic elevator system included an underground hydraulic piston casing with residual hydraulic oil. Removal of all hydraulic oil from the underground piston casing was required per NNSY requirements, and the abandoned underground piston casing was to be backfilled and the pit opening capped with solid concrete. This work required coordination with the structural discipline (pit floor infilling and waterproofing) and the hazardous materials program (hydraulic oil classification and disposal).

The plumbing scope was limited to pit drainage and the Building 234 hydraulic oil/piston casing work; no domestic water, sanitary, or other plumbing systems were within the elevator replacement scope at either building.
