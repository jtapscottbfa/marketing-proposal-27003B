Electrical engineering scope on the Taylor Mill Treatment Plant project spanned demolition of legacy chemical feed electrical infrastructure, new electrical systems for the PAC feed and UV disinfection improvements, hazardous area lighting, and integration with the plant's SCADA system — across both the chemical building and the main treatment building.

In the chemical building, the scope included disconnection and removal of motor connections and control panels serving legacy polymer feed valve motors, filter aid polymer feed pumps, a hybrid coagulant transfer pump, carbon mixer, carbon screw feeder, PAC feeder pumps, and a sump pump, along with associated polymer mixer and sump pump controllers and polymer feed valve control panels (salvaged for owner reuse). New explosion-proof LED luminaires (LE551, 4-ft, 2-row, 64W, 4000K, rated Class I Division 2 and Class II Division 2) were specified throughout NEC-classified areas of the chemical building. New electrical infrastructure supported the replacement PAC feed system.

In the main treatment building, electrical scope addressed the UV disinfection system replacement: removal of UV reactor units A1 and A2 and their associated wiring, demolition of the UV uninterruptible power supply (UPS) system and battery bank in the inter-building tunnel, and installation of a new UV system controller. Panel work encompassed LPB-1 and LPB-2 panelboards in the main treatment building.

Electrical specifications covered the full project: supplemental electrical requirements (spec section 260010, including SCADA integration), low-voltage conductors and cables (260519), grounding and bonding (260526), hangers and supports (260529), conduit systems — EMT, IMC, LFMC, and PVC (260533.13), boxes and covers (260533.16), system identification (260553), panelboards (262416), wiring devices (262726), and LED interior lighting (265119).
