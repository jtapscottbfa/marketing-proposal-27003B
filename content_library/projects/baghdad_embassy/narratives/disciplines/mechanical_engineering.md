Mechanical engineering scope on the Baghdad Diplomatic Support Center Upgrade Fire Protection System project was limited to HVAC design for the two fire pump houses supporting the upgraded fire suppression infrastructure.

Mini-split air conditioning units were sized and specified for climate control of both the existing fire pump house and the new PAX Terminal fire pump house. TRACE 700 thermal load calculations were performed for both spaces using Baghdad design conditions of 84°F outdoor dry-bulb / 62°F wet-bulb for summer and 33°F for winter. The existing fire pump house (approximately 195 SF) was calculated to require approximately 1.2 tons (14.9 MBh) of cooling capacity; the new PAX Terminal fire pump house (approximately 406 SF) required approximately 1.6 tons (19.0 MBh). Equipment selection and manufacturer product cutsheets were included in the CD-3 submittal package.

Mechanical specifications were produced per OBO standard sections, including split-system air conditioners (238126), testing, adjusting, and balancing for HVAC (230593), HVAC piping insulation (230719), commissioning of HVAC (230805), and instrumentation and controls for HVAC (230905). All specifications conformed to OBO standard requirements including NEMA and AABC/NEBB compliance where applicable. Calculations were performed by Bowman, Foster & Associates, Inc. as part of the design subconsultant team under the FPLS Engineering arrangement.
