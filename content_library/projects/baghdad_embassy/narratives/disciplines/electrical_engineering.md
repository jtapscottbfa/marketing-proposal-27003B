Electrical engineering on the Baghdad Diplomatic Support Center Upgrade Fire Protection System project provided power distribution and lighting design in support of the compound-wide fire protection upgrades.

Power distribution work included new electrical connections to existing panelboards for fire alarm control panels and HVAC mini-split units across multiple buildings, new underground conduit systems and fiber optic cable to support the compound-wide fire alarm network linking eight buildings to the Tactical Operations Center (TOC) in Building 602, and panelboard replacements as required to accommodate new equipment loads. Electrical distribution design was governed by OBO standard specification sections for common electrical and communications work (260508), low-voltage power conductors and cables (260518), panelboards (262417), wiring devices (262725), and underground ducts and raceways (337105), all conforming to NFPA 70 as amended by OBO standards.

Lighting design scope included replacement of interior corridor lighting in Building 602, associated with ceiling work required for the new sprinkler installation. Lithonia 2GTL2 2×2 LED fixtures at 18.39 W were specified for two corridors. Photometric calculations confirmed adequate illumination: the northwest corridor (24 fixtures) achieved approximately 17.2 footcandles average, and the northeast corridor (18 fixtures) achieved approximately 17.4 footcandles average. Lighting calculations were prepared and included in the CD-3 submittal package. Lighting design is credited to the IEP team member within the design sub-team under the subconsultant arrangement.
