Fire protection engineering was the primary design discipline on the Baghdad Diplomatic Support Center Upgrade Fire Protection System project. The scope encompassed complete fire protection system replacement across eight existing compound buildings ranging from passenger air movement terminals to aircraft hangars and a modular firing range.

Key deliverables included new wet pipe sprinkler systems in Buildings 278, 279, 306 (optional), 602, 606, and 610; new fire alarm systems in all eight buildings integrated via fiber optic cable to the compound Tactical Operations Center (TOC) in Building 602; and IR3 infrared flame detectors in the Kirby Hangars (Buildings 606 and 610) for large open-area detection. A new fire pump house was designed with redundant pump configuration, supplementing the existing pump house retained as secondary. The underground fire main network was redesigned to replace the existing main from Building 241 to Building 278 and extend new mains to Buildings 306, 606, and 610, with new fire hydrant branches at each tie-in point. Four existing 250,000-gallon water storage tanks were incorporated into an ITM program per NFPA 25. Complete AFFF system removal in Building 610 was coordinated with a PFAS disposal plan per ITRC guidance. An optional scope addition (CLIN 004) provided new low-level high-expansion foam suppression systems in both hangar buildings per NFPA 409. The Notifier fire alarm system in Building 602 was extended, and smoke detectors were designed for Sully Compound sleeping units. All fire protection design was produced under QFPE stamp per OBO Deliverable Requirements and conformed to NFPA 11, 13, 20, 24, 25, and 409.
