The Baghdad Diplomatic Support Center (BDSC) – Upgrade Fire Protection System project provided comprehensive fire protection design services across eight buildings and associated infrastructure at a 147-hectare U.S. State Department compound adjacent to Baghdad International Airport, Iraq. The project was issued by the Bureau of Overseas Buildings Operations (OBO) under solicitation 19AQMM23RBAGHDADFIRE and delivered as a design-build contract, with the design team serving as subconsultant engineer to prime Design-Build contractor Futron.

Scope included replacement fire alarm systems in all eight buildings, networked via fiber optic cable to the compound Tactical Operations Center; new wet pipe sprinkler systems in six buildings; a new fire pump house with redundant pump configuration; replacement and extension of the underground fire main network across the compound; complete AFFF system removal and PFAS-compliant disposal in Building 610; and optional high-expansion foam suppression systems for both aircraft hangars per NFPA 409. Supporting scope included HVAC mini-split systems for two fire pump houses, electrical connections and panelboard work across multiple buildings, and interior lighting replacement in Building 602. All design conformed to OBO International Building Code standards and NFPA standards 11, 13, 20, 24, 25, 70, 72, and 409. The contractual design period was eight months within a total 24-month period of performance. The CD-3 design submittal was completed in April 2025.
