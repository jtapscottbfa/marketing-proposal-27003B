Civil engineering scope for the vehicle washdown facility at HPDTA covered site survey, existing conditions documentation, site grading and drainage, erosion and sediment control, stormwater management, utility coordination, and site paving design to support the new wash building. The project site was the previous location of Building 5-2, requiring survey and assessment of remaining below-grade conditions including residual piling locations before site civil design could be finalized.

Site design was developed to accommodate access by vehicles up to 80,000 pounds, driving paving thickness and subgrade preparation requirements. Grading and drainage design directed site runoff away from the building and into the stormwater management system, with erosion and sediment controls required under the North Carolina Department of Environmental and Natural Resources (NCDENR) permitting framework. A stormwater pollution prevention plan (SWPPP) was prepared as part of environmental compliance.

Utility scope included coordination of the new wastewater lift station connection to the base wastewater treatment plant, integration with the domestic water supply, and coordination with the electrical discipline for the underground secondary lateral from the new pad-mounted transformer. Civil design was coordinated with the structural foundation extent and with plumbing trench drain and oil-water separator layouts to confirm grades and invert elevations. The civil scope was sequenced as part of the three-phase design process: site survey and existing conditions documentation preceded the design recommendation report, which was then incorporated into the final construction document package.
