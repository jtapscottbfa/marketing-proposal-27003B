Electrical engineering scope for the vehicle washdown facility at HPDTA encompassed a new medium-voltage service, power distribution, lighting, and telecommunications for a high-load industrial wash facility on a DoD installation. The scope was developed to UFC 3-500-01, UFC 3-520-01, UFC 4-211-01, NFPA 70, and applicable IEEE standards.

New electrical service was brought in via an aerial-to-underground medium-voltage transition at a new utility pole, feeding a 750 kVA pad-mounted transformer with an underground secondary lateral to the building. The main distribution panel (MDP) was rated at 800 amperes, 277/480V, 3-phase; a 150 kVA dry-type step-down transformer in the building supplied a 600-ampere 120/208V panel for lighting and receptacle circuits. The 480V distribution system serves mechanical and car wash equipment including AUTEC Car Wash Systems connections with individual disconnect switches, sized for a maximum 2% voltage drop on feeders and 3% on branch circuits.

Lighting in the wash bays used Lithonia D-series wet-location fixtures operating at 120 volts, selected per the HPDTA customer's specification. Exterior wall pack lighting was provided for a 100-foot perimeter coverage zone. A warning and annunciator signal light system was installed on the exterior north face of the building. Telecommunications infrastructure included a 48-strand single-mode fiber optic connection to Building 7-25 to establish base network connectivity at the new facility. All conduit is EMT with compression fittings indoors and intermediate or rigid metallic conduit in exterior and wall applications, with a minimum 3/4-inch trade size throughout.
