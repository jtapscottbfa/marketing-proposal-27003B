Mechanical engineering scope for the HPDTA vehicle washdown facility addressed HVAC design for a wet, chemically active industrial environment. The building houses two vehicle wash bays — one automatic and one manual degreasing bay — and a mechanical equipment room, each with distinct ventilation and climate requirements.

Building ventilation was designed to dilute and exhaust spray chemical fumes generated during wash and degreasing operations, a requirement driven by occupational safety considerations and the nature of detergent and degreaser agents used in the facility. A climate control system was also provided for the mechanical equipment room to protect wash system components from temperature extremes. Electric unit heaters serve space heating needs throughout the facility.

Mechanical design was coordinated with architectural and structural disciplines to resolve roof penetrations and equipment mounting on the light-gauge metal truss roof system. Equipment selection and layout was coordinated with the AUTEC Car Wash Systems car wash equipment vendor to ensure mechanical rough-in matched equipment connection requirements. Design was performed to UFC 3-500-01 electrical and mechanical standards applicable to industrial facility electrical and mechanical systems, with coordination of HVAC electrical connections through the electrical discipline. The mechanical scope at this facility is bounded by ventilation and heating for occupant and equipment protection; process water systems and car wash equipment utilities are captured under plumbing engineering.
