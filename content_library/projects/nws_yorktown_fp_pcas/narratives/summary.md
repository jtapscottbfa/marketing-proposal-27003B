The firm provided Post Construction Award Services (PCAS) for the upgrade of fire suppression and fire alarm systems in Building 476 at Naval Weapons Station Yorktown (WPNSTA Yorktown), Yorktown, Virginia. Building 476 is a single-story, 157,260-sf wood-framed warehouse with CMU siding and a metal roof, divided into eight separate bays and classified as a Moderate-Hazard Factory Industrial Occupancy (Group F-1).

The construction scope — executed by a separate general contractor — included replacement of the wet pipe fire sprinkler system in Bay 8, installation of a new fire pump serving all eight bays via new underground distribution piping, and installation of a new combined fire alarm and mass notification system throughout the building. In Bays 1–7, existing sprinkler systems were retained and integrated with monitoring components tied to the new building-wide fire alarm system. New notification appliances and initiating devices were installed in Bay 8. The occupancy classification of Bay 8 was maintained throughout.

As prime consultant providing PCAS, the multi-discipline team spanned fire protection engineering, electrical engineering, mechanical engineering, plumbing engineering, and structural engineering. The team supported construction submittal review, RFI response, and field observation through the construction phase. The project was executed under NAVFAC e-Projects number 1751889 using a design-bid-build delivery method.
