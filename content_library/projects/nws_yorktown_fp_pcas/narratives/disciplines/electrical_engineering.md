Electrical engineering supported the fire alarm and mass notification system scope on this project at Building 476, Naval Weapons Station Yorktown. The construction work included installation of a new combined fire alarm and mass notification system throughout all eight bays of the 157,260-sf warehouse building, integration of monitoring components for the existing fire alarm zone circuits in Bays 1–7, and installation of new notification appliances and initiating devices in Bay 8.

The combined fire alarm and mass notification system represents a building-wide life safety upgrade. The electrical discipline oversaw submittal review and RFI resolution for devices, panels, wiring, and notification appliance circuits across the entire structure. As a Group F-1 industrial occupancy facility on a DoD installation, the system was required to meet applicable NFPA and DoD/NAVFAC standards for fire alarm and mass notification. Integration with the existing zone circuits in Bays 1–7 — retained rather than replaced under this contract — required verification that the new system correctly incorporated legacy detection infrastructure.

The firm provided electrical engineering in its Post Construction Award Services (PCAS) capacity as prime consultant for NAVFAC. Specific system panel designations, appliance counts, and circuit details are not captured in the project source record.
