Fire protection engineering was the primary discipline on this project. The construction scope addressed fire suppression and life safety upgrades across Building 476 at Naval Weapons Station Yorktown — a single-story, 157,260-sf, eight-bay warehouse classified as a Moderate-Hazard Factory Industrial Occupancy (Group F-1).

The fire protection scope included full replacement of the wet pipe fire sprinkler system in Bay 8, addition of a new fire pump serving all eight bays via new underground distribution piping, and installation of a new combined fire alarm and mass notification system throughout the building. In Bays 1–7, existing sprinkler systems were retained and not connected to the new water supply; new monitoring components were integrated into the existing fire alarm zone circuits. New notification appliances and initiating devices were installed in Bay 8. The occupancy classification of Bay 8 was unchanged by the project.

The firm provided fire protection engineering in its Post Construction Award Services (PCAS) capacity as prime consultant for NAVFAC. The fire protection team supported contractor submittals, RFI responses, and field observation for the life safety scope. A key coordination challenge was managing the interface between the new fire pump water supply infrastructure and the retained legacy sprinkler systems in Bays 1–7, ensuring continuous fire protection coverage during construction and correct system separation between the upgraded and existing suppression zones.
