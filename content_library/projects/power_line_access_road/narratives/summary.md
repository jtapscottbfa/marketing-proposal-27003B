The HPDTA Power Line Access Road Design project involved the A/E design of a new gravel access road and three wetland-crossing bridges at Harvey Point Defense Testing Activity (HPDTA), a Department of Defense facility located in Hertford, North Carolina. The project was commissioned by Boeing Aerospace Operations (BAO), the facility operator, and required civil, structural, and electrical design for approximately 1,190 linear feet of new infrastructure — comprising 657.5 linear feet of 20-inch-thick gravel roadway and 532.5 linear feet of bridge structures crossing wetland areas. The firm served as prime consultant on the project and delivered issued-for-construction design documents, specifications, engineering calculations, and a construction cost estimate.

The design addressed heavy-vehicle access requirements, with roadway and bridge structures designed to support a minimum 91,000-pound vehicle load (AASHTO HL-93 loading standard). Three bridges were designed to traverse wetland areas along the alignment, with spans of 35 feet, 175 feet, and 315 feet and a 24-foot deck width. Alongside the roadway, a concrete-encased PVC ductbank — four 4-inch Schedule 40 conduits running approximately 1,400 linear feet — was designed to provide a dedicated raceway for future fiber cable installation, with NCDOT traffic-rated pull boxes distributed along the corridor.

Design services were delivered under a 180-calendar-day period of performance, with a Final Submittal basis of design completed December 8, 2025. Structural bridges over the wetland areas were incorporated as a selected design option, and Title II construction-phase services were included as an additional option under the contract scope.
