Electrical engineering design for the HPDTA Power Line Access Road project consisted of a concrete-encased PVC ductbank raceway system designed to run the full length of the new access road corridor at Harvey Point Defense Testing Activity (HPDTA) in Hertford, North Carolina. The ductbank provides dedicated conduit infrastructure for future fiber cable installation along the power line access corridor.

The ductbank system comprises four 4-inch Schedule 40 PVC conduits encased in a concrete envelope, installed approximately 18 inches below grade, and extending approximately 1,400 linear feet parallel to the new gravel roadway. Seven NCDOT traffic-rated 2-foot by 2-foot manhole pull boxes were designed at intervals along the ductbank, with pull box spacing established at approximately 400-foot centers per the Final Submittal basis of design. All pull boxes were specified as NCDOT traffic-rated to accommodate installation within the roadway-adjacent zone.

The ductbank alignment follows the new gravel access road from the Towers Boulevard connection point along the full corridor length. Electrical design required coordination with the civil roadway grading to maintain minimum cover depths and to locate pull boxes at grade-compatible positions along the profile. The ductbank is designed for future occupancy by fiber cable; no active electrical power circuits are part of the current design scope. The complete ductbank design was included in the issued-for-construction drawing package delivered with the December 8, 2025 Final Submittal basis of design.
