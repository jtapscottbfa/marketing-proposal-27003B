Civil engineering design for the HPDTA Power Line Access Road encompassed the full site civil scope for approximately 1,190 linear feet of new gravel access road and wetland-crossing infrastructure at Harvey Point Defense Testing Activity (HPDTA) in Hertford, North Carolina. The civil design scope included site grading and drainage, erosion and sediment control, and civil utilities coordination along the corridor.

The designed roadway section comprised 657.5 linear feet of 20-inch-thick gravel roadway, 20 feet wide, connecting to an existing asphalt road apron on Towers Boulevard. The roadway was designed to support a minimum 91,000-pound vehicle load to accommodate heavy equipment access along the power line corridor. The remaining 532.5 linear feet of the alignment traverses wetland areas, addressed through bridge structures designed by the structural discipline.

Civil design also included coordination with National Wetland Permit No. 14 (NWP 14) requirements governing the wetland crossings, and addressed stormwater permit requirements applicable to the project site. The design was developed from an existing 2012-vintage Koth Consulting civil drawing set and 2024 Boeing area-of-work drawings establishing the corridor alignment and existing conditions. The civil alignment connects the new gravel access road to the existing paved road network and terminates consistent with the project corridor scope, with the original roundabout connection shown on the 2012 drawings excluded from the current design scope.
