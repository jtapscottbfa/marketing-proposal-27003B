Structural engineering services at Building 1816, Naval Weapons Station Yorktown, focused on assessment of the existing roof structure and design of reinforcement to accommodate replacement rooftop HVAC and exhaust equipment. Building 1816 was originally constructed in 1976 and its roof structure — a bar joist system typical of that era — required evaluation to confirm adequacy for the new rooftop air handling units (AHU-14, AHU-15, AHU-16) and exhaust fans (EF-8 through EF-21) being installed as part of the mechanical systems replacement.

The structural scope included design of bar joist reinforcement and dunnage frames to support the replacement rooftop equipment at the required locations and load conditions. Roof penetrations for new or resized duct connections associated with AHU-14 and AHU-15 ductwork replacement were also addressed within the structural scope. The Basis of Design references structural drawings for bar joist reinforcement details and dunnage frame configurations; those drawings were not included in the available project intake package, so specific structural member sizes, span conditions, and reinforcement configurations are not captured in the available source record. The sub-discipline scope is structural existing assessment, reflecting the evaluation-first approach to a 1976-era building receiving new mechanical loads.
