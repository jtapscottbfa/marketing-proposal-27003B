Fire protection engineering was included in the A-E design team for Building 1816 Replace and Upgrade Exhaust Systems at Naval Weapons Station Yorktown. The fire alarm sub-discipline scope addressed modifications to the existing fire alarm system required in connection with the replacement of HVAC and exhaust equipment — including three rooftop air handling units and fourteen exhaust fans — and the associated ductwork and mechanical room modifications within the three fuel-handling rooms and affected building areas.

In a facility handling Otto Fuel II (a highly toxic and volatile torpedo propellant), the fire alarm system plays an important role in life safety response. The scope as captured in the available source record is fire alarm modification tied to the mechanical equipment replacement. Specific fire alarm design parameters — including detection device types, zone configurations, panel connections, and interaction with the building's existing fire alarm infrastructure — are not detailed in the Basis of Design or Scope of Work documents available; those details would be documented in the construction drawings and specifications, which were not included in the available project intake package.
