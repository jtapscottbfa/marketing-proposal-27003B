Electrical engineering services were provided in support of the mechanical system replacements at Building 1816, Naval Weapons Station Yorktown, a NAVFAC A-E project to replace and upgrade the HVAC and exhaust infrastructure at the depot-level MK-48 torpedo maintenance facility. The electrical scope addressed power distribution, motor control, and grounding and lightning protection requirements arising from the replacement of three rooftop air handling units (AHU-14, AHU-15, and AHU-16), fourteen exhaust fans (EF-8 through EF-21), a supplemental boiler with circulation pump, and a replacement hot water pump. Electrical design ensured that new and replacement equipment was properly served, controlled, and protected in a facility where uninterrupted exhaust system operation is required for occupant safety in areas handling Otto Fuel II.

The electrical scope was driven by the mechanical equipment replacement inventory rather than by a standalone electrical systems upgrade. Specific electrical design parameters — including service sizes, feeder routing, and distribution modifications — are not fully detailed in the available source record beyond the sub-discipline scope of electrical power distribution, motor control, and grounding and lightning protection. Additional detail on the electrical design basis would be recoverable from the construction drawings, which were not included in the available project intake package.
