Building 1816 Replace and Upgrade Exhaust Systems at Naval Weapons Station Yorktown, Virginia is a NAVFAC Design-Bid-Build A-E project for the depot-level MK-48 torpedo maintenance facility. The 48,373-square-foot building, originally constructed in 1976, required comprehensive replacement of the rooftop exhaust systems serving three rooms where Otto Fuel II — a highly toxic torpedo propellant — is handled during defueling and refueling operations. Negative-pressure ventilation (0.05–0.10 in. w.g.) in those rooms is mandatory to prevent propagation of toxic fumes, making system reliability a critical life-safety and mission requirement.

Scope of A-E design services included replacement of three rooftop air handling units (AHU-14, AHU-15, and AHU-16), fourteen exhaust fans (EF-8 through EF-21), new ductwork for AHU-14 and AHU-15, a supplemental boiler (1,624 MBTU with a 162 GPM circulation pump), hot water pump replacement (from 280 GPM to 500 GPM), DDC controls upgrade, structural modifications to support new rooftop equipment, hazardous material abatement (asbestos-containing HVAC roof curb flashing and duct mastic, lead-based paint, PCB-assumed ballasts, and mercury lamps), and fire alarm modifications. The project was delivered as prime A-E consultant under a NAVFAC IDIQ task order (Contract No. N4008520D0005, e-Project 1697374, Project No. RM 22-0238), with construction procurement via SBA-8(a) MACC set-aside using a low-price technically acceptable (LPTA) methodology.
