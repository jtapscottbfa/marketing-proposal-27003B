# Building 6-28A Title II Services — Project Summary

Building 6-28A, the Dynamometer Building, is a new approximately 10,000 SF steel pre-fabricated facility constructed at Harvey Point Defense Testing Activity (HPDTA), a Department of Defense installation located in Hertford, North Carolina. The project originated from a 2017 design completed by another engineering firm, which subsequently ceased operations before construction could begin — leaving the government without an active engineer of record at the time of construction solicitation.

BFA served as the prime A&E consultant, engaging as the replacement engineer of record for all disciplines. The scope encompassed two integrated phases: first, a thorough review and update of the original design package — incorporating government-directed changes across architectural, structural, civil, mechanical, electrical, life safety, fire safety, IT/communications, and intrusion alarm systems — and delivery of a complete 100% stamped/certified Approved-for-Construction (AFC) design package within 90 calendar days of Notice to Proceed. Second, BFA provided comprehensive Title II construction-phase services throughout the 365-calendar-day period of performance: pre-bid conference support, contractor submittal review and approval, periodic discipline-specific onsite inspections, commissioning oversight, and as-built drawing preparation.

The project's disciplines spanned mechanical and electrical engineering, plumbing, fire protection, structural, civil, architecture, telecommunications, and security/intrusion alarm rough-in. Key design updates included an 800A main distribution panel upgrade, new medium-voltage service routing via concrete-encased duct bank, a DOAS dedicated outdoor air system and packaged terminal HVAC units, intelligent addressable fire alarm system, and 48-strand single-mode fiber optic infrastructure. The project was procured via full-and-open competition by Boeing Aerospace Operations on behalf of the U.S. Government.
