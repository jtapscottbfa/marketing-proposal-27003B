# Telecommunications Engineering — Building 6-28A Title II Services

Telecommunications engineering on the Building 6-28A Dynamometer Building project involved designing a new IT/communications infrastructure for the facility, centered on the newly added communications support outbuilding, with BFA serving as engineer of record.

The primary telecommunications scope was the design of a 48-strand Corning single-mode fiber optic backbone routed from Building 6-23 to the new Building 6-28A communications outbuilding. This routing utilized existing communications duct bank infrastructure and new construction concrete-encased duct bank from the nearest manhole, with coiled slack loops in each manhole and 25-foot service loops at both terminal ends. BFA's design specified LC connectors at Building 6-28A (contractor responsibility) while final termination inside Building 6-23 remained a government responsibility. Db loss testing and a test report were required deliverables.

Within the communications outbuilding, six fiber optic drops were designed to designated wall locations: each drop using ½-inch EMT conduit with 6-strand single-mode fiber, terminated in single-gang boxes with Corning LC connectors and Corning WMO-35 faceplates, homeruns to a plenum box and rack-mounted CCH-03U patch panel using FAN-Bt25-12 frames with CCH-CP24-A9 inserts. Six copper drops were also designed using EMT conduit with four CAT5e conductors per drop, terminated in quad-port faceplates, homeruns to the same plenum box. IT/commo equipment — including a G430 Avaya phone switch, Cisco CBS350-24P switch, 45U rack, and UPS — was incorporated into the design and specification. Conduit rough-in for intrusion alarm drops at each door and six PIR locations was also included in the telecommunications/security scope.
