# Electrical Engineering — Building 6-28A Title II Services

The electrical engineering scope on Building 6-28A involved extensive design updates and re-certification of the 2017 electrical package to reflect a major 6 Area electrical infrastructure upgrade completed in 2022 — after the original design was finished — and to incorporate current HPDTA base standards.

Key updates included: replacement of the original medium-voltage direct-buried cable service with a new concrete-encased embedded duct bank from the existing utility pole, routed through manholes to the building; installation of a new 800A three-phase ABB SACE Tmax XT6 circuit breaker in switchboard MSB-1 at building 6-53 and a new 800A main distribution panel (MDP) in building 6-28A — upgraded from the original panel size; two 45 KVA dry-type 480V/208V step-down transformers; and comprehensive LED lighting upgrades to current base standards (33 Type A LED bay lights at 30,000 lumens each, plus Type B, D, and exit fixtures). Emergency lighting with inverter was added and coordinated per code. Electrical for a Rotary SPO12 12,000-lb electric car lift was incorporated. Receptacles were added in the communications outbuilding, at IT/Commo drop locations, and panel circuits were designated for future equipment.

BFA coordinated the electrical design against the 2022 as-built drawings from the 6 Area Electrical Upgrade project (prepared by Bowman Foster & Associates) to ensure the new service routing, manhole sequence, and existing conduit infrastructure were correctly captured. Manufacturer-certified commissioning of the emergency lighting inverter was specified. BFA provided periodic electrical inspections during construction and commissioning oversight.
