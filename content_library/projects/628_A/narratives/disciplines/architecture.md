# Architecture — Building 6-28A Title II Services

Architectural services on the Building 6-28A Dynamometer Building project focused on reviewing and updating the 2017 architectural design package to reflect government-directed programmatic and configuration changes, with BFA serving as engineer of record across all disciplines.

The architectural updates were among the most numerous in the design update scope. Key changes included: correcting outbuilding and equipment pad locations throughout the drawing set (Dwgs. A110, A210, A310) using an updated government reference floor plan; removing all roof skylights and substituting standard steel roof panels; relocating Vestibule 100 between Building 6-28 columns X2 and X3; adding a new climate-controlled communications support outbuilding between the sprinkler riser room and air compressor room (minimum 5-foot interior width, insulated out-swing door, ductless mini-split HVAC); and removing the originally designed dynamometer pit in favor of a surface-mount dynamometer configuration — a change that simplified the structural program and required revision of all applicable drawings and specifications.

Additional architectural coordination included the revised door location for entry door 110C, the updated interior layout for the communications outbuilding, and coordination with life safety drawing updates to reflect the current building configuration. The communications outbuilding addition was a multi-discipline coordination item, touching architectural, structural, mechanical (mini-split HVAC), electrical (receptacles, IT drops), and IT/communications (fiber and copper cabling) disciplines. All changes were incorporated into a complete 100% AFC architectural drawing package stamped and certified by BFA as engineer of record.
