# Civil Engineering — Building 6-28A Title II Services

Civil engineering on the Building 6-28A Dynamometer Building project involved review of the 2017 civil design package for code currency and coordination with site changes driven by other disciplines, with BFA serving as engineer of record.

The SOW noted that Boeing and the Government did not identify specific civil design changes from their own review; however, BFA was required to verify whether updates were needed to meet current code requirements. Civil scope also intersected with the mechanical and plumbing updates: the replacement and relocation of the existing sanitary lift station — required due to the new asphalt concrete roadway to Building 6-28A — involved civil coordination for the relocated utility routing and grading around the new road construction. The revised pad locations for outbuildings and equipment (incorporated through updated architectural drawings) carried civil implications for site grading and utility connections.

Site utility coordination was also an element of the electrical discipline's concrete-encased duct bank work — connecting from the existing utility pole through manholes to the building — which required civil engineering input on trench routing, encasement specifications, and handhole locations. BFA coordinated across disciplines to ensure the civil design package reflected the actual site infrastructure as updated through the 2022 6 Area Electrical Upgrade. Specific civil contract values and independently billable civil sub-scope details are not captured in the available source documents.
