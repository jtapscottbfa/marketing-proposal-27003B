# Fire Protection Engineering — Building 6-28A Title II Services

Fire protection engineering on the Building 6-28A Dynamometer Building project addressed both the new building's fire suppression and fire alarm systems and coordination with the existing Building 6-28 systems adjacent to the new structure.

For fire alarm, BFA specified the HPDTA base-standard intelligent addressable system: a Honeywell NFS 320 Notifier panel with battery backup, surge suppressor, and control module, equipped with an HS-NCM-SF high-speed single-mode fiber card for integration with the base network. The system included 24 ceiling-mounted horn/strobe units, 14 carbon monoxide detectors, 10 semi-recessed manual pull stations, 2 photoelectric smoke detectors, 2 duct smoke detectors with remote test stations, and one fire sprinkler riser flow switch and tamper switch. A dedicated circuit for the fire alarm panel was incorporated in the electrical design. Manufacturer onsite startup and commissioning was specified.

For fire suppression, BFA's design incorporated a new wet pipe sprinkler system for Building 6-28A. Additionally, the SOW required BFA to review the existing Building 6-28 fire sprinkler system for code compliance and to update the fire alarm drawing package to correct a floorplan that did not match existing building conditions.

Life safety drawing updates were also within scope: Dwg. LS110 was revised to remove an exterior equipment building incorrectly depicted in the 2017 package, using the updated floor plan as the basis. BFA provided periodic onsite fire protection inspection during construction and commissioning oversight for fire alarm and suppression systems.
