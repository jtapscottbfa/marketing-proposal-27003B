# Mechanical Engineering — Building 6-28A Title II Services

BFA's mechanical engineering scope on the Building 6-28A Dynamometer Building project centered on reviewing and updating the 2017 mechanical design package and providing mechanical Title II construction-phase services as engineer of record.

The primary mechanical systems in the updated design included: a Dedicated Outdoor Air System (DOAS-1) for ventilation of the building; multiple packaged terminal HVAC units (AC-1 through AC-6) serving the machine shop, vehicle maintenance area, and ancillary spaces; an electric ceiling unit heater (UH-1) for supplemental heating; a high-pressure exhaust blower rated at 5HP and 3,500 CFM at 500-degree exhaust capability for dynamometer fume extraction, with stainless steel ATEX-rated fume arms; and an Ingersoll Rand 15HP rotary screw air compressor system for compressed air supply.

Key mechanical design updates included incorporating a new structural concrete pad for the DOAS-1 and VEX-1 equipment, revising sanitary waste piping to account for a required replacement and relocation of the existing lift station serving buildings in the 6-area, and coordinating mechanical equipment selection changes from the original 2017 specifications. The final equipment selections, documented in BFA's March 2023 Pre-Final Mechanical Equipment Change Log (BFA Project #22-071), reflect equipment substitutions made during design development to address market availability and current base standards. Manufacturer-certified startup and commissioning was specified for all major mechanical equipment. BFA provided periodic onsite mechanical inspection during construction and participated in building commissioning oversight.
