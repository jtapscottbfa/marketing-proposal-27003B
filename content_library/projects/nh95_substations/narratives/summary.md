The design team served as prime A-E consultant for a facility-wide electrical infrastructure replacement program at Naval Support Activity Hampton Roads, Norfolk, Virginia — replacing all 12 unit substations (A through L) within Building NH-95 under NAVFAC Mid-Atlantic IDIQ Contract N40085-20-D-0005. The program addressed a critical end-of-life condition: the 1950s-era building's oil-filled transformers were leaking and original switchgear breakers could no longer be sourced for replacement. Work was executed across two sequential task orders — substations A, B, K, and L (estimated construction cost $6,475,000; construction completion estimated September 2022) and substations C/D, E/F, G/H, and I/J (estimated construction cost $32,331,000; construction completion estimated May 2024).

Design scope spanned all disciplines required to replace the substations under a Design-Bid-Build delivery: demolition of existing switchgear and oil-filled transformers; new exterior pad-mounted transformers (1,000 kVA and 2,500 kVA) at each substation; new service-entrance-rated double-ended switchboards with automatic transfer switches (Square D QED-2/QED-6 construction, MasterPact MTZ circuit breakers); primary and secondary ductbank and manhole modifications; architectural vault expansions; helical pile foundations for new additions; mechanical vault ventilation; fire protection modifications; civil site improvements; erosion and sediment control; arc flash and coordination studies; and circuit trace drawings for all 12 substations. The multi-discipline team also conducted a hazardous materials survey and prepared abatement specifications covering lead paint, asbestos-containing materials, and transformer oil PCB assessment, as well as a geotechnical investigation that characterized subsurface conditions and recommended helical pile foundations for the vault additions.
