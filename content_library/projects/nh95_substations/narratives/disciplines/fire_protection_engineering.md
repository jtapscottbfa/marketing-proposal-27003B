Fire protection engineering on the NH-95 Substations Replacement program at Naval Support Activity Hampton Roads, Norfolk, Virginia, addressed modifications to existing fire suppression and fire alarm systems necessitated by the vault expansion additions and replacement of electrical equipment within Building NH-95.

The existing fire suppression infrastructure serving the electrical vaults included both wet sprinkler and dry/pre-action systems — the building system taxonomy for this project records `fp_wet_sprinkler` and `fp_dry_pre_action` as in scope, consistent with typical protection configurations for electrical equipment rooms where water-sensitive equipment may preclude wet systems in portions of the space. The fire alarm system was also within scope, requiring modifications to detection and notification components in the affected vault areas.

The vault additions for substations A/B (approximately 42 ft × 19 ft) and substations C/D and F (approximately 37 ft × 11 ft) required extension or modification of existing suppression mains, new heads or nozzles within the addition footprints, and integration with the existing fire alarm network. William Wisilosky served as the Fire Protection Lead for the project team. Specific suppression system types, design densities, hazard classifications, and alarm device schedules are in the construction drawings and specifications, which are not part of the available source record for this project; the sub-discipline taxonomy records `fire_suppression_wet` as within scope.
