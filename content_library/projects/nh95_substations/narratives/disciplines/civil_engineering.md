Civil engineering scope on the NH-95 Substations Replacement program at Naval Support Activity Hampton Roads, Norfolk, Virginia, addressed exterior site work and below-grade utility modifications required to support the transition from internal oil-filled unit substations to exterior pad-mounted transformers at all 12 substations.

The primary civil work involved modification of existing primary and secondary ductbanks and manholes serving Building NH-95. Moving the new transformers to exterior pad locations required re-routing medium-voltage primary ductbank runs from the existing pad locations to the new exterior transformer pads, as well as modifying secondary ductbanks to carry power back into the building at each substation location. Civil site design also covered new exterior improvements associated with the transformer pad placements — grading, drainage, and hardscape around each new transformer pad location.

Erosion and sediment control measures were designed for the construction disturbance areas associated with ductbank excavation, transformer pad construction, and vault addition foundations. The project site is located on the north-central eastern portion of Naval Support Activity, Norfolk, on the west side of Building NH-95.
