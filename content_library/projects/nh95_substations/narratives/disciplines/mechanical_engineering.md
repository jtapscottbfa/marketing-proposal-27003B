Mechanical engineering supported the NH-95 Substations Replacement program at Naval Support Activity Hampton Roads, Norfolk, Virginia, with scope focused on the environmental conditioning of new and expanded electrical equipment vaults. The vault expansions required to accommodate new double-ended switchboards — approximately 42 ft × 19 ft for substations A and B, and approximately 37 ft × 11 ft for substations C/D and F — necessitated dedicated mechanical ventilation design for each addition.

The mechanical scope included HVAC air distribution system design for the new vault spaces to maintain the thermal environment required by the new switchboard and transformer equipment. Electrical equipment rooms housing large switchboards and associated components require controlled ventilation to manage heat dissipation and maintain equipment within manufacturer-specified operating temperature ranges. The DOR Project Manager on this project held a dual role as Mechanical Lead, with a dedicated Mechanical engineer also on the team.
