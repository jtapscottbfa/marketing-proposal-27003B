Architecture on the NH-95 Substations Replacement program at Naval Support Activity Hampton Roads, Norfolk, Virginia, addressed the building additions required to house the new double-ended switchboard equipment that replaced the existing unit substations within Building NH-95. The new switchboards — larger than the original equipment — could not fit within the existing vault footprints, necessitating building additions at multiple substation locations.

For substations A and B, the vault addition measured approximately 42 ft × 19 ft with a ceiling height of 14 ft-6 in., providing the clearances required for the new 3,000A double-ended switchboards. For substations C/D and F, the vault addition measured approximately 37 ft × 11 ft, with new exterior walls and floor finish designed to match existing construction. The architectural design established the envelope, openings, and interior finishes for each addition, integrating them with the existing 1950s-era building fabric to meet NAVFAC design standards and functional requirements for electrical equipment rooms.

The vault additions required close coordination with the structural discipline for the helical pile foundation design and with the mechanical and fire protection disciplines for penetrations and system routing through new exterior walls. Floor finish selections and wall construction at each vault addition were designed to match existing adjacent construction as documented in the SAES and Basis of Design. Specific exterior wall construction type, roofing assembly, door hardware, and finish schedule details are in the construction drawings and specifications, which are not part of the available source record for this project.
