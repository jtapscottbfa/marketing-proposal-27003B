## Electrical Engineering — Raw Water No. 2 Treatment Plant Electrical Coordination Study

Electrical engineering was the sole discipline on this engagement. The scope comprised a complete electrical coordination study for the pump distribution system at the Raw Water No. 2 Treatment Plant in Gloucester, Virginia, performed in accordance with ANSI/NEMA MTS-2019.

The electrical engineering work included a field survey of the installed pump electrical distribution equipment — three pole-mounted 50 kVA transformers feeding a 480 V automatic transfer switch (ATS) with a 200 A main circuit breaker, and downstream 50 A and 100 A branch breakers protecting Pumps No. 2, 3, and 4 respectively. A system model was developed in SKM Power Tools to support a short-circuit analysis and time-current curve (TCC) coordination study of the overcurrent protective devices.

The TCC analysis established that selective coordination between the 200 A MCB and the pump branch circuit breakers is not achievable with the installed equipment, and that compromised coordination as defined by ANSI/NEMA MTS-2019 is the appropriate performance standard. An Equipment Evaluation confirmed that all installed devices remain within rated fault-current capacity. Recommendations were developed for an immediate settings-based optimization — repositioning the MCB instantaneous trip to its maximum setting — and a longer-term equipment upgrade to a MCB with adjustable or static trip characteristics to achieve a near-coordinated distribution system.
