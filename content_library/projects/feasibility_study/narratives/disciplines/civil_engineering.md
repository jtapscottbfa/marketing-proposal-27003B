Civil engineering addressed site design and utility infrastructure for the Melwood Recreation Center feasibility study, with the project's rural setting in Nanjemoy, Maryland driving the primary technical challenges. The campus at 9035 Ironsides Rd is a wooded rural property without access to public water supply or municipal sewer, requiring entirely on-site civil utility solutions for all three proposed new buildings.

Site placement analysis identified two primary building zones: the Multipurpose Activity Building and Classroom Building were located in an open field near the Caretaker's House in the south-central portion of the campus, while the Medical Cabin was sited within the existing cabin area to the northwest. An annotated aerial site map was produced during the feasibility study, identifying these zones in relation to existing campus structures, the adjacent Potomac River tidal watershed, and neighboring parcels.

Civil utility scope encompasses new on-site septic systems for the new buildings, supporting the plumbing engineering team's infrastructure planning. Sewage easement plats were produced during the feasibility study, and proposed septic locations for the new buildings were documented in civil drawings. Existing MRC campus septic systems were mapped as part of the site infrastructure assessment. Site grading and drainage design will be required to support building placement in the open field zone and to manage stormwater in a rural watershed context.

No civil construction documents or grading plans were produced at the feasibility study stage. Civil site design and civil utilities sub-disciplines are both reflected in the project taxonomy and in the ROM cost estimate. Specific civil design details — grading extents, drainage infrastructure, utility routing, and septic system sizing — are to be developed in subsequent design phases.
