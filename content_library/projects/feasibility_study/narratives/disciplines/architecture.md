Architecture led the concept design for the Melwood Recreation Center new construction program, producing Concept C floor plans for two of three proposed buildings on the rural campus in Nanjemoy, Maryland. The Multipurpose Activity Building (approximately 11,810 SF, single-story) was organized around a full basketball court with two half-court configurations, a stage with prop and costume storage, an indoor rock wall, and accessible restrooms with lobby entry. The Classroom Building (approximately 4,492 SF, single-story) was designed to house a Music Studio, Arts and Crafts Studio, Photography Studio, Pottery Studio with kiln storage, and associated support spaces. Both buildings were developed as single-story structures with IBC-compliant occupancy configurations appropriate for a population that includes individuals with disabilities.

The architectural scope encompassed program analysis, concept layout development through multiple options culminating in Concept C, and coordination of building footprint and site placement with civil and engineering disciplines. Site placement balanced client program requirements with available open land: the Multipurpose Building was located in an open field near the Caretaker's House, while the Medical Cabin was sited within the existing cabin area. No architectural floor plan for the Medical Cabin was available at the feasibility study stage; that building's program (two-story, Level 1 treatment area with sick bays and medication administration) was captured through the fire protection engineering analysis.

The concept drawings were produced in collaboration with a co-architect and reflect the evolved three-building scope that emerged during the feasibility study period, resolving an earlier two-building program concept from the original RFP.
