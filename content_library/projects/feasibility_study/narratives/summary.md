The Melwood Recreation Center Feasibility Study engaged the design team as prime consultant to evaluate the new construction of three buildings at a rural camp and recreation campus in Nanjemoy, Maryland: a Multipurpose Activity Building (approximately 11,810 SF), a Classroom Building (approximately 4,492 SF), and a two-story Medical Cabin. The campus, operated by Melwood Horticultural Training Center, Inc., serves individuals with intellectual and developmental disabilities through a therapeutic recreation program operated in partnership with a children's hospital. Total program area is approximately 22,661 SF with a Rough Order of Magnitude (ROM) estimated construction cost of approximately $13.5 million and a total project cost of approximately $15 million.

The feasibility study addressed a technically demanding rural site: the campus has no access to public water or municipal sewer, requiring new private well infrastructure, on-site septic systems for each building, and a dedicated 70,000-gallon water storage tank with a 500-gpm fire pump to support code-compliant fire suppression across all three buildings. The scope encompassed architectural programming and concept design (Concept C floor plans for two buildings), as well as discipline-level feasibility planning for mechanical, electrical, plumbing, fire protection, and civil systems. The Medical Cabin's dual-occupancy nature — combining sleeping accommodations with a medication administration function — drove specialized fire and life safety analysis, including occupancy classification determinations and emergency system requirements that informed the ROM estimate. The six-month feasibility study contract was structured as a design-bid-build standalone engagement.
