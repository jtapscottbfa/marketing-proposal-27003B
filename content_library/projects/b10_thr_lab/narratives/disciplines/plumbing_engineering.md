# Plumbing Engineering — Building 10 Room 9N104 Renovation

BFA's plumbing engineering scope covered domestic water, drainage, and laboratory gas utility systems for the 211 SF NHLBI Cellular and Molecular Therapeutics laboratory at NIH Building 10.

Existing eyewash, domestic water piping, and gas piping were demolished back to the building mains as part of the demolition scope.

New plumbing work included:

**Sink and eyewash:** An 18×15" Durcon epoxy resin sink was installed, connected to domestic hot and cold water. A WaterSaver EW deck-mounted AutoFlow counter-mounted eyewash (EW swivel, GS-Plus spray heads, in-line strainer, ANSI Z358.1 compliant) was mounted to the sink deck. A WaterSaver FAP3600 thermostatic tempering valve (ASSE 1071 certified, factory preset to 85°F, fail-safe cold bypass on hot water loss) was installed to supply tepid water to the eyewash per ANSI Z358.1-2014.

**Faucets:** Chicago Faucets 1100-GN8BVBE2-2XKC deck-mounted manual sink faucets with 8" fixed centers, gooseneck spouts with atmospheric vacuum breakers, vandal-proof single-wing handles, and ceramic quarter-turn cartridges with integrated check valves were specified. Foot-pedal actuators were included per the NHLBI program requirements.

**Laboratory gas:** Two sets of bench-mounted Chicago Faucets 980-WS909AGVCP single ball valve turrets with laboratory nozzles were installed for compressed air and vacuum supply at the casework, connected to building lab gas distribution.

**Drainage:** New waste and vent piping was connected to the existing building sanitary system. Waste and vent riser diagrams were included in the final submittal drawings (sheet P-101).

All work was designed in compliance with NIH DRM, IPC 2018, NFPA 45 (fire protection for laboratories using chemicals), NFPA 99 (healthcare facilities — medical gas), and ANSI Z358.1 (emergency eyewash/shower).
