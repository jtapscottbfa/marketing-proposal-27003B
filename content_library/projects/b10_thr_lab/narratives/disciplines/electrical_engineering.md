# Electrical Engineering — Building 10 Room 9N104 Renovation

BFA's electrical engineering scope covered lighting, power, and telecom/data systems for the 211 SF laboratory renovation at NIH Building 10, Room 9N104.

The existing lighting, receptacles, and data outlets were demolished. New work included:

**Lighting:** Six Lithonia Lighting FML4W 48" LED wrap fixtures (4000K, 5000 lm, 50W, 0–10V dimmable) were installed as pendant-mounted overhead fixtures, and nine Snite LED 18" units (4000K, 1,131 lm, 13W) were installed as under-shelf task lighting on the casework shelving. Lighting photometric calculations (DIALux, dated 03/03/2021) confirmed an average of 87.7 footcandles across the room with task lighting providing up to 185 FC at bench level — meeting NIH DRM and UFC 3-530-01 standards for laboratory occupancy.

**Power:** 120V EMT wiring was run from existing panels 1264A36 and 1264A38 (GE A-Series, 208/120V, 3Ø/4W, 225A max) located in the 9th-floor corridor. Wiremold 3000 Series steel raceway was installed along the lab benches, providing quad 120V outlets at knee-space locations, an outlet below the window sill countertop, and a grommet hole for undercounter outlet access. Voltage drop calculations confirmed 0.64% (circuit from 1264A36) and 0.15% (circuit from 1264A38) — within the 2%/3%/5% design criteria. All conductors are copper THHN/THWN in EMT with compression fittings; minimum 12 AWG for power circuits.

**Telecom/Data:** Conduit and data outlet rough-in was provided at each of the three workstations (minimum one outlet per workstation) and one outlet below the window sill counter for a shared printer.

All work was designed in compliance with NIH DRM and FDM, NFPA 70 (NEC 2020), UFC 3-500-10N, UFC 3-520-01, UFC 3-530-01, and ANSI/ASHRAE/IES 90.1. Fire alarm coordination included Wheelock E-Series speaker-strobe units referenced in the cut-sheet package. Pre-construction electric metering of panels was required per the SOW.
