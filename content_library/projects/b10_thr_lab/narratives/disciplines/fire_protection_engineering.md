# Fire Protection Engineering — Building 10 Room 9N104 Renovation

BFA's fire protection engineering scope for the Room 9N104 renovation was limited in character: the project was a small interior renovation within Building 10's existing sprinkler and fire alarm infrastructure, and the design intent was to tie into and coordinate with those existing systems rather than install independent fire protection systems.

Per the BOD Executive Summary, the existing building fire sprinkler piping remained in place: "Existing sprinkler piping shall remain." Architectural and construction notes on drawings confirm the project coordinated with the existing wet-pipe sprinkler system (NFPA 13) for any adjustments required by the new ceiling configuration. The open (exposed) ceiling and new casework layout were reviewed to confirm compliance with sprinkler coverage requirements.

The NIH Division of the Fire Marshal (DFM) served as the Authority Having Jurisdiction on the NIH Bethesda Campus for all fire protection and life safety matters. DFM approval of the design and construction documents was required prior to the issuance of the construction permit and prior to beneficial occupancy. Fire Marshal approval was obtained — evidenced by the DFM signature on the drawing G-001 cover sheet (dated 4/19/21).

The life safety plan (drawing LS101) was included in the final submittal package, confirming egress travel distances (IBC Table 1017.2, Group B, 300 ft maximum) and fire-rated construction boundaries on the 9th floor. The new 1-hour fire-rated door (door 104 per the door schedule) with limited vision panel (≤100 sq in) was a core life safety element delivered under the architectural scope and confirmed compliant with NIH Fire Marshal requirements.

Governing codes included NFPA 1, 13, 72, and 101, as listed on drawing G-001. Fire alarm devices (Wheelock E-Series speaker-strobes referenced in electrical cut-sheets) are consistent with coordination into the building's existing fire alarm system. No standalone fire alarm panel or suppression system was installed as part of this scope.
