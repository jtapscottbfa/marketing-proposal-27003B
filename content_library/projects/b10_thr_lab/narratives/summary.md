# Building 10 – Room 9N104 – Cellular and Molecular Therapeutics Laboratory Renovation

**Client:** National Institutes of Health (NIH), National Heart, Lung, and Blood Institute (NHLBI)
**Location:** Building 10, 9th Floor, B-Wing, NIH Bethesda Campus, Bethesda, MD
**Project No. (BFA):** 20-090 | **Work Request:** C109043
**Delivery Method:** Design-Build | **Status:** Complete

Bowman, Foster & Associates (BFA) served as prime design-builder for the renovation of Room 9N104, a 211-square-foot single-module laboratory on the 9th floor of NIH's Building 10 Clinical Center Complex in Bethesda, Maryland. The room had been partially demolished years earlier under a prior project, then left vacant; BFA's scope was to bring it to full functionality for the National Heart, Lung, and Blood Institute's Cellular and Molecular Therapeutics Branch (CMTB) — a team led by Dr. John F. Tisdale conducting gene therapy research aimed at curing sickle cell disease.

The project involved complete interior renovation within an active, occupied hospital wing. Scope included architectural fit-out (new 1-hour fire-rated door, new window and treatment, vinyl composition tile flooring, black phenolic resin casework, adjustable-height workstations for three users), mechanical HVAC (replacement induction unit with new DDC controls tied to the building BAS, lab compressed air and vacuum dispense turrets), plumbing (18×15" epoxy lab sink with counter-mounted eyewash, foot-pedal faucets, tempering valve), and electrical (LED pendant and under-shelf task lighting, 120V receptacle raceways, data/telecom conduit and outlets). Construction was coordinated within the rigorous regulatory environment of NIH Building 10, including compliance with The Joint Commission (TJC) Environment of Care standards, NIH Division of the Fire Marshal (AHJ), Construction Risk Assessment (CRA), and the Building 10 Contractor Single Point of Entry Program.

The final design submittal package was completed April 7, 2021. The estimated period of performance was 276 calendar days.
