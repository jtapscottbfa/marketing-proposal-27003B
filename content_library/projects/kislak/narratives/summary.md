The Voices of the Early Americas — The Jay I. Kislak Collection is a permanent museum gallery installation at the Library of Congress, 101 Independence Avenue SE, Washington, DC, procured under federal solicitation 030ADV23Q0282. The exhibit presents the Jay I. Kislak Collection through a gallery comprising two interconnected spaces: a circular Rotunda Gallery with concentric exhibit cases and display totems, and a linear exhibition hall accommodating multiple casework zones and display walls. The design team was led by Ralph Appelbaum Associates (RAA) as the prime exhibit designer; the firm served as electrical engineering subconsultant responsible for lighting power systems, dimming controls, and display case electrical coordination.

The electrical scope encompasses an all-LED lighting system with fourteen fixture types (F2–F14) addressing ambient, directional, casework-integrated, and graphic display illumination, predominantly at 3000 K color temperature. Low-voltage 24 VDC track and display systems operate alongside 120 V line-voltage adjustable monopoints, with phase-dimming and 0-10 V controls throughout. Scope extended through the construction and installation phase: the firm assembled and issued a submittal package documenting internal lighting control components for Goppion-manufactured display cases — including PWM LED dimmers, constant-current drivers, time relays, and selector switches from European specialty manufacturers — confirming compliance with installation requirements. Design documents reached Final Design Revision 1 status in July 2023; construction-phase engagement is confirmed through at least March 2025.
