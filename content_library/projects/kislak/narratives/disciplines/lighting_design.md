The lighting design scope for the Voices of the Early Americas — Jay I. Kislak Collection exhibit at the Library of Congress produced a comprehensive all-LED fixture system for a gallery comprising a circular Rotunda Gallery and a linear exhibition hall. The Lighting Workshop (Brooklyn, NY) served as the lighting design subconsultant, producing the lighting floor plan (sheet L101, Final Design Revision 1, July 14, 2023) and the complete lighting fixture schedule. The firm provided the electrical engineering for the lighting system's power feeds, driver selection, and dimming controls.

The system encompasses fourteen fixture types (F2–F14), all LED and predominantly 3000 K for artifact and display viewing. Specific fixtures address graphic reproduction requirements at different color temperatures: F10 (window graphic lightboxes) at 6000 K and F14 (edge-lit reader panels) at 6500 K. The fixture types cover the full range of gallery illumination conditions: F2 (flexible LED tape in coves), F3 (adjustable monopoints in the Rotunda Gallery), F5 (linear grazers at display totems), F6H, F6W, F6T, F7, F7A, F7B, F11, and F12 (casework-mounted track and display fixtures), F8 and F13 (linear wall-mounted fixtures), F9 (inscribed feature wall uplights), and F10 and F14 (graphic display elements). Manufacturers represented in the schedule include LUXAM, Q-TRAN, BK Lighting, BEULUX, SEG Systems, and EVOLITE. The lighting design scope for this project was performed by the Lighting Workshop; the scope details above are drawn from the fixture schedule and lighting floor plan produced by that subconsultant.
