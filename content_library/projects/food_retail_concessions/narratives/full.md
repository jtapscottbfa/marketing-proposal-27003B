## Project Overview

The NEXCOM Food & Retail Concessions project is a new single-story, approximately 5,500 net square foot food and retail concessions shell building at Naval Station Norfolk, Norfolk, Virginia. Commissioned under the Navy's Military Construction (MILCON) program as FY2020 Project N753417, the facility provides two food vendor spaces — one approximately 2,000 SF and a second approximately 3,500 SF — housed within a single 6,080 gross square foot structural envelope. The project serves Navy personnel and the Naval Station community through the Navy Exchange Service Command (NEXCOM), the user activity that operates exchange and concessions facilities across Navy installations. NAVFAC Mid-Atlantic (NAVFAC MIDLANT) administered the task order under existing A-E IDIQ contract N62470-16-D-6005 with an authorized program amount of $5,300,000 and an Estimated Construction Cost of $3,875,000.

The building is sited at the northeast corner of CV Towway Drive and Fourth Avenue on Naval Station Norfolk, positioned to serve base personnel with accessible food service. A drive-thru facility serving the vendor spaces was included in the site design.

## Scope

The design was structured as a white-box shell building: the design team delivered the building envelope, structural system, civil site work, and infrastructure rough-ins, while intentionally deferring interior tenant systems to individual vendor upfit contracts. HVAC, interior finishes beyond minimum conditions, kitchen hood fire suppression, and fire alarm/mass notification were excluded from the base building scope, allowing NEXCOM to bring in different food service operators with varying equipment and layout requirements without modifying the base building.

Design scope encompassed:

- **Architecture:** Single-story structural steel frame building with metal wall panels on metal stud framing, glass mat gypsum board sheathing, PVC membrane roofing, and curtain wall glazing. Shell interior includes two demised vendor spaces separated by stud wall partitions supported on a pile-supported concrete structural slab at the center bay, plus an electrical room.

- **Structural Engineering:** Deep foundation system using timber piles; concrete structural slab on piles at the center bay; structural steel superstructure.

- **Civil Engineering:** Full site design including grading and drainage, pavement, curb and gutter, drive-thru configuration, sidewalks, utility connections (water, sanitary, gas, communications, electrical primary), stormwater best management practices, and erosion and sediment control.

- **Mechanical Engineering:** No base-scope HVAC. Vendor spaces were delivered as white box with no mechanical systems — all HVAC designated for installation by food service operators during tenant upfit.

- **Electrical Engineering:** 500 kVA, 34.5kV-to-208Y/120V pad-mounted feed-through transformer; 35kV primary underground distribution fed from existing switch SW-296; separately metered services for house loads (100A), Vendor Space #1 (400A), Vendor Space #2 (800A), and traffic signalization (20A); Class I lightning protection per UFC 3-575-01; LED exterior wall packs and pole-mounted area lights; temporary interior LED lighting in vendor spaces.

- **Plumbing Engineering:** 1-inch domestic cold water stub-in per vendor space; 4-inch sanitary waste stub-in per vendor space; natural gas service and individual meters for each side of the building. Hot water generation deferred to vendor upfit.

- **Fire Protection Engineering:** No sprinkler system (standalone exchange building under 8,000 SF per UFC 3-600-01 §4-8.2); portable fire extinguishers per NFPA 101; fire alarm/mass notification and kitchen hood suppression deferred to vendor upfit.

- **Environmental Engineering:** Passive air vapor intrusion mitigation system (VIMS) beneath the entire building slab; contaminated groundwater dewatering treatment for construction-phase excavation within the CERCLA Site 20 LUC boundary.

- **Geotechnical Engineering:** Subsurface investigation and foundation recommendations for the deep timber pile foundation system in a contaminated groundwater environment.

- **Landscape Architecture:** Site landscape design for grounds surrounding the building and drive-thru facility.

- **Telecommunications Engineering:** Structured cabling infrastructure provisions and communications stub-outs for vendor spaces.

Applicable codes included IBC 2018, NFPA 101 (2021), NFPA 70 (2020), NFPA 72 (2019), UFC 1-200-01, UFC 3-600-01, UFC 4-010-01, FC 1-300-09N, ASHRAE 90.1-2019, and ABA Accessibility Standards for DoD Facilities.

## Role

The design team served as prime consultant with full multi-discipline A-E responsibility, providing services under the NAVFAC MIDLANT IDIQ contract as a task order scope. All eleven design disciplines were carried under a single prime engagement. Design was completed through 100% design submittal in August 2022, following a design period that began with the A-E Statement of Services (SAES) issued in FY2020.

## Key Challenges and Resolutions

**CERCLA Site 20 environmental constraint.** The project site lies within the Land Use Control boundary of CERCLA Site 20 at Naval Station Norfolk — a contaminated groundwater area with confirmed PFAS from historical aqueous film-forming foam (AFFF) use associated with adjacent hangar and airfield operations. This required designing a passive vapor intrusion mitigation system (VIMS) beneath the entire building slab, incorporating a spray-applied vapor barrier, AASHTO #57 stone sub-slab aggregate layer, perforated sub-slab collection piping, plenum boxes, and vertical vent risers extending above the roof. Construction-phase excavation and dewatering required groundwater treatment for PFAS-contaminated discharge. This environmental layer added significant design complexity and construction cost to what would otherwise be a straightforward small retail shell building.

**White-box shell building scope coordination.** Delivering the building as a white-box shell required clear and disciplined scope delineation across all disciplines. Each discipline defined what was and was not within the base building contract, and ensured that stub-outs and rough-ins were sized to accommodate the full range of anticipated vendor equipment loads. The separately metered electrical services — 400A for Vendor #1 and 800A for Vendor #2, reflecting the anticipated commercial kitchen demands — and the per-space plumbing stub-ins are products of this coordination.

**Soft-soil foundation conditions.** Naval Station Norfolk's low-lying waterfront peninsula site has soft compressible soils requiring a deep foundation system. Timber piles were selected, with a concrete structural slab pile-supported at the center bay to carry the demising wall and partition loads between vendor spaces and the electrical room.

**Program scope evolution.** The A-E Statement of Services (issued February 2020) described a 6,500 NSF program including a 1,500 SF pop-up retail space in addition to two primary vendor spaces. During design development, the pop-up retail component was eliminated and the second vendor space was enlarged from 3,000 SF to 3,500 SF, resulting in the 5,500 NSF final design reflected in the 100% Design Submittal. The design team adapted to this scope refinement over the course of the design period.

## Outcomes

Design was completed at the 100% submittal level in August 2022, delivering a full set of construction documents for a new single-story food and retail concessions shell building on Naval Station Norfolk. The project achieved GBI DoD Guiding Principles Compliance for new construction. The building supports NEXCOM's provision of food service and retail amenities to Naval Station personnel. Anti-terrorism/force protection requirements were satisfied under UFC 4-010-01 at the Very Low protection level (Category I), consistent with the facility's type and location. Construction contract award date and construction completion data are not captured in the available project record.
