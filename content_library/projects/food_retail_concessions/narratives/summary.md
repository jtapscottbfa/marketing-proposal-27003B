The NEXCOM Food & Retail Concessions project is a new single-story food and retail concessions facility at Naval Station Norfolk, Norfolk, Virginia. Designed as a white-box shell building, the facility provides two independent vendor spaces — approximately 2,000 SF and 3,500 SF — totaling approximately 5,500 net square feet (6,080 gross square feet) within a single structural envelope. The project was executed as a design-bid-build Military Construction (MILCON) task order under NAVFAC Mid-Atlantic contract N62470-16-D-6005, with NEXCOM as the end-user activity, for a total program amount of $5,300,000 (Estimated Construction Cost: $3,875,000).

The building required a deep timber pile foundation system driven by the soft compressible soils typical of the Naval Station's tidewater site. A critical environmental constraint shaped the entire building design: the site lies within the Land Use Control boundary of CERCLA Site 20, where confirmed PFAS-contaminated groundwater required a passive vapor intrusion mitigation system (VIMS) beneath the full building slab, along with dewatering treatment for construction excavation. The design team delivered the complete building infrastructure — including separately metered electrical services for each vendor space, utility stub-outs sized for commercial kitchen operations, a drive-thru facility, and GBI DoD Guiding Principles sustainable design compliance — while managing intentional scope exclusions for HVAC, interior finishes, kitchen hood fire suppression, and fire alarm, which were deferred to individual vendor upfit contracts. Design was completed at the 100% submittal level in August 2022. The design team served as prime consultant, providing full multi-discipline A-E services including architecture, structural, civil, mechanical, electrical, plumbing, fire protection, environmental, geotechnical, landscape architecture, and telecommunications disciplines.
