The civil engineering scope for the NEXCOM Food & Retail Concessions project encompassed full site design for a new food and retail concessions shell building at Naval Station Norfolk, Norfolk, Virginia, situated at the northeast corner of CV Towway Drive and Fourth Avenue.

Site work included grading and drainage design, pavement design for vehicular access and the drive-thru circulation route, curb and gutter installation, and sidewalk construction. Utility coordination provided stub-up connections for domestic water, sanitary sewer, natural gas, communications, and 35kV electrical primary service to the building pad. Stormwater management incorporated best management practices (BMPs) appropriate for the site, along with erosion and sediment control measures for the construction phase.

The drive-thru facility was integrated into the civil site design, coordinating vehicular circulation flow with pedestrian access routes and utility routing constraints. Traffic signalization tie-in for the drive-thru exit was included as part of the civil scope. Civil design was closely coordinated with the environmental engineering discipline given the project site's location within the CERCLA Site 20 Land Use Control boundary with confirmed PFAS-contaminated groundwater — particularly for excavation, utility trenching, and dewatering activities associated with site utility installations. Design was governed by applicable UFC, NAVFAC, and Naval Station Norfolk installation requirements.
