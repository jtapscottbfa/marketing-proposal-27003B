The mechanical engineering scope for the NEXCOM Food & Retail Concessions facility was intentionally limited by the project's white-box shell building delivery model. Heating, ventilation, and air conditioning (HVAC) systems were explicitly excluded from the base building design scope — both vendor spaces were delivered as unfinished shells with no mechanical systems, with all HVAC designated for installation by individual food service operators during their respective tenant upfit contracts. This approach accommodates the significantly varied equipment loads, kitchen ventilation requirements, and exhaust configurations that different food service operators bring to their spaces.

No base-building HVAC equipment, ductwork, controls, or mechanical infrastructure is included in the construction documents for this project. Temporary minimal lighting was the sole installation in the vendor spaces provided under base building scope. The absence of base-scope mechanical systems is explicitly documented in the project record as a design intent — not a scope deficiency.

Mechanical engineering discipline participation on this project was focused on confirming and documenting the white-box scope delineation, verifying that the building shell design accommodates future tenant HVAC penetrations and rooftop or wall-mounted equipment installations, and confirming compliance with applicable UFC and building code requirements for the shell building configuration. Specific mechanical system design details beyond this coordination role are not captured in the available source record for this project.
