The plumbing engineering scope for the NEXCOM Food & Retail Concessions facility delivered the utility infrastructure rough-ins necessary to support future tenant fit-out by individual food service operators at each vendor space. Consistent with the white-box shell delivery model, plumbing systems were provided as stub-ins at each vendor space boundary rather than as full interior plumbing installations.

Each vendor space received a 1-inch domestic cold water stub-in and a 4-inch sanitary waste stub-in, sized to accommodate typical commercial kitchen demand while leaving interior distribution, fixture connections, and grease interceptor provisions to vendor upfit contracts. Natural gas service was designed to each side of the building, with individual gas meters provided per vendor space to allow independent metering and billing of gas consumption. Storm drainage was addressed within the building and site scope.

Hot water generation was explicitly excluded from the base building scope and designated as a vendor upfit responsibility — each food service operator would install water heating equipment appropriate to their menu and operational requirements. Similarly, interior gas distribution beyond the meter, kitchen fixture rough-ins, and grease interceptor installations are tenant scope. The plumbing stub-in sizing decisions reflect coordination with the electrical engineering discipline regarding the anticipated operational demands of commercial kitchen tenants. Design was governed by applicable UFC criteria, IBC 2018 plumbing provisions, and Naval Station Norfolk utility standards.
