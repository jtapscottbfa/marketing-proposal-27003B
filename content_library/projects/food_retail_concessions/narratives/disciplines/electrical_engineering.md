The electrical engineering scope for the NEXCOM Food & Retail Concessions facility provided complete power distribution infrastructure for the new shell building and site at Naval Station Norfolk, Norfolk, Virginia. Despite the white-box delivery model, the electrical design was comprehensive — providing separately metered, independently serviceable power feeds to each vendor space scaled to accommodate high-demand commercial kitchen operations.

Primary power is supplied from the existing Naval Station 35kV distribution system, fed via new underground primary distribution from existing switch SW-296 to a 500 kVA, 34.5kV-to-208Y/120V feed-through pad-mounted transformer serving the building. Secondary services include a 100A, 208Y/120V service for house loads (exterior lighting, temporary interior lighting, and miscellaneous systems); a 400A, 208Y/120V individually metered service for Vendor Space #1; an 800A, 208Y/120V individually metered service for Vendor Space #2; and a 20A, 208Y/120V metered service for traffic signalization.

Exterior lighting design provides LED wall packs and pole-mounted area lights for the building perimeter and site. Temporary interior LED lighting was provided in both vendor spaces as a base-building installation. A Class I lightning protection system was designed per UFC 3-575-01, and an electrical grounding system was included. Design was governed by NFPA 70 (2020), UFC 3-575-01, and applicable Naval Station infrastructure standards.
