The architectural scope for the NEXCOM Food & Retail Concessions project encompassed design of a new single-story, approximately 5,500 net square foot (6,080 gross square foot) food and retail concessions shell building at Naval Station Norfolk, Norfolk, Virginia. The building houses two independently demised vendor spaces — approximately 2,000 SF and 3,500 SF — within a single structural envelope, each delivered as a white-box shell for future fit-out by individual food service operators.

The building is classified as Group A-2 (Assembly) occupancy under IBC 2018. The structural system is a single-story structural steel frame with metal wall panels on metal stud exterior framing, sheathed with glass mat gypsum board, curtain wall glazing at primary elevations, and a PVC membrane roofing system. Interior demising at the center bay is accomplished with stud wall partitions supported on the pile-supported concrete structural slab, providing structural separation between the two vendor spaces and the building's electrical room.

The facility incorporates a drive-thru facility coordinated with the civil site design. Sustainable design compliance was achieved under the Green Building Initiative DoD Guiding Principles Compliance framework for new construction. Anti-terrorism and force protection requirements per UFC 4-010-01 were addressed: the single-story configuration qualified as exempt under AT-FP Standard 6 (Very Low level of protection, Category I explosive weight). Design was governed by IBC 2018, UFC 1-200-01, UFC 4-010-01, ASHRAE 90.1-2019, and ABA Accessibility Standards for DoD Facilities.
