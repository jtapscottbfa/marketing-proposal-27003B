The structural engineering scope for the NEXCOM Food & Retail Concessions project addressed the design of a new single-story building on the challenging, soft-soil conditions of Naval Station Norfolk, Norfolk, Virginia — a low-lying waterfront peninsula in the Hampton Roads tidewater environment where compressible soils are common.

A deep foundation system was required. Timber piles were selected as the foundation type, and a concrete structural slab on piles was provided at the center bay of the building to support the stud wall demising partition between the two vendor spaces and the electrical room partition — areas requiring a more robust structural slab due to partition and equipment loads. The building superstructure is a structural steel frame supporting the metal wall panel exterior system and PVC roof assembly.

The single-story building configuration was analyzed under UFC 4-010-01 Anti-Terrorism / Force Protection criteria. The structure qualified as exempt under AT-FP Standard 6 given its Very Low level of protection (Category I explosive weight), which simplified structural hardening requirements. Structural design was governed by IBC 2018 and applicable UFC criteria. Foundation design was coordinated with the geotechnical discipline, which conducted the subsurface investigation in an environment complicated by confirmed PFAS-contaminated groundwater within the CERCLA Site 20 Land Use Control boundary.
