The fire protection engineering scope for the NEXCOM Food & Retail Concessions facility was shaped by the building's size classification, use group, and applicable UFC criteria, and by the white-box shell delivery approach that deferred tenant-triggered fire protection systems to individual vendor upfit contracts.

The building is classified as Group A-2 (Assembly) occupancy under IBC 2018. Per UFC 3-600-01 paragraph 4-8.2, a fire sprinkler system is not required for a standalone commissary or exchange building less than 8,000 square feet in area; accordingly, no sprinkler system was designed for or included in the base building. Portable fire extinguishers were specified per NFPA 101 (2021) as the primary active fire suppression measure within the base building scope.

Fire alarm and mass notification system design was deferred to individual tenant upfit contracts — each food service operator's fit-out triggers the applicable fire alarm requirements based on actual occupancy type, cooking equipment configuration, and tenant-specific code compliance obligations. Kitchen hood fire suppression systems were similarly deferred to vendor fit-out. The fire protection engineering discipline confirmed the sprinkler exemption criteria under UFC 3-600-01 §4-8.2, documented the life safety compliance approach for the base building, and coordinated the delineation of tenant-deferred fire protection scope with the overall project design approach. Design was governed by UFC 3-600-01, NFPA 101 (2021), and NFPA 72 (2019).
