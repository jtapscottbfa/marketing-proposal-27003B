The mechanical engineering scope at Honest Pastures comprised the complete design of a kitchen exhaust and make-up air system renovation at a single-story commercial food service facility in Virginia Beach, Virginia. The design team produced two mechanical drawings — a floor plan (M-101) and a roof plan (M-102) — documenting all new mechanical work at 1/4" = 1'-0" scale.

At the floor level, the design specified a CaptiveAire Type 1 kitchen exhaust hood (Model 5/30, 10'-½" wide × 5'-0" deep × 29-½" tall, 1,100 CFM, 208V 3-phase) positioned above a new commercial gas cooking line consisting of an ATOSA countertop hotplate/stove (64,000 BTU total), an ATOSA gas griddle (60,000 BTU total), and a Blodgett gas double-deck convection oven (3-phase, 6.0A). At the roof level, the design replaced the existing rooftop exhaust fan with a Dayton Model 4YF16 (approximately 1,800 CFM) and added a CaptiveAire variable-speed make-up air handling unit (Model A1G10, 2,000 CFM) connected to the hood via two 10" × 22" square-to-round duct transitions. The make-up air intake was located 10'-0" from the exhaust fan discharge per design general notes to prevent recirculation. The roof structure — metal decking and metal joists — is noted in the Sheet M-102 general notes.

Electrical connection requirements for the exhaust hood and convection oven are documented in mechanical construction notes; no separate electrical discipline drawings were produced as part of this design package. Gas rough-in for the cooking equipment was not addressed in the mechanical drawings.
