The Honest Pastures project is a kitchen exhaust system renovation at a commercial food service facility in Virginia Beach, Virginia. The design scope was mechanical: replacement of the existing rooftop exhaust fan and installation of a complete Type 1 kitchen exhaust and make-up air system, coordinated with the introduction of new commercial gas cooking equipment.

The mechanical design team produced floor plan and roof plan drawings documenting the full scope of new work. Key systems installed included a CaptiveAire Type 1 exhaust hood (10'-½" wide, 5'-0" deep, 1,100 CFM, 208V 3-phase), a Dayton rooftop exhaust fan (approximately 1,800 CFM), and a CaptiveAire variable-speed make-up air handling unit (2,000 CFM) connected to the hood via square-to-round ductwork transitions. The make-up air intake was positioned 10'-0" from the exhaust fan discharge to prevent recirculation. New gas-fired commercial cooking equipment — an ATOSA countertop hotplate/stove (64,000 BTU), an ATOSA gas griddle (60,000 BTU), and a Blodgett gas double-deck convection oven — was specified and coordinated with the exhaust system design. The building is single-story with a metal decking and metal joist roof structure.

The project was completed as a standalone design engagement, with final mechanical drawings issued July 22, 2021. The design firm served as prime consultant, responsible for the full mechanical design package.
