The Lynchburg Readiness Center HVAC Upgrades project provided complete mechanical and electrical design services for the replacement of the HVAC system at the Virginia National Guard's Lynchburg Readiness Center in Lynchburg, Virginia, for the Virginia Department of Military Affairs (DMA). The project was procured via the Virginia Construction and Professional Services Manual (CPSM) qualifications-based selection process with a not-to-exceed construction budget of $1,099,000. Design services encompassed a full HVAC system replacement—including removal and replacement of all rooftop units based on a CPSM life-cycle cost analysis, evaluation and replacement or cleaning of existing ductwork and piping, and installation of infectious disease prevention technologies (bipolar ionization, high-efficiency filtration, UV lighting, and cold-fusion systems). The design also included an upgrade of the Building Automation System (BAS) to incorporate humidity and temperature controls, HVAC zoning redesigned to reduce airborne particle migration, and associated electrical work to serve new equipment and restore interior lighting in affected ceiling systems. A site investigation performed in December 2022 documented existing electrical distribution infrastructure and confirmed field conditions prior to design. The team served as prime consultant under a design-bid-build contract structure, completing design services through final submittal in early 2024.
