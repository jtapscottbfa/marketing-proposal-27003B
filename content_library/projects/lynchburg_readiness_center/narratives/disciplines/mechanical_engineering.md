Mechanical engineering services for the Lynchburg Readiness Center HVAC Upgrades encompassed complete replacement of the facility's rooftop HVAC system for the Virginia Department of Military Affairs. Equipment selection was guided by a CPSM life-cycle cost analysis, ensuring replacement units met Commonwealth of Virginia procurement standards for long-term cost efficiency.

The mechanical design addressed the full distribution system, evaluating existing ductwork and piping for replacement or cleaning as warranted by field conditions. A central scope element was the integration of infectious disease prevention technologies: the design incorporated bipolar ionization, high-efficiency filtration, ultraviolet lighting, visible lighting, cold-fusion systems, and other emergent air-quality technologies to reduce the potential spread of airborne pathogens within the readiness center. HVAC zoning was redesigned to limit cross-zone migration of airborne particles.

The Building Automation System (BAS) was upgraded to provide humidity and temperature monitoring and control, enabling the client to actively manage indoor environmental conditions. A site investigation conducted in December 2022 confirmed field conditions and documented existing mechanical infrastructure ahead of design. Mechanical design services progressed through final Basis of Design submittal in January 2024.
