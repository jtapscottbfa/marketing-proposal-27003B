Electrical engineering services for the Lynchburg Readiness Center HVAC Upgrades supported complete replacement of the facility's rooftop HVAC system for the Virginia Department of Military Affairs. A site investigation performed in December 2022 documented the existing electrical distribution infrastructure. The facility is served by an 800-ampere, 208Y/120V, three-phase, four-wire main distribution panel (MDP) located in Mechanical Room A27, with additional 208Y/120V panelboards distributed throughout the building—including Panels LA, PB, PBR (Mechanical Room A27), PR (Corridor A29), PK (kitchen/storage area), and LB (Corridor A3).

The electrical design scope encompassed removal of all electrical devices, wiring, and conduit associated with existing HVAC equipment back to the serving panels; installation of new circuits for the replacement rooftop HVAC units; and temporary support of interior lighting fixtures in spaces where ceiling systems were demolished for ductwork replacement. Following ductwork installation, existing lighting fixtures were reinstalled in the reconstructed ceiling systems. The investigation also identified approximately 15 locations with displaced ceiling tiles resulting from a prior fire protection system installation, a missing ceiling grille in the kitchen area, and concerns about panel directory accuracy for several panels—conditions that directly informed the electrical design approach and construction sequencing.
