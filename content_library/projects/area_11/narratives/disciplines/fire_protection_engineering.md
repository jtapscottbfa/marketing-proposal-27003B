# Fire Protection Engineering — HPDTA 11 Area Electrical Utility Upgrade

Fire protection engineering was included in the HPDTA 11 Area Electrical Utility Upgrade design team as a required discipline for life safety calculations and construction-phase services on this DoD installation project.

Life safety calculations were a required deliverable at the 35%, 90%, and 100% design submittals, as specified in the Statement of Work. These calculations address the life safety design of the new buildings—the Area 11 substation PEMB and the four Electrical Control Buildings—in accordance with applicable codes and UFC standards.

During Title II construction administration services (Bid Option #1), the fire protection discipline was identified for periodic onsite visits at major system testing milestones, specifically including fire sprinkler and alarm system testing. This reflects the standard DoD construction oversight model where the A/E team provides multi-discipline technical monitoring at key construction verification points.

The fire protection scope on this project was modest relative to the electrical and civil scope—the new structures are small utility buildings with straightforward occupancy classifications. The discipline's primary contribution is code compliance verification for the life safety systems within the conditioned control buildings and the PEMB substation, and technical support during construction testing.
