# Mechanical Engineering — HPDTA 11 Area Electrical Utility Upgrade

Mechanical engineering scope on the HPDTA 11 Area Electrical Utility Upgrade was focused on two elements: engine generator fuel venting systems and HVAC conditioning for the Electrical Control Buildings.

For the Area 11 substation PEMB, the mechanical team designed the fuel venting mechanical systems for the new engine generator, ensuring safe venting of generator exhaust and fuel system gases in compliance with applicable codes and UFC standards.

For the four Electrical Control Buildings distributed along the perimeter fence line, HVAC conditioning was required by the nature of the equipment housed within each building. The fiber-optic camera electronics, UPS systems, and communications gear generate heat and require controlled temperature and humidity to maintain operational reliability. Each 12-foot by 12-foot building is designed to be fully heated and cooled to protect these mission-critical systems.

The mechanical scope was limited relative to the overall project—the primary design effort and technical complexity on this project resided in the electrical, civil, and structural disciplines. Mechanical services focused on the generator venting interface and the conditioning requirements for the small control buildings, with all mechanical calculations and specifications included in the 100% Basis of Design Report submitted in February 2026.
