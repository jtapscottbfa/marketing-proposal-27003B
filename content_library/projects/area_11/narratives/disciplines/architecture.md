# Architecture — HPDTA 11 Area Electrical Utility Upgrade

BFA's architectural team served as the prime discipline lead on the HPDTA 11 Area Electrical Utility Upgrade, responsible for cross-discipline coordination, meeting documentation, and coherent package assembly across all design submittal phases (35%, 90%, and 100% Release for Construction).

Architectural scope centered on two facility types. The first is an approximately 1,250-square-foot pre-engineered metal building (PEMB) to enclose the Area 11 generator, switchgear, and transformers. The PEMB features a galvanized steel deck roofing system supplied by the PEMB manufacturer with gutters on one side, hot-dipped galvanized superstructure, and a concrete foundation with slab-on-grade. The second facility type comprises four 12-foot by 12-foot (144 SF) Electrical Control Buildings distributed along the installation perimeter. These buildings employ exterior split-face CMU walls (painted) on conventional concrete foundations, with wood-framed roofs covered with asphalt fiberglass composite shingles (30-year minimum) and R-30 insulation. Interior finishes consist of painted water-resistant GWB over 2-inch rigid foam insulation board and vapor barrier against the masonry walls (R-19 wall assembly). Each control building is fully conditioned due to the heat-sensitive electronic equipment housed within. Adjacent to each control building, BFA designed an aluminum screen wall enclosing an exterior generator on a raised concrete equipment pad.

Design was executed to UFC 3-101-01 (Architecture), UFC-1-300-09N (Navy and Marine Corps Design Procedures), NCBC 2018, IBC 2018, and ABA 2015. With no existing site drawings available for the 11 Area, BFA was responsible for developing all existing-condition documentation from field survey.
