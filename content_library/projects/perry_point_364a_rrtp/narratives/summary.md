The design team — operating as the Hartigan, Bowman, Foster & Associates (HBFA) SDVOSB joint venture — provided architectural and engineering design services to convert a 17,000 SF former "Locked Inpatient Psychiatry STAR Unit" inside Building 364 at the Perry Point VA Medical Center into a new Mental Health Residential Rehabilitation Treatment Program (RRTP) facility. The renovation transformed the unit from an institutional, restrictive environment into a recovery-oriented, residential-style setting per the VA Mental Health Design Guide standards in the VA Technical Information Library (TIL). Scope included replacement of anti-ligature plumbing fixtures with commercial hotel-type fixtures and finishes; replacement of saloon-style bathroom doors with standard doors and hardware; demolition and rebuild of the Nurses' Station as an open four-workstation design maintaining line-of-sight to all wings and the Day Room/Recreation Area; conversion of North-wing bedrooms to offices and group meeting rooms; installation of patient code access locks, monitored exit devices, and CCTV at the Nurses' Station; and acoustic and finish renovation across the Day Room/Recreation Area, bedrooms, bathrooms, and circulation spaces. The design was developed to IBC 2015, NFPA 101 (2015), ABAAS, and VA TIL Mental Health Design Guide standards. The 95% Submittal Basis of Design was issued May 7, 2019 (BFA Project 18-069B, VA NRM No. 512A5-19-303).
