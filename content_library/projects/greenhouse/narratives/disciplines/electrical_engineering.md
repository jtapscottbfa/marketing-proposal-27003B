The electrical engineering scope on the Melwood Greenhouses project encompassed full power distribution design for three new controlled-environment greenhouse structures in Nanjemoy, Maryland. Working as prime engineering consultant, the design team produced a complete signed-and-sealed five-sheet electrical drawing set (project 23-017, August 2023) covering site power, distribution, emergency standby, and all agricultural equipment branch circuits.

The design established a new SMECO pad-mount utility service feeding a 175A, 1-phase main distribution panel, with individual 100A sub-panels serving each greenhouse. Two 30×100 ft structures and one 30×50 ft structure each received dedicated circuits for unit heaters, dehumidifiers, exhaust and circulation fans, vent motors, intake dampers, sump pumps, and GFCI weatherproof receptacles. A 60kW propane-fueled standby generator with a 300A automatic transfer switch provides continuous power during utility outages, critical to protecting agricultural operations. All panelboards are NEMA 3R rated; wiring methods include GRS conduit for indoor and outdoor runs, with PVC Schedule 40 where indicated on plans. The electrical specifications — incorporated directly on drawing sheet E-801 — address NEC and NFPA 70 compliance, equipment labeling, grounding, and field quality control requirements including 600V wiring tests and GFCI verification.
