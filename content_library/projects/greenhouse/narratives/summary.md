The Melwood Greenhouses project involved electrical engineering design for three new controlled-environment greenhouse structures at the client's agricultural facility in Nanjemoy, Maryland. The design team served as prime engineering consultant, delivering a complete signed-and-sealed electrical drawing set (project 23-017, sealed August 2023) covering site power distribution, panelboard design, emergency standby power, and all agricultural equipment connections.

The electrical design provides utility service from a new SMECO pad-mount transformer through a 175A main distribution panel (MDP), distributing power via individual feeders to three dedicated 100A sub-panels — one per greenhouse. Two 30×100 ft structures (GH1, GH2) and one 30×50 ft structure (GH3) are each served with power for climate-control equipment including unit heaters (160K BTU in GH1/GH2; 80K BTU in GH3), dehumidifiers, exhaust fans, vent motors, circulation fans, and sump pump connections. GFCI weatherproof duplex receptacles are distributed throughout. A 60kW propane-fueled standby generator with a 300A automatic transfer switch ensures continuous power for agricultural operations during utility outages. The design conforms to NEC and NFPA 70 requirements, with GRS conduit for indoor and outdoor applications and weatherproof enclosures throughout the site.
