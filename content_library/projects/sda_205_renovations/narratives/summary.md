SDA-205 Repairs and Renovation encompassed comprehensive upgrades to a circa-1940s, single-story Type II-B steel-framed warehouse at Naval Support Activity (NSA) Norfolk, Virginia, occupied by the DLA Disposition Service for logistics and disposition operations. Spanning approximately 118,029 gross square feet across five storage bays, the project delivered full-facility renovation under a NAVFAC Mid-Atlantic IDIQ task order (Contract No. N40085-17-D-0347) with an estimated construction cost of $6.78 million and a program amount of $7.32 million. The design team was led by prime A-E Dills Architects, P.C., with the Final Design Submittal completed 3 March 2021.

Renovation scope addressed every major building system across all five bays. Mechanical work demolished all existing systems and installed three heat pump split systems for conditioned office and restroom areas, radiant heat in the warehouse bay, and exhaust for restrooms and storage rooms. Electrical work consolidated two aging services into a single distribution system, replaced all interior and exterior lighting with LED fixtures, and implemented wireless lighting controls with daylight harvesting. Plumbing scope demolished and replaced all fixtures, piping, and DWV systems in the renovated bays, adding emergency eyewash stations and a new fire main connection to the 9th Street water main. Fire protection provided a fully automatic wet-pipe and dry-pipe sprinkler system throughout all five bays, a new 1,250 gpm electric fire pump, and a combined fire alarm and base-wide mass notification interface. Hazardous material abatement addressed confirmed asbestos-containing floor tile, lead-based paint, and mercury-containing lamps identified during pre-design surveys. The project was procured as Design-Bid-Build.
