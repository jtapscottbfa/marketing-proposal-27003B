Electrical engineering scope for SDA-205 addressed full replacement of an aging dual-service distribution infrastructure and all interior and exterior lighting systems throughout the 118,029 SF facility. The existing building was served by two separate electrical services: a 300 KVA, 11.5 kV-to-208Y/120V pad-mounted transformer with an 800A MCB main distribution panel in Bay 3, and a 225 KVA, 11.5 kV-to-208Y/120V pad-mounted transformer with an exterior 800A MCB distribution panel. The design consolidated these two services into a single service, retaining the 300 KVA transformer and associated 800A MCB. The overhead service lateral serving Service #2 was removed back to the exterior distribution panel. A new secondary service lateral was added from the 300 KVA transformer to serve the new 60 HP electric fire pump. New 208Y/120V branch circuit panelboards were provided in the new administrative spaces constructed in Bays 1 and 5.

All interior and exterior lighting was demolished and replaced with LED fixtures throughout: high-bay and low-bay fixtures for warehouse areas, recessed troffers for office and restroom spaces, and pendant fixtures as applicable. A wireless lighting control system with daylight harvesting sensors and occupancy controls was implemented to meet NAVFAC energy requirements. Battery backup inverter systems provide emergency egress lighting per life safety code.
