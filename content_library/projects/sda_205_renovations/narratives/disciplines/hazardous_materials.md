Hazardous materials scope for SDA-205 encompassed pre-design surveys and abatement design for multiple categories of hazardous materials present in the existing warehouse facility, which was constructed in the early 1940s. Survey findings identified the following materials requiring abatement or assessment:

Asbestos-containing materials (ACM): 9-inch green floor tile and mastic (bottom layer) totaling 504 SF were confirmed as ACM and required abatement prior to construction activities disturbing the floor. Lead-based paint was identified within the facility. Mercury-containing fluorescent lamps were confirmed present and required disposal per applicable regulations. PCB-containing light fixture ballasts were assessed; laboratory testing confirmed assumed non-PCB status, removing PCB hazardous waste disposal from the construction scope.

Abatement scope was incorporated into the overall renovation sequence to precede MEP demolition, electrical fixture replacement, and concrete floor resurfacing operations that would disturb the identified materials. Hazardous materials scope was coordinated with the architectural demolition, electrical, and plumbing disciplines given the direct intersection of ACM floor materials with flooring resurfacing and fixture replacement activities throughout the five warehouse bays.
