Fire protection engineering scope for SDA-205 designed a complete new fire suppression and life safety system for the approximately 118,029 SF Type II-B warehouse across all five bays. The facility had no prior compliant fire suppression system. A fully automatic supervised wet-pipe and dry-pipe sprinkler system was designed throughout in compliance with applicable NFPA standards; dry-pipe configurations were used in areas where freezing conditions preclude wet-pipe coverage.

A new 1,250 gpm / 60 psi electric fire pump was required because available municipal water supply — measured at 69 psi static, 54 psi residual, and 1,130 gpm flow at a hydrant flow test conducted 1 September 2020 — was insufficient to meet system demand for the full facility footprint. A new fire pump room was designed with one-hour fire barrier separation per NFPA 20, and a new 60 HP electrical service lateral was coordinated with the electrical scope to provide a dedicated supply to the pump.

The fire alarm and mass notification system provides combined voice evacuation and installation-wide mass notification capability. A Federal Signal UVRI transceiver interfaces with the base-wide mass notification system at NSA Norfolk. Reporting to the central fire alarm station is provided via a zoned telegraphic transmitter (DET 16). Manual pull stations are specified at all exits; carbon monoxide detection is provided in rooms containing fuel-fired equipment; duct smoke detectors are designed per NFPA 90A.
