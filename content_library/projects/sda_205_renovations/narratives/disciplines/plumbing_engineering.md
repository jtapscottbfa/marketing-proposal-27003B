Plumbing engineering scope for SDA-205 involved complete demolition of all existing plumbing systems in the renovated bays and installation of new domestic water, sanitary drainage, and support systems. New restrooms were constructed in Bays 1 and 5, each served by new domestic cold and hot water distribution piping, sanitary DWV systems, and restroom fixtures including floor-mounted water closets, wall-mounted lavatories, urinals, and countertop sinks. Electric water heaters were provided for domestic hot water service. Thermostatic mixing valves set to 110°F were incorporated at restroom lavatories to meet applicable scald protection requirements.

Emergency eyewash stations were installed in Bays 1 and 5, reflecting the DLA warehouse occupancy and potential chemical exposure hazard associated with industrial logistics operations. A new 8-inch ductile iron fire main was cut into the existing water main on 9th Street adjacent to the site, providing a new underground service connection to supply the facility's new fire protection systems. This utility connection was coordinated with NAVFAC base water distribution infrastructure and the concurrent fire protection scope.
