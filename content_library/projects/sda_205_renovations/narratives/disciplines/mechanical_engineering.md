Mechanical engineering scope for SDA-205 encompassed complete demolition of all existing mechanical systems throughout the facility and installation of a new HVAC system designed for the renovated mixed-occupancy warehouse. All existing mechanical equipment was demolished, and replacement systems were selected to match the distinct operational requirements of each bay type.

Three heat pump split systems were installed serving the office and restroom areas in Bays 1 and 5, providing heating and cooling for the conditioned administrative and support spaces added as part of the renovation. Bay 4 received a new radiant heating system suited to the open warehouse storage environment, where distributed air systems would be impractical in a high-bay configuration. A dedicated exhaust system was provided for restroom and storage room ventilation throughout the facility. Motorized two-position outside air dampers were incorporated to control ventilation in compliance with applicable NAVFAC and mechanical code requirements.

The HVAC approach reflects the mixed occupancy of the facility: conditioned split systems for administrative and restroom areas requiring climate control, and radiant heat for warehouse bays where air distribution would conflict with racking layouts and high-bay clearances. Equipment was selected for compatibility with the 208V/120V electrical service configuration established under the concurrent electrical scope.
