Architectural scope for SDA-205 addressed the repair and renovation of a circa-1940s, single-story Type II-B steel-framed warehouse at Naval Support Activity Norfolk, Virginia. The facility encompasses approximately 118,029 gross square feet organized into five warehouse storage bays. Primary occupancy is Group S-1 Storage with accessory Business Group B office areas. The renovation addressed the building interior, finishes, and new construction of office and support spaces while maintaining the facility's active warehouse mission for the DLA Disposition Service.

Work included resurfacing of approximately 116,000 SF of existing concrete warehouse flooring and demolition of overhead coiling doors per the final design scope. New construction in Bays 1 and 5 provided restroom facilities and administrative spaces including office, customer service area, and conference/training room functions. Interior finishes throughout the renovated spaces were upgraded to current NAVFAC standards. An existing Video Teleconferencing system was relocated from the existing conference room to the newly constructed conference/training room. Architectural scope was coordinated across all five bays with concurrent MEP, fire protection, structural, civil, and hazardous materials disciplines to achieve a code-compliant, fully coordinated renovation of an active federal warehouse facility under NAVFAC IDIQ design-bid-build delivery.
