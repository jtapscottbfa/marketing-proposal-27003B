Civil engineering scope for SDA-205 encompassed site utility design in support of the comprehensive facility renovation. The primary civil utility work involved design of a new 8-inch ductile iron fire main connected by cut-in to the existing water main on 9th Street adjacent to the Naval Support Activity Norfolk site. The new fire main routes into the facility to supply the new fire protection system and was coordinated with NAVFAC base utilities, base water distribution infrastructure, and the concurrent plumbing and fire protection engineering scopes. The underground utility connection represents the principal new civil infrastructure element for the project.

Sub-disciplines included civil site design and civil utilities. Site civil scope addressed exterior improvements supporting the renovated facility's operational needs. Specific site civil design details — grading, drainage, pavement, and other exterior site elements beyond the fire main connection — are not captured in the available source record and represent a gap in extracted project documentation.
