The New River Tap House Renovation is a commercial interior renovation of a two-story taphouse and bar in Virginia Beach, Virginia. The design team served as prime consultant, providing architecture, mechanical, plumbing, and electrical engineering services for the permit set, dated March 2022 (BFA project no. 22-012).

The project focused on the second floor, with the first floor retained in its existing condition. Second-floor work included selective demolition — removal of an existing wall and covering of existing skylights per owner requirements — followed by new construction of a uni-sex ADA-compliant restroom and reconfigured bar counter areas. The new restroom was designed to full ADA accessibility standards, with compliant clearances for the toilet, lavatory, and grab bar configurations, plus a full complement of stainless steel accessories. Bar counter zones feature knee walls with 44-inch-high countertops and a bar sink and three-compartment sink serving the food-and-beverage program. Interior partitions were framed in 6-inch metal studs with acoustical batt insulation and 5/8-inch gypsum board. A 2×2 drop-in ceiling grid was introduced in the restroom, with mechanical exhaust and electrical lighting integrated by the engineering disciplines.
