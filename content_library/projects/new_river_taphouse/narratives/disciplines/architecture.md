Architecture was the lead discipline on the New River Tap House Renovation, a two-story commercial taphouse and bar in Virginia Beach, Virginia. The permit drawing set, dated March 2022, was produced under BFA project no. 22-012, with the design team serving as prime consultant.

Architectural scope on the second floor included selective demolition — removal of an existing partition wall and covering of existing skylights per the owner's direction — followed by new interior construction. Key deliverables included a uni-sex ADA-compliant restroom designed to meet required accessibility clearances for the toilet, lavatory, and grab bar configurations, with stainless steel accessory packages and tile wainscoting. Bar counter zones were laid out with 4-foot partition walls and 44-inch-high knee walls with countertops. Interior partitions were specified as 6-inch metal studs at 16 inches on center, 5/8-inch gypsum board, and 6-inch acoustical batt insulation with slotted deflection track — an assembly providing both structural accommodation and sound control appropriate to a bar environment.

Architectural deliverables included a cover sheet, general notes and legend sheet, demolition floor plans, new-work floor plans at both overall and enlarged scales, and a detail, elevations, and wall section sheet. The first floor was documented as existing-to-remain. Architectural drawings coordinated MEP scope through explicit cross-references to mechanical, plumbing, and electrical drawings produced by the engineering disciplines.
